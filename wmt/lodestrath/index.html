The house doctrine

Order. Direction. Discretion.

The name says it: lodestar, the star that guides, and strath, the wide valley a river cuts through the hills. Together they mean the guiding line through the valley. The news cycle gives you more noise every day. LODESTRATH gives you, once a quarter, the line through it.

I · ORDER

Depth over speed.

We publish once a quarter, so each Quadrant can go further than any daily brief: full scenarios, model evidence and a source behind every line.

II · DIRECTION

Dated, not vague.

Every assessment comes with the dates that will test it. You know which way things are moving and when you will find out.

III · DISCRETION

No hype, ever.

No tips, no price targets, no promised returns. Facts and assessment stay apart, and every assessment carries a stated level of confidence.

The LODESTRATH standard

Trust is the product.

We don't show client logos or testimonials we can't verify. Instead we commit to six rules, in public, and every Quadrant is held to them.

Rule 1

Facts and views never mix

Reported facts and our assessment sit in separate, labelled parts of every plate.

Rule 2

Confidence, stated

Each assessment carries a graded confidence level, so you know how hard to lean on it.

Rule 3

Every claim sourced

Rule 5

We publish information and analysis. We don't give investment advice or price targets, and we don't trade on our own analysis.

Questions

Before you subscribe.

Why quarterly?

Because depth needs time. A quarter is long enough to see a pattern and short enough to act on it. Each Quadrant gives you the map, the scenarios and the dated signposts for the next ninety days, and the week-6 check keeps it current.

Is LODESTRATH investment advice?

No. LODESTRATH publishes information and analysis for professional decision-making. It gives no recommendations to buy or sell, no price targets and no promised returns. The Decision Deck asks questions; it doesn't give instructions.

How is it different from ISOLINE's quarterly report?

ISOLINE's reports read the world across all regions for a general reader. A Quadrant goes deep on one desk, with scenarios, model evidence, signposts and a public scorecard, for a professional remit.

Can my whole team read it?

Each licence is for one named reader. For teams of three or more, a house licence brings volume pricing and seat management.

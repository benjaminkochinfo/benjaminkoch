2 · Scope

This notice applies to the ISOLINE front-end at benjaminkoch.info/wmt/wm_terminal.html, the sample briefs under /wmt/landing/, and these legal pages. ISOLINE is a project and brand of Dr. Benjamin Koch.

The live WMT terminal at /wmt/terminal.html is a related static page on the same host. This notice describes the ISOLINE front-end. The main site at benjaminkoch.info/privacy.html has its own, more general privacy information.

This site is designed around data minimisation. It does not use first-party analytics, advertising trackers, social-media pixels or profiling, and it does not require an account to be read.

3 · Hosting · GitHub Pages

The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation. GitHub Pages content is commonly delivered through GitHub’s own infrastructure and a content-delivery network operated by Fastly, Inc. (USA).

When you request a page, the hosting and CDN providers necessarily process technical connection and server-log data in order to deliver the site securely and reliably. This typically includes your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested URL, the referring page if any, and browser or device information (user agent). We do not have a separate application server and we do not run our own log analysis.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing, securing and maintaining the website). GitHub and Microsoft are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Further information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

4 · Cookies and browser storage

The ISOLINE front-end itself does not set cookies and does not use localStorage, sessionStorage or similar browser storage. No first-party analytics cookie is present.

Third-party services whose servers are contacted when you load the page (in particular the unpkg library CDN, which is served via Cloudflare) may process technical request data on their own domain and, in some cases, set a short-lived technical cookie such as Cloudflare’s bot-management cookie (__cf_bm) on the unpkg.com domain. That cookie is not set by ISOLINE and is not read by this site. Google Fonts, as configured here, is not used to set advertising cookies.

6 · Live public data sources

Selected modules of the front-end request public data directly from your browser so that figures can be computed on your device. We do not operate these APIs and we do not store the responses on our servers. The providers receive technical request data and, where a query includes a place, the coordinates or search term you chose. Legal basis for the default, page-load requests (earthquakes, natural events, market prices, FX, satellite catalogue): Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in showing live public context on an intelligence desk). Legal basis for place search and weather at a chosen location: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your action of searching or computing a place). Several of these providers are in the United States or other third countries; see International transfers.

CelesTrak

celestrak.org

Provider: CelesTrak (United States), public satellite catalogue. Purpose: two-line element sets for the “stations” and “visual” groups, used in your browser to estimate overhead satellite passes and the ISS position on the live wire. The request does not include your location.

U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)

earthquake.usgs.gov

Provider: U.S. Geological Survey, United States Department of the Interior. Purpose: the public M2.5+ earthquake feed for the past seven days, used on the live wire, This Week counters and the Watched exposure card. The request does not include your location; nearby events are filtered in your browser.

NASA EONET

eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov

Provider: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), Goddard Space Flight Center, United States. Purpose: the public feed of open natural events (last seven days), used on the live wire, This Week and the Watched card. The request does not include your location.

Open-Meteo

api.open-meteo.com · geocoding-api.open-meteo.com

Provider: OpenMeteo GmbH, Hintere Schilligmatte 6, 6463 Bürglen (UR), Switzerland. Purpose: (1) after you search for a place, the geocoding API resolves the name you typed; (2) when a place is computed, the forecast API is called with that latitude and longitude for current temperature and wind. Open-Meteo states that free-API logs, which may include IP addresses and coordinates, are deleted after 90 days. Further information: Open-Meteo terms and privacy.

CoinGecko

Provider: the operator of CoinGecko (Gecko Labs / CoinGecko, Singapore). Purpose: illustrative Bitcoin and Ethereum spot prices and 24-hour change on the live wire. The request does not include an account or an identifying query beyond the public price endpoint.

Editorial source links in briefs, This Week and the elections tracker point to independent third-party websites. Those sites are only contacted if you follow a link, and their own privacy practices then apply.

7 · Optional device location

The Watched section offers a “Use my location” control. It is never activated automatically. Only if you click it will your browser, subject to your permission, provide approximate coordinates via the W3C Geolocation API. Those coordinates are used in your browser to compute the exposure card and are sent to Open-Meteo’s forecast API as described above. They are not written to cookies or browser storage by this site, and they are not sent to CelesTrak, USGS or NASA; those feeds are downloaded as global datasets and filtered locally.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), which you give by confirming the browser prompt. You can deny or later revoke location access in your browser or device settings.

8 · Newsletter · Brevo

If you subscribe to ISOLINE emails (the waitlist and free weekly signal), we process the email address you enter, the fact and time of your signup and confirmation, and a locale value of “en”. A hidden honeypot field is included only to discourage automated submissions and should be left empty. A cadence preference is shown on the form for your orientation; it is not currently transmitted to the mailing list.

The form is submitted to Brevo, operated by Sendinblue SAS, 106 boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France (trading as Brevo). Sending is configured with double opt-in: you will first receive a confirmation email from ISOLINE <briefs@benjaminkoch.info>, and you are added to the “ISOLINE Briefs” list only after you click the confirmation link. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email, or by writing to briefs@benjaminkoch.info.

Brevo logs the signup and the confirmation (typically including timestamp and IP address) as proof of consent. By default, Brevo also records whether and when a newsletter is opened and which links are clicked (standard email delivery and engagement measurement, often via a tracking pixel and instrumented links). You may object to this engagement tracking by using plain-text viewing where offered, disabling remote images in your mail client, or contacting us; objection does not affect the sending of the newsletter itself.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing up to that point. For as long as we must demonstrate that consent was obtained, a record of the opt-in may be retained on the basis of Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR. Further information: Brevo Privacy Policy.

11 · Legal bases at a glance

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — consent: newsletter signup; optional device location; place search and the weather request that follows a chosen place.

Some of the providers listed above are in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area (GitHub / Microsoft, Fastly, Google, Cloudflare in front of unpkg, USGS, NASA, CelesTrak, and CoinGecko in Singapore; Open-Meteo is in Switzerland, which is covered by an EU adequacy decision). Switzerland benefits from an adequacy decision.

13 · Retention

  • Hosting and CDN logs are retained only according to the providers’ own policies; we do not keep a separate copy.
  • Live API responses exist in your browser’s working memory for the session and are not stored by us.
  • Device coordinates, if you share them, are not stored by us.
  • Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Proof of consent may be kept for as long as claims in relation to the signup remain possible. Brevo’s own logs follow its retention rules.
  • Contract and invoice data related to subscriptions are kept for the statutory commercial and tax retention periods applicable in Germany (generally up to ten years).
  • Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request, and thereafter only where a legal retention duty applies.

The name

Sidera. The stars, and the line between them.

Sidera is Latin for “the stars”. Two everyday words are traditionally traced back to it: consider, to look closely, as if with the stars, and desire, to miss the stars you once steered by. SIDERA lives between the two. Each month it helps you look closely at your life, and it helps you find again what you were steering by before the noise.

I · LIGHT

Reflection, not prediction.

We don't tell you what will happen. We give you a clear frame for what is already moving in you, and the days on which to stop and look.

II · LINE

Real sky, precise work.

Every date in the letter is a real event in the sky, calculated, never invented. Every insight comes from a named framework, not from mood.

III · STILLNESS

No fear, no fate.

No warnings, no dark omens, no “do this or else”. Each letter is written to leave you calmer and clearer than it found you.

The SIDERA standard

Trust is the light.

No testimonials we can't verify, no promises about your future. Instead, six house rules, published here, and every letter is held to them.

Rule 1

Reflection, never prediction

We don't forecast events, people or outcomes. We offer a frame for looking at your month, and you draw the conclusions.

Rule 2

Real sky, never invented

Every date is a real astronomical event, calculated and listed in The Ledger. No “energies” on dates where nothing happens.

Rule 3

Prompts, not instructions

The letter asks; it doesn't command. You are the authority on your own life, and the letter is written that way.

Rule 4

No fear, no fate

No warnings, no omens, no “difficult months”. Pressure Points name where friction tends to show up, and always what helps.

Rule 5

Nothing sold inside a letter

No upsells, no affiliate links, no “book a session” banners. The letter is the whole product.

Rule 6

Five answers, and you can leave

Privacy by design. Cancel at any time through the billing portal, and on request we delete your five answers.

Questions

Before you subscribe.

Is SIDERA astrology?

It uses the sky the way people have always used it: as a calendar for turning inward. Your sign and the real dates of the month give the letter its rhythm and its symbolic frame. The substance comes from Dr. Benjamin Koch's frameworks, so you don't need to believe in astrology to get a great deal out of it.

How is it different from a horoscope or a card reading?

A horoscope tells you what might happen to you. SIDERA helps you see what is already happening in you. There is no forecast and no draw of chance. Instead you get a fixed eight-plate format, real dates, named frameworks, and a Reflection Log that lets you see your own change over the year.

Can I stay anonymous?

In your letters, yes. They are written to the nickname you choose, and a private email alias works fine. We never ask for your date of birth, your birth time or your story. Stripe's checkout asks only for what your card needs (no home address); those details stay with the payment and never appear in, or shape, your letter.

When does my first letter arrive?

The first letters go out on Monday, 4 January 2027, and then on the first Monday of every month. Each one is written for you after you subscribe. If you join mid-month, your letter for the current month arrives within a few days. Your welcome email asks which path you would like and what name to write to.

Which path should I choose?

If you came here from the Clarity Institute, or your questions are about decisions and direction, start with the Clarity Path. If you came from The Circle of Synaptics, or your questions are about attention and inner life, start with the Synaptic Path. You can switch at the start of any month, or take both with the Inner Circle.

Do I need to know the books or the frameworks?

No. Each letter explains what it draws on in plain words, and The Ledger names its sources. If you know the work already, you will recognise it on every page.

Is this therapy or advice?

No. SIDERA is for reflection and self-development. It is not medical, psychological, financial or legal advice, and it does not replace care from a qualified professional.

How do I cancel?

Any time, through the secure billing portal linked in your welcome email. There is no minimum term on monthly subscriptions. Yearly subscriptions run to the end of the year you paid for.

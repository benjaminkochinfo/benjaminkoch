GEO-ECON · LEAD

Three accounts of one strait, and the one that keeps a ledger

Tehran says Hormuz stays shut until Washington accepts its terms. Washington says traffic is 80 to 100% of normal. The Revolutionary Guards say their mines stopped three tankers. Only one of these is a count.

On Tuesday, three official voices described the Strait of Hormuz. Each was confident, and they did not agree with one another. The lesson is not that one of them is lying. It is that a statement and a count are different instruments, and only one of them is built to be checked.

**Tehran's version.** Foreign minister Abbas Araghchi told Iraqi television that indirect talks with the United States continue through Pakistan and Qatar, and that the strait will stay closed unless Washington accepts Iran's conditions. Iran has offered a seven-day plan under which the waterway would fully reopen on the sixth day. Araghchi said Washington had accepted the conditions but wanted "additional provisions". "The negotiating table is still there," he said.

Before you close the tab

One thing you can do this week

This week, pick one public count you rely on, such as a weather warning map, a fuel-price site or a weekly shipping tally, and note when it is next updated. Météo-France, for example, updates its warnings at least at 06:00 and 16:00 1. Knowing when the next number arrives makes the statements in between easier to set aside.

A habit, not advice. Nothing here tells you what to buy, sign or switch.

What went right

In Congo's Ebola response, the laboratories are getting faster. Situation report 157 records 500 samples analysed in a day, against 423 on 11 October, an increase of 18.2%, while the share testing positive fell from 20.8% to 19.4% 2. Twenty more people recovered that day, bringing the total to 2,550 2. More tests and a slightly lower positive rate are what a response gaining ground looks like in the early stages.

The day in one sentence

Three capitals described one strait three ways while the ship count said open but thin, companies settled the market with their quarterly reports, and a €220 billion figure in Berlin turned out to have a footnote.

In euros at home

The Deutschlandticket costs €63 a month and rises to €66.80 in January 3. Desk arithmetic: that is €3.80 more a month, or €45.60 more a year for someone who keeps it all twelve months. A single fare in Berlin is €4 4, so sixteen single trips a month cost €64, and the seventeenth takes you past the new ticket price. The Länder transport ministers are discussing a tariff that would charge occasional riders by the trip and stop at the ticket price 3.

Desk arithmetic on the cited fares. Local fares differ by region and transport association. Nothing here is advice.

Small words

Special committee

When a company's leader or largest owner stands on both sides of a deal, the board can hand the decision to a special committee made up only of independent directors, people with no stake in the outcome beyond their duty to shareholders. The conflicted person stays out of the room. SpaceX and Tesla each set one up on Tuesday to look at a merger, and Elon Musk said he cannot take part because he is "a conflicted party" 5.

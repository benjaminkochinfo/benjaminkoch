MONEY · DESK NOTE

France's ten-year yield wrote the evening comment

OATs jumped more than ten basis points toward 4.89%; the premium over Bunds widened to about 145 basis points. The euro hovered near $1.12. That is the market's after-action report.

Money, FX & Committees Dr. Benjamin Koch / The Desk· Monday, 19 October 2026, 17:43 CEST· 2 min read·3 sources

ShareXLinkedInWhatsAppTelegramEmail

French ten-year yields rose more than ten basis points on Monday, trading close to 4.89%, while the spread over German Bunds widened to about 145 basis points — the highest in two weeks on that report1. Other desks saw the French ten-year near 4.875% with Bunds around 3.43%3. The euro slipped toward $1.1221,3. None of those figures require a press conference.

The economic backdrop was already tight: fiscal strain, a 2027 presidential race, and a minority government that still has to pass a budget and a deficit path2. Weekend unrest layered political risk on top. Deutsche Bank’s Jim Reid called it “another layer of political risk for markets”1. French bank shares, which feel sovereign stress quickly, fell harder than the index1,3.

The constructive reading is institutional, not fatalistic. Spreads widen when buyers demand compensation for uncertainty; they narrow when the uncertainty is reduced by dated plans — curfew hours, operational reviews, budget arithmetic. The screen is already doing the homework. The government still has to hand in its own.

What to watch

  • OAT–Bund spread into the evening close and Tuesday open.
  • Whether Lecornu's minority government can still map a path on the 2027 budget2.
  • Banque de France commentary versus the screen — Moulin already called political uncertainty the driver of higher French yields2.

In Colonial Hills, a neighbourhood of 1960s homes in Southaven, Mississippi, estate agents acting for MZX, the subsidiary that runs xAI's power plant there, are offering to buy houses on condition that owners do not join a class action over the plant's noise, The Guardian reports [1]. Five homes have been bought since 9 October, according to county records cited by the paper; one resident says an agent mentioned a target of 418 [1]. The average house in the area costs about $180,000 [1].

The company denies the claims in the lawsuit and has said it wants to be a good neighbour, the paper notes [1]. Southaven's mayor says city monitors have not found a breach of the local 70-decibel limit, and calls the buyout partly a goodwill gesture [1]. A permanent plant is due to replace the temporary turbines by next summer [1]. The machinery of the dispute is ordinary: decibel readings, county deeds, and a contract clause.

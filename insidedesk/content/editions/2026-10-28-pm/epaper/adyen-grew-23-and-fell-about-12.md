Adyen, the Dutch payments company, said third-quarter net revenue rose 23% to €738.1 million and processed volume 30% to €451.1 billion [1], both above analysts' estimates, Reuters reports [2]. It then cut its 2026 EBITDA margin target to 50% from around 52% and dropped its medium-term margin objective "to retain flexibility" while it steps up investment [1], [2]. The shares fell about 12% in early trading, taking the year's loss to roughly 40% [2].

The company kept its revenue growth target of 21%–23% for 2026 [1]. The market was not punishing growth; it was repricing a promise. A target withdrawn is information too. Nothing here is investment advice.

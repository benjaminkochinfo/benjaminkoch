The United States has told several Gulf Arab countries to be ready for a short but intensive military offensive after the 3 November midterm elections, The Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday evening, citing people briefed on the discussions [1]. According to the report, a Patriot air-defence battery is being sent from Texas and troops are being moved away from bases exposed to Iranian fire [1]. The White House declined to comment on "sensitive military plans that may or may not exist" and said Donald Trump kept "all options on the table" [1].

The same day, the head of the UN nuclear watchdog told the Security Council something less dramatic and more checkable. "Since June 2025, we have not identified any activity to repair or reconstruct any of the facilities damaged in the military attacks of 2025 and 2026," Rafael Grossi said, while asking Iran for access to all its sites [1]. The agency still cannot verify the 440.9 kilograms of 60% enriched uranium Iran held before the June 2025 war [1].

And the bill keeps arriving: Pentagon data reviewed by Reuters puts US spending on the war above $47 billion, more than 25% above the Defense Department's July estimate, including over $29 billion to replace munitions [1]. A leaked plan is a signal; an inspector's report and a munitions invoice are records. If you want to know where this is going, read the records first.

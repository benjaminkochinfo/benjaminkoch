In its 10:30 CET outlook on Wednesday, the Deutscher Wetterdienst said that where skies clear tonight, mainly in the southern half of Germany, there may be isolated light frost down to −1 °C [1]. It also warned of dense fog in places over central and southern Germany, with visibility below 150 metres [1].

What follows is a quiet spell. For Thursday to Saturday the service expects high pressure, weak winds, thick fog and low cloud, and highs of about 8 °C under the fog and up to 14 °C where the sun breaks through [1]. There may be more light frost on Friday night and in the southern uplands on Saturday night [1].

If you garden, bring tender pots in tonight. If you drive early, allow for fog. A dull week is not news, and it doesn't need to be.

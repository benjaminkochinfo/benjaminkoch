In the early hours of Wednesday a young man rushed at a police officer outside Israel's embassy in Belgrade with two knives and was shot dead, Serbia's interior minister Ivica Dacic said, calling it a "terrorist attack" [1]. The attacker, a Serbian minor, was being monitored as a known supporter of Islamic State [1]. The officer had a minor wound to the face and needed no hospital care; Israel said no embassy staff were hurt and the mission was closed [1]. Serbia raised its threat level to "red alert", with more checks at infrastructure and crowded places [1]. It is the second attack on the embassy in under three years, after a crossbow attack in June 2024 [1]. The guard held, the threat level is now public, and the checks are visible.

Very Severe Cyclonic Storm Arnab crossed the coast of Andhra Pradesh and Yanam between 2 am and 8 am local time on Wednesday (21:30 Tuesday to 03:30 Wednesday CET), about 40 km south-west of Yanam, the India Meteorological Department said [1]. At landfall it had sustained winds of 125–135 km/h, gusting to 150 km/h. It has since weakened to a severe cyclonic storm over land [1].

The rain was heavy. Between Tuesday morning and early Wednesday, Kannuripalem in Anakapalli district recorded 261.5 mm, and several places in Anakapalli, Vizianagaram and Visakhapatnam passed 200 mm [2]. Officials reported hundreds of fallen trees, downed power poles, a coastal road washed away near Kakinada, and long power cuts around Visakhapatnam [1][2]. Two people died after touching live wires that had come down [2].

Two deaths are two too many. They are also a reminder of what preparation buys. Before landfall the state government said it had opened 290 cyclone shelters, moved 84,396 people out of harm's way, brought more than 30,000 fishing boats back to shore and put 8,400 staff on standby for power repairs [3]. On Wednesday the chief minister asked officials for road clearance within two hours and for preliminary crop-damage estimates [2]. Heavy rain is expected to continue into Thursday [2].

The forecast came in time and the plans were followed. That is what made the difference.

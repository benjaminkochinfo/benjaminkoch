At 30 September, an estimated 588,864 people in Scotland were waiting for a new outpatient appointment, an inpatient admission or a day case. That is about one in nine of the population, Public Health Scotland said on Tuesday [1].

The headline is stubborn. The tail is improving. Outpatient waits of more than 52 weeks fell by 1,756 in a month to 12,683, which PHS called consistent with a fall over the past year [1]. Inpatient and day-case waits of over a year fell by 884 to 15,064 [1]. The outpatient list is 9.3 per cent shorter than a year ago [1].

The pace is still slow. Only 61.3 per cent of new outpatients were seen within the 12-week standard, against a target of 95 per cent [1]. The next figures are due on 24 November [1].

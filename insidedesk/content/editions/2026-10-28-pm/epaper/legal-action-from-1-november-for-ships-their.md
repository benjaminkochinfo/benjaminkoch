Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority, the body Tehran set up to handle passage requests through Hormuz, said all vessels of South Korea's Sinokor would face "legal action" from 1 November, citing the most crossing violations of any company in three months and "appropriate actions" against at least seven ships [1]. The Korea Shipowners' Association replied on Wednesday that the vessels were not owned by Sinokor but sub-chartered to an overseas oil company, with no South Korean crew [1]. Sinokor was checking the details [1]. A threat aimed at a name meets a charter party. In shipping, the paperwork usually decides who is liable.

At 19:00 CET the Federal Reserve kept the federal funds target range at 3.75% to 4%, by ten votes to two [1]. Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack voted against, preferring a quarter-point rise [1]. It is Cook's first dissent since she joined the Fed in 2022 [2].

The statement is short, so read it whole. Activity is now "expanding at a strong pace", where September said "solid" [1], [2]. Then the working sentence: "Inflation remains elevated", and "the accumulation of more information" will guide the next step [1]. The sentence explaining September's rise was removed [2]. Reuters reads the new wording as a hint of the forward guidance Chairman Kevin Warsh says he wants to avoid [3].

Small scorecard: this evening's lead quoted JPMorgan's analysts expecting at least two dissents. There were exactly two. Futures had priced less than a 10% chance of a rise, and still point to December [2].

The useful documents come tomorrow: September's PCE index and the ECB's decision. The press conference began at 19:30 CET; adjectives from it will not change the invoices. Nothing here is investment advice.

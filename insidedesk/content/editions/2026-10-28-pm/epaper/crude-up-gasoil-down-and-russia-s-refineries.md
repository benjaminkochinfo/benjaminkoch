Brent rose to $115.40 a barrel on Wednesday morning, about $1.50 above Tuesday's close of $113.88, HeizOel24 reports [1]. Gasoil, the wholesale product behind diesel and heating oil, went the other way: it closed Tuesday $57.25 lower at $1,463.00 a tonne and fell again to $1,437.25 on Wednesday morning [1].

The explanation is in the refineries. Citing industry sources quoted by Reuters, HeizOel24 says Russia processed about five million barrels of crude a day in the second half of October, about a quarter more than at the start of the month, and that its diesel output rose by at least 50,000 to 225,000 tonnes a day [1]. Extra export volumes could ease competition for diesel, though no confirmed deliveries exist yet [1]. Crude is the headline. Refining capacity is what fills the tanker to your tank.

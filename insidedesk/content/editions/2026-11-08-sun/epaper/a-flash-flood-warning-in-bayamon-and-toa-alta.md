The US National Weather Service in San Juan issued a flash-flood warning for Bayamón and Toa Alta at 14:46 local time on Saturday, after radar indicated thunderstorms had dropped 1 to 2 inches of rain with another 1 to 2 possible [1]. Flood advisories covered San Juan, Guaynabo, Carolina, Fajardo and other municipalities [1]. Saturated soils raise the chance of quick river rises and isolated mudslides; forecasters expected showers to ease from Sunday into Monday, with a high risk of rip currents on northern beaches through the weekend [1]. The warning was based on radar, not confirmed damage [1]. A warning issued early is a system working, not an alarm overdone.

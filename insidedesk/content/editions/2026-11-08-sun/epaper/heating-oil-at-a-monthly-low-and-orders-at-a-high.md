Households with an oil tank did something sensible this week: they watched. HeizOel24 reports that as the domestic heating-oil price fell to its monthly low after Wednesday's crash in crude, demand climbed to a monthly high [1]. On Friday morning the price moved back up by about three cents a litre, to a national average of €1.71, as crude recovered part of its fall [1]. On Sunday at 09:00 the average for a 3,000-litre order stood at €169.43 per 100 litres [1].

Desk arithmetic: 3,000 litres at Sunday's €169.43 per 100 litres costs €5,082.90. A three-cent move on that order is €90.

**Why it jumps around.** Heating oil follows gasoil, the wholesale product, more than crude itself, and gasoil has its own worries: China is exporting less diesel in November because its own stocks are low, HeizOel24 notes [1].

**The quiet tip.** Before ordering, know how much is actually in your tank, HeizOel24 advises, so you buy the quantity you need [1]. That sounds obvious. It is also the part of the price you control. A habit, not advice.

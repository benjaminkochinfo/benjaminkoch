Russia launched 172 drones and 36 ballistic missiles at Ukraine, including North Korean-made KN-23 missiles, President Zelenskyy said on Saturday; only four of the missiles were intercepted [1]. "Unfortunately, there is still a significant shortage of interceptors for Patriot systems, and the United States is in the best position to help address it," he said [1]. The attacks killed at least two people and damaged energy facilities and a key bridge in Kyiv [1]. US-brokered talks between Russia and Ukraine are due in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday [1]. Air defence is a stock-and-flow problem: what matters is not the launcher but the magazine behind it.

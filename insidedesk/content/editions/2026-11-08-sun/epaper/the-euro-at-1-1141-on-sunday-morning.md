The euro stood at $1.1141 on Sunday at 09:00, on HeizOel24's price panel [1]. It had closed Friday 30 October at $1.1183 and slipped about 0.36% over the week to Friday, as the dollar index rose about 0.4% [2]. Desk arithmetic: from $1.1183 to $1.1141 is 0.42 US cents per euro. For households the euro-dollar rate matters mostly because oil and its products are priced in dollars: a weaker euro takes back a little of what a falling Brent gives. This week Brent fell 13%, so the currency's slide was small beside it. Nothing here is investment advice.

Since 2025, every electricity supplier must offer a dynamic tariff to customers who have a smart metering system [1]. On such a tariff you pay the exchange price of each hour plus the fixed charges [1]. On Sunday the cheapest hour is 13:00 at 21.14 cents per kilowatt-hour wholesale and the dearest is 18:00 at 31.71 cents [1]. A dynamic tariff pays off if you can shift large loads such as a heat pump, a wallbox or a home battery into the cheap hours; if you cannot, you carry the full risk of expensive evenings [1]. It is a tool for homes with flexible loads, not a bargain for everyone.

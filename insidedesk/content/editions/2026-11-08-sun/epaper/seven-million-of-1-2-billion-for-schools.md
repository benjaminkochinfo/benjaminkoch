Of the €1.2 billion earmarked this year from the federal special fund for education infrastructure, only about €7 million has flowed into schools, nurseries and research buildings, the Rheinische Post reports, citing the finance ministry [1]. That is not even 1% of the money available [1]. The ministry blames administrative agreements between the federal government and the states that were concluded late [1]. The Greens' deputy group leader Khan called it "scandalous" [1]. Money that exists but cannot move is the most frustrating kind of budget problem, and the dullest to fix: it needs signatures, not speeches.

After meeting his security advisers on Thursday, President Trump announced a decision on new strikes soon, HeizOel24 reports [1]. Citing Axios, it says three paths are on the table: new attacks, more economic pressure, or a deal that would require Washington to move substantially towards Iran [1]. Qatar continues to mediate; Tehran has sent a proposal on the nuclear dispute to Washington via Doha, but demands that seven conditions be met before talks, including an end to sanctions [1]. Oil traders priced the middle of those outcomes this week. A choice between three paths is not yet a path.

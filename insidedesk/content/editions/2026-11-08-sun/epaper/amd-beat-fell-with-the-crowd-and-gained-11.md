Advanced Micro Devices reported third-quarter revenue of $13.3 billion, ahead of expectations, with profit of nearly $2.43 billion, roughly in line, and guided fourth-quarter revenue above the consensus, Investopedia reported on Wednesday [1]. Its shares were up about 3% that morning [1]. Then Thursday's chip sell-off arrived, and on Friday AMD added 11% as semiconductors bounced 4.2% [2]. The company's numbers did not change between Wednesday and Friday; the market's mood about its customers did. Results are the slow evidence beneath fast prices. Nothing here is investment advice.

On Monday, Marvell Technology announced it would buy optical-components maker Lumentum for $115 billion; shares of the two fell about 13% and 18% that day [1]. By Friday the week's tally was in: Lumentum was the second-worst performer among the US stocks Morningstar covers, down 35.96%, and Marvell the fifth-worst, down 28.12% [2]. Seagate, another data-infrastructure name, lost 28.43% [2]. Big deals are usually priced on the buyer's confidence. This one was priced on the market's doubts about the size of the AI build-out, which the election then deepened. Nothing here is investment advice.

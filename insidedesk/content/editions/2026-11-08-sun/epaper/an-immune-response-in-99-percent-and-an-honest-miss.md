In the phase 2 AMPLIFY-7P trial, published in Nature Medicine, 144 people with KRAS-mutated pancreatic cancer who had finished surgery and chemotherapy were randomised to the experimental vaccine camupepimut or observation [1]. The vaccine activated KRAS-targeting T cells in nearly 99% of the vaccine group [1]. But it missed its primary endpoint: median disease-free survival was 23.8 months against 21.2 months, not statistically significant [1]. A phase 3 trial is planned [1]. "Seeing the cancer is the first step," said oncologist Jason Williams, who was not involved. "Clearing it is another" [1]. An honest miss is how medicine learns where to look next.

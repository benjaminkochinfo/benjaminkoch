Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia are among the NATO countries that Pentagon officials have, in recent weeks, asked to keep public statements about the Russian threat in check, four people familiar with the matter told Reuters [1]. One said the Pentagon policy chief, Elbridge Colby, told a Baltic official there should be no "poking" of Russia [1]. Another European official summed up the message as: "We are helping you. The Americans are there. Just don't be loud. Don't make noise" [1]. None of the sources described the exchanges as confrontational [1].

"Private conversations among allies are just that," a Pentagon official said [1]. The Baltic defence ministries did not respond and NATO declined to comment [1]. Tone is a matter for allies to settle among themselves. What you can check is the alliance's ledger of contracts, troop posture and exercises, and that ledger does not change with the volume of a speech.

On the harmonised measure used for euro-area comparisons, Germany's inflation fell to 2.8% in October, from 3.3% in September [1][4]. France's rose to 3.8%, above the 3.6% analysts polled by Reuters expected, from 3.4% [2][4]. Italy's jumped to 5.6%, from 4.2% [3]. Istat puts most of the Italian rise on energy: regulated energy prices rose 59.5% on the year, against 25.8% in September [3].

Desk arithmetic: the gap between the lowest and highest of the three widened from 0.9 points in September to 2.8 points in October. One currency, one central bank, and national energy bills that are set in very different ways. Eurostat publishes the euro-area flash on 4 November [4]. Nothing here is investment advice.

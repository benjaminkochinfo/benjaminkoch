This morning's ePaper had In-fa heading over Agrihan. It went over it, and this evening the paperwork caught up. The typhoon warnings for Alamagan, Pagan and Agrihan were cancelled at 2 a.m. local time on Saturday, 17:00 in Berlin [1]. The weather service's latest local statement says the storm "has passed over Agrihan" and has moved far enough away for the warnings to end [2].

At 16:00 Berlin time the centre was about 140 miles north-west of Agrihan, moving west-north-west at 22 mph [1]. Peak sustained winds have eased to 140 mph, from 145 mph three hours earlier [1], [3]. That is still Category 4, and the forecasters expect it to hold that strength through Saturday night before weakening gradually [1], [2].

The next part is the odd one. In-fa is expected to turn north and slow sharply, then make a loop as its motion becomes "slow and erratic" west of the Marianas [1]. A stalled storm over open water is mainly a problem for shipping and for Iwo To, about 370 miles to the north-north-west [1]. For the islands, the statement still asks people to stay sheltered until the all clear is given locally [2]. Cancelling a warning is a forecast; the clear-up is an inventory, and that starts at first light.

Subpolar maritime air and high pressure set Germany's weather for now, the Deutscher Wetterdienst says [1]. Tonight brings patchy dense fog with visibility below 150 metres, and light frost down to -2 degrees at the Alpine edge and in some upland hollows [1]. On the High Black Forest summits, an easterly wind can gust to 70 km/h, at times around 80 km/h [1].

Saturday stays largely cloudy and foggy, at 6 to 13 degrees, with drizzle on the northern edge of the eastern uplands [1]. From Saturday night into Sunday morning, snow is expected in the Alps and the Bavarian Forest, with the snow line falling to about 1,000 metres; a few centimetres are possible above it, amounts still uncertain [1]. On the night into Monday, lows fall to between 5 and -2 degrees away from the northwest, lower on high ground, with ice in places [1]. Leave five minutes for the windscreen, and dip the headlights in the fog.

PET spunbond is a polyester non-woven fabric used as a carrier for waterproofing, for example on roofs [1]. On Friday the Commission imposed definitive anti-dumping duties of 39.8% to 48.1% on imports from China for five years, replacing provisional duties of 45.6% to 50% in force since May [1]. Its investigation found dumped imports were harming EU producers in Germany, Italy and France, in a market worth €200m [1].

A day earlier China's commerce ministry said it would take "all necessary measures" if the EU extended its hybrid-vehicle understanding to other sectors such as chemicals [2]. The same spokesperson also said recent consultations had "made positive progress" [2]. Trade defence is rarely dramatic. It is an investigation, a finding and a percentage, and both sides are still talking.

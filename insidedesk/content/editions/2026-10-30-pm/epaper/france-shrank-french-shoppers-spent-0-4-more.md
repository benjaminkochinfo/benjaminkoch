France's economy shrank by 0.1% in the third quarter, INSEE's first estimate showed on Friday, against a 0.1% rise expected by analysts polled by Reuters [1]. The fall came mostly from exports, down 1.7%, with transport equipment, chiefly aerospace, down 9.1% [1].

Households did the opposite. Their consumption rose 0.4% in the quarter, after 0.2% in the spring [1]. "Consumption of transport equipment is on the rise again, driven in particular by purchases of electric vehicles," INSEE said [1]. One caution, from the same Reuters report: consumer spending fell more than expected in September itself, on manufactured goods and food [1]. A quarter of steady shopping does not guarantee the next. But the household ledger was not the one that broke.

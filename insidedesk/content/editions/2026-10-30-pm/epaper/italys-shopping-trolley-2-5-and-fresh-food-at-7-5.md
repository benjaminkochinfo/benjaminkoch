Italy's national consumer price index rose 5.4% on the year in October, from 4.2% in September, Istat's preliminary estimate showed on Friday [1]. Prices rose 0.9% in a single month [1]. Istat says the rate is back at its level of August 2023 [1].

**The trolley.** Istat publishes a line known as the "carrello della spesa", the shopping trolley: food, household cleaning and personal-care goods. It rose 2.5% on the year, up from 1.5% [1]. Items bought most often, the "high-frequency" basket, rose 5.8%, from 5.2% [1].

**The fresh shelf.** Unprocessed food rose 7.5% on the year, up from 5.5%, and 1.8% in the month alone [1]. Processed food is still slightly cheaper than a year ago, at -0.2%, though the fall is fading [1]. Transport services rose 3.1%, from 1.6% [1].

**The bill behind it all.** Energy rose 31.4% on the year, from 22.6%, and Istat links it to the international geopolitical situation [1]. Underlying inflation, excluding energy and fresh food, rose to 2.0% from 1.6% [1].

**One line moving the other way.** Recreational, cultural and personal-care services fell 0.9% on the month [1]. Desk arithmetic: the trolley at 2.5% is less than half the headline 5.4%. For an Italian household that does not heat or drive much, October was dearer, but not by the number on the news. For one that does, it was worse. The ledger is the same; the receipts differ.

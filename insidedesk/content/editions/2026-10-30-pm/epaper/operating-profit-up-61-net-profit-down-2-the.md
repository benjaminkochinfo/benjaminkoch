Two profit lines in Amazon's third quarter went in opposite directions. Operating income rose to $28.1 billion from $17.4 billion; net income fell to $20.7 billion from $21.2 billion, according to the company's Form 10-Q [1]. Desk arithmetic: up about 61% and down about 2%.

**The gap.** A year ago Amazon booked $7.2 billion of "upward adjustments" to its private stake in Anthropic, as the AI company raised money at higher prices [1]. This quarter the same line was $39 million [1]. Over nine months, though, those adjustments came to $62.9 billion, against income before tax of $147.6 billion [1]. Desk arithmetic: about 43% of pre-tax income came from revaluing one holding, not from selling anything to anyone.

**The size of the stake.** At the end of September Amazon carried its Anthropic preferred shares at about $92.5 billion and its Anthropic convertible notes at an estimated fair value of $146.2 billion [1]. It also holds $50.0 billion of OpenAI preferred stock, after paying the last $21.3 billion of a $35.0 billion commitment in the quarter [1]. The filing classes these as Level 3 measurements, estimated with valuation models and judgment rather than quoted market prices [1].

**For you.** None of this is hidden; it is on page after page of the filing. The rule of thumb is old and still good: operating income is what the business earned, other income is what the market said. Read both before you read the headline. Nothing here is investment advice.

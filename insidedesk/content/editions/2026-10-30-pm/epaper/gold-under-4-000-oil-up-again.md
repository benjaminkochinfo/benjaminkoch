Gold fell more than 1% on Friday morning and broke below $4,000 an ounce, XTB's research desk reported; silver fell nearly 3%, to around $50 [1]. Oil went the other way. Front-month Brent futures were up 2.2% at $113.27 a barrel at midday, Sharecast reported, after another attack on a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz [2]. The Stoxx 600 was 0.1% lower at 648.23, a step back from Thursday's seven-week closing high [2].

The pairing is a small lesson. Gold is bought as a store of fear; oil is bought to be burned. On a day when a strait is still dangerous, the commodity tied to an actual delivery rose. Nothing here is investment advice.

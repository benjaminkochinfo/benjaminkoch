December is now the front month, and on its first day in the headline it rose. Dutch TTF futures for December delivery were up 4.9% at €102.78 per megawatt hour on Friday, CNBC reported; a separate ICE-based report had them at €102.85 [1][2]. That is the highest level in almost four years, and about 36% up on the month, CNBC said, citing Trading Economics [1].

**The tank.** EU gas storage is about 75% full, the lowest level for the season since records began in 2011, CNBC reported, with Hormuz shipping disruption blamed for slow refilling over the summer [1].

**The weather bet.** Peter Taylor of Macquarie said Europe needs "quite a warm winter to make it through unscathed", and warned that cold weather could set Europe competing with Asia for cargoes [1]. BlackRock estimates the energy shock has already cut Europe's output by about 0.5%, against 0.1% in the US [1].

**For you.** Perspective helps: in December 2022 the same contract was at €139, and that August it touched €339 [1]. Today's level is painful, not unprecedented, and storage figures are published daily. A degree less on the thermostat is the cheapest molecule there is. Nothing here is investment advice.

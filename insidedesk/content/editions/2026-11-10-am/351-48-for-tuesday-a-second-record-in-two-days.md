The day-ahead auction for Tuesday 10 November cleared at a German-Luxembourg daily average of €351.48 per megawatt hour, the SMARD series shows [1]. That is €5.84 above Monday's €345.64, which had itself been the dearest delivery day of 2026, so the year.s record has been broken on two consecutive delivery days (Desk arithmetic) [1]. On 10 November 2025 the same series stood at €109.89; Tuesday's price is about 3.2 times that (Desk arithmetic) [1].

[[chart:de-power-day-ahead-10nov]]

**The shape moved.** Monday's pain sat in the evening, with €644.18 at 17:00. Tuesday's dearest hour is 08:00 at €494.05, about €150 lower than Monday's peak (Desk arithmetic) [1]. The breakfast block is the expensive one now: Desk arithmetic: the four hours from 07:00 to 11:00 average €461.00. A second hump follows in the late afternoon, €483.35 at 16:00 [1]. The gap between the dearest and the cheapest hour is €274.39, against €420.91 on Monday (Desk arithmetic) [1].

**The reason has not changed.** SMARD's generation forecast puts onshore and offshore wind together at about 99.1 gigawatt hours for Tuesday, up from about 71.6 GWh on Monday, but still less than a quarter of the roughly 416.9 GWh forecast for Wednesday 4 November (Desk arithmetic, from [2]). A little more wind flattened the evening; it did not bring the average down.

**For you.** A fixed tariff absorbs days like this; the auction sets the wholesale leg, and grid fees, taxes and levies sit on top. On a dynamic tariff, Tuesday asks for a different habit from Monday: the morning, not the evening, is the block to avoid for the dishwasher or the car charger [1]. The price for Wednesday will be public, free, by early afternoon. Nothing here is investment advice.

The coalition has until Thursday. That is when the Bundestag's Budget Committee begins its final "Bereinigungssitzung" on the 2027 budget, which traditionally runs into the early hours of Friday [3]. Bild reported a Monday-evening mediation meeting of the two parliamentary group leaders, Thorsten Frei (CDU) and Matthias Miersch (SPD), Merkur writes [3]; no result had been reported by the time this edition closed.

**The frame.** The draft plans core spending of €555.4 billion and new core borrowing of €118.7 billion; including the special funds for the armed forces and infrastructure, new borrowing reaches €203.7 billion [1]. The autumn tax estimate brings the federal government about €3.9 billion more, but the debt brake lets it borrow €2.5 billion less because the economic forecast improved, leaving about €1.4 billion of extra room on paper [1].

**The wish list.** The plastics tax would raise about €670 million in 2027 and about €1.5 billion a year from 2028 [1]. "Die Plastiksteuer machen wir nicht," Frei said on ZDF ("We won't do the plastics tax") [1]. The sugar tax, worth about €690 million next year, looks likelier to come, though parts of the Union want to delay it to 1 January 2028 [1][2]. The Union wants an electricity tax cut for everyone and a further offset of bracket creep, each costing about €5 billion, with no counter-financing [1]. The SPD wants to keep the €25 children's top-up and avoid housing-benefit cuts, about €770 million together [1][3]. Environment Minister Carsten Schneider said the Union now has to propose "wie wir das Loch im Haushalt schließen" ("how we close the hole") [2].

**The year that matters.** The finance ministry's own figures show an uncovered gap of about €25 billion in 2028, €40 billion in 2029 and €50 billion in 2030, about €115 billion in total [1]. Every permanent tax cut agreed this week lands in those years.

For you, the headlines will be about plastic and sugar. The line to read on Friday morning is the one that says how each new promise is paid for.

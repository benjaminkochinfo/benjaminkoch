A long-range Ukrainian drone attack started a fire at the KINEF oil refinery near St Petersburg, one of Russia's largest, Leningrad regional governor Alexander Drozdenko said on Monday [1]. Ukraine's general staff said its forces also struck a plant near the city that produces solid rocket propellant for Russia's Iskander-M ballistic missiles [1].

Zelenskyy said the long-range strikes, including on oil-industry sites, were also working, the Guardian reports [1]. The same night, Russia hit a warehouse in Odesa holding food for kindergartens and schools, killing three people, local officials said [1]. Damage assessments from such strikes take days; the first claims are a starting point, not a balance sheet.

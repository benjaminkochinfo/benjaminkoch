"For Europe, it is clear: we do not need to establish a new security architecture for Europe," German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul said on Monday, according to AFP [1]. He urged Moscow to "give up maximalist demands" and warned against a "negotiation show" in Abu Dhabi [1]. After meeting Marco Rubio at the G7 in Paris he added: "Europe belongs at this table" [2].

Berlin's leverage here is not volume but presence: officials from the UK, France, Germany and Italy, plus NATO and the EU, will hold separate talks with Ukraine's delegation in Abu Dhabi, its chief negotiator Rustem Umerov said [1]. The Kremlin says the negotiations stay "trilateral" [1].

Today at 14:30 CET the US Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes consumer prices and real earnings for October [1]. Wednesday is Veterans Day in the US, a federal holiday on the bureau's calendar [1]. On Friday 13 November, again at 14:30 CET, come US producer prices for October [1]. Germany's next ZEW economic-sentiment reading follows on Tuesday 17 November at 11:00 [2]; October's stood at plus 28.1 points, down from the month before [3]. A calendar is the most underrated document in finance: it tells you when to pay attention, and, just as usefully, when not to. Nothing here is investment advice.

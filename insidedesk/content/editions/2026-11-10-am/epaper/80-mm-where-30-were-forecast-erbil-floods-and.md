Severe thunderstorms brought deadly flash floods to northern Iraq on Saturday [1]. In Erbil an estimated 80 mm of rain fell where 30 mm had been expected; streets flooded, homes and businesses were damaged, and two people were killed [1].

In Europe, Storm Bruna is forecast to bring gusts of up to 80 km/h to Portugal's west coast this week, with heavy rain and thunderstorms and up to 200 mm of rain by the end of the week; Tenerife recorded a gust of 80 mph on Saturday night [1].

Japan is going the other way: Sapporo was forecast to sit about 10°C below average early this week, with a high of about 0°C on Tuesday and 30–50 cm of snow expected around Kiroro and Niseko [1]. Meanwhile, with Pacific sea temperatures about 3.5°C above average at their late-October peak, Peru has run 5–10°C above normal [1]. A forecast that misses by 50 mm is a reminder why warning systems need margins.

The VIX, the index of expected volatility on the S&P 500 often called Wall Street's fear gauge, fell 22.31% on Monday to 19.92 [1]. That is the market saying it expects calmer weeks than it did on Friday; it is not a promise. Gold futures, meanwhile, added 0.22% to $4,123.50 a troy ounce on Tuesday morning [1]. Desk arithmetic: a fall of 22.31% to 19.92 implies the VIX stood near 25.6 before Monday. The next test of that calm is scheduled, not random: US consumer prices for October at 14:30 CET [1].

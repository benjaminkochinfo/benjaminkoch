Google has unveiled two variants of its Tensor Processing Units, India Today reports [1]. The TPU 8tx is aimed at confidential computing for governments and regulated industries, and pairs Google's chip with an AMD processor so that data and models stay encrypted at hardware level while being processed. The TPU 8i Max packs two TPU chips into one unit for double the memory and computing power [1]. Google's chip chief Amin Vahdat says the future lies in specialisation [1]. Bloomberg Intelligence projects TPU sales rising from roughly $12 billion this year to over $135 billion by 2028, the report says; every TPU Google makes is currently contracted to a buyer [1]. The quieter point: buyers who want a second supplier now have one more option, and competition is the cheapest regulator there is.

Japan has approved a partial change to the authorisation of Shionogi's COVID-19 vaccine COVGOZE, switching its antigen to Omicron LP.8.1, the recommended strain for the 2026/2027 season, the Osaka company said on Monday [1]. Shionogi says it is preparing to supply the vaccine in fiscal year 2026 [1].

COVGOZE is a recombinant-protein vaccine, developed and made in Japan, using insect cells to express the antigen protein, which is then purified [1]. The company points out that the same family of technology has long been used for influenza vaccines [1].

For you, the interest is in the plumbing. A strain change approved as a routine "partial change" is what a mature vaccine system looks like: a recommended strain, a manufacturer that updates, a regulator that signs off, a season that starts on time. More than one technology and more than one supplier is resilience, not redundancy.

Australia's Westpac–Melbourne Institute consumer sentiment index fell 2.7% on the month to 78.2 in November, its lowest level since November 2022, NDTV Profit reports [1]. It is the third monthly fall in a row, after a 4.7% drop in October [1]. The reasons are familiar to anyone with a variable-rate loan: persistent cost-of-living pressure and higher borrowing costs, which weigh on mortgage affordability [1]. The same morning, Australia's main share index rose about 1% [1]. A share index tells you how investors feel about companies; a sentiment survey tells you how people feel about their kitchens. Both deserve a place on your screen.

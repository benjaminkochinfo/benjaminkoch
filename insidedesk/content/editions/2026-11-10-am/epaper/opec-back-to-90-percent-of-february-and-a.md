OPEC crude output rose by 1.38 million barrels a day in October to 22.87 million, a Reuters survey found, led by Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Iraq; that is about 90 percent of the 25.48 million pumped in February, before the war, OilPrice.com reports [1]. A Bloomberg survey put October at 22.8 million [1]. Iran's output fell to 1.9 million barrels a day, the lowest in that survey's data back to 1989 [1].

On the same day, Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline, its main route around the Strait of Hormuz to the Red Sea port of Yanbu, was targeted in the Medina region; an Energy Ministry official said a limited fire was put out with no casualties and no disruption to operations [1]. OPEC+ ministers meet on 29 November to set 2027 targets [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

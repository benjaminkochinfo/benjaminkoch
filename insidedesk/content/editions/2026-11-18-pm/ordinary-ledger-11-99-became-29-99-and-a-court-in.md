The Higher Regional Court in Hamm ruled on Wednesday that the streaming service DAZN's one-sided price rises in running contracts in 2021 and 2022 were invalid, the Federation of German Consumer Organisations (vzbv) reports [1]. Almost 8,800 people joined the class action [1]. The ruling (I-12 VKl 1/24) is not yet final [1].

The ledger lines are plain. In August 2021 DAZN raised the monthly price from €11.99 to €14.99 and the annual subscription from €119.99 to €149.99 [1]. In August 2022 the monthly price nearly doubled, from €14.99 to €29.99, and annual subscriptions rose to as much as €299.99 [1]. Desk arithmetic: a monthly subscriber on the original price was paying €18 more a month by late 2022, two and a half times the starting fee.

Those who registered by 25 September 2026 can get back the difference between the agreed and the raised price, depending on how long they paid it, plus interest awarded by the court [1]. New contracts were not part of the case, and registration is closed [1].

What makes it a first is the form. Under this kind of action, an "Abhilfeklage", customers receive the payment directly instead of having to sue again individually, the vzbv says [1]. "A state under the rule of law must ensure that people who do not hire law firms themselves also get their rights," said vzbv board member Ramona Pop [1].

The money is not on its way yet. The court has asked the parties for a written settlement proposal; without one, payment waits until the ruling is final, and either side can still go to the Federal Court of Justice [1]. If you were a long-standing subscriber, the useful thing is to keep your old bank statements. Courts move slowly; receipts keep.

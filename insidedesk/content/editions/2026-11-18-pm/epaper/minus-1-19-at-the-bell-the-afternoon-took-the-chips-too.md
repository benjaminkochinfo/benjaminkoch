The DAX closed down 1.19% at 25,736.24 points; the MDax lost 0.89% to 29,878.63 [1]. At 15:42 CET, when our Markets note was written, it stood at 25,876.50, so the last stretch of trading took another 140.26 points (Desk arithmetic). dpa-AFX notes the index broke below its 21-day line for the first time since the end of October [1]. The Stoxx 600 fell 6.67 points, or 1.03%, to 644.05: three losing days in a row, 3.03% in all, the largest three-day fall since 23 March, and the lowest close since 27 October [2].

The chip trade held; little else did. Infineon closed 1.1% higher, Siemens Energy 1.3%, Aixtron 1.8% and Suss 2.6% [3]. SAP lost 3.8%, Deutsche Bank another 3.1% after Tuesday's 6%, and Commerzbank 3.5%; traders cited falling bond yields weighing on banks, which in turn lifted Vonovia 2% and LEG 2.3% [3]. Deutz fell 16.6% after the short-seller report; the company says it is checking the claims [3].

Traders pointed to US rate worries ahead of the Fed minutes and caution before Thursday's Anthropic listing [1][3]. A strong quarter is a receipt, not a guarantee for the next bill. Not investment advice.

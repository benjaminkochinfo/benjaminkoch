On 17 November the FDA granted accelerated approval to neladalkib (Nelkarmi, Nuvalent) for adults with advanced ALK-positive non-small cell lung cancer who have already had alectinib, or two or more ALK inhibitors plus platinum chemotherapy [1]. It is a 150 mg tablet taken once a day [1].

The single-arm ALKOVE-1 trial measured tumour response [1]. After alectinib alone (67 patients), 49% responded; after two or more inhibitors and chemotherapy (155 patients), 32% did [1]. About half of the responders in each group were still responding at 12 months [1]. Warnings cover the liver, kidneys and eyes, among others [1].

Response rates from an open-label trial with no comparison arm are a promissory note, not a receipt. The receipt is how long patients live, and this page does not report it yet. The word "accelerated" on the label says as much.

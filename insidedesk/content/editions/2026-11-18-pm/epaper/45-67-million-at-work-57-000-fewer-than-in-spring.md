About 45.67 million people worked in Germany in the third quarter, Destatis said on Wednesday, 57,000 or 0.1% fewer than in the second quarter after seasonal adjustment [1]. The office says employment had also fallen by 0.1% quarter on quarter since the second quarter of 2025 [1]. Year on year the count was down 222,000, or 0.5% [1]. The split matters more than the total: public services, education and health added 172,000 people (+1.4%), while manufacturing excluding construction shed 164,000 (-2.1%) and trade, transport and hospitality 113,000 (-1.1%) [1]. A payroll that shrinks by a tenth of a percent a quarter makes no headlines. A string of them is the headline.

Moldova restricted its airspace at 16:27 local time on Tuesday after a Shahed-type drone was reported flying from the south towards Chisinau's Botanica district, the state agency Moldpres reports [1]. Seven drones crossed the country's airspace that day, and Russia's ambassador-designate was summoned to the foreign ministry [1]. President Maia Sandu said "Russia cares neither about neutrality, sovereignty, nor human life", and admitted: "We still do not have the defense capabilities necessary to protect ourselves" [1]. Drones have rarely come close to the capital before, the Guardian notes [2]. Sandu pledged to press for more sanctions [1]. A small state that says plainly what it cannot yet do is halfway to getting it done.

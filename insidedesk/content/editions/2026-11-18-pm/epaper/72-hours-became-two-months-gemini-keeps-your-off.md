Google will keep temporary Gemini chats, and chats made with history turned off, for around two months rather than the 72 hours its privacy hub previously stated, TechCrunch reports [1]. The change rolls out globally over the coming weeks [1]. Google cites safety, security, feedback and possible system failures, and says temporary chats are not used to train its models [1]. For comparison, OpenAI says it may keep ChatGPT temporary chats for up to 30 days, and Anthropic keeps Claude's incognito chats for 30 days by default [1].

There is a gain too: chat history and AI training now have separate switches, where one "Keep Activity" setting used to govern both [1]. If you had turned that setting off, both new switches start off [1]. "Temporary" is a retention policy, not a promise. Check the settings page, not the label.

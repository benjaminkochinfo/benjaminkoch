Researchers at UT Southwestern and UT Dallas gave 29 women with mild to moderate major depression a week each of 500 mg or 800 mg of pregnenolone a day, or placebo, in a double-blind crossover trial published in the Journal of Clinical Psychiatry [1]. Only 11 had usable brain scans from all three conditions [1].

At 500 mg, connectivity between the amygdala and the left frontal pole fell compared with placebo; at 800 mg it did not [1]. Both doses raised blood levels of allopregnanolone and were well tolerated [1]. The next step, the team says, is to see whether the change tracks any improvement in symptoms [1].

Eleven scans are a reason to run a bigger study, not to buy a supplement. The authors say exactly that, which is to their credit.

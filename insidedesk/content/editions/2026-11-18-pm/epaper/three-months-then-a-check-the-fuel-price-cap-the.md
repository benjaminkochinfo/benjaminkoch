Alongside the electricity-tax cut, the coalition committee decided that the cabinet should adopt a bill and a regulation on 2 December to "activate" a fuel-price cap from 1 January 2027, ARD reports [1]. The paper cites the "extraordinary crisis situation" around the Strait of Hormuz [1]. The legal basis is limited to twelve months, and the regulation lapses after three unless an extension is approved before then [1]. Länder are to be allowed to set higher or lower regional maximum prices, a change to Economy Minister Katherina Reiche's draft that the SPD had called toothless [1]. The current fuel discount, in force since early October, ends in December [1]. A cap with a three-month fuse is a cap that must keep earning its renewal.

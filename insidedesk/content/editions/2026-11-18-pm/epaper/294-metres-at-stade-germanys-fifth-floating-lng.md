The "Energos Force", a 294-metre regasification ship, went into service at Stade on the Elbe on Monday, NDR reports [1]. "Über das Terminal in Stade können 2027 voraussichtlich bis zu 3,2 Milliarden Kubikmeter Erdgas in das deutsche Gasnetz eingespeist werden," said economy minister Katherina Reiche: up to 3.2 billion cubic metres next year [1]. It is the fourth terminal run by the state-owned Deutsche Energy Terminal, after two in Wilhelmshaven and one in Brunsbüttel; each cargo carries enough gas for 70,000 households for a year, DET's Peter Röttgen said [1].

The project was meant to start in 2024 and slipped because of work on the site; a larger onshore terminal is due to replace the ship in 2029 [1]. The environmental group DUH warned against swapping one dependency for another, as most recent German LNG came from the United States [1]. Infrastructure is not glamorous. It is the part of security you can tie a ship to.

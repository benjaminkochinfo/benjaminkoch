Deutz shares fell at times by more than 17% to €7.56 on Wednesday, the biggest loser in the MDax, after the short seller Ningi Research published a critical report and said it was betting on a falling price [1]. Ningi alleges that Deutz did not disclose that 45% of FFG Industries, a growth unit of the roughly €1.6 billion Flensburger Fahrzeugbau Gesellschaft acquisition, belongs to a former Ukrainian member of parliament and his company [1][2]. These are a short seller's allegations, not findings.

Deutz said it had taken note of the report, was examining its claims carefully and would respond "on the basis of the relevant facts", in our translation [1][2]. In dpa-AFX's 14:41 CET report the shares were still nearly 12% lower [3].

A short report is a document with a position attached. The useful reply is another document: the acquisition paperwork, line by line. Nothing here is investment advice.

**Car insurance.** For most policies, your cancellation must reach your insurer by 30 November if you want to switch at the turn of the year [1]. The good news: according to a Verivox analysis cited by t-online, policies cost on average only 2% more than a year earlier at the start of the switching season, and in the cheapest segment prices fell by almost 2% [1].

**Tax.** If you know you will claim high work-related expenses or special costs, you can apply to the tax office for a wage-tax reduction (Lohnsteuerermäßigung) [1]. Apply by 30 November 2026 and the allowance counts for 2027 and backdates to the whole of 2026, applied in December's payslip [1]. It can be done online through Elster [1]. Two forms, one date, and the money arrives in your account rather than at tax-return time.

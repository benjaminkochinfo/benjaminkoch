In Harris County, which includes Houston, 862,700 people had voted early by Wednesday, an Associated Press analysis found, against 564,500 at the same point in 2022 [1]. Desk arithmetic: about 53% more. Statewide, Texas has passed 7 million early votes, 1.5 million more than in the whole 2022 early period, according to University of Florida data [1]. On the ballot: a Senate race between the Republican attorney general, Ken Paxton, and the Democrat James Talarico, into which hundreds of millions of dollars have poured, and Governor Greg Abbott's re-election against Gina Hinojosa [1]. Democrats have not won a statewide race in Texas for more than three decades [1]. Turnout is not a result; Tuesday is.

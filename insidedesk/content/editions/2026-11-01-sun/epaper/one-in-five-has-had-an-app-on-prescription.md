Germany's digital health applications, the "app on prescription", are known to 56% of people aged 16 and over, according to a representative Bitkom survey of 1,132 people published on Friday [1]. 20% have been prescribed one and 15% have redeemed the prescription [1]. 44% would use an app to bridge a long wait for treatment, and 49% think doctors and psychotherapists should tell patients about them more often [1]. The makers are gloomier: in a separate, non-representative poll of 34 providers, 85% said their situation had worsened over two years and 76% rated investment security as low or very low, though half plan new apps within 24 months [1]. Bitkom is the industry's own association, so read the second half as a plea as well as a finding. The first half is simpler: if you are on a waiting list, ask whether an approved app could help in the meantime.

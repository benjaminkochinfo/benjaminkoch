Lloyd's List Intelligence recorded 1,195 Suez Canal transits, 111 million deadweight tonnes, from 28 September to 25 October, up from 1,151 in the previous four weeks; it says the canal is on track for another record-breaking month [1]. Crude tanker voyages through Suez, 301 in four weeks, have surpassed 2023 levels [1]. Containerships are returning from the Cape of Good Hope: 281 in four weeks, against 474 in October 2023 [1]. In tonnage terms the canal is still 36% below its pre-Houthi level, and the threat to shipping remains "substantial", although UKMTO reports no attacks in the Red Sea since 24 August [1].

One strait is still choked; another gate is filling up again. Routes are not destiny. They are choices, made weekly by people who read the risk reports.

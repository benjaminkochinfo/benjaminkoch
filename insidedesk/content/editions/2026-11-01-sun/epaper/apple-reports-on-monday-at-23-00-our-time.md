Apple's conference call on its fiscal fourth quarter is scheduled for Monday 2 November at 14:00 Pacific time [1]. Desk arithmetic: with US clocks going back today, that is 23:00 CET. Monday's numbers cover Apple's fiscal fourth quarter [1], so they say little about this week's launches, which The Verge's weekly round-up led with a new iPad mini and a touchscreen MacBook Pro [2].

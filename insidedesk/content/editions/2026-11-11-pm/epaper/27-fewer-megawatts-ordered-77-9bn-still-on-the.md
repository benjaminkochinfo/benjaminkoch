Vestas shares fell 8% on Wednesday morning [2] after third-quarter revenue of €5.68bn and operating profit before special items of €483m both missed analysts' estimates [1]. Turbine order intake fell 27% by volume to 3,377 megawatts, mainly on weaker onshore orders in the US, though the average selling price rose to €1.22m per megawatt [1].

The outlook was narrowed, not cut: 2026 revenue of €20.5bn to €21.5bn instead of €20bn to €22bn, and an operating margin of 7.5% to 8.5% instead of 7% to 9% [1]. The combined turbine and service backlog stands at €77.9bn [1]. Chief executive Henrik Andersen said the narrower range reflected improved visibility [1]. A tighter range is less exciting than a raised one, and more useful. Nothing here is investment advice.

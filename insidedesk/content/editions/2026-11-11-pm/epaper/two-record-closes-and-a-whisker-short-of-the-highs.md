The Stoxx Europe 600 closed up 0.72% at 663.05, a record close [1], 0.36 points under its intraday high of 663.41 (desk arithmetic) [3]. The DAX closed up 1.20% at 26,662.77 [2], also a record close by Dow Jones's count [2], and about 30 points under last Thursday's intraday high of 26,692 [4]. Siemens Energy led, up 10.8% [4]; the arms maker CSG fell 21.6% after cutting its margin forecast [3]. Société Générale called the rally "somewhat stretched" [3]. A record close is a fact about yesterday's buyers, not tomorrow's. Not investment advice.

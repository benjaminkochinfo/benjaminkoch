In more than two million California deliveries from 2016 to 2021, 10% of women diagnosed with cannabis use disorder during pregnancy made a psychiatric emergency-department visit in the year after giving birth, against 15.6% of women with alcohol use disorder and under 3% of women with neither [1]. Over the six years, the diagnosis among pregnant women rose by 40% while alcohol use disorder stayed flat [1]. The study, in Annals of Epidemiology, was led from UC Merced [1].

"This study is not designed to prove that cannabis use disorder causes psychiatric emergencies," says Sidra Goldman-Mellor [1]. Its use is plainer: it marks a group that screening in antenatal and postpartum care should not miss [1].

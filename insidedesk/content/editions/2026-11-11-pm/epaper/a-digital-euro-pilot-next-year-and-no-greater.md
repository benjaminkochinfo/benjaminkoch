At the Paris Peace Forum on Wednesday, ECB President Christine Lagarde said the digital euro "will start next year with the pilot", with the political agreement being concluded "as we speak", Teleborsa reports (our translation) [1]. "We are improving payment infrastructure enormously," she said [1].

As ECB president, she said, she sees no more important urgency than building a real capital markets union [2]. In plain terms: a saver's money in one country finding a firm in another without a border of paperwork in between. She called the ECB the guardian of the euro, the currency of 21 countries [1]. Lagarde leaves the ECB on 7 February [3].

Payments and markets are dull infrastructure. That is why they last longer than speeches.

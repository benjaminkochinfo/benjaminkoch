CoreWeave's third-quarter revenue rose 168% to $3.7 billion, and the company raised its 2026 outlook to $13 billion to $13.3 billion of revenue, against $12.86 billion expected by analysts polled by LSEG [1]. Its shares jumped 20% on Wednesday [1].

The headline quote was about demand: chief executive Michael Intrator said the company is "10x oversubscribed" on its remaining 2027 capacity [1]. Treat that as a speech. The printed figure that matters more is financing: growth was backed by contract-secured financing and around $9 billion in customer prepayments and deposits [1]. That matters because earlier this month spreads on CoreWeave's credit default swaps widened past 11%, a level generally viewed as distress [1].

A prepayment is a customer putting cash behind a promise. It is the least glamorous line in the report, and the most persuasive. Nothing here is investment advice.

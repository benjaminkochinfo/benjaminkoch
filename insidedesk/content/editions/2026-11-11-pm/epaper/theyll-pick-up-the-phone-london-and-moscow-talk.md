UK Defence Secretary Wes Streeting told the Financial Times that Britain and Russia need more channels of communication, especially direct military-to-military contact, to avoid "miscalculation" [1]. On Wednesday, Russian foreign ministry spokesperson Maria Zakharova replied at her briefing: "I can confidently recommend that the British defence secretary contact the Russian ministry of defence. They'll pick up the phone and have a conversation," Reuters reported [1].

The tone was mocking. The content is worth keeping anyway. Hotlines are the dullest part of security policy: a number, a duty officer, a log of who called whom. They do not end wars. They keep a drone off course or a jet too close from turning into something nobody chose. If a sarcastic answer leads to a working phone line, that is a fair trade.

Hotels, inns, guesthouses and campsites in Germany recorded 389.8 million overnight stays from January to September, 0.6% more than the previous record set a year earlier at 387.6 million, the Federal Statistical Office said today [1]. Guests from Germany accounted for 324.7 million (+0.4%), guests from abroad for 65.0 million (+1.5%) [1]. September alone brought 48.9 million nights, 0.8% more than a year before; domestic guests stagnated while more came from abroad [1].

One footnote: the count covers only businesses with at least ten beds or ten camping pitches, so many private holiday flats are not in it [1]. The record is real; it is also a record of the places that file returns.

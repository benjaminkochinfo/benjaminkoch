grüüün, the brand of Münch Energie GmbH & Co. KG, has announced at short notice that it will stop supplying household customers on 31 October 2026, this Saturday. It had previously advertised a ten-year price guarantee [1]. The Federation of German Consumer Organisations (vzbv) said on Thursday that it is examining a class action [1].

Ronny Jahn, who leads the federation's class-action team, said the company is stopping supply with "only three weeks' notice" (our translation), and that whoever advertises such promises must be held to them [1].

The federation's view is that the announcement does not automatically end existing contracts: terms and notice periods bind the supplier as well as the customer [1]. grüüün cited expected changes in the law; the vzbv says such changes are a normal business risk, and that customers could be entitled to damages covering the extra cost of a new contract [1]. Whether it sues depends partly on how many people are affected [1].

If you are a grüüün customer, the federation recommends [1]:

- Register, free and without obligation, for its email alert at sammelklagen.de/grüüün.
- Object to the supply stop in writing; a template letter is on the same site.
- Compare the recommended replacement offer with other tariffs before signing.
- Screenshot your price comparison with the date, postcode, consumption, unit price and standing charge, and note your old and new prices.
- Save the order confirmation, tariff sheet and any emails about the guarantee outside the customer portal.
- Read and photograph your meter on 31 October, then check the final bill.

The federation says that even customers who accepted the recommended replacement offer may still have claims [1]. Paperwork is the cheapest protection you have; it costs an evening.

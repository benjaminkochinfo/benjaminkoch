The gap between French government bond yields and German Bunds, the market's main gauge of French worries, stood at 151 basis points on Thursday, Reuters reports [1]. Last week it touched almost 178, a near 15-year high [1]. Desk arithmetic: that is about 27 basis points narrower.

Christine Lagarde said the ECB would keep watching rising French borrowing costs and fragmentation [2]. UBS's Dean Turner said France's fiscal challenge is not expected to change the direction of policy "in the absence of broader financial stress" [2]. France votes for a president in the spring [1]. Desk note: a spread is a price for uncertainty, and the uncertainty has a date. Nothing here is investment advice.

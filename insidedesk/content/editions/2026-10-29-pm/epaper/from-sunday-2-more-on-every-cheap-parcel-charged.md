From 1 November, sellers shipping parcels worth under €150 into the EU from outside it pay a €2 handling fee per parcel [1]. Since 1 July there has been no duty-free threshold; instead a flat duty of €3 applies per category of goods, until 1 July 2028 [1]. The vzbv's example: one parcel with a silk blouse and a wool blouse counts as two categories, so €6 in duty [1].

About 12 million such small parcels reach the EU every day [1]. Stiftung Warentest recently found that of 162 low-value marketplace products tested, nearly two-thirds failed EU standards and a quarter were potentially dangerous [1].

Desk arithmetic: for that two-blouse parcel, duty plus handling fee comes to €8. The fees are meant for the seller, not you, and the federation says any extra costs must not be passed on through hidden mark-ups [1]. So check the total at checkout before you pay.

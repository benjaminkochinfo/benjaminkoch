Europe's underground gas stores are about 74.6% full, Investing.com reports, citing Gas Infrastructure Europe [1]. That is the lowest level for late October in records going back to 2009, against a five-year average of 86% and more than 95% at this point last year [1].

Prices eased on Thursday morning: the Dutch TTF front-month contract fell 3.9% to €96.80 per megawatt hour as traders took profits, though it is still on course for an October gain of about 34% [1]. Oil fell too, with Brent down 2% to $112.99 a barrel after reports of renewed US–Iran peace efforts, CNBC reports [2]. Desk note: a cheaper morning does not refill a tank. The storage figure, not the price, is the number that decides how calm the winter is. Nothing here is investment advice.

The advisory this evening's ePaper promised arrived on schedule, and it brought a change. At 16:00 Berlin time In-fa had been upgraded to a super typhoon again, with maximum sustained winds of 150 mph, up from 140 mph three hours earlier [1], [2]. It was about 305 miles east of Alamagan, moving west at 18 mph, and the track still takes it close to Agrihan on Friday evening local time [1].

The headline figure is the peak wind. The working figure is the reach. Typhoon-force winds now extend up to 80 miles from the centre, against 35 miles in the previous advisory; tropical-storm-force winds reach 175 miles, against 90 [1], [2]. A wider storm means more islands inside the damaging band for longer, even at the same peak.

The typhoon warning for Alamagan, Pagan and Agrihan stands [1]. The forecasters expect slow weakening through Saturday [1]. The next intermediate advisory is due at 05:00 local time, 20:00 in Berlin, and the next full one at 23:00 Berlin time [1]. The timetable, again, is the reassuring part.

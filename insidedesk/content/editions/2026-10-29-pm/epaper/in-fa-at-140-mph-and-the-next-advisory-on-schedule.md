Typhoon In-fa was about 365 miles east of Alamagan in the Northern Mariana Islands at 13:00 CET, with maximum sustained winds of 140 mph, moving west-northwest at 20 mph, according to the National Weather Service office on Guam [1]. A typhoon warning is in effect for Alamagan, Pagan and Agrihan [1]. The forecast track takes it close to Agrihan on Friday late afternoon or evening local time, which is Friday morning in Berlin; the office expects it to hold near this intensity through Saturday and then weaken to a Category 3 typhoon [1]. Typhoon-force winds reach up to 35 miles from the centre [1].

A typhoon warning means damaging winds are expected within 24 hours [1]. That day of notice is what the dull machinery buys: an advisory on a fixed schedule, the next one at 02:00 local time on Friday, which was 17:00 Berlin time on Thursday [1]. If you have family on the islands, the local advisories are the ones to follow, not the satellite pictures.

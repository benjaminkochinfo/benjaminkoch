The UF Election Lab's state files now add up to 40,532,275 early ballots for the 3 November midterms: 20,910,050 cast in person and 19,628,751 mail ballots returned, across 49 states and DC, according to an independent tracker that compiles them [1]. The Oct 27 file stood at 35.1 million [1]. Most state files are as of 28 October [1].

Texas leads with 5,673,510 ballots, already 69.6% of its total 2022 turnout; California follows with 4,307,811 and Florida with 3,896,546 [1]. Desk arithmetic: the national total grew by about 5.4 million between the two files.

The tracker repeats the caveat that matters: early vote counts show who has voted, not how [1]. Officials do not begin counting until Election Day. Until then, the queue is the news.

Three tankers exploded and caught fire after hitting sea mines while trying to use an "unauthorized route" south of the Strait of Hormuz over the past 24 hours, Iran's Revolutionary Guards said in a statement carried by state media on Thursday, according to Reuters and AFP [1]. The Guards said the ships had switched off their radar and navigation systems, ignored warnings from the Guards' navy and had been abandoned on the southern shores of the strait [1]. No casualty figures were given.

The independent part of the record is smaller. UKMTO, the British maritime agency, said it had received a report from the master of another ship of a vessel on fire nine nautical miles, about 17 km, north of Khasab in Oman, without giving a cause or any casualties [1]. Whether that is one of the three tankers the Guards describe has not been established in the reports.

The background, as Reuters and AFP set it out: since the war began in February, Iran has sought to control the waterway and has attacked ships it accuses of passing without permission, and the United States answers with a counter-blockade of Iranian ports [1].

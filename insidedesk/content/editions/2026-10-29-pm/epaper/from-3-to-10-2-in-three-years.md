Among adult tobacco users in the United States, current use of nicotine pouches rose from 3% in 2021 to 10.2% in 2024, and the share who had ever tried one rose from 16.4% to 31.1%, according to a University of Cincinnati study published in the journal Addiction [1]. It compared nationally representative YouGov surveys from the two years [1].

Use was most common among men, adults aged 21 to 45 and people already using tobacco, especially smokeless tobacco [1]. That points to pouches being added to other products as much as replacing them [1]. "We don't yet know the long-term health consequences," said the lead investigator, Ashley Merianos [1].

Pouches are sold as the tidy alternative. Tidy is not the same as tested. If you use them, tell your doctor or dentist; they cannot discuss what they do not know you use.

Saudi civil defence said 11 people were wounded on Tuesday when a strike hit a tower in Riyadh's financial district, the capital's tallest building and headquarters of the Public Investment Fund [1]. It was the second strike on the tower in less than a week; the first, on 19 November, caused damage but no injuries [1]. The Houthis claimed an attack on the district "in three phases" [1]. The group has targeted Saudi airports, oil infrastructure and buildings since Yemen's frozen war reignited in July, The National reports [1]. A war that was about pipelines is now also about office floors.

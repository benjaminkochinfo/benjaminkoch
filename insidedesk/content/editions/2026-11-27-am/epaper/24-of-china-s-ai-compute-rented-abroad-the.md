Chinese companies have been using Nvidia chips remotely from data centres in Southeast Asia, TechCrunch reports, a route the Commerce Department closed earlier this month [1]. Citing the Wall Street Journal, it notes an Institute for Progress estimate that such remote data centres supply about 24% of China's AI computing power [1]. CNA, publishing the FT's report, adds that China's leading AI labs still rely heavily on Nvidia processors for compute-intensive early training [2]. If the estimate is right, the halt in four countries tests a quarter of a rival's capacity. That is an estimate from a think tank, not a measured figure.

Japan's Nikkei was up 0.7% early on Friday, taking its weekly gain to 2.7%, against a loss of 8.4% for November, Reuters reported [1]. MSCI's broadest Asia-Pacific index outside Japan was up 1.9% for the week and still down 8.2% for the month [1]. China's blue chips slipped 0.2% after hitting their lowest level since June 2025 the day before [1]. Desk arithmetic: after an 8.4% fall you need about a 9.2% rise to get back. One good week is about 30% of the way there.

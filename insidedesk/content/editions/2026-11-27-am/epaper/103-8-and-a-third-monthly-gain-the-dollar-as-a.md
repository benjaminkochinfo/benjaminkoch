The dollar index was at 103.8 on Friday, up 1.8% in November and heading for a third monthly gain, Reuters reported, supported by expected Fed rate rises and safe-haven demand during the equity sell-off [1]. The euro was flat at $1.0964 after the well-telegraphed choice of Pablo Hernández de Cos as next ECB president [1]. Two-year Treasury yields rose 3 basis points to 4.6705% and ten-year yields 4 basis points to 4.8918% as cash trading resumed after the holiday [1]. A strong dollar makes your dollar-priced imports, oil included, a little dearer in euros.

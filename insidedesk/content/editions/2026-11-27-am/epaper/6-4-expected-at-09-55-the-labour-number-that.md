The Federal Employment Agency publishes November labour-market data at 09:55 CET [1]. Economists polled by Dow Jones expect seasonally adjusted unemployment to rise by 6,000 after 4,000 in October, with the rate unchanged at 6.4% [1]. Those are expectations, not results; the real figures come out this morning. Shopping weekends make headlines. Payrolls decide whether January's card bills get paid.

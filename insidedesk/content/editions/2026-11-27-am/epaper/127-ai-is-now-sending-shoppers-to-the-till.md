AI-referred visits to US retailers' websites are up 127% on a year earlier this holiday season, Adobe Analytics reports [1]. It expects increases of 159% on Thanksgiving, 95% on Black Friday and 88% on Cyber Monday [1]. In Adobe's own survey of 1,000 consumers, 81% said AI improved their online shopping [1]. Growth rates from a small base flatter; Adobe does not give AI's share of total visits in this report. The question for retailers is less "how fast" than "how much of the till".

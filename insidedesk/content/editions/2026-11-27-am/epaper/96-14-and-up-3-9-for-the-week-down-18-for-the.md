Brent crude futures slipped 0.4% early on Friday to $96.14 a barrel, Reuters reported, but were still up 3.9% for the week, the first weekly rise in four, and down 18% for the month [1]. AFP's 03:30 CET snapshot had Brent at $96.31 and West Texas Intermediate up 2.1% at $85.47 [2]. The driver is supply risk: attacks on Gulf energy sites this week [1][2]. Desk arithmetic: an 18% monthly fall to $96.14 implies a level near $117 a month ago. Cheaper than then; not cheap.

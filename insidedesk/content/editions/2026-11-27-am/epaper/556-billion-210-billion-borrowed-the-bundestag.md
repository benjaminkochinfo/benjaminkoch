The Bundestag votes today on the 2027 federal budget, Deutschlandfunk reports [1]. The coalition plans spending of €556 billion, about €31 billion more than this year; more than a third goes to labour and social affairs, and almost €140 billion to defence [1]. New borrowing across the core budget and special funds is about €210 billion [1]. The last dispute was the Union's demand for a lower electricity tax for everyone [1]. Desk arithmetic: borrowing equals about 38% of planned spending, though part of it sits in special funds outside the core budget.

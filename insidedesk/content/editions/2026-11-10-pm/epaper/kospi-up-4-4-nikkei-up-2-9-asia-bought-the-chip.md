South Korea's Kospi rose 4.4% on Tuesday and Japan's Nikkei 2.9%, while MSCI's broadest index of Asia-Pacific shares gained 2%, moving away from the seven-month low it hit last week, Reuters reported [1]. XTB attributed the strongest gains to the markets most exposed to semiconductors, with SK hynix, Samsung, Kioxia and Tokyo Electron among the movers, and noted that TSMC reported October revenue up 59.7% on the year [2].

The rebound followed last week's sell-off after the US midterms [1]. XTB's own reading was cautious: the move "still looks more like a powerful relief rally" than a trend reversal [2]. That is the right temperament. A monthly revenue line from a foundry is an invoice; an index jump is an opinion about it. Nothing here is investment advice.

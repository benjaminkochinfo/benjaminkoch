Apple now sells extra AI. In Logic Pro, Creator Studio subscribers who reach their monthly limit can buy an "intelligence usage pack" for $49.99, a one-time purchase [1]. Apple's support page says the packs are offered in Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, India, Mexico, the UK and the US, cannot be shared with family, and expire 12 months after purchase; unused usage rolls over from month to month, and sales are final except where the law says otherwise [2].

The monthly allowance is spelled out in minimums: at least 50 images, 250 shapes or 50 presentations, if used for one feature only [2]. Apple's page says some features run on Google Gemini or OpenAI models [2]. Read the terms before you tap "Buy More Usage": a top-up that expires is a budget line, not a gift.

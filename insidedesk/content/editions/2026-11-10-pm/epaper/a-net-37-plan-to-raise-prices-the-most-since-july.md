Before Tuesday's consumer price index, America's small firms had already filed their own report. The NFIB Small Business Optimism Index for October was 97.8, down 0.1 points and just below its 53-year average of 98.0 [1]. A seasonally adjusted net 33% of owners said they had raised average selling prices, and a net 37% plan to raise them, up 6 points and the highest since July 2022 [1]. A net 13% reported paying a higher rate on their latest loan, the most since August 2024 [1].

There is a sturdier line in the same survey: 69% rated their business's health good or excellent, and uncertainty fell 6 points to 84 [1]. Desk arithmetic: 15% excellent plus 54% good. The till is busy, the price list is under review, and the owners say so in plain numbers. Nothing here is investment advice.

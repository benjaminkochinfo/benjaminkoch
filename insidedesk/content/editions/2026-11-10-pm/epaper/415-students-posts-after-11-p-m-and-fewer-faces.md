Researchers at the University of California San Diego analysed Instagram data from 415 college students, more than 60% with private accounts, Health Chosun reports; the study is in npj Mental Health Research [1]. Students with more emotional loneliness posted more between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m., showed fewer faces, used more negative captions and mentioned family and friends more often [1]. Social loneliness went with fewer likes and comments on others' posts and fewer weekend posts [1].

The study cannot say whether loneliness shapes posting or the other way round [1]. For you, the cheaper detector remains a phone call to the friend who has gone quiet.

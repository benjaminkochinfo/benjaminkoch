While his negotiators sat in Abu Dhabi, President Zelenskyy was in Copenhagen for talks with Nordic leaders on military aid, air defence and protecting Ukraine's energy system from Russian strikes, the Guardian reports [1]. Denmark announced 4.8 billion kroner (about $720 million) in military aid, without saying what it contains or when it arrives [1]. Zelenskyy has been urging allies to speed up deliveries of Patriot interceptors, the most effective weapon Ukraine has against ballistic missiles [1].

The timing is the point. Overnight Russia hit five substations that move power between regions [1]. Talks decide what the war ends with; interceptors decide what is still standing when it does.

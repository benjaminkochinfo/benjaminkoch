The Dutch TTF front-month gas contract, Europe's benchmark, fell 0.45% to €82 per megawatt hour early on Tuesday, its lowest in a month, Investing.com reported; Britain's NBP touched a one-month low of 205 pence per therm [1]. Brent crude was down $2.39 at $90.18 at the time, about 23% below its six-month high of $118 on 30 October [1].

The report names two drivers: President Trump's statement that he would hold off on new strikes against Iran, and US-brokered talks between Russian and Ukrainian officials scheduled for Tuesday, their first direct talks since February [1]. Both are words, not molecules. Shipping attacks near the Strait of Hormuz remain on traders' screens, the report notes [1].

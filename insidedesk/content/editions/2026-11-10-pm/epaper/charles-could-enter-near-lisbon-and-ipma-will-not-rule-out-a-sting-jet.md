IPMA's second bulletin on Charles, issued at 18:07 CET, says the low's centre will most likely cross the coast between Sines and Cabo Carvoeiro on Wednesday, with "elevada incerteza" (high uncertainty) over track and strength [1]. Gusts of 100 to 120 km/h are expected on its southern flank, locally 140 km/h, with more than 100 mm of rain in 24 hours in places and maximum wave heights of up to 13 metres south of Cabo Raso [1]. At a civil-protection briefing, IPMA's Jorge Ponte called a sting jet, a narrow descending blast that can exceed 160 km/h, "very difficult to predict", but "on the table" [2]. A warning that admits what it does not know is still a warning worth taking.

New-lease rents in German apartment buildings rose 3.5% in the third quarter from a year earlier, according to the Association of German Pfandbrief Banks (vdp), after 3.2% in the second quarter [1]. Prices for residential property rose only 1.0%: single-family houses 1.3%, owner-occupied flats 2.3% [1]. The vdp's chief executive, Jens Tolckmitt, said the situation was worsening especially in the big cities [1].

Tolckmitt blamed much higher interest rates for making property finance harder; ZDF notes ten-year mortgage rates recently climbed above 4% [1]. The Ifo institute expects completions below 200,000 in both 2027 and 2028, against the roughly 320,000 a year the federal building research institute BBSR says are needed to 2030 [1]. For you as a tenant: check any rent increase against the local rent index (Mietspiegel) before you sign.

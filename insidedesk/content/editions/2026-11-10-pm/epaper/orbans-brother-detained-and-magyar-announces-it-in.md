Hungary's national asset protection and recovery office and the police detained four people and searched homes in Budapest and Pest county, the Guardian reports [1]. Prime Minister Péter Magyar, speaking in Berlin, linked one arrest to Áron Orbán, 49, the former prime minister's brother: "Three ex-ministers of Orbán are already in custody" [1]. Police say the four are suspected of helping more than 100 Vietnamese citizens obtain visas at Hungary's missions in Vietnam in 2024, in exchange for a recruitment fee [1].

Viktor Orbán called it a "political witch-hunt" and said: "I am the real target" [1]. The three former ministers detained in earlier cases, including ex-central bank governor György Matolcsy, deny wrongdoing [1]. The test of the new office is not the arrests. It is whether the cases hold up in court.

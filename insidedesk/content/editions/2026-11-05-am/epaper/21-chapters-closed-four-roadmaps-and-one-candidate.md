Montenegro has provisionally closed 21 negotiating chapters and, if member states agree, could close the rest by the end of this year, the European Commission said in its 2026 Enlargement Package; work on its accession treaty is under way [1]. Albania aims to finish talks by 2027, Moldova by early 2028, and the Commission published indicative roadmaps with milestones for these two, Montenegro and Ukraine [1]. At the other end, Serbia made no substantive progress, the gap with Türkiye has "widened further", and Georgia remains, in the Commission's words, a candidate country "in name only" [1]. The Commission's own background note calls enlargement "a merit-based process" [1]. It is now for EU governments in the Council to decide the next steps [1]. Reform is slow; so, it turns out, is backsliding's bill.

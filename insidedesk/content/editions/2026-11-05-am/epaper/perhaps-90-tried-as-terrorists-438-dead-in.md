"Perhaps 90 percent" of Ukrainian detainees brought before Russian courts, including military courts, are prosecuted on terrorism-related charges rather than for taking part in hostilities, Mariana Katzarova, the UN Special Rapporteur on human rights in Russia, told Ukrinform [1]. She noted reports that 438 Ukrainians have died in Russian captivity and said she does not expect the Russian authorities to investigate [1]. Under the Geneva Conventions, prisoners of war may not be charged with terrorism simply for fighting [1]. Her remedy is unglamorous: document each case for future trials, use the International Criminal Court and universal jurisdiction, and fund the work [1]. The first UN mandate in 80 years to monitor one of the Security Council's five permanent members is, she says, her most important achievement [1]. Files outlast regimes.

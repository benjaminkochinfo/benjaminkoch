HP's board has unanimously appointed Emma Walmsley, who ran GSK from April 2017 to December 2025, as chief executive from 4 January 2027 [1]. She has sat on Microsoft's board for seven years and spent 17 years at L'Oréal before GSK [1]. Bruce Broussard, interim chief executive since February, becomes chair on the same day, succeeding Chip Bergh [1].

Walmsley promised to move "with clarity and conviction" in "the AI era" [1]. The less glamorous context is visible elsewhere in the same day's news: dearer memory chips are pushing up device prices and squeezing demand, as Qualcomm's results showed [2]. A chief executive from outside the industry will be judged on the dull parts first: component contracts, inventories and margins.

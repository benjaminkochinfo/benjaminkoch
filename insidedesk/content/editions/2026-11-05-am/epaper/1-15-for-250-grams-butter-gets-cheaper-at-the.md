A 250-gram pack of Lidl's own-brand German butter now costs €1.15, 4 cents less, or €4.60 a kilo; Norma, Aldi Nord and Aldi Süd followed a day later [1]. At the same time the Kiel raw-milk value rose 9.2% in October to 46.5 cents a kilo, and many dairies raised what they pay farmers [1]. The butter quotation in Kempten, at €4.35 to €5.00 a kilo for packs, has not moved for weeks [1].

agrarheute calculates that, net of VAT, the shops keep about €4.30 a kilo, below the bottom of that range [1]. Butter is a price the customer uses to compare shops, so rivals match every change at once [1]. Enjoy the cheaper pack for the Christmas baking. It is a shop's advertisement, not a signal that milk is getting cheaper.

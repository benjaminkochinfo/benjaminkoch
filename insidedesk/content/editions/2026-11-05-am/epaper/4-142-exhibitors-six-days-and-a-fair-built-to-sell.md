The China International Import Expo opened its ninth edition in Shanghai on Thursday and runs until 10 November [1]. It has drawn 4,142 overseas exhibitors on more than 440,000 square metres, and over 480 products will make a global, Asian or Chinese debut, the most yet, Xinhua reports [1]. President Xi Jinping launched the fair in 2018 to show China's commitment to opening its market; it has become a venue for foreign governments and companies to sign purchase agreements and memoranda, and organisers said tentative deals worth more than $80 billion were struck last year, Reuters reports [2]. Reuters' headline sets this year's opening against record trade imbalances [2]. "Tentative" is the word to keep. A memorandum is a handshake; an invoice paid is trade.

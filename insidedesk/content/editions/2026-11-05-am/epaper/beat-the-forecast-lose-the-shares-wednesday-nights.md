CNBC's after-hours list on Wednesday read like a lesson in expectations [1]. Arm beat estimates with adjusted earnings of 52 cents a share against 47 expected and dipped almost 2%. Figma earned 10 cents against 5 expected and fell almost 7%. Fastly beat on earnings, revenue and guidance and dropped 16%; Klaviyo beat and raised its full-year revenue guidance, and slid about 5% [1].

Where the forecast carried the beat, the shares rose: AppLovin jumped more than 20% after adjusted EBITDA of $1.84 billion against $1.73 billion expected, and Coherent gained 7% on earnings and guidance above estimates [1]. HubSpot fell more than 8% on a fourth-quarter outlook short of forecasts [1]. After-hours prices are thin; the open is the first real vote. Nothing here is investment advice.

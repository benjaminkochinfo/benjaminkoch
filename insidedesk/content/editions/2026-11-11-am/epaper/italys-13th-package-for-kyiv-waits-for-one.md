Italy's thirteenth package of military aid for Ukraine has been signed by Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani and Defence Minister Guido Crosetto. It still needs Economy Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti, who was at Ecofin in Brussels and, because the document is classified, could not sign it remotely, Il Sole 24 Ore reports [1]. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni called it "a temporary physical impediment" [1].

The politics sits elsewhere. Matteo Salvini's League wants to stop money and weapons going to Kyiv, and the next real test is the framework decree for 2027 [1]. Crosetto says Ukraine now needs means against faster drones that target civilian objects [1]. A wet-ink signature is old-fashioned. That is part of how a ministry keeps classified decisions controlled.

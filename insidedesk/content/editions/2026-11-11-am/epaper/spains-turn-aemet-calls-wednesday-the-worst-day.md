Spain's weather service, AEMET, expects Wednesday to be "el día más adverso del episodio", with Charles entering through the south-west of the peninsula [1]. Its special warning, issued at 19:19 on Tuesday, gives a 70% probability and runs to Thursday [1]. From midday gusts of 100–120 km/h are possible not only on mountains but in lowlands of southern Castilla y León, Extremadura and western Andalucía, with a chance of tornadoes or waterspouts in the Gulf of Cádiz and seas of 5 to 6 metres [1]. The line to read twice is about land: across the whole episode, totals may locally exceed 300 mm in the Sistema Central, where summer fires raise the risk of landslides [1]. A burnt slope does not hold water. The warning says so before the mud does.

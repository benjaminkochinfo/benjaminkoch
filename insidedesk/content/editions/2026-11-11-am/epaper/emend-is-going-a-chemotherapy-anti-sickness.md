The European Medicines Agency lists Emend (aprepitant), used to prevent nausea and vomiting caused by chemotherapy, as a shortage that began on 6 November [1]. Merck Sharp & Dohme is withdrawing it permanently for commercial reasons, not because of a quality or safety problem, and expects it gone from all EU/EEA countries by May 2028 [1]. Other aprepitant medicines and the infusion Ivemend (fosaprepitant) remain alternatives; the gap is for children from six months, for whom "no other medicine is available" as an oral suspension [1]. EDQM has guidance for pharmacists to prepare a liquid from adult capsules [1]. If you or your child takes it, EMA's advice is simple: speak to your pharmacist well before supply runs out, and do not stop without asking [1].

AMD has guided fourth-quarter revenue to about $17 billion, plus or minus 3%, while Intel guided to $15–16 billion for the same months, TechRadar notes; if both land inside their ranges, AMD will outearn Intel in a quarter for the first time [1]. Intel's guide includes about $300 million of lost revenue from the 14 October explosion at its Kiryat Gat plant in Israel, its finance chief said [1].

The crossover is about what is sold, not how many. Intel still held 65.9% of the x86 processor market in the second quarter, per Mercury Research [1]. AMD's data-centre revenue was $8.5 billion in the third quarter, and its chief financial officer expects AI accelerators and racks to outsell its server CPUs this quarter [1]. The ramp has a bill: AMD's inventory rose about $3 billion to $11.5 billion, and free cash flow was $554 million [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

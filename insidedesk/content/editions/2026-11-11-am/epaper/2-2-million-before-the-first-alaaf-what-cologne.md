The opening of the carnival season today, on 11.11, costs the city of Cologne at least €2.2 million, Radio Köln reports [1]. The city confirmed €1.9 million for the private security contractor and its subcontractors, including setting up and taking down, plus about €300,000 for extra ambulance services and aid organisations [1].

Not included: the many hours worked by city employees. The administration could not say what those cost, nor what the day costs the city in total [1]. Desk arithmetic: the security contract alone is about 86% of the known €2.2 million.

Barriers, floor plates and hundreds of urinals now line the city centre [1]. That is the dull machinery of a party: someone invoices it, someone pays it, and if it works nobody notices. For you, if you are going: the fences are not decoration, so follow them.

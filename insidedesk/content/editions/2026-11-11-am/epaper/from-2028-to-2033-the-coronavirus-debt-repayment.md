Finance Minister Lars Klingbeil plans to postpone the start of repaying the debt taken on during the coronavirus years from 2028 to 2033, Merkur reports [1]. The plan has added to the coalition's budget dispute. Hanno Kube, a professor at Heidelberg University, said the minister was contributing "to the further increase in public debt" and that it fits into "a by now very worrying overall picture" (as quoted by Merkur; our translation) [1].

The same report puts the federal government's uncovered financing need for 2028 to 2030 at around €115 billion [1]. Desk arithmetic: a five-year delay means the first repayments would fall after that window, not inside it.

The budget committee's clean-up session starts on Thursday and is expected to run into Friday morning [2]. For you: a repayment schedule is a promise to your future taxes. Watch whether it survives the session unchanged. Nothing here is investment advice.

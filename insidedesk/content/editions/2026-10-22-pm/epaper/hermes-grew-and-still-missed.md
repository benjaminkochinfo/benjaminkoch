Hermès sales rose 4.3% at constant currency to €4.10 billion in the third quarter, below the 5.2% analysts expected and its weakest growth since early 2020, Reuters reports [1]. Leather goods rose 10.8%; ready-to-wear and accessories fell 2%, mainly on weak Chinese demand for accessories [1]. The shares were down nearly 8% by midday [2]. The finance chief called China "very difficult" but "stable" [1]. A slower quarter is still a growing one. Nothing here is advice.

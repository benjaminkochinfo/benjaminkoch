Nigeria's first Ebola case of this outbreak is the Bundibugyo strain that is driving Congo's epidemic, officials said [1]. "We did three levels of tests, but all molecular. The strain was Bundibugyo," said Olajumoke Babatunde of the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control's laboratory service [1]. The WHO confirmed the result [1]. The patient, a diplomat, travelled from Uganda to Abuja on 3 October; 44 contacts are being monitored, and infection control is being "ramped up" at the private hospital where she sought care [1]. In 2014 Nigeria held its outbreak to 19 infections [1]. A laboratory that answers clearly is the first line of that record.

The motoring club ADAC's weekly comparison, published on Wednesday afternoon, has Super E10 at a national average of €2.110 a litre, 4.1 cents less than a week earlier [1]. Diesel rose 0.8 cents to €2.255 [1].

The oil price went the other way. Brent closed on Tuesday at about $109 a barrel, roughly three dollars more than a week before and the highest level since May [1]. So why is petrol cheaper? ADAC's reading is that E10 had room to fall, and that most of the fuel-tax cut now seems to reach the pump for petrol [1]. Diesel is held up by something else: demand for heating oil, a close cousin, rises as the weather cools [1].

Desk arithmetic: a 50-litre tank of E10 costs €105.50 at the average, €2.05 less than a week ago; 50 litres of diesel cost €112.75, 40 cents more. The gap between the two fuels is now 14.5 cents a litre.

ADAC also repeats a pattern it observes every day: prices are usually lowest shortly before noon, jump at 12:00 and then ease [1]. Its app compares the current prices at more than 14,000 stations in Germany [1]. Nothing here is advice.

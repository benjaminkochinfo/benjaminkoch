EU member states are "temporarily shelving" their demand for lower fees for European students at UK universities, the Financial Times reported on Thursday [1]. That demand had been part of the youth mobility talks [1]. The Commission asked capitals on 21 October to hold back [1]. "It is a gift to the UK so they can continue their good momentum," one EU diplomat said [1]. A Commission proposal suggests that all three reset agreements, on youth mobility, food and plant checks, and linking carbon markets, should be closed at negotiators' level before the 20 November summit [1]. Diplomats call the food-checks deal "pretty much complete" [1].

The Met Office named Storm Boelo on Thursday, to follow Storm Austen with wind and rain across the whole of the UK on Saturday [1]. Austen's amber warning covers Cornwall, Scilly, Plymouth and parts of Devon from 01:00 to 10:00 on Friday, with gusts of 60–70 mph [1]. The Government of Jersey has told all schools to close on Friday [1]. The BBC notes that storms often come in clusters, and that in about half the years since 2015 the first named storm arrived after 1 November [1]. This year it came early, and so did the warnings.

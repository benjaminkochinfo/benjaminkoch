South Korea's benchmark KOSPI fell 201.8 points, or 3.52%, to 5,533.77 on Thursday, a day after rebounding more than 3% [1]. Samsung Electronics closed 4.03% lower at 214,500 won and SK hynix 5.49% lower at 1.22 million won [1]. Samsung Electro-Mechanics opened higher on news of its Nvidia substrate deal and still closed 1.97% down [1].

The explanation offered was not about chips at all. "Although Meta and Tesla reported earnings that exceeded market expectations, their results failed to ease concerns over profitability amid massive AI investments," said Lee Kyoung-min of Daishin Securities [1]. He added that the end of SK hynix's share buyback "reduced a source of support for market demand" [1].

Read the plumbing. Trading was light, at 201.17 million shares, and decliners outnumbered advancers 706 to 174 [1]. Institutions sold a net 46.3 billion won while foreigners and individuals were small net buyers [1]. Desk arithmetic: on 13.7 trillion won of turnover, the institutions' net sale was about 0.3%. A thin day with one buyer gone can move an index a long way without anyone changing their mind about memory chips. The won, for its part, firmed slightly, to 1,324.2 per dollar [1].

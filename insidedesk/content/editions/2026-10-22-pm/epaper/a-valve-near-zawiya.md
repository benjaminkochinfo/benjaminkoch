Libya's National Oil Corporation said on Thursday that individuals entered the crude receiving station near the Zawiya refinery and closed the valve on the line from the Hamada and Tahara fields, cutting output at both [1]. The NOC urged the authorities "to intervene immediately to reopen the valve and secure the site" [1]. Hamada normally produces 50,000 barrels a day of crude; Libya's total is about 1.4 million [1]. Desk arithmetic: Hamada alone is under 4% of that. In September an armed group shut a valve on the Sharara line in the same way [1]. Supply security is sometimes a navy, and sometimes a guard at a gate.

Paris city hall has urged residents to take "extreme caution" during Saturday's protests [1]. Its advice is practical: bring rubbish bins, bicycles and outdoor furniture inside "from midday Saturday" [1]. The city has started clearing public spaces of barriers and movable building-site equipment. It is emptying street litter bins and removing glass recycling containers, and more municipal staff will be on duty over the weekend [1]. About 450 businesses in Paris were damaged last weekend, roughly half of them looted [1]. Prevention rarely makes the news, which is partly why it works.

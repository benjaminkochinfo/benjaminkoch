Nvidia said on Wednesday that Samsung Electro-Mechanics will join its package-substrate supply chain, first from existing Korean lines and later from upgraded and new plants in Sejong and Busan [1]. With Japan's Ibiden and Taiwan's Unimicron, Nvidia aims to raise substrate capacity for its products 2.5 times over three years [1]. Separately, it and Amkor plan to lift interposer-wafer capacity in Korea by more than 300% by the end of 2028 [1]. Unlike a speech, a supply contract names factories.

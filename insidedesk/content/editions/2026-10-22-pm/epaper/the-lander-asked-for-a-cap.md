Germany's state premiers, at their conference in Berlin on Thursday, asked the federal government to put forward a proposal for capping fuel prices, Deutschlandfunk reported at 17:46 [1]. Lower Saxony's premier, Lies of the SPD, said the instrument should give people the assurance that they are not paying for higher company profits at the pump, and that Berlin now has to settle how things go on after 1 January [1].

That date is the dull part, and the important one. At the turn of the year the lower tax rate currently applied to petrol and diesel expires [1]. In September the federal government said it would talk to the oil industry about a cap; so far that is a conversation, not a text [1]. The premiers also asked for more federal support for the seaports and for a new criminal offence covering fundraising for terrorist organisations [1].

So read the evening in order. A cap is a request from the Länder to the Bund. The tax change is already in law with a date on it. For your own budget, the date is the thing you can plan around; the cap is the thing you can watch. Nothing here is advice.

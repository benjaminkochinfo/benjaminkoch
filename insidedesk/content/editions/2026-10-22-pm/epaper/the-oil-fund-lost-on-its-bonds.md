Norway's Government Pension Fund Global returned -0.5% in the third quarter, an accounting loss of 278 billion kroner, its manager said on Thursday [1]. Fixed income lost 3.5% "as rising interest rates led to weaker results", while equities returned 0.5% and renewable energy infrastructure 7.5% [1]. A stronger krone cut the fund's value by a further 472 billion kroner; it stood at 22,014 billion kroner on 30 September [1]. Higher rates mark bonds down first and pay coupons later.

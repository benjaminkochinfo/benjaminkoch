Météo-France put Finistère, Morbihan and the Côtes-d'Armor on orange alert for wind from 22:00 on Thursday, as Storm Austen crosses the Channel overnight [1]. On Friday twelve more départements join them: all of Normandy, Ille-et-Vilaine, Loire-Atlantique, the Nord, Pas-de-Calais, the Somme, the Yvelines and the Val-d'Oise [1]. That makes 15 [1].

The forecaster expects gusts of 100–110 km/h inland, locally 120 km/h, and up to 130–140 km/h on the coast [1][2]. "Le département de la Manche sera particulièrement concerné," it added [1]. Austen is the first storm of the autumn to reach France, and heavy rain is expected on Friday over Brittany and Normandy [2].

Météo-France makes one point that is easy to miss: the trees still carry much of their foliage, which makes them more likely to fall in strong wind [2]. Its advice for an orange wind warning is plain: secure what the wind can take, limit journeys, stay off roofs, and run any generator outside the house [2].

The useful thing about an orange warning is that it arrives hours before the wind does. The map changed in daylight, with the evening still ahead.

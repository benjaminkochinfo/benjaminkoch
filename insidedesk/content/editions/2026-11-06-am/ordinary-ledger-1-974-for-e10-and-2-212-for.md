**The ledger.**
- Super E10, national average of open stations reporting to the federal price register this morning: €1.974 a litre [1].
- Diesel: €2.212 a litre [1].
- Super E5: €2.029 a litre [1].
- Midweek comparison: on Wednesday the ADAC put the national E10 average at €2.041 and diesel at €2.260, both lower than a week earlier [2].
- Desk arithmetic: a 50-litre tank of E10 at this morning's average costs about €98.70; the same tank of diesel about €110.60. Against Wednesday's ADAC averages that is roughly €3.35 less for E10 and €2.40 less for diesel, if the early print holds.

Early-morning averages are snapshots. Prices move by station, brand and hour, and they often firm after midday [1]. Brent's slide earlier this week is the wholesale backdrop; the pump is where it either arrives or does not [2]. Nothing here is advice about when to fill up — only a record of what the register showed before breakfast.

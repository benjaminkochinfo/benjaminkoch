Prof. Torsten Bornemann, co-author of the digital-detox attitude scale, says it can now be used to study when and why people want to cut digital use — and whether a changed attitude helps them take more control of technology [1]. That is the bridge from survey to habit.

Seven studies built the tool across US panels, European students, Reddit communities and consumer-behaviour experts [1]. The paper sits in *Psychology & Marketing* [1]. For a newsroom reader the implication is plain: stop asking whether people "know" screens are tiring. Ask which defaults, calendars and rooms make the approved break happen.

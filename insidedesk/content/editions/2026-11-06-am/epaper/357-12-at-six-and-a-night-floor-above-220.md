Friday's day-ahead peak for Germany/Luxembourg is €357.12 in the hour from 18:00 [1]. The morning hump reaches €323.06 at 07:00; noon eases to €226.97; the night floor is €222.38 at 03:00 [1]. The dearest-to-cheapest spread is €134.74 [1].

If your tariff tracks the auction, those four numbers matter more than the €275.64 average [1]. Run the flexible load through the troughs; treat 17:00–20:00 as dear unless you have no choice. The auction is free and daily.

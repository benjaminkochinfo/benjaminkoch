The Department of Health and Social Care has issued a Serious Shortage Protocol for Ramipril 1.25 mg capsules, limiting community pharmacies to one month's supply per prescription [1]. More than 30 million Ramipril items were dispensed in England in 2025 [1]. Alternative tablets and oral solutions remain available, though officials warn surging demand could strain those too [1].

A protocol is how the system rations fairly without leaving it to whoever arrives first. Ask your pharmacist before you assume a multi-month repeat will fill as usual.

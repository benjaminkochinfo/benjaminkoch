Prof. Jan Landwehr of Goethe University says many people feel overwhelmed by the constant digital flow and want to disconnect; his team with Bath colleagues built the Attitude towards Digital Detox scale to make that "vague positive attitude" measurable [1]. More than eight in ten participants viewed a temporary cut in digital use positively [1].

The scale predicted how long people watched online videos in the study, and tracked with mindfulness and self-control [1]. It did not measure whether anyone then took a break [1]. Wanting a detox is common; designing one into the week is still the scarce skill.

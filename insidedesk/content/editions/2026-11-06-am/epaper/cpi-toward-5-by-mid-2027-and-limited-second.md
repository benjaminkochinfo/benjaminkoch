UK CPI was 3.5% in September and is now expected to rise to around 5% by mid-2027 before falling back as energy base effects fade and restrictive policy bites [1]. Members still see "limited evidence" of second-round effects on wages and other prices, even as they judge that risk has risen — the tension behind an 8–1 vote for 25 basis points rather than 50 [1].

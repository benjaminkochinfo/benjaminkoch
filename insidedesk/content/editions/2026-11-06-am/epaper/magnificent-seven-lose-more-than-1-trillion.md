The seven megacap technology names that markets call the Magnificent Seven lost more than $1 trillion of combined market value in Thursday's session [1]. Nvidia led, down more than 9% and more than $440 billion lighter, turning negative for 2026 [1]. Tesla fell about 10.8%, Meta about 3.9%, Microsoft about 3.6%, Alphabet and Amazon about 2.2% each, and Apple about 1.5% [1].

Micron and AMD each dropped about 17%; CoreWeave about 10% [1]. The point for a non-trader is concentration: when a handful of balance sheets carry so much of an index, a single political read on data centres moves a trillion dollars before Europe's morning coffee [1]. That is a fact about market structure, not a tip.

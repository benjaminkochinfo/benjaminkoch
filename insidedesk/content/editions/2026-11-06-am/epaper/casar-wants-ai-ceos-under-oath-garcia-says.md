Rep. Greg Casar, chair of the Congressional Progressive Caucus, said after Democrats were projected to win the House that AI company CEOs "should be called to testify under oath" and that protecting jobs, freedom and lives from "AI billionaires" should be a top majority priority [1]. Rep. Robert Garcia, the presumptive next House Oversight chair, summarised the message to executives in two words: "Lawyer up" [1].

Hearings are not statutes. They are how a chamber sets the agenda for the statutes that follow. Casar called the data-centre revolt "the early tremor of a much bigger AI earthquake" [1]. Watch whether the first subpoenas match the first quotes.

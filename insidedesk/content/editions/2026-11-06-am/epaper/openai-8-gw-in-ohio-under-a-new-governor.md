OpenAI says it has secured 8 gigawatts of computing capacity at the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Ohio — described as the largest planned AI data-centre campus in the world — with first-phase construction due to start this year and capacity from 2028 [1]. SoftBank and SB Energy are developing it with the US Energy Department; Nvidia is backstopping a project that could cost more than $500 billion [1].

Governor-elect Amy Acton supports a "conditional moratorium" unless projects cover 100% of utility costs and use union labour [1]. Megawatts on a slide are not megawatts on a grid. The permit is the plot.

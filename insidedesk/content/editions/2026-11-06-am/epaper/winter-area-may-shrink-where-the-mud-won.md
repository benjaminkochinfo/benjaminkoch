Persistent rain has brought winter-crop sowing to a standstill in parts of the Baltic states, the Commission monitors say, and with the planting window about to close the winter area is expected to decrease, some fields shifting to spring crops [1]. Finland faces the same waterlogging drag on late harvest and sowing [1].

A hectare that misses winter wheat is not gone; it is re-timed. The balance sheet shifts towards spring barley and other substitutes, with yield and price effects that show up months later in feed and flour markets [1]. Watch the next MARS update for how large that shift is.

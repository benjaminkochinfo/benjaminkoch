Germany's DAX printed a fresh intraday record near 26,692 on Thursday morning on cheaper oil and a 4.4% rebound in September factory orders, then faded as the US chip sell-off landed [1][2]. It closed down about 0.43%, with defence and technology among the soft spots; Spain's IBEX led Europe higher even as Italy's FTSE MIB fell about 1% [1][2].

A record high and a down-close on the same day is not a paradox. It is a morning priced on European data and an afternoon priced on New York's AI panic. Watch which open Europe chooses on Friday.

Germany's temporary cut in fuel tax runs to the end of 2026 [1]. The ADAC's midweek note on falling petrol and diesel prices treated cheaper Brent and that tax relief as the twin reasons the national averages eased [1]. When the cut expires, the same wholesale price implies a higher sticker at the pump unless something else gives.

Mark the diary for January tariffs and January forecourts. Policy calendars move bills as surely as tankers do. A temporary cut that people get used to is still temporary when the statute says so.

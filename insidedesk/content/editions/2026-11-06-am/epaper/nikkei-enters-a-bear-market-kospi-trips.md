Japan's Nikkei 225 fell into a bear market on Friday, more than 20% below its June peak, as chip-related stocks tracked Wall Street's AI sell-off overnight [1]. Advantest dropped about 12% and SoftBank about 11–12% in early accounts; Tokyo Electron and Kioxia were also hit hard [1].

South Korea's Kospi plunged more than 8% at one point, enough to trigger circuit breakers and a 20-minute trading halt, and slipped below the 5,000 mark [1]. SK Hynix fell more than 8%, Samsung Electronics about 7%, and Taiwan's TSMC about 9.6% as the same argument moved from New York to Asia [1].

A bear market label is a distance from a peak, not a forecast. The common thread is the midterm read on data-centre rules and the chip chain that supplies them [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

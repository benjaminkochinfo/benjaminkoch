The European Commission's crop monitors, as reported by Agriland, say Irish tillage farmers have enjoyed an almost perfect autumn planting season, with winter oilseed rape and cereals now well established [1]. That sits beside patchy rapeseed in France, southern Belgium, Luxembourg and southern Germany, and waterlogged standstills in the Baltics and Finland [1].

Weather averages hide maps. One island's good autumn does not lift a continent's below-average yields, but it does show establishment is still possible when rain and trafficability arrive together [1].

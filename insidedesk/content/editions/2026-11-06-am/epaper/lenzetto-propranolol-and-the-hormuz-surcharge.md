Lenzetto, an HRT spray for hot flushes and night sweats, and Propranolol, used for blood pressure, angina and abnormal rhythms, are among medicines on the official UK shortage list pharmacists use to manage stock [1]. Britain imports about three-quarters of its drugs; Hormuz disruption has lengthened air-freight times and raised costs even for medicines finished in Europe from Asian ingredients [1][2].

The IPA wants ministers to review hoarding restrictions and give pharmacists more flexibility to adjust prescriptions when a pack vanishes [1][2]. The patient habit that helps rather than harms: order a little early, do not clear the shelf.

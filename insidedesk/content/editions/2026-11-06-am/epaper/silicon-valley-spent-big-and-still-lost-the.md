CNBC's FEC analysis found that 14 of the 20 largest midterm donor groups backed Republicans almost exclusively, with crypto, AI and online-gambling interests pouring in more than $377 million [1]. Elon Musk spent at least $474 million, nearly half in Texas, and lost both of that state's marquee races [1]. His post-election line on X: "Data centers in space looking pretty appealing right now" [1].

Money bought airtime. It did not buy the 40 local ballot outcomes that went against data centres [1]. That gap is the political fact under Thursday's chip rout.

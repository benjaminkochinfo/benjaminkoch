Gina Hinojosa, Texas's governor-elect, told CNN that Democrats could still have a "strong working relationship" with Elon Musk and his companies, but that deals would have to be done "on our terms" [1]. As a candidate she made AI data-centre regulation a focus and said the state should stop the current build-out "until we have measures to protect Texans" [1]. On Thursday she also asked Texas Parks and Wildlife to pause talks on a public-land sale that would help SpaceX expand Starbase [1].

Tesla and SpaceX are both headquartered in Texas; they are jointly developing a chips factory and data centre at Terafab in Grimes County [1]. A governor who can pause a land sale and set permit conditions is more consequential for the build-out than a single day's share price [2].

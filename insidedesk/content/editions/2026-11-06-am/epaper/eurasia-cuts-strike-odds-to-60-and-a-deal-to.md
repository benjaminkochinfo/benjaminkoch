Eurasia Group lowered its estimated chance of renewed US strikes on Iran to 60% from 65% after the midterms, cut the probability of a deal to 10% from 20%, and raised the chance of a continued standoff to 30% from 15% [1]. It still warns that escalation could push Brent back into a $110–$125 range [1].

Those are scenario weights from one shop, not odds you can buy. They matter because oil traders used a softer political read to dump crude midweek, then bought it back when tankers were attacked again [1]. Probability tables move faster than ceasefires.

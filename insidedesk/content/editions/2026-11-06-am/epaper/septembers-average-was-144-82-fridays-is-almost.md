September's average day-ahead price for DE-LU was about €144.82 per megawatt hour [1]. Friday 6 November clears at €275.64 — roughly 1.9 times that level [1]. Every delivery day since 29 October has sat near or above €250 except Wednesday's €248.99 [1].

Households on fixed tariffs feel this through their supplier's hedging, not overnight. Dynamic tariffs feel it by the hour. Either way, the autumn ledger is open: nights are no longer cheap by September's standard [1].

London Heathrow said a Thursday threat was "deemed not credible" and posed no risk to passengers, staff or aircraft [1][2]. Birmingham Airport said it received a threat and "immediately" started processes with emergency services and partners; a Jet2 departure returned to stand and passengers moved to a replacement aircraft after nothing was found [1].

Different words, same duty: assess, act in proportion, say what you did. The Met's aviation command monitored four Heathrow-related aircraft threats and identified "no security issues" after assessment [2]. Transparency after a hoax is part of the deterrent.

Electricity for delivery in Germany and Luxembourg on Friday 6 November averages €275.64 per megawatt hour on the day-ahead market, according to SMARD [1]. That is €18.26 above Thursday's €257.38 and the highest daily average since the €314.85 of Thursday 29 October [1].

[[chart:de-power-day-ahead-06nov]]

**The shape.** Prices climb from €229.65 at 05:00 to €323.06 at 07:00, ease to €226.97 at noon, then rise into the evening and peak at €357.12 from 18:00 [1]. The spread between the dearest and cheapest hour is €134.74 [1].

**The floor.** Friday's cheapest hour, €222.38 at 03:00, would have been a dear whole day in September: only two September delivery days averaged more [1]. Desk arithmetic: Friday's average is about 1.9 times September's €144.82 [1]. A high peak says a few hours are tight. A high floor says the system is expensive even when demand is lowest.

Most households do not pay the day-ahead price directly. If you have a dynamic tariff, the least-bad windows remain the small hours and the midday trough; the 17:00–20:00 block is where a dishwasher or an electric car costs the most [1]. The auction publishes around noon every day. It is one of the freest useful documents in the country. Nothing here is investment advice.

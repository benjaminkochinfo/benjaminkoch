On preliminary data, the S&P 500 fell 143.26 points, or 1.89%, to 7,418.41; the Nasdaq Composite lost 2.47% to 24,516.11 and the Dow 721.40 points, or 1.38%, to 51,626.15 [1]. The Nasdaq now stands more than 10% below its 6 October record close, which is the textbook line for a correction [1]. Chip makers, cloud renters and server builders led the fall, a day before Nvidia reports and days before Anthropic's listing [1]. Some days the AI names "absolutely rip", said Ross Mayfield of Baird, and the next "they're in a freefall" [1]. CME FedWatch put the chance of a quarter-point Fed rate rise in December at 96.0% [1].

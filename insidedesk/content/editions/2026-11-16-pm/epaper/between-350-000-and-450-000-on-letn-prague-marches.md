Hundreds of thousands gathered on Prague's Letná Plain on Sunday, in what police confirmed was the largest protest since the independent Czech Republic was established in 1993 [1]. Organisers, Million Moments for Democracy, estimated between 350,000 and 450,000 people [1].

The core grievance is a funding line. The government plans to replace licence fees for public service media with money from the state budget, which protesters fear would cost those broadcasters their independence [1]. Speakers included students and signatories of Charter 77; the demonstration ended peacefully at around 16:45, with no incidents reported by police [1].

The US Food and Drug Administration has declined to approve ivonescimab with chemotherapy for patients with EGFR-mutated lung cancer whose disease progressed after targeted therapy, Summit Therapeutics said on Sunday [1]. The HARMONi trial met its progression-free survival endpoint, but overall survival showed only "a positive trend" without statistical significance, and the FDA said that without it, effectiveness was not established [1]. Summit points to a post-hoc hazard ratio of 0.76 and says no approved regimen in this setting has shown a significant survival benefit [1]. The drug has been approved in China since May 2024 [1].

A regulator that asks "did they live longer?" is not being cruel. It is doing the one job nobody else can. Nothing here is investment advice.

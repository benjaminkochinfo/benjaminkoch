The front-month TTF contract, Europe's gas benchmark, fell 0.5% to €90.50 per megawatt hour in early European trading on Monday, Investing.com reports [1]. Britain's NBP moved in step, down 0.5% to 225.90 pence a therm [1].

It did so on a bad weekend for the Gulf: another merchant tanker caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz, and Tehran reportedly attacked a US base in Bahrain [1]. Investing.com's reading is that traders are weighing the physical volume of supply at sea more heavily than the risk of outages [1]. It also cites the 90-day extension of a Jones Act waiver by President Trump, which lets foreign-flagged ships keep carrying oil between US ports [1].

For you: a calm price on a violent weekend is not complacency; it is inventory speaking. Nothing here is investment advice.

In a six-week randomised trial of 126 unemployed US adults with elevated depressive symptoms, 68.3% of those given the DRIVEN app found work, against 50.8% of a control group sent weekly emails with job-search links, Northwestern University reported last week [1]. Desk arithmetic: 17.5 percentage points. The app paired self-guided exercises based on behavioural activation with job tips and three video sessions with a coach; participants also reported better mood and fewer depressive symptoms [1]. The study appeared in the Journal of Medical Internet Research [1].

The method is deliberately small: find "the corner pieces", tasks you can finish in 15 minutes, says senior author Stewart Shankman [1]. The sample is small too, and the follow-up short.

For you: motivation often follows action, not the other way round.

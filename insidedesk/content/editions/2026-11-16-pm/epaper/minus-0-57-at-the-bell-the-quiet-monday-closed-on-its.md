The DAX closed down 0.57% at 26,598.64 points, on its low of the day; the MDax lost 0.56% to 30,528.26 [1]. At 15:42 CET, when our lead was written, it had been down just 0.05% [1]. The Stoxx 600 fell 1.83 points, or 0.28%, to 662.31, snapping a five-day winning run that had ended on Friday's record [2]. The CAC 40 closed 0.2% lower and the Stoxx basic resources index fell 2.3% as copper slipped [3]. Traders pointed to inflation and rate worries and to Nvidia's results, due after Tuesday's US close [1]. The chip maker's numbers are "a crucial test of whether the AI boom can maintain its momentum", said Axel Rudolph of IG [3]. A flat afternoon is a draft, not a verdict. The closing auction signs it.

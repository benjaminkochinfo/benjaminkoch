Scott Bessent called on Sunday for a "dramatic shakeup" at Nvidia and said its board should look outside the company for its next chief executive [1]. "If this were a bank and they hadn't complied like this, the OCC would be in there right now, and a lot of the executives would be removed," the Treasury Secretary told Fox News [1].

The sharper point concerned a line in the accounts. Bessent pointed to a surge in reported Singapore revenue as China sales fell; Nvidia stopped reporting Singapore revenue separately in November 2025, saying a method based on customers' headquarters rather than billing addresses better reflected where its sales went [1]. Nvidia, now led by interim chief executive Colette Kress, reports quarterly results on Tuesday [1].

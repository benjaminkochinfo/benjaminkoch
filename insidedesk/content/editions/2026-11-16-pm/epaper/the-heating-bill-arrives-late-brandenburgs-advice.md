"The currently higher prices for fossil fuels will make this problem worse again next year," says Joshua Jahn, energy expert at the Verbraucherzentrale Brandenburg [1]. For tenants, his centre notes, the effect often arrives with a delay: when the service-charge statement comes and a back payment is due, which can hit low-income households hard [1].

Its advice is unglamorous and right. Use the current Heizspiegel, the heating-cost benchmark, to see what heating typically costs for your flat size and system, and compare the statement with last year's [1]. The centre runs free online talks on reading the statement, on 16 November and 2 December, and offers energy advice by appointment [1]. For legal advice, it points tenants to a local tenants' association or a specialist lawyer [1].

For you: the statement is a document, not a verdict. Check it.

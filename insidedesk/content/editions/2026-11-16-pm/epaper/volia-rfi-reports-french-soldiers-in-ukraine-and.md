French soldiers have been operating in Ukraine for several months, training Ukrainian troops and helping maintain equipment, Radio France Internationale reported on Monday, citing sources [1]. The confidential mission, led by a French general, is codenamed "Volia", Ukrainian for both "freedom" and "will", and has been present since at least the summer of 2026 [1].

RFI described a "light and flexible" structure of small teams on short assignments, and said French lawmakers had not been informed [1]. According to its sources, the official "Gerfaut" training mission in Poland serves as cover and gateway [1]. The report gave no troop numbers [1].

For you: a secret mission can be prudent. A secret kept from the parliament that pays for it is a different matter, and that is the question Paris will now have to answer.

Typhoon Cempaka entered the Philippine Area of Responsibility at 08:40 local time on Monday and was named Samuel, the country's 19th tropical cyclone of 2026 and the first of November [1]. At 10:00 it was 1,370 km east-northeast of extreme northern Luzon, with sustained winds of 120 km/h and gusts to 150 km/h [1]. PAGASA, the state weather service, says it may peak at 150 km/h sustained by Tuesday morning before weakening, and will leave the area by Monday evening or early Tuesday without affecting land [1].

The rougher news is closer in: the northeast monsoon is bringing heavy rain to parts of northern Luzon and waves up to 5.5 metres off Batanes [1]. A storm that misses is still a forecast that worked. Good bulletins are how you know which one to worry about.

Xetra closed the DAX 1.73% higher at 26,267.19, above the midday 26,102 in the evening note; the Euro Stoxx 50 gained 1.9% [1]. The Stoxx 600 settled 1.26% higher at 656.52, with defence up 4.2% and euro-area banks up 3.1% [2]. Hochtief closed only 1.3% lower, Hannover Re 1.4% lower [1]. Brent was down 4.6% at $93.77 by early evening, below the $95.08 quoted at 17:30 [3].

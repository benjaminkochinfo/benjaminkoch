The Russian-occupied Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant lost all off-site power on Sunday when its sole 330 kV line disconnected, and ran its cooling and safety systems on emergency diesel generators, the IAEA said [1]. About ten hours later the agency confirmed power was restored [1]. The Guardian named the line as Ferosplavna; the cause and location of the fault remain unknown [2][1].

"It is the 27th such event during the conflict," said IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi [1]. In June a similar outage lasted 15 hours [1]. The plant produces no electricity but needs outside power to keep its fuel cool [2]. The quiet work belongs to the IAEA team that has stayed on site since September 2022 [1]. Count that presence, not only the outages.

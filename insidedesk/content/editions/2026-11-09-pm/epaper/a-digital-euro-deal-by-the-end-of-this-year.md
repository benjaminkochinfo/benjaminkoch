Valdis Dombrovskis told Monday's Eurogroup press conference that the co-legislators negotiating the digital euro "are moving at a steady pace", with the aim of an agreement by the end of this year [1]. He called the goal "within reach" but said it needs "constructive engagement from all sides" [1]. Ministers also heard from the chairs of the ECB's Supervisory Board and the Single Resolution Board; their assessment, he said, "confirms that the European banking sector remains financially sound" [1]. The money in your current account rests on exactly this kind of unexciting assurance.

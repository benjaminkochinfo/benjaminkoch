Heating oil eased by about 1.5 cents a litre from Friday at midday Monday, HeizOel24 reports, and mid-October's bills were higher still [1]. The more practical numbers are two others. In Hamburg a litre cost €1.57, in Frankfurt €1.73, a gap of 16 cents [1]. Desk arithmetic: on a 3,000-litre order that is €480. And average delivery times are four to five weeks, so anyone who wants oil delivered this year should keep the calendar in view, the portal notes [1]. Compare by postcode, then count the weeks.

Reuters put the S&P 500 up 2.41% at 7,554.67, the Dow up 2.21% at 52,417.54 and the Nasdaq up 2.67% at 25,235.49; all 11 S&P sectors rose, while Nvidia fell about 6% [1]. Front-month Brent settled 5.85% lower at $92.57, its lowest settlement since 31 August and 21.31% down for the month so far [2]. It kept falling after settlement, more than $7 down on the day by Reuters' last reading [3]. October US consumer prices are due on Tuesday [1].

This is a budget week in Berlin. On Wednesday the Bundestag's budget committee takes the parliament's own budget and then the finance ministry's, and on Thursday it begins the Bereinigungssitzung, the final session in which every departmental plan is called up again and debated with ministers [1]. The Bundestag's own information service expects it to run "probably into the early hours of Friday" [1]. The 2027 budget accompanying act is also due for final committee discussion on Thursday [1].

The coalition carries two quarrels into the room: the sugar tax and the plastics tax, where Union MPs are breaking ranks although the revenue was already pencilled in [1]. The final plenary vote is planned for the week of 23–27 November [1]. It is the least glamorous meeting of the year, and it decides more than most speeches do.

Not everything rose on Monday. Hochtief shares fell as much as 7.1% after the Spanish infrastructure group ACS denied considering a full takeover of the German builder [1]. A bid premium that was never offered is easy to lose. Hannover Re also slipped even though it reaffirmed its 2026 profit target; a trader told Reuters investors disliked the lower solvency ratio [1]. For an insurer, that ratio is the cushion between a bad year and a bad decade, and the market read it before the headline. Nothing here is investment advice.

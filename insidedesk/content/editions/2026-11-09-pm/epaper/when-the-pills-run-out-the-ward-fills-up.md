The South African Society of Psychiatrists (SASOP) has asked Health Minister Aaron Motsoaledi to investigate reports of nationwide shortages of medicines for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder and major depression in the public sector [1]. Most reports come from Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, the Western Cape and the Eastern Cape [1]. SASOP's Dr Mvuyiso Talatala said psychiatrists have used the costlier amisulpride when olanzapine runs short, but it too is in limited supply [1]. Relapses fill scarce beds, leaving patients waiting hours to days in emergency departments not designed as wards [1]. The society says no explanation offered so far accounts for so many drugs from different makers running out at once, and offers to help the minister [1]. For you: a stockout is a pharmacy problem for a week and a hospital problem after that.

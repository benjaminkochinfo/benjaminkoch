If your electricity price follows the exchange, Tuesday's day-ahead auction has already written your timetable. The cheapest full hour is 23:00, at €219.66 per megawatt hour; the dearest is 08:00, at €494.05 [1]. The night before is not much help: the hours from midnight to 05:00 all clear between €256.44 and €268.02 [1]. Desk arithmetic: shifting a load from 08:00 to 23:00 cuts its wholesale cost by more than half, about 27 cents a kilowatt hour (€274.39 per megawatt hour) before taxes, levies and network fees. The washing machine is the most reliable grid tool you own.

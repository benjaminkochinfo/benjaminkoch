Today's day-ahead auction set Tuesday's German-Luxembourg power price at a daily average of €351.48 per megawatt hour, SMARD's data show [1]. That is €5.84 above Monday's €345.64, which this desk called the dearest delivery day of the year this morning. Tuesday now takes that title on the same series [1].

[[chart:de-power-day-ahead-09pm]]

The average hides a change of shape. Monday's dearest hour was 17:00, at €644.18 [1]. Tuesday's is 08:00, at €494.05 — about €150 lower [1]. The evening block that scared everyone on Monday has flattened. What has risen is everything else. Between midnight and 05:00 on Monday, hourly prices sat between €223.27 and €231.49; on Tuesday the same hours clear between €256.44 and €268.02 [1]. The noon trough is higher too: €315.09 at 12:00 on Monday, €354.09 on Tuesday [1].

Desk arithmetic: Tuesday has eight hours at or above €400 (07:00–10:00 and 15:00–18:00), against five on Monday [1]. In the finer quarter-hour products, the dearest slot is 16:45 at €534.66 and the cheapest 23:45 at €207.71 [1].

A year ago the comparison was gentler. On Monday 10 November 2025 the daily average was €109.89 [2]. Desk arithmetic: Tuesday is about 3.2 times that.

Why does the shape matter to you? Few households buy at the spot price. But suppliers who do buy there pay for the whole curve, and a higher floor is harder to dodge than a higher peak. You can move a dishwasher out of 17:00. You cannot move the night. If you are on a dynamic tariff, Tuesday's cheapest full hour is the last one, 23:00, at €219.66 [1].

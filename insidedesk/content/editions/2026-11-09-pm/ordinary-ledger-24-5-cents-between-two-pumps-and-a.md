**The ledger.**
- Super E10, ADAC national daily average for Sunday: €1.996 a litre, 7.6 cents below a week earlier [1].
- Diesel, same day: €2.241 a litre, down only 4.1 cents [1].
- E10 on 8 October: €2.140. Diesel on 8 October: €2.242 [1].
- Desk arithmetic: in a month E10 fell 14.4 cents; diesel fell 0.1 cents.

[[chart:de-fuel-e10-diesel-09pm]]

The headline is that petrol is below €2 again, the cheapest daily average in more than four months, according to dpa [1]. The more useful line is further down. Diesel is now 24.5 cents a litre dearer than E10, which dpa calls one of the largest gaps ever recorded; only in April and during the first fuel-tax rebate of 2022 was it temporarily wider [1].

That is upside down. Diesel is taxed less than petrol, so in normal times it is the cheaper fuel, often by around 15 cents, dpa notes [1]. The reason it is not now is plain supply: diesel is more vulnerable in crises, partly because Germany imports more finished diesel than petrol [1]. The current tax rebate cuts both fuels by roughly 17 cents a litre, so it does not explain the gap [1].

Desk arithmetic: on a 50-litre tank, Sunday's averages mean about €99.80 for E10 and €112.05 for diesel, a difference of about €12.25 per fill.

What you can do is unglamorous. Prices vary widely by region and time of day, and since the noon rule was introduced the cheapest window is the later morning [1]. On Monday morning many regions had offers just above €1.80 a litre, some even below, dpa reported [1]. That is nearly 20 cents under the national average for E10. You will not change the diesel market. You can change the hour you visit it.

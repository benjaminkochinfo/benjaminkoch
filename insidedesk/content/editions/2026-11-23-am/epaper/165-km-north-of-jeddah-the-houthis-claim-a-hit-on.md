Yemen's Houthis said on Sunday they had carried out an operation against Saudi Arabia's Rabigh oil refinery on the Red Sea coast and claimed a direct hit, Reuters reported [1]. There was no indication of damage, and the Saudi authorities did not immediately comment [1].

Rabigh is home to Petro Rabigh, an integrated refining and petrochemical complex developed by Saudi Aramco and Japan's Sumitomo Chemical, about 165 km north of Jeddah with deep-water export facilities [1]. The Houthis have repeatedly claimed attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure in recent months, including the East-West pipeline and refineries at Jazan and Ras Tanura; Saudi authorities have not confirmed any attacks on refineries [1].

For an energy reader, the discipline is to separate the claim from the cargo. A claimed hit moves headlines; a confirmed outage moves export schedules. Until a loading programme changes, the second has not happened.

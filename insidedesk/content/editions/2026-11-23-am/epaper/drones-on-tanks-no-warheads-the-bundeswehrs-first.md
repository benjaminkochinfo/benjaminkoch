The Bundeswehr has for the first time practised the tactical use of so-called kamikaze drones in Lithuania, BR reported: soldiers flew the drones into battle tanks on a training area, without explosive charges because it was a test [1]. The test is part of the Bundeswehr's largest exercise in Lithuania so far, in which it also tried AI-based target-recognition software days earlier [1].

Equipment that was exotic three years ago is now in the training plan. That is how armies change: not in speeches, but in exercise schedules.

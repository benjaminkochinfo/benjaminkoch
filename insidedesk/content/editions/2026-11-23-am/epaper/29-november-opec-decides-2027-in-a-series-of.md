OPEC+ ministers will hold a series of meetings on 29 November to decide the group's output policy for 2027, Reuters reported this morning [1]. The group, which includes Russia, meets after a week in which Brent fell 4% and COP31's talks collapsed over whether to mention a phase-out of fossil fuels [1].

Reuters notes that Saudi Arabia and other large producers lobbied to keep fossil fuels out of the climate text, according to observers [1]. One week, two rooms, the same subject: in one they argued about words; in the other they will set barrels.

Markus Söder wants the coalition's open pension questions decided quickly, ideally before the end of the year, BR reported from his interview on ARD's Sunday programme [1]. On retirement without deductions after 45 contribution years, he suggested a compromise, for example for people in physically demanding jobs [1]. He also repeated his call for a cold-progression adjustment, which Bavaria says it would help pay for [1].

A pension rule for "hard jobs" sounds simple. In practice someone has to define the job list, and that list is where the reform will be won or lost.

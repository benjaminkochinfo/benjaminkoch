More than 50 people at a Sunday service in the castle chapel of Bad Grönenbach in the Allgäu showed signs of carbon monoxide poisoning; 16 were treated in hospital and most have since left, BR reported, citing police and the Red Cross [1]. Police found first indications that the automatic carbon monoxide shut-off of a heater had failed [1].

Carbon monoxide is odourless; the first signs are headache, dizziness, nausea and tiredness [1]. The cause here was a safety device that did not switch off. Devices are maintained by people with a service diary; ask to see yours.

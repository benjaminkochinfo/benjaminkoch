Tatyana Kim has stepped down as head of Wildberries, Russia's largest online retailer, Russian media reported on Saturday, days after the European Union added her to its Russia sanctions list [1]. TASS and RIA, citing a corporate register, said Alexei Kulikov had been appointed chief executive [1].

The EU listed Kim on 16 November, which means an asset freeze in the EU plus travel and transaction bans [1]. Brussels said Wildberries is "one of the largest taxpayers" in Russia and facilitates the sale of military and dual-use goods; the company and the Kremlin say it does not supply the army [1]. Wildberries said the sanctions would not affect its operations because it has no assets or business in the EU [1].

Sanctions are often judged by whether they stop a war. They are better judged by smaller things: who resigns, who is replaced, which register records it. Those are the parts you can check.

Justice Minister Stefanie Hubig wants to tighten the law on killings with "gender-specific" motives, DW reported, citing her interview with Bild am Sonntag [1]. "A man kills, or attempts to kill, his partner or ex-partner almost every day in Germany," she said [1].

Her point is about how courts weigh motives. In some cases, she said, a man's deep sadness or fear of losing a partner can count as an extenuating circumstance and lead to a manslaughter conviction instead of murder [1]. She opposes a separate crime of femicide, which some women's rights groups demand, arguing it would bring "much bigger disadvantages" [1]. The proposal is still being debated within the coalition; it is to go to the Länder and associations before the end of the year, then to cabinet and parliament [1].

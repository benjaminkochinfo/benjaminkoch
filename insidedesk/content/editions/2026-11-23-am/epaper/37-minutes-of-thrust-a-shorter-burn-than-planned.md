The European-Japanese BepiColombo mission entered orbit around Mercury on Saturday after a 37-minute engine burn, the second spacecraft ever to do so, Space.com reported [1]. On Sunday the team said the burn had been shorter than planned "due to a software issue"; the spacecraft is safe and engineers at ESOC are investigating, with updates promised for Monday [1]. ESA's MPO and JAXA's Mio are due to separate on 9–10 December; science begins in April 2027 [2].

Eight years of travel, and the headline is a careful sentence about a software issue. That is what competent operations sound like.

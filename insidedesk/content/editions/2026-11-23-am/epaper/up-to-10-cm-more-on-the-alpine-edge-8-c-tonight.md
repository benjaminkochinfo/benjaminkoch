Polar sea air reaches Germany on a northerly flow, the German Weather Service said at 05:00 CET: from the southern Black Forest to the Alpine edge, above 500 to 800 metres, 1 to 5 cm more snow by early afternoon, up to 10 cm higher up; light to moderate frost tonight between 0 and −8°C, locally lower, with dense fog in places [1].

In Malaysia the number of flood victims in relief centres in Selangor rose to 1,884 from 532 families on Sunday morning, while Perak's fell to 467, Bernama reported via Malay Mail [2]. Two weathers, one morning: frost here, water there.

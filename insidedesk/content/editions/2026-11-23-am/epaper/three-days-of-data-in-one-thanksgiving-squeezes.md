US markets close on Thursday for Thanksgiving and shut early on Friday, so the week's data are pulled forward, Newsquawk's calendar shows: on Wednesday come jobless claims, the October PCE price index, third-quarter GDP, personal spending and durable goods, followed by the Fed's Beige Book [1]. Danske Bank lists the PCE and GDP releases at 14:30 CET, its table shows 3.8% for headline PCE inflation in October, after 3.6% [2].

Before that: flash PMIs today, Germany's Ifo on Tuesday at 10:00 CET, and the Bank of Korea's decision and the ECB's minutes on Thursday [1][2]. A short week is not a quiet one. It is the same paperwork, delivered faster. Nothing here is investment advice.

Researchers at McMaster University report in the Journal of Infectious Diseases that male mice infected with Zika were markedly more prone to neurological reactions such as weakness and paralysis than females, Deutschlandfunk reported [1]. The team argues that biological sex should always be considered when treating infectious diseases, and that men may need medication sooner [1].

The finding is about mice and Zika. It is a reason for doctors to record sex in trials, not a reason for anyone to stay in bed longer.

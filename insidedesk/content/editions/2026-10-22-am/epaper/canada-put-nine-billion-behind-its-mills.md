Canada and Ontario unveiled a C$9 billion ($6.4 billion) package on Wednesday for steel and car makers hit by US tariffs [1]. More than C$5 billion goes into a steel action fund, so mills can upgrade machinery, make more of what Canada needs and sell to non-US markets [1]. C$2.5 billion supports electric-vehicle production, and Ottawa will spend C$1 billion on roads to the remote, mineral-rich Ring of Fire [1]. A tariff is answered fastest by a customs officer; it is answered best by a new customer.

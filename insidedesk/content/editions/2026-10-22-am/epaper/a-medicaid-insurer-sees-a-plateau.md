Molina Healthcare, which runs Medicaid health plans in the United States, beat third-quarter expectations on Wednesday but left its full-year premium revenue guidance at roughly $42 billion; its shares dipped 4% after hours, CNBC reports [1]. Chief executive Joseph Zubretsky pointed to "the apparent plateauing of medical cost trend, which remains at elevated levels", and to rate updates for 2027 [1]. A plateau at a high level is still high. But for insurers, and the states that pay them, the slope matters as much as the level.

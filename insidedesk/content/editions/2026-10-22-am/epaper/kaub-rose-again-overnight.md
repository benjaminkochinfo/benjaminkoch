The Rhine gauge at Kaub read 19 cm at 05:00 on Thursday, up from 11 cm on Wednesday afternoon, according to the federal waterways administration [1]. It had touched 21 cm early on Tuesday [1]. The station's lowest daily reading in its 2010–2020 reference period was 25 cm, on 22 October 2018; its "equivalent water level", the low-water reference for shipping, is 77 cm [1]. Desk arithmetic: Thursday's reading is still 6 cm below that 2018 low. A gauge reading is measured from a fixed zero, not from the riverbed, so it is not the depth of the channel.

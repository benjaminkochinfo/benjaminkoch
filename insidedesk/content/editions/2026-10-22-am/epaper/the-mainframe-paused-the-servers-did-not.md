IBM's third-quarter revenue rose 5% to $17.1 billion, with software up 8% to $7.8 billion [1]. Inside infrastructure, the two halves went opposite ways: IBM Z mainframes fell 20%, while distributed infrastructure rose 31% [1]. Mainframe sales tend to follow product cycles, so one quarter is not a verdict. Free cash flow was $3.0 billion, up $0.7 billion, and IBM now expects full-year free cash flow to rise by more than $1 billion [1]. It also noted that it has paid a dividend every quarter since 1916 [1]. Nothing here is advice.

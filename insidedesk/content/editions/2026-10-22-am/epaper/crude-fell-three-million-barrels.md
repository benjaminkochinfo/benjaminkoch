US commercial crude stocks fell 3.1 million barrels to 412.5 million in the week to 16 October, 3% below the five-year average, the Energy Information Administration reported; refineries ran at 93.4% of capacity [1]. Analysts polled by Reuters had expected a draw of 820,000 barrels [2]. Brent futures still settled just 3 cents higher on Wednesday, at $108.82, and WTI fell 67 cents to $97.15 [2]. Reuters cites the Kremlin's word that Putin agreed to resume US-mediated talks with Ukraine as the weight on the other side [2]. An inventory is a fact; a talk is a possibility. For one session, they cancelled out.

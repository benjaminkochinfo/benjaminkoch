France's Défenseur des droits, the independent rights ombudsman, said on Tuesday that it had received 46 complaints since the school pupils' movement began, "more than ten" of them about events last weekend [1]. AFP notes that the policing strategy is still being sharply questioned [1]. On the same day President Macron said from Qatar that the "hand must not tremble at the top of the state" [1]. Both can be true. A state that is firm in the street and answers to its ombudsman afterwards is the kind worth trusting.

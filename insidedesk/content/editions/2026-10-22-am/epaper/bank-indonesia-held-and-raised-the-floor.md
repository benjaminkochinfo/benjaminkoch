Bank Indonesia kept its benchmark rate at 5.75% on Wednesday but raised its deposit facility rate by 25 basis points to 5%, Governor Destry Damayanti said; the lending facility stayed at 6.5% [1]. Banks parking money at the central bank now earn more, which narrows the corridor from below. It also sweetened incentives for hedging through its domestic non-deliverable forwards, to attract foreign capital and keep the rupiah stable [1]. The inflation target stays at 2.5%, plus or minus one point, for 2026 and 2027 [1]. The headline rate held; the plumbing moved.

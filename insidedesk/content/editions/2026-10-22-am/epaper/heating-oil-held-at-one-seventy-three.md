A hundred litres of heating oil cost €173.38 on the German average on Thursday morning, for a 3,000-litre order, unchanged on Wednesday, according to HeizOel24 [1]. That is €3.00 more than a week ago and €40.16 more than three months ago; the high of the last three years was €179.02 on 16 September 2026 [1]. Desk arithmetic: a 3,000-litre delivery costs about €5,201, some €1,205 more than in July. Orders are binding once placed, and delivery usually takes two to four weeks [1]. Nothing here is advice.

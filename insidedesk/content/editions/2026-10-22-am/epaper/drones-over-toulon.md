Between 5 and 16 October, unauthorised drones appeared near Toulon, home port of the aircraft carrier Charles de Gaulle, and near the Hyères base, during an exercise to protect them, France 2 reported on Wednesday [1]. The maritime prefecture says its sensors quickly told them apart from the military's own drones, and that no sensitive infrastructure was threatened [1]. None was recovered [1]. The military prosecutor's office in Marseille is investigating [1]. "We need to put the means in place to detect these drones properly and, if possible, intercept them," said Patrick Dutartre, a retired air force general [1]. A detected intruder you cannot catch is still a finding; it is also a budget line.

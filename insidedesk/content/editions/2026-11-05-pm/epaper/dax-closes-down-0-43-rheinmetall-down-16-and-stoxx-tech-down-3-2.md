The evening's Markets note left the DAX up 0.67% at 26,615 in the afternoon, after a morning high near 26,693. Xetra has since closed it 0.43% lower at 26,325.01 points [1]. At its best the index stood just under 26,693, a new record above the August high, then gave the gain back as Wall Street's AI and chip sell-off gathered pace [1]. The MDax closed 0.92% lower at 30,891.56 [1]. The Stoxx Europe 600 ended 0.38% lower at 654.87, having pulled back from near an all-time high earlier in the session [2].

The sorting the afternoon already showed held to the bell. Rheinmetall closed 16.14% lower after cutting its 2026 sales outlook for the second time in three months [1]; Reuters put the defence sector down 2.6% on that drop [2]. Infineon fell 8.54%, Siemens Energy about 4.7% to 5%, and Hochtief nearly 5% [1][3]. On the Stoxx 600, technology shares fell 3.2%: Soitec 16.7%, Siltronic 12.3%, Aixtron 9.8%, ASML 6.6% [2]. The CAC 40 finished 0.34% higher; the FTSE 100 closed 0.43% lower on the same day the Bank of England raised Bank Rate to 4% [2][3]. Air France-KLM rose about 12% to 13% on its quarter [2][3].

IG's Salah-Eddine Bouhmidi called it "Auf die Wahleuphorie folgt die Ernüchterung" — after the election euphoria, sobering up — with worries that stricter AI rules could slow data-centre build-out [1]. A record at lunch and a red close by the bell is a mood swing, not a ledger. Nothing here is investment advice.

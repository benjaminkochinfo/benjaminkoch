Meta has bought Conductor, a two-year-old start-up whose software lets programmers run several AI coding agents side by side in separate workspaces; Meta confirmed the deal but gave no terms [1]. The agents themselves come from labs such as Anthropic and OpenAI, not from Conductor [1].

The founder, Charlie Holtz, promised "something new" early next year [1]. The more useful line is the dull one: the core features keep working until then, but Conductor's cloud service shuts down on 20 November, after which the company will begin deleting user cloud data [1].

If your team uses it, the job this week is not to admire the deal. It is to export what lives in that cloud, write down which tools depend on it, and decide on a replacement before the date arrives. Small vendors get bought; good inventories survive the purchase.

Veronica Gundu, lead negotiator for mitigation at the Africa Group of Negotiators, says 51% of climate financing is beginning to flow in debt form [1]. Her question for Antalya is about quality rather than headline size: of the new goal of $300 billion a year, and the $1.3 trillion it seeks to mobilise, "how much is going to flow in grant form?" [1]. She points to Article 9.1 of the Paris Agreement, which calls on developed countries to provide public finance, and says many African countries are already too indebted to borrow for adaptation [1]. She also wants adaptation finance tripled and notes that the Adaptation Fund missed its $300 million mobilisation target last year [1].

It is a reasonable accounting point. A loan and a grant of the same size appear as the same figure in a pledge table. They look very different on a finance ministry's balance sheet ten years later.

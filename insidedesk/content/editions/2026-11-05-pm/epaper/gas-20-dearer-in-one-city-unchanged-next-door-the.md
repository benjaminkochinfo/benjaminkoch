Energieversorgung Halle said on Tuesday that natural gas for private customers in basic supply will cost 20% more from the turn of the year [1]. Its chief, Olaf Schneider, points to wholesale gas prices that have more than tripled since the Iran war began [1]. "We are back in full crisis mode," he said [1].

In the same region, enviaM says it will keep basic-supply gas and electricity prices stable at the turn of the year, because it buys very far ahead [1]. The newsletter's author adds a fair caution: a stable tariff is not automatically a cheap one [1].

The lesson for you is local and practical. Wait for your own supplier's letter, compare the price per kilowatt-hour rather than the percentage, and remember that a price rise gives you a special right to cancel. Nothing here is investment advice.

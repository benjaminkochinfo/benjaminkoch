Initial claims for US unemployment benefits fell by 6,000 to 189,000 in the week to 31 October, the Labor Department said on Thursday at 14:30 CET [1]. That matches the 189,000 of mid-July as the lowest weekly figure in the department's table for 2026 [1]. A year earlier the count was 228,000 [1].

Unadjusted, claims fell 1.6%, while the seasonal factors had expected a rise of 1.4% [1]. The people already drawing benefits rose by 23,000 to 1,718,000 in the week to 24 October [1].

The reading is plain: few people are being let go, while the number already on benefits edged up rather than down. A payroll office counts both. Nothing here is investment advice.

By Thursday afternoon seven of the CDU's 17 regional chairs had backed Ilse Aigner, the 61-year-old CSU president of the Bavarian Landtag, to succeed Frank-Walter Steinmeier, Handelsblatt counts, among them Baden-Württemberg's Manuel Hagel and the three eastern CDU premiers [1]. The CSU has backed her and the SPD has signalled approval [1]. Baden-Württemberg's Green premier, Cem Özdemir, called her "down to earth", and asked for "a little more restraint" so that speculation does not come at the cost of the candidates and "the dignity of the office" [2]. Chancellor Friedrich Merz has so far committed only to a woman, and insists the right to propose lies with the CDU as the strongest force [2]. Other names mentioned from the CDU include Julia Klöckner, Karin Prien, Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer and Ines Claus [1][2]. The office has no army and no budget. Its power is that most people trust it. Choosing quietly is part of keeping it so.

ASML, ASM International, BE Semiconductor and STMicroelectronics each rose more than 10% on Monday, and Arm Holdings 18%, ADVFN reported [1]. In Frankfurt, Infineon gained almost 4% by midday; its shares had lost about half their value since a record in June [2]. Europe's semiconductor champions mostly sell machines, designs and power chips, ordered months or years ahead. A chief executive's return moves their share prices in an hour; their order books will tell you next quarter whether anything else moved.

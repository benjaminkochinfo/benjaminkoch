If you care for a relative at home, you may know the monthly allowance of up to €42 for consumable care supplies such as hand disinfectant and disposable gloves. The government's care reform would end it as a dedicated benefit and move the items into a new relief budget [1]. The Bundesrat on Friday asked for the change to be dropped, calling the move "alien to the system"; about 3.1 million people needing care are looked after by relatives alone, and for them, the Länder say, the dedicated benefit is the only tool that reliably secures infection control [1]. The draft foresees savings of €300 million in 2029 and €800 million in 2030 [1]. Small boxes, delivered monthly, keep people out of hospital.

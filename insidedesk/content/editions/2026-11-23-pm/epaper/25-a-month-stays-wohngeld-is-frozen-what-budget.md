Two quieter lines sit inside the budget the Bundestag votes on this Friday. The child supplement (Kindersofortzuschlag) of €25 a month will continue to be paid, except to families receiving asylum-seeker benefits [1]; ZDF noted on Monday that the supplement, once due to be cut, is being kept [2]. Housing benefit (Wohngeld), drawn by 1.3 million households, keeps its current level but will not be raised for at least a year, after the government dropped planned cuts of more than €1 billion [1]. Desk arithmetic: €25 a month is €300 a year per child. Small numbers, paid on time, are what most people mean by "the state".

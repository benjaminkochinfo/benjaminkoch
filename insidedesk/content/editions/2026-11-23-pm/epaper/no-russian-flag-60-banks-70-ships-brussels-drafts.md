The European Commission has proposed its next sanctions package against Russia, Ursula von der Leyen announced on Monday [1]. It would add 60 more Russian banks and more than 70 further shadow-fleet ships to the list, bar Europeans from sitting on the supervisory boards of Russian companies, and tighten limits on European help building or repairing Russian energy infrastructure [1]. It also reaches sport: Russian teams and athletes could no longer compete under the Russian flag at events in the EU [1]. Soldiers who have fought for Russia since February 2022 would face an entry ban [1].

None of it applies until the 27 member states agree; Brussels is aiming for a deal before Christmas [1]. A proposal is a draft, not a law.

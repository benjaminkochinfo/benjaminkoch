On Monday morning there were 22 cm of snow on the Feldberg in the Black Forest and a closed cover around Oberstdorf, the Deutscher Wetterdienst reported [1]. Tonight snow showers in the east can bring 1 to 5 cm locally in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania or Brandenburg, while the clear south cools to −5°C to −8°C [1]. From Wednesday, rain or snow in the south-west can freeze on the ground; on Thursday a low over northern Italy may bring heavier snow to southern and south-eastern Bavaria, though the meteorologist says model differences are "still very large" [1]. Above 300 to 500 metres it stays below freezing all day [1]. A first ordinary winter week: drain the garden tap, find the scraper.

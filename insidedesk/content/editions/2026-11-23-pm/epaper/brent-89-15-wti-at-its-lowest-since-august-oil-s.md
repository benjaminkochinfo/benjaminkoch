Oil fell again on Monday, extending a three-week losing streak, as concerns about global growth weighed on demand expectations; the unresolved conflict involving the United States, Israel and Iran limited the fall, ADVFN reported [2]. WTI was down 3.5% at $80.55 a barrel in New York's morning, its lowest since August [1]; Brent was quoted at $89.15 at 17:39 CET [3]. A barrel that falls while shares soar tells you markets fear a slower economy more than a closed strait, for now.

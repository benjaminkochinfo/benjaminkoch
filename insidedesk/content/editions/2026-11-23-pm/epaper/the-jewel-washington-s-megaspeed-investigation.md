Megaspeed says it is "a legitimate and lawful business" and that she left in August 2025 [1].

The Commerce and Justice departments said last week that the inquiry began in 2024 over possible export-control violations; the Justice Department now leads a criminal investigation [1]. Singapore's interior ministry said police found insufficient evidence that Alice Huang committed offences over possible inaccurate declarations, and did not investigate her for espionage [1].

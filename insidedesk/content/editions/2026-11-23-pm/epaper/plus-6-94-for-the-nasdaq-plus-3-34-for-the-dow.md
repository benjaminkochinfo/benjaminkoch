New York opened Monday the way Europe closed it, only louder. At 17:39 CET the Nasdaq Composite stood at 24,187.15, up 6.94%, and the Dow Jones at 49,753.08, up 3.34%, according to Boursorama's quotes [2]. In the morning Investopedia had the S&P 500 up 5.1% [1]. Last week the Nasdaq fell 10% and the Dow and S&P 500 each lost more than 8% [1].

The rally followed Nvidia's supply chain. Marvell and Micron rose 19%, Intel 12%, AMD 8%; Dell, a major Nvidia partner, gained 21%; Nebius, CoreWeave and Lumentum each jumped more than 20% [1]. Not everything rose: CrowdStrike fell 12% after announcing an all-stock deal to buy Zscaler valued at about 4 billion, and Zscaler rose 14% [1]. Anthropic, last week's record listing, was about 1% lower at around 8, against an offer price of 3 [1].

US markets close on Thursday for Thanksgiving and shut early, at 13:00 New York time, on Friday [1]. Desk arithmetic: a 10% fall needs an 11.1% rise to be undone; Monday's 6.94% gets the Nasdaq a bit more than half way. Nothing here is investment advice.

Researchers at UCSF and UC San Diego analysed about 23,000 people who wore a smart ring for an average of 154 days and computed a Sleep Regularity Index from 0 to 100 [1]. People with few or no depressive symptoms averaged 84; those with severe symptoms 75 [1]. An SRI of 84 means going to bed and waking roughly an hour earlier or later than the day before; 75 is closer to an hour and a half, said first author Severine Soltani [1]. Irregular sleep may be a cause or a symptom, said senior author Ashley Mason; the study, published in Scientific Reports on 12 November, was partly funded by the ring's maker, and two authors disclose ties to it [1]. A regular alarm costs nothing to test.

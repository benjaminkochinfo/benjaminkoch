Ukraine's security service, the SBU, said seven domestically made naval drones were launched from a mothership on Sunday against two Russian vessels at Benghazi, sinking the corvette Merkury, one of Russia's newest ships, and hitting the dry cargo ship MV Angara, which Volodymyr Zelenskyy said was carrying Russian weapons [1]. The operation was about 2,400 km from the front line [1].

The Merkury had docked to refuel and resupply under a cooperation agreement between Russia and the eastern Libyan authorities of Khalifa Haftar, the Institute for the Study of War said, while escorting Russian cargo ships [1]. The UN-recognised government in Tripoli condemned the operation and said Libya rejects any foreign military operations on its territory or in its waters; the UN mission called for "an immediate, independent and transparent investigation" [1]. Russia had not commented [1].

The geo-economic point is the route. Russian logistics to North Africa and the Sahel run through ports like Benghazi; an attack there raises the cost of insurance, escorts and port calls for everyone using them. Jalel Harchaoui of RUSI called the strikes "exceptionally brazen and provocative" and asked how Russia, and the Haftar family, will respond [1].

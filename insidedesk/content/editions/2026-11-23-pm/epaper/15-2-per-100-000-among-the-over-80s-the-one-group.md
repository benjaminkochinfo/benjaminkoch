Across the population, about 3.5 people per 100,000 are newly infected with Covid in a week; among people over 80 it is 15.2, MDR reports from the RKI's week-46 data [1]. Desk arithmetic: more than four times the average. The variant circulating most is XFG, which the WHO rates as a low additional risk [1]. Because the very old still face a high risk of dying from an infection, the RKI recommends a prompt booster for them [1]. If you visit someone over 80 this winter, the useful question is not "how bad is Covid?" but "when was your last jab?".

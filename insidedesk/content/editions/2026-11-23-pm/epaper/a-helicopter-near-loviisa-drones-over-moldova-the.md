On Saturday Finland reported a suspected airspace violation by a manned Russian military helicopter near the south-eastern town of Loviisa, DW reports [1]. Moldova, which is in neither the EU nor NATO, also reported airspace violations, most recently at the weekend [1]. Romania, Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia have reported violations before [1].

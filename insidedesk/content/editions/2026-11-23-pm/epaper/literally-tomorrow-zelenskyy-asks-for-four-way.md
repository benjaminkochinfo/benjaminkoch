"We must do everything to make sure that the four-party meeting – Ukraine, Europe, America and Russia – happens as soon as possible," Volodymyr Zelenskyy told the 72nd NATO Parliamentary Assembly in Berlin by video link on Monday [1]. Partners, he said, wanted to try to make it happen before Christmas: "We hope it happens sooner. We are ready to do this literally tomorrow" [1].

The same morning in the same city, Germany's foreign minister Johann Wadephul told the assembly: "We will defend every centimeter of NATO territory," and, addressing Vladimir Putin, "we may be a defensive alliance but we are prepared to defend our countries and our alliance" [1]. NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte was due at the Chancellery in the afternoon for an official reception with Friedrich Merz [1].

Two sentences, one direction: a seat at a table, and a fence around the room. Neither is a date. The useful thing to watch is not who says "as soon as possible" but who sends out invitations with a city and a day on them.

Drax, the British power generator, gained 7% on Monday after forecasting core profit above market consensus, ADVFN reported [1]. On the same afternoon European consumer-staples shares went the other way, with Unilever down 1.6% and Nestlé down 1% as money rotated out of defensive stocks [1]. A utility rising on guidance during a tech rally is a small reminder that power stations, too, have order books, and that a winter of high evening prices is somebody's revenue line.

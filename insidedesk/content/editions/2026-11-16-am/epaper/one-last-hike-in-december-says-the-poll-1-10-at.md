Most economists polled by Reuters expect the European Central Bank to make its final deposit-rate rise of this cycle next month, though more than a third expect another in early 2027, FXStreet reports [1]. The euro still drifted to about $1.1025 in early Asian trade on Monday [1].

The reason is across the Atlantic. Markets price a 93.5% chance of a quarter-point Fed rise at its 8–9 December meeting, with another expected in January [1]. ING says short-dated swap differentials are at their widest of the year and that "a break below 1.10 is not a given, but that level is certainly at risk into year-end" [1].

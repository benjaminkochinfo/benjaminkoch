Iran's Revolutionary Guards said on Sunday that two oil tankers on an "unauthorised route" in the southern Strait of Hormuz struck naval mines, and that one, a supertanker "carrying over two million barrels of crude oil", sank [1]. They gave no names and no flags, and there was no independent confirmation [1]. The UK's maritime monitor UKMTO said it was aware of an Indian report of an attack on a tanker in the Gulf, unclear whether the same incident; damage was unknown and the crew were safe [1]. The US military says it has cleared mines from the main routes [1]. On the same day the AIS counters saw six commercial crossings, two of them tankers [2]. Until a ship has a name, a claim is a press release. Watch for an insurer's notice or a port's list, not a broadcast.

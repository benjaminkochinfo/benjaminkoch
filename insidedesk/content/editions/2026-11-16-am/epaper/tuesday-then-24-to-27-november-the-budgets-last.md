The coalition committee of CDU, CSU and SPD meets on Tuesday, with the €5.4 billion electricity-tax cut still unfunded in the €560.9 billion draft budget for 2027 [1]. If the leaders agree, ZDF lays out the only route: an amendment from the coalition groups during the second reading of the departmental budgets between 24 and 26 November, with the general debate on the chancellor's budget on 25 November and the third reading and final vote on Friday 27 November [1]. The funding ideas on the table are not new: surpluses on the EEG account, shifts in the Climate and Transformation Fund, more subsidy cuts, and from Bremen's mayor Andreas Bovenschulte a windfall, plastics or sugar tax [1]. Bavaria's CSU social minister Ulrike Scharf called a CDU idea to drop the extended mothers' pension instead "shabby" [1]. Timetables are where promises meet procedure; this one is short.

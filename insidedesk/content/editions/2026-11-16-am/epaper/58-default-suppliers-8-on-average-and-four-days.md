By Verivox's count, 58 default electricity suppliers have announced higher prices for 1 January, by 8% on average, the Berliner Zeitung reports, citing the trade magazine ZfK [1]. For a household using 4,000 kWh that is about €117 a year more [1]. Rises run from just over 2 to 5.5 cents per kWh, and the main driver is the wholesale market, not grid fees [1]. Default suppliers must announce by Friday 20 November [1]. In the default tariff you can leave at any time with two weeks' notice; on other contracts, a price rise lets you cancel without notice to the day it takes effect [1]. Read your meter on 31 December and photograph it [1]. Compare in the second year's terms, without the welcome bonus [1].

COP31 in Antalya enters its decisive second week as ministers arrive; Germany's environment minister Carsten Schneider is to co-lead the talks on emissions cuts with India's Bhupender Yadav, tagesschau reports [1]. The Turkish summit chief Murat Kurum wants the closing declaration to rally countries behind a global electricity target, ideally a concrete number: 35% electrification by 2035 [1]. How many states back it, Turkey has not yet said, and Saudi Arabia and Russia are sceptical [1]. Money remains the glue: developing countries, above all in Africa, want clarity on adaptation finance after last year's pledge to triple it left the base year open [1].

A target with a number can be counted. That is why it is hard to agree, and why it is worth agreeing.

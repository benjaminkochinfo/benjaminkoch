Tesla has posted the "Cybercab Autonomy Subscription Agreement" for the two-seater it has started offering to select customers at $29,990 plus a mandatory $299 a month, Electrek reports [1]. The key line: "Your Cybercab will not operate without an active Autonomy Subscription" [1]. Tesla can change that price with a month's notice, and the car works only inside Tesla's Robotaxi geofences, which Tesla "may expand, reduce, relocate, or otherwise modify" at any time [1]. Use is personal only, so no ride-hailing income without Tesla's written authorisation, and cabin-camera data is kept for 30 days by default [1]. Desk arithmetic: $299 a month is $3,588 a year; Electrek puts five years at $47,631 before insurance and charging [1]. The contract is the product. Read it as you would a lease.

JKM, the Asian liquefied natural gas benchmark, rose 5.91% to $31.35 per million British thermal units in data stamped 01:09 CET on Monday, EnergyReader reports [1]. Europe's TTF front-month stood at €90.86 per megawatt hour in data stamped 21:05 CET on Sunday, with Germany's THE month-ahead at €92.33 [1].

Analysts told Montel on Friday that trade through the Strait of Hormuz will probably struggle to return to normal even if the US and Iran agree a peace deal, because shipowners' and insurers' judgments on security would still govern how quickly vessels return [1]. The next signal, EnergyReader writes, is whether the JKM jump extends into TTF [1].

Over the weekend, the Financial Times reported that ousted chief executive Jensen Huang was discussing a possible return to Nvidia, CNBC TV18 notes [2]. Newsquawk reports that allies in Silicon Valley were pressuring the board to reinstate him, while Treasury Secretary Bessent said the company needs a "shake-up" with an external CEO and an assessment of its compliance with US export controls [1].

Futures listened to both. Nasdaq 100 contracts were up 0.5% early on, per CNBC TV18 [2], before mostly retreating, Newsquawk reported by 04:49 CET [1].

For you: on Tuesday Nvidia publishes audited numbers. Those outrank any weekend report about who sits in the chair. Nothing here is investment advice.

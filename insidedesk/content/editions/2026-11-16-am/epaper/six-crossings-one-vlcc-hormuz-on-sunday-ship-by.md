Six AIS-visible commercial ships crossed the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, three in and three out, hormuz.now's count shows [1]. The biggest was the KIARA M, a San Marino-flagged very large crude carrier of 319,000 tonnes deadweight, inbound at 01:58 CET with Dubai declared as its destination [1]. The three outbound ships were all bulk carriers, declaring Khor Fakkan, Umm Qasr and a destination coded IRBND [1]. The seven-day average is 6.6 crossings a day, against a pre-crisis baseline of 84.8 [1]. Desk arithmetic: that is about 7.8% of normal. Another eight small craft crossed outside the commercial definition [1]. A supertanker going in is not oil coming out. It is, at least, a shipowner betting on a return trip.

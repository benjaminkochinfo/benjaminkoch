The evening's Markets note left the DAX at 15:41, up 2.13%. Xetra has since closed it 2.22% higher at 26,437.67 points [1]. At its best the index stood near 26,542, only 78 points short of its record from late August, and the MDax closed 1.41% higher at 31,178.27 [1]. The Stoxx Europe 600 ended 1.82% higher at 657.40, its highest since 26 August and its steepest one-day gain since 12 June [2].

The sorting the afternoon hinted at held to the bell. Euro Stoxx banks rose 4.6%, the most since early April; Morningstar's Michael Field said a Democratic Congress limits Washington's ability to impose "requirements or sanctions" on European banks in the US [2]. Orsted gained 4.6% and Nordex 4.8% [2]. On the other side, Siemens Energy shed 8% and Infineon, ASML and Soitec fell between 3.4% and 11.8%, on the prospect of stricter oversight of AI [2]. Heidelberg Materials closed 9.6% higher on its quarter, and Novo Nordisk 5.6% on a raised forecast [2]. Brent fell 8%, and oil shares with it [2].

Dekabank's Joachim Schallmayer read the vote as a signal that Trump's policy is "nicht grenzenlos durchsetzbar", not limitlessly enforceable, though he expects no dramatic change of course [1]. A close near a record is a mood; the invoices arrive later. Nothing here is investment advice.

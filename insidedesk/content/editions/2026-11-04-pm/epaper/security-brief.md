Britain and France will lead Exercise Koalicja in Poland from 9 to 22 November, the first major exercise of the Multinational Force Ukraine, the military arm of the coalition of the willing [1]. About 2,800 troops from 11 nations take part: Belgium, Estonia, France, Germany, Lithuania, the Netherlands, Poland, Spain, Sweden, Türkiye and the UK, which sends 300 personnel, RAF Typhoons and a Voyager tanker [1]. The force would move into Ukraine only once fighting has stopped and at Kyiv's request, and it "would not carry out peacekeeping or ceasefire-monitoring duties", the UK Ministry of Defence says [1]. What it rehearses is dull and essential: moving, protecting and supplying troops, and, from Istanbul, clearing mines so merchant ships can return to Ukraine's ports [1]. Peace, when it comes, will need lorries, fuel and minesweepers on the first morning.

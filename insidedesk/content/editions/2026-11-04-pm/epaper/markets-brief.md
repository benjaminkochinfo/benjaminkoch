Heidelberg Materials was the best performer in the DAX on Wednesday afternoon, up 9.96% at €172.15 at 15:41 CET [2]. The reason was a quarter without fireworks. Third-quarter revenue rose 10% to €6,413 million and the result from current operations 9% to €1,281 million, driven, the company says, by Europe, North America and "uncompromising" cost and price management [1].

The full-year range of €3.40 to €3.65 billion was confirmed, not raised [1]. What was new is housekeeping: the cost programme will beat its €500 million target this year, and a further €300 million of savings is planned, in nearly equal parts in 2027 and 2028 [1]. Cement, quarries and asphalt plants rarely make headlines. On a day of election trades, they made the top of the table. Nothing here is investment advice.

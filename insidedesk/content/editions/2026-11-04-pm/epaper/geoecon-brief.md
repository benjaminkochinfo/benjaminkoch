The European Commission hosted Ukraine, the US, France, Germany, the UK, NATO and the World Bank in Brussels on Wednesday, with post-war reconstruction and "prosperity" on the agenda [2]. The figure behind the word: Ukraine faces a €70 billion hole in its 2027 finances, with just €4 billion pledged from outside the EU, bne IntelliNews reports [1]. Germany's draft 2027 budget carries €11.6 billion for Ukraine, falling to €8.5 billion a year for 2028–2030 under the cabinet's April plan [1]. The Bundestag's budget committee holds its final session on 12 November and the final vote is on 27 November [1]. NATO allies were also €13 billion short of the €70 billion in military aid agreed for 2026, according to the Commission [1]. Prosperity plans are drafted in meeting rooms; they are paid for in budget committees, line by line.

Control of the Senate is settled, but two seats are not. CBS News still rated Alaska and Mississippi as toss-ups on Wednesday morning [1]. In Alaska, Republican Senator Dan Sullivan and Democrat Mary Peltola are in a tight race that CBS says could take some time to count [1]. It was shaken up by allegations that operatives linked to Peltola recruited a second candidate also called Dan Sullivan; her team denied it [1]. The Trump administration had sued to block certification if the incumbent got fewer votes than Peltola, and a federal judge dismissed that suit on Sunday [1]. In Mississippi, Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith and Democrat Scott Colom are in what CBS calls an "unexpectedly close" race [1]. Neither result changes who runs the chamber. Both will tell you something about whether the slow, dull count is still trusted to finish the job.

COP31 runs in Antalya from 9 to 20 November, with the leaders' summit on 11 and 12 November [1]. Most pre-summit news is about halls and visitor numbers: about 100,000 expected in the negotiating Blue Zone, 1.5 million in the public Green Zone [1].

The more useful item is a platform. Türkiye's industry ministry, the EBRD and the World Bank's IBRD and IFC will unveil an industrial decarbonisation investment platform for iron and steel, aluminium, cement and fertiliser [1]. It aims to back more than €5 billion of investment by 2030 and cut 20 million tonnes of CO₂-equivalent a year, starting with $250 million from the Climate Investment Funds and €5.7 million from the EU [1]. The EIB, Asian Development Bank and Islamic Development Bank are due to sign up at the summit [1].

Speeches end on 20 November. Loan books keep running.

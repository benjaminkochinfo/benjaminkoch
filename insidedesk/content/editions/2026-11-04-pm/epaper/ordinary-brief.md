Stiftung Warentest has compared 183 buildings insurance tariffs from 69 providers, all including "Elementarschutz", the add-on for floods, backflow and other natural hazards [1]. Premiums rose 4.2% in 2026 from building costs and wages alone, the testers say, and the spread is wide: for their model house in Bremen, very good cover starts at €695 a year, while another very good tariff costs €2,822 [1]. In Regensburg, very good cover starts at €635 [1].

56 of the 183 tariffs were rated poor; weak tariffs, the testers say, do not cover damage caused by gross negligence, or not adequately [1]. Flood cover is still missing on nearly half of all residential buildings, especially in old contracts [1]. If you own a house, the dull task this week is to find your policy and check for that one word.

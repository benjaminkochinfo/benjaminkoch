Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang on 22 November, effective immediately, its filing with the US Securities and Exchange Commission says; Colette Kress continues as chief financial officer [2]. The press release adds the parts that will matter longer than Monday's share price [1].

First, the investigation. A committee of independent directors, with independent counsel, is looking into "recent reporting that Mr. Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort"; the board "reiterated that the committee's investigation will proceed to conclusion", and Huang supports it [1].

Second, a new National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors to oversee trade compliance and national security risks [1]. Third, a search for a new Chief Compliance Officer reporting to the board's nominating and governance committee [1]. Nvidia says it will keep cooperating with government investigations [1].

Governance experts told Reuters the reversal "raises questions about its independence" [3]. That is fair. The answer, if there is one, will be in how those committees work: who sits on them, what they publish, and whether they ever say no. Watch the boring appointments; they are where the next crisis is caught or missed.

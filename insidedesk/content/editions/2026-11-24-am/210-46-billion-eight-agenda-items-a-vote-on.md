The Bundestag's 110th sitting starts at 10:00 with the 2027 budget law, according to the published agenda [1]. Today's eight items run from the President's office through finance (10:05–11:50), digital affairs and state modernisation, the environment and climate ministry, health, and finally research, technology and space until 18:50 [1].

The committee version that MPs are debating plans net borrowing of €122.97 billion in the core budget and €210.46 billion including two special funds, €57.84 billion for infrastructure and climate neutrality and €29.65 billion for the Bundeswehr fund, EU Debt Map reports from Bundestag documents [2]. The budget committee finished its work on 13 November; the final vote is set for 27 November [2].

The line to keep is lower down. The committee set €41.39 billion for debt service in the federal debt chapter, €11.20 billion more than planned for 2026 [2]. Interest rises because borrowing rises and because each new bond is priced at today's rates, not yesterday's.

Speeches this week will argue over priorities. The interest line has no party. It is paid first, every year, before anyone's priorities.

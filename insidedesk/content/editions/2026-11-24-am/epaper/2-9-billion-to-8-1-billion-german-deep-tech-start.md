Investment in German "deep tech" start-ups, in fields such as robotics and defence, is set to rise from €2.9 billion last year to €8.1 billion this year, according to a study by the German Start-up Association and the Joachim Herz Foundation presented on Monday, DW reports [1]. The study described the rise as a "major success for Germany as a business location" [1].

Desk arithmetic: €8.1 billion is about 2.8 times €2.9 billion. The figure for this year is an estimate made before the year is over, so it is a prediction, not a count. The test comes later, in invoices: how many of these companies sell something, to whom, and at what price.

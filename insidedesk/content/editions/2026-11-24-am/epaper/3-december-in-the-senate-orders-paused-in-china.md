Jensen Huang has agreed to testify at a public US Senate hearing on 3 December, the Senate Banking Committee said, according to Reuters [1]. Lawmakers across parties objected to his reinstatement and called for the Chip Security Act, a bill to stop legally exported advanced chips being diverted to China [1].

Reuters also reported that Huang's exit had threatened Nvidia's China business, with some customers there cancelling or hesitating over orders [1]. Nvidia faces several investigations and a shareholder lawsuit, Reuters notes [1]. A rehiring is one meeting. Hearings, audits and lawsuits are many, and they run to their own timetable.

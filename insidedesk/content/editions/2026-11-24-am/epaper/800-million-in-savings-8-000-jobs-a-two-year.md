The warning strikes reach companies that are also negotiating cuts. Mercedes-Benz is seeking €800 million in annual savings in Germany, Handelsblatt reported, and longer working hours without extra pay were among options discussed, according to SWR, Euronews notes [1]. BMW previously sought to cut nearly 8,000 jobs through voluntary severance, according to Münchner Merkur [1].

Volkswagen, in separate talks with IG Metall covering about 120,000 employees, is seeking a two-year pay freeze and more flexible working hours, according to Reuters [1]. Friday's nationwide walkouts drew 151,000 workers, the union said [1].

Read the percentages in the regional pay round against that background. For many workers the question is not only how much more, but whether the job and the hours stay the same.

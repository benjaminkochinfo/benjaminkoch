Germany will produce the Ukrainian "Babka" reconnaissance drone at home, defence minister Boris Pistorius said after meeting his Ukrainian counterpart in Berlin; Germany is providing about €34 million to build production capacity, tagesschau.de reports [1]. The German company Wingcopter and Ukraine's Shadow Flytech have set up a joint venture for it [1].

The two countries also signed a letter of intent on further developing Ukraine's "Delta" battle-management system, which merges separate pictures into one real-time situation picture and which the Bundeswehr is to use in future [1]. The announcement fills in an agreement Chancellor Merz and President Zelenskyy signed in April [1].

This is the less visible side of support: not a delivery, but a production line, and software learned under fire coming the other way.

The SPD and CDU groups in Saarland's state parliament view positively federal justice minister Stefanie Hubig's plan to add gender-specific or other contemptuous motives to the murder paragraph, SR reported [1]. SPD group leader Ulrich Commerçon said killings of women because they are women must be clearly reflected in criminal law; CDU group leader Stephan Toscani said preventing femicides, for example by enforcing restraining orders, was even more important; AfD group leader Josef Dörr said the existing rules were sufficient [1].

Toscani's point deserves the attention: a better paragraph punishes after the fact; a checked restraining order acts before it.

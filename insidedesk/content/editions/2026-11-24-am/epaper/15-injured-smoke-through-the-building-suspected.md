Police are investigating suspected aggravated arson after a fire at a hospital in Berlin's Lichtenberg district on Sunday evening; investigators believe a patient set fire to his bed in a first-floor room, DW reports [1]. Fifteen people were injured, among them a nurse and the suspect, who were treated for smoke inhalation, and 13 others with minor injuries; smoke spread and parts of the hospital were evacuated [1].

Firefighters put out the flames [1]. Evacuating patients who cannot walk is one of the hardest things a hospital has to practise. It is practised for exactly this kind of Sunday evening.

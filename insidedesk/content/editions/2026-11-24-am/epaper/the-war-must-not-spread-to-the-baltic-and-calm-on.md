After the suspected Ukrainian attack on a Russian freighter in the Gulf of Finland, Pistorius said Ukraine has the right to strike military targets in Russia but bears "a special responsibility": "The war must not spread to the Baltic Sea" (translated), tagesschau.de reports [1]. He praised Finland for acting "very wisely and very calmly" (translated) [1].

On reports that Russia may launch a nuclear-armed satellite, he urged calm: it was unclear what weapons were involved, and too early to speculate publicly [1]. Chancellor Merz had called the reports "shocking" (translated) [1]. A minister who declines to speculate is doing his job.

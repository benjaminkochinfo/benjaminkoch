Global observed liquids inventories rose 52 million barrels in October, implying oversupply of 1.7 million barrels a day, Deutsche Bank said in a research note cited by Reuters [1].

Supply is also recovering in Libya: the Sharara pipeline has reopened and oil has begun flowing into storage tanks at the Akakus facility in Zawiya, the National Oil Corporation's chairperson told Reuters [1]. ANZ still calls the market "under severe strain", with thin buffers and constrained logistics [1]. Both can be true. A surplus in October does not refill a tank emptied in the spring.

Taiwan's Taiex led Asia on Tuesday morning, up more than 9%, with Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. and MediaTek both up nearly 10%, AP reported [1]. SBS put the Taiex at 40,147.91 at 11:00 local time and said TSMC hit the daily limit of 10% at 2,230 New Taiwan dollars [3].

In Seoul the Kospi was up 7.19% at 5,047.15 on Tuesday morning, after the gains triggered a five-minute "sidecar" curb on programme buying, the South China Morning Post reported [2]. AP later had it up 6.5% at 5,014.41, with Samsung Electronics up 7.5% and SK Hynix 8.6% [1]. Japan's Nikkei 225 rose about 3.9%, Tokyo Electron 10.5% and Advantest 14.1% [1].

Last week the same exchanges wrote rules to slow the fall. This morning a curb slowed the rise. A rulebook that works in both directions is doing its job.

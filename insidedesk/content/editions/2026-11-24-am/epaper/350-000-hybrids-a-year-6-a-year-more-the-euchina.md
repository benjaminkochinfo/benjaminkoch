EU countries backed the agreement with China on hybrid cars on Friday, clearing it to take effect on 1 December, Euronews reports [1]. It sets country-specific quotas allowing 350,000 Chinese hybrid vehicles a year onto the EU market for four years, with a fixed annual adjustment of 6%; EU officials expect it to more than halve current Chinese hybrid exports to the bloc [1].

Trade Commissioner Maroš Šefčovič is expected to speak to his Chinese counterpart Wang Wentao by video in January, ahead of a meeting in March 2027 [1]. Quotas are dull, countable and reversible. That is their virtue.

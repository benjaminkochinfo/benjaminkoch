In the committee version of the 2027 budget, the federal debt chapter sets aside €41.39 billion for debt service, about €385 million less than in the government's draft but €11.20 billion more than planned for 2026, EU Debt Map reports from Bundestag documents [1]. Interest on the Bundeswehr special fund sits in that fund's own plan, so the figure is not the whole bill [1].

The two special funds together bring €87.49 billion of planned borrowing, about 42% of the €210.46 billion total [1]. Germany's general government debt stood at 63.4% of output at the end of June 2026, according to Eurostat [1].

Desk arithmetic: the rise in debt service alone, €11.20 billion, is about 27% of the €41.39 billion total. Choices about relief are made inside the space the interest line leaves.

Environment minister Carsten Schneider vowed on Monday that Germany would keep moving away from fossil fuels despite the failure of the COP31 declaration in Antalya, DW reports [1]. "We will clearly maintain our course on climate protection in Europe and Germany," he said in Potsdam, "but above all one that makes us independent of oil and gas" [1].

His ministry's 2027 budget is debated in the Bundestag today between 13:40 and 15:25 [2]. Courses are kept in budget lines, not in communiqués.

The Verbraucherzentrale is warning about an e-mail with the subject line "Ihre Mailservice VerwaltungsCenter" that pretends to come from the Deutsche Rentenversicherung [1]. It asks you to review and complete your details in the pension portal, citing supposedly new EU rules on storing personal data, and offers a link to do it [1].

The tells are the usual ones: no personal greeting, an odd sender address, and a link [1]. Do not click it, do not reply, and move the message to spam [1]. If you want to check your pension account, go to the Rentenversicherung's official website yourself [1]. A real institution does not need you to hurry.

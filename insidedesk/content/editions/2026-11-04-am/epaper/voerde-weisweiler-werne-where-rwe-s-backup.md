The first backup-plant auction now has addresses. RWE says it won contracts for three hydrogen-capable gas plants in North Rhine-Westphalia: about 730 megawatts in Voerde, 700 in Weisweiler and 700 in Werne [1]. Those are "reduced megawatts": under the law, only 85% of a gas plant's installed capacity counts in the auction [1]. Desk arithmetic: about 2,130 counted megawatts implies roughly 2,500 installed.

RWE says it wants to start building the first plant "in a few weeks" and plans to invest about €9 billion by 2031 in flexible generation, batteries and hydrogen production [1]. It runs about 4,200 megawatts of gas plants in Germany today [1]. A press release is a promise; the building site will be the receipt.

Special police units have arrested a Turkish national in the Reutlingen district. Baden-Württemberg's criminal police office says he instructed two men to set fire to a Starlink ground station in Poland on the night of 23 September [1]. He is held on suspicion of preparing a terrorist offence, and eight mobile phones and ammunition were seized [1]. The two alleged arsonists, arrested in Nürtingen in mid-October, face extradition to Poland [1]. Polish prime minister Donald Tusk has called the fire an "act of hybrid warfare" [2]. Warsaw suspects Russian intelligence was behind it [1]. Starlink is a strategically important part of Ukraine's defence [1]. A warrant is a quieter answer than a speech, and a more durable one.

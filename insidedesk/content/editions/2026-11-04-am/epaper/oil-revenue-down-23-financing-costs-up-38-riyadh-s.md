Saudi Arabia ran a deficit of SR84.3 billion ($22.38 billion) in the third quarter, according to the Finance Ministry's quarterly report [1]. Revenue was SR259.1 billion and spending SR343.4 billion [1]. Oil revenue fell 23% from a year earlier to SR116.5 billion, while non-oil revenue rose 20% to SR142.6 billion [1]. Over the first nine months, financing expenses climbed 38% to SR54.8 billion and subsidies 49% to SR41.7 billion [1]. The ministry now estimates a full-year deficit of SR165.4 billion, and in September it projected SR191 billion, or 3.6% of GDP, for 2027 [1]. *Desk arithmetic:* in the third quarter non-oil income exceeded oil income by SR26.1 billion. That is diversification, and it shows in the ledger, not the speeches.

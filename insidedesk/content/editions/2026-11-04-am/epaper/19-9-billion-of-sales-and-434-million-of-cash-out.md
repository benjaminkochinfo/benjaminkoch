Super Micro, which builds AI servers, reported record quarterly sales of $19.9 billion, against $5.0 billion a year earlier, and net income of $1.59 billion [1]. It expects $72 billion to $82 billion of sales in the fiscal year to June [1].

The cash-flow statement tells the other half. Operations used $434 million of cash in the quarter [1]. Money owed by customers rose to $9.69 billion from $6.13 billion in June, and inventories to $15.05 billion from $12.90 billion [1]. Desk arithmetic: those two lines grew by about $5.7 billion in three months, partly offset by $3.05 billion more in customer prepayments [1]. The figures are preliminary and unaudited, the company says [1]. Profit is an opinion until the invoices are paid. Nothing here is investment advice.

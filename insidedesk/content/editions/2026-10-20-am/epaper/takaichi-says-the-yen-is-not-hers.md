Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi rejected the idea that she is responsible for a weak yen and high interest rates [1]. She said her government would strengthen confidence in the currency by building a strong economy. She also said it would appropriately control Japanese government bond issuance in next fiscal year's budget [1]. The dollar was near ¥158.50 in Tuesday's Asian session [1]. When the budget draft is published, its issuance figure will show whether she kept that promise.

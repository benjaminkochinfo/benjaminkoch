Kenya has traced and quarantined more than 99% of the contacts of its Ebola cases, the International Rescue Committee said on Monday [1]. ECDC counts eight cases, including one death, as of 18 October. All are linked to the first patient, who travelled from Congo through Uganda [2]. Most of the later cases were already in quarantine when they tested positive, among them three health workers; the eighth was a patient attendant [2]. The IRC is helping the health ministry with screening at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and in 13 high-risk counties [1]. The work still needs funding that the IRC says is arriving "neither fast enough nor at the necessary scale" [1].

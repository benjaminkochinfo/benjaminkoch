Jordan Bardella said on Monday that neither he nor Marine Le Pen will attend the meeting of party leaders that Sébastien Lecornu is to host after the violence; the RN will "très certainement" be represented by a vice-president [1]. No date has been announced [1]. Lecornu asked them to reconsider because "la sécurité de la campagne présidentielle" will be on the agenda, though he said the invitation letter had not yet gone out [1]. A meeting without a date is an intention. Watch for the letter.

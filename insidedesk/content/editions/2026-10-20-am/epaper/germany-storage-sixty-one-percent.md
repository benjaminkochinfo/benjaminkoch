On gas day 18 October, the latest published in GIE's AGSI+ data, EU storage stood at 74.6% full, or 845 of 1,133 TWh [1]. That is 8.2 points below the same day last year and 14.6 below the five-year average [1]. Germany, the largest store at 247 TWh, was 61.2% full, 26.8 points below its five-year average, though still injecting (+0.9 points in seven days) [1]. Poland was at 98.8% [1]. In winter, how close the gas is to you matters as much as the EU-wide average.

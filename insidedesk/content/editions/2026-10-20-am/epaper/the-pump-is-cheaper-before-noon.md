At 05:12 on Tuesday the national average at open stations was €2.089 a litre for Super E10 and €2.225 for diesel [1]. The ADAC's last weekly figures, published on 14 October, were €2.151 and €2.247 [2]. Desk arithmetic: that is about 6 cents lower for E10 and about 2 cents lower for diesel. But the two are measured differently. Under the noon rule, stations may raise prices only once a day, at 12:00 [2], so a pre-dawn snapshot catches the day's falling end. The fuel-tax rebate runs to the end of December [2]. Nothing here is advice. The ADAC says the cheapest time to fill up is now just before midday [2].

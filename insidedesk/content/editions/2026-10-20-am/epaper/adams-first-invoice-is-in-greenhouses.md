El Ejido, in Almería, put its first valuation of storm Adam's material damage at more than €5 million. About 100 hectares of greenhouses were hit after 70 litres per square metre fell in roughly two hours on Saturday. Damage on rural land comes to €4.34 million, €3.9 million of it on farms [1]. In Alicante, the government's sub-delegate asked everyone affected to photograph and document their damage, both for insurance and for future public aid [1]. The camera comes before the broom, because the claim needs the picture.

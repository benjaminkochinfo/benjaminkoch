King Hamad receives President Emmanuel Macron in Bahrain today on an official visit, the Royal Court announced [1]. Macron met UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed in Abu Dhabi on Monday, after starting the tour in Kuwait [1]. The visit ends in Qatar tomorrow [1]. All four states have had energy sites attacked during the US–Iran war [1]. An Elysée source said Macron would push for "freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz" [1]. Fuel supply contracts would be stronger evidence of results than the communiqués.

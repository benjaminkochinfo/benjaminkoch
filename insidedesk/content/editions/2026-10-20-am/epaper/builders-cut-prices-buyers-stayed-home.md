The NAHB housing market index for October came in at 29, far below the 50 line that separates good conditions from bad [1]. Prospective buyer traffic fell to 18 from 23 [1]. 43% of builders cut prices, the highest share in the 2020–2026 period, with an average cut of 6%, and 65% offered incentives [1]. With the US ten-year yield near 5.14% [1], a discount on the house is competing with the price of the mortgage. Mortgage costs usually win that contest.

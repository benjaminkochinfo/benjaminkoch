School students had called a rally for 11:00 today on Rennes's esplanade Charles-de-Gaulle. On Monday night the Ille-et-Vilaine prefecture banned it by order, citing "les graves troubles à l'ordre public constatés le 17 octobre" and the risk of new violence [1]. The order describes an undeclared gathering with 1,500 to 2,000 people expected [1]. Earlier that evening the prefect had also banned night-time gatherings in parts of the city centre and around the Colombia and Alma shopping centres [1]. A ban with a reason attached can be contested. That is the point of writing it down.

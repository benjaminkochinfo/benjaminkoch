At Monday's "Invest in Germany" summit, the economy and research ministries signed a declaration of intent with Nvidia. It aims to develop advanced AI models for "physical AI", meaning robots and other machines [1]. Nvidia says it will open its models to small and mid-sized firms and research institutes, not only to large industry [1]. The federal government gave no figure for any investment [1]. A media report speaks of a three-digit million-euro sum, partly to rent computing capacity in Deutsche Telekom's AI cloud [1]. A declaration is not a contract. Watch for the budget line and the data terms.

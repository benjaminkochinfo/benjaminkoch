Yemen's Iran-aligned Houthis said on Monday that they had launched several attacks on Saudi energy, airport and military targets [1]. The sites they named were an Aramco facility at Yanbu, the Shaybah oilfield, King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh and Abha airport [1]. Saudi authorities did not immediately confirm the claims, and Reuters could not verify them independently [1].

Separately, a Houthi attack on a government security-forces training camp in Yemen's Shabwa province killed 58 people and injured more than 200, according to reports [1]. Asian traders cited the renewed attacks on Saudi energy facilities as one reason oil rose and risk appetite fell on Tuesday morning [2].

So far this is a list of claims, not a damage report. Yanbu is Saudi Arabia's Red Sea export outlet, and Shaybah is a major field. If either had lost output, it would show up in loading schedules and in Aramco statements within days. Until then, the reasonable position is that something was attempted and the scale of any damage is unknown. By early Tuesday, Brent was up only 21 cents [1]. You will learn more from the next confirmed tanker loading than from the next claim of responsibility.

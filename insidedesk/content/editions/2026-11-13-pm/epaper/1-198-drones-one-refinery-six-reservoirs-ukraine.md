Russia's defence ministry said it intercepted 1,198 Ukrainian drones overnight to Friday across more than 15 regions, with oil infrastructure among the targets [1]. Ukraine's General Staff said it struck one of Russia's largest oil refineries in Volgograd, hitting oil processing units, six reservoirs and other parts of the complex [2]. Regional governor Andrei Bocharov said attacks on an industrial area in Volgograd killed four people [1]. Ukraine has concentrated its fire this year on Russia's oil infrastructure to cut the export earnings that fund the invasion [1].

For you: refinery damage shows up first in Russia's own fuel balance and export schedules, not in your pump price. Watch product-export data, not the videos. Nothing here is investment advice.

Nvidia representatives told Illinois congressman Bill Foster that the anti-smuggling measures in the Chip Security Act could be implemented, Foster told MIT Technology Review on Thursday, even though an industry group had called them "potentially infeasible" [1]. The bill, which Foster co-sponsors, would require geotracking to verify where advanced AI chips are and stop them being smuggled to China [1]. It is getting renewed attention after the resignation of Nvidia's chief executive, the magazine notes [1].

Export rules only work if someone can check the paperwork against the pallet. If the maker says the check is buildable, the argument moves from whether to how much, and who pays for it.

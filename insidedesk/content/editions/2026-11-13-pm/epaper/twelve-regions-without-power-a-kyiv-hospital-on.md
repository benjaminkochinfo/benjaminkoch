Russian strikes on Ukraine's energy infrastructure overnight to Friday caused power cuts in 12 regions, from Kyiv and Kharkiv to Odesa and Chernihiv, Deputy Energy Minister Artem Nekrasov said: "new attacks and constant air raid alerts are complicating and delaying repairs" [1]. A hospital in Kyiv caught fire after a drone strike at about 3am local time, injuring one person [2]. In Odesa two people were killed, a 17-year-old was killed in Zaporizhzhia, and a strike in Rivne damaged a kindergarten and wounded six people, including two children [1].

For you: repair crews, not communiqués, decide how many Ukrainian homes are warm tonight. That is why the grid's spare-parts lists matter as much as the talks.

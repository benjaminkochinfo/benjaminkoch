Beijing put dates on the calendar on Friday. Xi Jinping will chair and address the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen on 18 and 19 November, give a keynote at the APEC CEO Summit on 17 and 18 November, and hold bilateral talks with visiting leaders, China's Foreign Ministry announced [1]. The theme is "Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together" [1].

The meeting that would make the headlines is not on the published programme. Asked about a possible meeting of Xi, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said on Thursday: "China is in communication with relevant parties regarding a meeting between the three heads of state", adding that more would be released when available [1]. In Dublin, Friedrich Merz already asked Washington for "no surprises" from expected Ukraine talks with Putin in China [2].

The forum itself is less glamorous and more durable. APEC has 21 member economies, from China, the US and Russia to Japan, Canada and Vietnam, and accounts for more than 60% of global GDP and around 40% of the world's population, Anadolu reports [1]. China hosted it in 2001 and 2014; Shenzhen is the third time [1].

For you: summits are judged by their communiqués, and communiqués by what ministries do in the following months. Watch the customs and tariff schedules after Shenzhen, not the handshake photographs.

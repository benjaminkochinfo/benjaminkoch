The Stoxx 600 closed up 0.13 points, or 0.02%, at 664.14: a new record close, its fifth gain in a row and its best week since early July, up 2.44% [1]. The DAX closed down 21.93 points, or 0.08%, at 26,749.95, its second-highest close on record, still up 3.6% on the week [2]. Infineon fell 4.3% at the bottom of the DAX as chip shares sold off [3]; Wise closed up 7.7% at the top of the Stoxx 600 and Acciona down 8% at the bottom [4]. Investors are caught "between record mood and loss aversion", wrote Timo Emden of CapTrader (our translation) [3]. One index made a record and the other missed one, both by less than a tenth of a per cent. The week did the work; Friday only signed it. Not investment advice.

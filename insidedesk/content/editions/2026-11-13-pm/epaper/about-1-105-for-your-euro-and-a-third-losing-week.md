On Friday afternoon the euro traded around $1.1050, close to its lowest since May 2025 and heading for a third straight weekly loss, FXStreet reported at 15:42 CET [1]. The dollar index stood near 103.20, just below a 19-month high [1]. The softer-than-forecast US producer price report changed little: traders still fully price a quarter-point Fed rise in December and lean towards another in January [1]. On the euro side, Q3 GDP grew 0.4% on the quarter, as expected, but employment was flat against a forecast 0.2% rise [1].

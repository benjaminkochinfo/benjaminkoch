The labour and social affairs ministry remains by far the largest single budget, and for the first time it passes €200 billion, rising from €197.3 billion to €203.3 billion in 2027 [1]. Inside it sits a quieter line: with high unemployment-benefit costs, the Federal Employment Agency is expected to need a loan of €8.2 billion, which would leave it owing about €20 billion by the end of 2027 [1]. The federal subsidy to the pension insurance is cut by €3 billion, while about €1.75 billion is set aside to cover the cost of holding the pension level at 48%, around €408 million more than first planned [1].

For you: unemployment insurance is paid from your payslip, and a fund that borrows today is a fund that must be repaid from contributions or the federal budget later. Watch the agency's contribution rate, not only the headline debt.

The DAX stood barely changed at 26,767 points on Friday afternoon [1]. Two shares were not part of the calm.

Rheinmetall fell 1.9% to its lowest level since February 2025, dpa-AFX reported; since January's high of €1,966 its loss adds up to about 60% [1]. Deutsche Pfandbriefbank, the commercial-property lender, dropped 6.5% to a record low, about 38% down since the start of the year, a day after weak quarterly figures and a surprise cut to its annual targets [1].

Different stories, one lesson. A defence boom priced as a certainty and a property book priced as a recovery both met the same auditor: the next set of numbers. Nothing here is investment advice.

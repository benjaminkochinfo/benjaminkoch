Spain's olive harvest for the 2026/27 season is beginning, and the first prices are in. In the week from 7 to 13 November, bulk extra virgin olive oil traded at an average of €3.18 a kilo at origin across 50 deals and 2,357 tonnes, according to the Poolred system compiled by the farmers' union COAG Jaén [1]. Virgin oil averaged €2.84 and lampante €2.76 [1]. Over the month from 14 October, extra virgin averaged €3.19 across 8,882.7 tonnes [1]. Andalusia's price observatory had €3.03 for the week to 8 November [1].

These are mill-gate prices, unpacked and before bottling, tax and the supermarket's margin. What reaches your shelf follows them slowly, and only if the harvest keeps its pace.

A litre of Super E10 costs €1.866 on the German national average, 7.2 cents less than a week ago, and a litre of diesel €2.085, down 9.6 cents, the ADAC said on Wednesday [1]. Over the same week Brent eased slightly and settled into a corridor between $90 and $95 a barrel, closing on Tuesday at around $91, about two dollars below the week before [1].

The motoring club's reading is that the risk premiums built up during the uncertain months are slowly melting away, most visibly in diesel, since pump prices keep falling while crude merely stabilises [1]. But it warns against calling this normal. The fuel rebate, the Tankrabatt of about 17 cents a litre, expires at the turn of the year [1].

Desk arithmetic: on a 50-litre tank of diesel, this week's fall is worth about €4.80; the rebate, if it ended today with nothing else changing, would be worth about €8.50 a tank. The first is a price movement. The second is a policy decision, already dated.

The ADAC's practical advice is the same as ever: fill up shortly before 12:00, when prices are at their lowest of the day; at noon they jump and then drift down slowly [1]. Its app compares prices at more than 14,000 stations [1].

The cheapest litre of the week is still the one bought at 11:45 rather than 12:05. Habits beat headlines.

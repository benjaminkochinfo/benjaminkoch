Data from Russia's state statistics agency Rosstat show that at least 490,334 Russian soldiers have been killed in the war since February 2022, according to a BBC analysis of confidential government figures leaked this week, as reported by The Independent [1]. The figures show the invasion became the leading cause of death for 18- to 24-year-olds in Russia, with 65% of those in that group who died last year killed in the conflict [1]. The same live report notes that Ukraine is bracing for a winter of heavier attacks on its energy system [1]. Statistics offices are dull places.

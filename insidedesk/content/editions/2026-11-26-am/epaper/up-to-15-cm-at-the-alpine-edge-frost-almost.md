High pressure and cold air masses govern most of Germany, the Deutscher Wetterdienst says in its 05:00 warning summary [1]. Expect widespread light to moderate frost this morning, between 0 and -7 degrees, with fog in places below 150 metres' visibility and slippery roads from refreezing, freezing drizzle or a little industrial snow [1]. From late morning, snow spreads from the Alpine edge into the southern and eastern foothills: 3 to 10 cm in places, up to 15 cm at the Alpine edge east of the Inn [1]. Overnight, 1 to 5 cm is possible in Saxony [1]. If you drive early, leave a few minutes sooner.

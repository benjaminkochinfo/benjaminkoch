US envoy Jared Kushner said in a podcast interview that Europeans and Ukrainians had not offered Washington an alternative route to peace: "We've asked the Europeans or the Ukrainians if they have a better idea on how to get there, but nobody has presented us with one so far," LBC reports [1]. He said he had pressed Vladimir Putin on Europe's position that a ceasefire should come first, and that the Russian leader "doesn't seem that interested" [1]. He called Putin a "grand master of politics" and said of the territorial dispute: "Right now there's no way to bridge that last issue" [1]. An Article 5-style guarantee without Nato membership would be "a pretty substantial development", he added [1]. Praise is free; guarantees are the part that has to be written down.

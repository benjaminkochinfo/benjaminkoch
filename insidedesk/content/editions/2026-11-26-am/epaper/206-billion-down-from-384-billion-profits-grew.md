The 4.3% GDP headline got the attention on Wednesday. The same release carried the profit account: US corporate profits from current production rose $206.0 billion in the third quarter, after a rise of $384.0 billion in the second [1]. Real gross domestic income, the same economy measured from the income side, grew 4.4%, and the average of GDP and GDI also 4.4% [1]. When the two sides of the ledger agree, you can trust the number a little more. Desk arithmetic: the profit gain was $178.0 billion smaller than the quarter before. Profits still grew; they grew less. Nothing here is investment advice.

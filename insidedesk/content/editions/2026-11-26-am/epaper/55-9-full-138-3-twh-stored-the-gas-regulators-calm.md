German gas storage stands at 55.9%, or 138.3 terawatt hours, the Bundesnetzagentur said in its assessment of 25 November, clearly lower than in the same period of previous years [1]. Pipelines brought in 19.2 TWh between 17 and 23 November and the LNG terminals 2.2 TWh, an average terminal utilisation of 45.1% [1]. The regulator says falling levels are "kein Indiz für eine Mangellage", no sign of a shortage, and that meeting the 1 February fill targets is not strictly necessary for security of supply [1]. It still calls a careful use of gas sensible given higher household prices, and expects comparatively low storage at the end of winter [1]. A storage tank is a buffer, not a trophy. It is meant to be emptied in winter.

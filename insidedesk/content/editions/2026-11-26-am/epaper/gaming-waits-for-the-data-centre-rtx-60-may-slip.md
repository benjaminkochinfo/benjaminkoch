Nvidia is reportedly scaling back shipments of GeForce RTX 50 gaming GPUs as it prioritises AI products, and mass production of the next RTX 60 series, originally targeted for late 2027, could be delayed, TrendForce reports, citing Taiwan's Commercial Times [1]. Memory makers are favouring server DRAM, limiting GDDR7 output and lifting graphics DRAM prices; Micron has reportedly discontinued its 2GB GDDR7 chips [1]. A September TechSpot report suggested Rubin-based RTX 60 cards could be pushed to 2028 [1]. Lead times for RTX Pro workstation GPUs now exceed 26 weeks [1]. When capacity is scarce, the allocation spreadsheet decides who gets the future first.

Germany's retail association expects shoppers to spend €6.2 billion on Black Friday and Cyber Monday this year, a record and "knapp sechs Prozent", just under 6%, more than last year [1]. It is a recovery, not a habit: spending on the two days rose every year from 2016, stalled in 2024 and fell for the first time in 2025, the HDE says [1]. Black Friday falls on 27 November, Cyber Monday on 30 November [1].

The ledger behind the forecast is a survey of about 1,000 online shoppers, run by IFH Köln in October [1]. 51% plan to hunt for bargains on Black Friday and more than a third on Cyber Monday [1]. About €1.9 billion of the spending is expected to go on Christmas presents; 58% of respondents use Black Friday for Christmas shopping, 48% Cyber Monday [1]. Desk arithmetic: that puts close to a third of the two days' takings on Christmas presents.

That last point is the economists' caveat. Gerrit Heinemann of Hochschule Niederrhein told the WDR that many consumers simply bring forward Christmas purchases they had planned, which does not necessarily mean extra sales for retailers [2]. DekaBank's Andreas Scheuerle said: "Mir fehlt die Fantasie, eine echte Konsumbelebung zu erwarten" — he lacks the imagination to expect a real revival in spending [2].

Now the useful part for you. A retailer advertising a price cut must also show its own lowest price of the past 30 days, with exceptions such as pure comparisons with the manufacturer's recommended price, the UVP [2]. Consumer advocates are blunt about the UVP. "Mit der UVP zu werben, ist Quatsch," says Andrea Steinbach of the Verbraucherzentrale Rheinland-Pfalz: a discount on a price nobody pays is no discount [2].

The rest is old-fashioned bookkeeping. Pay unknown shops by invoice or direct debit, not prepayment, advises the Verbraucherzentrale NRW [2]. GMX and Web.de counted about 257 million spam emails a day in October, 41% more than a year earlier, many dressed as parcel services and payment providers [2]. The countdown clock is a sales tool. Your own shopping list, written before Friday, is the better one.

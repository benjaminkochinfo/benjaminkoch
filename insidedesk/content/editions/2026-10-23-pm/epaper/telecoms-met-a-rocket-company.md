US telecom shares fell before Friday's open after Bloomberg reported that SpaceX is in talks to get access to at least 80,000 cell sites across the country, a step towards becoming a wireless carrier [1]. AT&T and T-Mobile US fell nearly 5% in pre-market trading and Verizon about 4% [1]. A talk is not a network. Building one means masts, spectrum and years of dull permits, which is exactly why incumbents are worth something.

The maritime prefecture for the Channel and North Sea warned of south to south-west winds of force 8 to 9, sometimes 10, from the Belgian border to La Hague, from Friday until Saturday morning [1]. That means mean wind speeds of 34 to 55 knots, or 62 to 102 km/h [1]. Leisure sailors were told not to go out "under any pretext" [1]. A forecast that tells you to stay in harbour is the cheapest safety equipment there is.

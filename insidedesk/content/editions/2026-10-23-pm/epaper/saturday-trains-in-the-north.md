SNCF has halted Normandy lines as a precaution and warned of disruption on Saturday too [1]. On Friday no trains ran between Amiens and Rouen, Creil and Beauvais or Amiens and Compiègne, and traffic stopped from 10:00 between Paris and Beauvais and between Paris and Laon [1]. RER lines C, D and E in the Paris region were badly disrupted [1]. P&O kept its Calais sailings; DFDS cancelled Dover–Calais until Saturday morning [1]. Check before you leave.

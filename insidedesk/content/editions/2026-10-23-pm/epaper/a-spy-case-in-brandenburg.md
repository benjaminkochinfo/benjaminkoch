A judge at the Federal Court of Justice ordered the detention of a Ukrainian man arrested by police in Brandenburg on Thursday, federal prosecutors said on Friday [1]. He is accused of spying on German and NATO military sites for Russian intelligence, including the production of armoured vehicles, explosives and munitions [1]. He is presumed innocent.

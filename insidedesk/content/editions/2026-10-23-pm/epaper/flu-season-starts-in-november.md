WHO Europe said on Friday that the flu season in the region usually starts between mid-November and mid-December, peaking in January or February [1]. It recommends annual vaccination for older people, pregnant women, children under five, people with chronic conditions or weak immune systems, and health workers [1]. Last year's protection wanes, and the vaccine is updated each year [1]. Its other advice is pleasingly plain: stay home if unwell, cover coughs, open the windows [1].

Germany is releasing more of its emergency oil. The economy ministry said on Friday that a regulation took effect on Thursday under which the national stockholding agency may temporarily cut its required stock from 90 days to 82 [1][2]. Under the International Energy Agency's key, Germany is to contribute 19.5 million of 100 million barrels; 6 million had been released by October, and for the remaining 13.5 million the ministry will start with diesel and heating oil [1]. The Bundesrat consented to the text, which runs to two sections [2].

On the same Friday a Malta-flagged tanker, the Agioi Fanendes, was heading from the Russian Baltic port of Primorsk to New York with about 40,000 tonnes of ultra-low-sulphur diesel, according to ship-tracking data from Kpler and LSEG reported by Euronews [3]. It is due on 7 November, and the buyer and seller are not known [3]. If it arrives, Kpler says it will be the first Russian diesel or gasoil cargo to the US since July 2023 [3].

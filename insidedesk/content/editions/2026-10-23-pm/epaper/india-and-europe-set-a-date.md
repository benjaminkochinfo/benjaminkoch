India's commerce minister, Piyush Goyal, said on Thursday that the trade deal with the European Union should be signed before the end of 2026 and become operational by March 2027 [1]. He called it the "fastest trade deal" the EU has entered into [1]. The deal, announced in January after more than two decades of talks, covers partners that together make up about 25% of global GDP [1]. In a week of blockades, a tariff schedule with a start date is the quiet good news.

Shares in Kering fell 12% in early trading on Friday after Gucci, its main brand, reported a 6% fall in same-store sales [1]. Bernstein's Luca Solca pointed to costs of living, and then to something harder to fix: "an underlying lack of interest in Gucci's core product" [1]. L'Oréal, selling smaller luxuries, beat forecasts the same morning [1]. The desk's reading: when budgets tighten, people trade down in size before they give up the treat.

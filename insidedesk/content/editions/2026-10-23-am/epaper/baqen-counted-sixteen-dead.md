The magnitude 7.0 earthquake that struck Baqen County in Nagqu at 22:33 Beijing time on Thursday has killed 16 people and injured 54, China's emergency ministry said on Friday morning [1]. The epicentre, Maru township, lies about 70 km from the county seat, where power and telecoms were cut [2]. The national response was raised from level four to three [1]. Of 350 firefighters and 83 vehicles sent, 120 people had arrived by 06:30 local time; the rest were already inside Nagqu [1].

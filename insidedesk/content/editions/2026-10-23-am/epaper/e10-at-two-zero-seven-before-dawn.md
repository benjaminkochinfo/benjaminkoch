At 05:11 on Friday, the stations open across Germany averaged €2.078 a litre for Super E10, €2.133 for E5 and €2.240 for diesel, based on reports to the federal fuel market transparency unit [1]. Among the Länder, Saarland had the cheapest E10 at €2.032 and North Rhine-Westphalia the dearest at €2.129 [1]. ADAC's Wednesday national averages were €2.110 for E10 and €2.255 for diesel; night and day prices differ, so the two are not a like-for-like change [1][2]. ADAC says prices are usually lowest shortly before noon [2]. Desk arithmetic: diesel is 16.2 cents above E10 this morning. Nothing here is advice.

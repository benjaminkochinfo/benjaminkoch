Senate Democrats plan to examine the administration's deal easing sanctions on Russian diesel, a Senate Democratic aide told The Washington Post [1]. The Senate Foreign Relations Committee has asked companies involved to preserve their documents ahead of a possible inquiry [1]. Earlier this month the president waived the sanctions until April [1]. A preservation letter is not a verdict. It is the paper trail being kept.

Thursday's flight to safety skipped the metal that is supposed to be its destination. Gold fell nearly 3% overnight and was flat at $4,021.99 an ounce in Asia on Friday, Reuters reported, while Treasuries had their biggest daily rally in more than three years and the yen and Swiss franc gained [1]. Safety, it turns out, is not one asset but a ranking, and on Thursday the ranking favoured things that pay interest. The order can change; the lesson is that no shelter is guaranteed. Nothing here is advice.

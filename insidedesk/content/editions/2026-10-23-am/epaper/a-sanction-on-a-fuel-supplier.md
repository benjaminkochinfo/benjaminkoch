On Thursday Israel's defence ministry sanctioned Coral Oil, a UK-registered energy company. The ministry says Coral Oil transferred about 130,000 litres of fuel to Al-Amana, a fuel company owned by Hezbollah [1]. It describes Coral Oil as a Lebanese company controlled by the Yamin family, linked to firms worth hundreds of millions of dollars [1]. Its argument is logistical: fuel moves weapons, runs generators and keeps underground sites working [1]. Whether the listing bites depends on banks and shippers outside Israel, not on the announcement.

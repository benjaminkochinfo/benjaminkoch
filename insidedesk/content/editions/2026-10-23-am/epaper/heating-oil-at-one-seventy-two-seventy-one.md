A hundred litres of heating oil cost €172.71 on the German average on Friday morning, for a 3,000-litre order, according to HeizOel24 [1]. The portal shows the change on the previous day as 0.00% [1].

**The longer view.** That is €3.42 more than a week ago and €38.67 more than three months ago [1]. The three-year range runs from €85.59 on 1 June 2025 to €179.02 on 16 September 2026; the average over that period is €104.83 [1]. Desk arithmetic: a 3,000-litre delivery costs about €5,181 at today's average, some €1,160 more than three months ago, and about €189 below the September high.

**The exchange.** At 05:09 Brent stood at $113.79 a barrel, up 0.73%, and gasoil, the wholesale cousin of heating oil, at $1,486.50 a tonne, up 0.61% [1]. The euro bought $1.1212 [1]. Only about half the price you pay comes from the oil itself; taxes and the CO₂ levy, which adds about 17.4 to 20.6 cents a litre in 2026, make up much of the rest [1].

**The machinery.** An order is binding once placed and cannot be cancelled; the agreed price holds until delivery, which usually takes two to four weeks [1]. In the portal's own reader poll, 90% said they would wait [1]. A poll is not a forecast, and neither is this. Nothing here is advice.

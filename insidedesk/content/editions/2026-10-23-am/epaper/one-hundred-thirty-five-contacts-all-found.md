Kenya has reported nine Bundibugyo Ebola cases, including one death earlier this month of a Kenyan who had worked in Congo and flew home via Entebbe [1]. "Immediately after, we commenced on contact tracing," said Patrick Amoth, director general of Kenya's health ministry: 135 contacts were traced "and we have been able to account for them, 100 per cent" [1]. The WHO's Mohamed Janabi put the general rule plainly: "success is possible where surveillance is strong, where contacts are found and followed" [1]. A list with no missing names is the dullest and best news an outbreak can produce.

SK Hynix shares fell 8% in Seoul on Friday morning after the chipmaker said its US subsidiary Solidigm "will halt its review of potential pre-IPO and IPO options," CNBC reported [1]. The company cited recent capital market conditions and share price trends, and said it is exploring other ways to fund a new US chip plant for AI memory [1]. A listing is a window, and windows close when prices fall. The plant still needs paying for; only the route to the money has changed.

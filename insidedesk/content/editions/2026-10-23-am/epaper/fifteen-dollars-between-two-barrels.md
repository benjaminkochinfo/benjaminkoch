In early Asian trade on Friday, Brent crude futures stood at $112.96 a barrel and US West Texas Intermediate at $98.12, Reuters reported [1]. Desk arithmetic: a gap of $14.84. Brent, which settled on Thursday at its highest since 4 May, is set for a third successive weekly gain; WTI is on track to end two weeks of gains and finish the week lower [1]. The difference is geography. Brent prices the barrels that pass the Gulf and the Red Sea; WTI prices the ones that do not have to. Nothing here is advice.

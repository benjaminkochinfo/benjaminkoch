In a Tuesday memo reviewed by The Washington Post, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth barred service members and department staff from seeking personal gain from non-public military information, classified or not [1]. He named Polymarket and Kalshi. "Our enemies watch movements in these markets," the memo says; a trade on a military outcome "creates a signal" [1]. The Post notes it is unclear whether contractors are covered [1]. A rule that names the platform is a rule people can follow.

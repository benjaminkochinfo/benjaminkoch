Customers have withdrawn $3.64 billion from Binance since the exchange resumed withdrawals after Thursday's hack, the company said, n-tv reported [1]. Binance puts that at 2.1% of assets under management, which it gave as $171 billion at the end of October [1]. It disputed the analytics site DefiLlama's figure of $5.81 billion, saying that count included internal transfers to new wallets of its own [1]. Binance said it has replaced 95% of the stolen customer funds from its own reserves [1].

Bitcoin recovered to above $78,000 after a six-week low on Friday, but $643 million flowed out of US spot bitcoin ETFs that day [1]. The lesson is an old one: a reserve is only as good as the count of it. Nothing here is investment advice.

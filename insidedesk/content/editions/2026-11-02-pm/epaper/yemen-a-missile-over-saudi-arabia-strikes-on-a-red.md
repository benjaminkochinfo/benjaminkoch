The Saudi-led coalition said on Monday its air defences "intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile" aimed at southern Saudi Arabia, after downing a drone on Sunday evening [1]. Houthi-affiliated media reported that coalition strikes on Hodeidah wounded seven people: four at the Ras Isa port dock and three at an oil company site in Bajil [1]. The coalition did not comment on those reports [1].

On land, the city of Taiz remains besieged; its governor says 120 health centres have stopped working since supply routes were cut in early October [1]. Watch the port and the clinics, not the communiqués: they show what the war costs the roughly 43 million people of Yemen [1].

Bulgaria's national meteorological bureau issued a Code Orange warning for heavy rain on Monday for seven districts: Varna, Bourgas, Dobrich, Silistra, Shoumen, Razgrad and Turgovishte [1]. Ten more, including Plovdiv, Rousse and Stara Zagora, were on Code Yellow; the west of the country was green [1].

The bureau spells out what orange means: possible flooding of properties and transport networks, possible disruption to power, communications and water supply, and partial evacuation if necessary [1]. Significant rain was expected along the central and northern Black Sea coast, with snow above 1,800–2,000 metres in the mountains [1]. If you drive in eastern Bulgaria this week, the bureau's own warning is the relevant one: aquaplaning and poor visibility [1].

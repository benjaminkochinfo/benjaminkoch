Alongside its attitudes survey, South Korea's National Center for Mental Health released its status report for 2025. It counted 6,367 people per 100,000 treated for a mental illness last year, up every year from 5,520 in 2022, 5,824 in 2023 and 6,113 in 2024, Health Chosun reported [1].

The money was spread less evenly. The national average community mental-health budget was 9,722 won per person; Jeonbuk spent 17,309 won and Gyeongnam 7,379 [1]. Desk arithmetic: Jeonbuk spends about 2.3 times as much per head as Gyeongnam. More people in treatment is good news if the clinic down the road can take them. Whether it can depends on which province you live in.

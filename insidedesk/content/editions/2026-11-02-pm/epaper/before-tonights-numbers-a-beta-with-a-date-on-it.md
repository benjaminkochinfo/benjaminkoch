Apple's quarterly results come out after the US close tonight, later than this edition, and we will not guess them. A smaller item already has a date. Apple's website in China says iOS 27.3 will switch on some Apple Intelligence features there: Writing Tools, Genmoji, Clean Up, notification summaries, priority notifications, Visual Intelligence and Live Translation with AirPods [1][2]. Siri AI and Image Playground are not included [1].

Apple's own Chinese newsroom fixes the dates: testing opens with the latest iOS developer beta from 4 November, and the features reach users in China when iOS 27.3 is released in December [2]. Apple says it has partnered with Alibaba so that Qwen can be used inside Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence, and that support on other platforms will follow once the necessary approvals are granted [2]. Until then the features are limited to the iPhone 15 Pro and newer [1]. A launch that waits on a regulator is planned around the regulator, and this one is.

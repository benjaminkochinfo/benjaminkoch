Further down Tuesday's ballot than Congress, nine US states elect members of the boards that regulate utilities, the bodies that approve electricity rates and the grid that data centres depend on [1]. In Arizona, Republicans hold all five seats on the Corporation Commission and two Democrats are running [1]. In Georgia, a Democratic sweep of the two seats on offer would give them 3-2 control of the five-member Public Service Commission [1].

Few voters can name a utility commissioner. Most of them pay the commissioner's decisions every month. If you want to know what an election changes at home, start with the board that signs off the tariff.

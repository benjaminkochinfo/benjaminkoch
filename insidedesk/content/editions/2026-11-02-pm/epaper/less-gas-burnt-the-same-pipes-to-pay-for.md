Germany burns less gas than it did before 2022, but the network still has to be maintained. Verivox's energy expert Thorsten Storck explains the result: network costs are spread over fewer kilowatt hours, which tends to push fees up [1].

For 2027, Verivox finds gas network fees up about 3% on average, around €19 a year for a house using 20,000 kWh [1]. Berlin rises most, by 8% or €38, ahead of Hamburg at 7% or €35 [1]. Rhineland-Palatinate and Brandenburg rise by 4.4% [1]. Saxony (−0.7%, −€5) and Thuringia (−0.5%, −€3) fall slightly [1].

Storck's point is that this is the small line on the bill: the network fee will have "little influence" compared with wholesale prices [1]. The pipes cost €19 more. The gas inside them is the line to check when your letter comes.

This evening's Energy lead left one number open: the first day-ahead auction after Monday's fall in gas. SMARD now shows it. Power for Tuesday in Germany and Luxembourg averages €252.85 per megawatt hour, after €259.31 for Monday [1]. Desk arithmetic: that is about 2.5% cheaper, against a Dutch gas price that fell about 6.5% between 08:45 and 15:40 [2].

The gap is the story from the lead, now with a receipt. Tuesday's cheapest hour, from noon, is €222.07; the dearest, from 17:00, is €313.58 [1]. The evening peak is when the gas plants are needed most.

And they are still needed. SMARD's provisional figures for Monday up to 18:00 show 231.4 gigawatt hours from gas, against 282.6 for the whole of Sunday [1]. Offshore wind has returned, with 93.0 GWh so far against 15.1 on Sunday, but onshore turbines produced only 43.1 GWh [1]. These figures are provisional and will be revised [1].

So the auction did what auctions do. It paid a little less for fuel and the same for scarcity. The next number to watch is not a quote from Amsterdam but how much of Tuesday's grid the wind covers. Nothing here is investment advice.

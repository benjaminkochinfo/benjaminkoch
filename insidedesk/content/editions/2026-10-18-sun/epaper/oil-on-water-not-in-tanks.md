Global observed oil stocks fell by only 10 million barrels in September, the IEA says, because a surge in oil on water largely offset a further 113-million-barrel drop on land [1]. On-land stocks are at their lowest since at least 2016, and global inventories ended September 509 million barrels below their end-February level [1]. Oil in transit is a promise; oil in a tank is cover.

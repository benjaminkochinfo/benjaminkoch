At the Warsaw Security Forum on 12 October, NATO Deputy Secretary General Radmila Shekerinska said: "Building stronger defences is our top priority at NATO." That means investing 5% of GDP a year in defence, she said, adding that Europeans are spending a lot more, "but not yet enough", with Poland leading by example [1].

Her reading of Moscow's hybrid campaign was blunt. Russia uses these attacks and threats because "they want to undermine our unity"; the result, she argued, is a "stronger NATO, committed to support Ukraine even more" [1]. She also said there is no strong defence without a strong defence industry [1].

The desk's translation: sabotage and drones are cheap ways to test whether 32 governments still agree. The 5% target is the expensive way of saying they do. Watch the budgets, not the panels.

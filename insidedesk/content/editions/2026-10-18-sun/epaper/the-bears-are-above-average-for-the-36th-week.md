In the latest AAII Sentiment Survey, bearish sentiment rose 2.6 points to 41.6%. That is above its historical average of 31.5% for the 36th consecutive week. Bullish sentiment fell 1.5 points to 38.7%, and neutral sentiment, at 19.7%, has been below average for 32 weeks running [1]. The bull-bear spread is −2.9 [1].

The survey's special question asked members how their approach had changed lately. 35.3% said they had become slightly more conservative and 6.6% much more so. 21.3% had made only modest changes, 4.9% had become more aggressive and 32.0% had changed nothing [1].

That is the behavioural point. Thirty-six weeks of above-average pessimism and the largest group still made small adjustments or none. Mood is loud; behaviour is sticky. Read sentiment polls as weather, and your own plan as climate.

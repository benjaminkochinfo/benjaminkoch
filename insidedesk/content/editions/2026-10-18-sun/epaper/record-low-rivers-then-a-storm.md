Copernicus reports record-low September river flows in parts of the Rhine, the Danube, the Vistula and the Garonne-Dordogne. Low water disrupted shipping, water supply and farming in several countries [1]. Late-month rain brought some relief and also floods in parts of France and Spain [1]. Too little water, then too much at once, is how this Sunday's storm Adam should be read too.

On 7 October the federal cabinet set the social-insurance figures for 2027. The contribution ceiling for pensions and unemployment insurance rises to €8,850 a month (€106,200 a year), and the ceiling for health insurance to €6,375 a month (€76,500 a year) [1]. The figures follow wage growth in 2025 of 4.38% [1]. The health-insurance ceiling and the compulsory-insurance threshold also rise by an extra €300 a month on top of that, as the law requires [2].

The ministry's own sentence is the useful one: only people who already earned above these limits are affected, and for the large majority of insured people "nothing changes" [1]. The Bundesrat still has to approve the regulation [1].

If your gross pay is below €6,375 a month, this is background. If it is above, your January payslip will show it. Note it now rather than in January.

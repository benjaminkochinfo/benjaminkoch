Italy's temporary border controls were due to expire on Thursday evening. Rome extended them by a week to line up with Spain's, which expire on 22 October, and the two interior ministers are due to agree replacement measures by then, Euronews reported [1]. Two governments that were trading checks now share a deadline. That is not a solution, but it is the shape most solutions take first.

TSMC reported third-quarter revenue of NT$1,494.24 billion, up 50.9% on the year, or $46.72 billion in dollar terms. Net income rose 68.7% to NT$763.06 billion [1]. Gross margin was 66.4%, operating margin 58.7% and net margin 51.1% [1]. Chips of 7 nanometres and below made up 79% of wafer revenue, with 2-nanometre already at 8% [1].

The guidance points up again: fourth-quarter revenue of $52.3 to $54.3 billion, with the chief financial officer expecting "the steep ramp-up" of 2-nanometre technology [1].

Set this next to this edition's tech note on CoreWeave. The AI build-out has invoices and it has IOUs. In the week the bond market marked the IOUs down, the biggest invoice in the chain was paid in full, with margin to spare. That is not a contradiction. It is a reminder to ask which end of the chain a company sits on.

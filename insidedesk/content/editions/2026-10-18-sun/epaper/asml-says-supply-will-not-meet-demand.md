ASML reported record third-quarter net sales of €11.7 billion, a gross margin of 57.5% and net income of €5.0 billion [1]. It expects fourth-quarter sales of €14.2 to €15.2 billion and full-year sales of €44 to €45 billion [1].

The sentence that matters is about capacity. Customers keep raising their equipment forecasts, and "some customers are expressing an interest in placing orders with us for 2029 and even 2030," chief executive Christophe Fouquet said [1]. ASML now expects that its supply "as currently planned for both 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet the demand", and is looking at adding capacity for 2028 beyond the 30% increase it had already announced [1].

A shortage of machines is a European bottleneck in an American and Asian boom. It is also a natural brake: no one can build the AI capacity faster than lithography tools ship.

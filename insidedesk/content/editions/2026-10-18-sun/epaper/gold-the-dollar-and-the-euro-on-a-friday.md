The dollar index fell 0.26% on Friday and was on track for its first weekly decline after four weeks of gains, as lower Treasury yields made it less attractive. The euro rose 0.16% to $1.1238, up from its recent 17-month low. Gold added 0.07% to $4,167.14 an ounce [1]. A week of fear about AI debt ended with money in bonds and bullion, not in the dollar.

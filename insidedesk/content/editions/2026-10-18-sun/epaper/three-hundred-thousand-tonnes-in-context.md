President Trump said Russia would supply 300,000 metric tons of diesel to the world market [1]. EU Commissioner Valdis Dombrovskis said the volumes are "not going to have any macroeconomic significance" [1]. The IEA said Russia's real limit is refinery capacity damaged by drones, not export rules [2]. Desk arithmetic: at about 7.45 barrels a tonne, that is roughly 2.2 million barrels, about three days of September's 700,000-barrel-a-day fall in global diesel exports [2].

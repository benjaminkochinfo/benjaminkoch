INTERPOL and the WHO signed a cooperation agreement on 16 October. It covers falsified medicines, vaccines and devices, counterfeit protective equipment, organ trafficking, unusual biological events and health risks at mass gatherings [1]. "Health security is global security," said WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus [1]. A fake vaccine is a crime and a health threat at once, and now both agencies work it together.

At its September meeting the Bank of England's Monetary Policy Committee voted 6–3 to keep Bank Rate at 3.75%; the three dissenters wanted 4% [1]. UK CPI inflation had risen to 3.1% in August and is "likely to rise further over coming quarters", the Committee said. It judged the risks to the inflation outlook "tilted to the upside" [1]. The next decision is due on 5 November [1].

The Office for National Statistics publishes September's inflation figure on 21 October at 07:00 UK time, 08:00 in Berlin [2].

A 6–3 vote is a committee with its hand on the door. The question for Wednesday is not whether inflation went up; the Bank already expects that. It is whether anything outside energy moved with it.

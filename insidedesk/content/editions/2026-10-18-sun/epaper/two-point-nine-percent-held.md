Germany's GKV estimating group expects the average extra contribution rate to stay at 2.9% next year. Spending growth should fall below 3%, after nearly 7% this year, the health ministry says [1]. The ministry announces the official average by 1 November [1]. Each insurer then sets its own rate, so the letter from your Krankenkasse in December is the number that counts.

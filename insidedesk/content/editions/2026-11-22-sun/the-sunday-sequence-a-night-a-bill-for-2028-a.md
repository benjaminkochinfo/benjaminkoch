The Bundestag had a week without plenary sittings [6], which is exactly why the sequence matters: everything that happened, happened in rooms with fewer cameras. Here it is in five steps.

**1. The night.** The coalition committee of CDU, CSU and SPD met on Tuesday and agreed in the early hours of Wednesday to cut the electricity tax for all consumers and companies, but only for 2027 [2]. Until now the lower rate applied only to manufacturing, agriculture and forestry [3]. The missing €5.4 billion is to come from projected room on the EEG account; how the years after are paid for is open [2]. The SPD had wanted more than a one-year financing, ARD reported, because the finance minister would otherwise stand alone when the money for 2028 has to be found [1].

**2. The bill for 2028.** Within hours the Union's parliamentary leader Thorsten Frei put a number on that worry: a funding gap of €25 billion for 2028 as things stand, €5 billion larger because of the tax cut [2]. Desk arithmetic: €30 billion. "The challenges are enormous," he said, adding that the coalition could not move "in small steps of 100 million euros" and would want 4% annual growth to pay for every wish [2]. The federal finance plan, as cited by ARD, puts the 2028 hole at around €22 billion and €48 billion by 2030 [1]. Two numbers for the same year are not a scandal; they are a reminder that a gap is an estimate until the budget law says otherwise.

**3. What did not happen.** There was no agreement on offsetting cold progression, so a pay rise that only matches inflation can still leave less in real terms [1]. First estimates put household savings from the tax cut at roughly €3 to €8 a month, while statutory patients will pay €5 to €7.50 more per prescription in 2027 [1].

**4. The Länder's Friday.** The Bundesrat approved the emergency-care reform and active cyber-defence powers for the security authorities, and sent the start-chances law for early childhood to the mediation committee because the Länder say the Bund sets new Kita standards without funding them [4].

**5. The week ahead.** The budget committee is expected to meet in special session on Tuesday evening or Wednesday morning to re-open a budget it has already approved, and the Bundestag is due to pass the 2027 budget on 27 November [2][3]. The same sitting week opens with the first reading of the care-insurance reform [5].

**For you.** Ignore the choreography of laptop bags and late nights. The documents that will decide your electricity bill and your payslip are the budget committee's amendment next week and the 2028 budget the Union wants to start before Christmas [2].

Germany's gas storage stands at 56.73% full in the latest reading, 0.25 points down on the day before, against a European average of 69.36%, the Allgäuer Zeitung's tracker reports [1]. On 1 November the German figure was 61.17%; a year earlier, on 1 November 2025, it was 75.00% [1]. Desk arithmetic: that is 4.44 points drawn in three weeks, and a gap of 12.63 points to the European average. The Bundesnetzagentur said in mid-October that supply was "stable", because pipelines from Norway and LNG terminals carry much of the load [1]. Storage is a buffer, not the whole supply chain. Nothing here is investment advice.

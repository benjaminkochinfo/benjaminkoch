Saturday was a day of delegate arithmetic. In Pfiffelbach, Björn Höcke was re-elected leader of Thuringia's AfD with 314 yes votes, 39 no and five abstentions, which the party put at 88.95% because abstentions are not counted [1]. The state branch is classified as confirmed right-wing extremist by Thuringia's domestic intelligence service, which Höcke called a "political instrument of struggle" [1][2]. In Ilmenau, the SPD elected Thuringia's social affairs minister, Schenk, to succeed the outgoing leader Maier, who did not stand again [2]. In Bad Oldesloe, Nicole Baer won the AfD's top list place for Schleswig-Holstein's April election in a run-off with 166 of 331 delegate votes against 158 for her rival [1].

Desk arithmetic: Baer's margin was eight votes. Conventions are where parties count themselves before voters do.

The EMA's human medicines committee recommended a new 100 mg strength of the dapivirine vaginal ring, which reduces the risk of HIV-1 infection through vaginal intercourse in women aged 16 and over [1]. The new strength lets the ring be replaced every three months instead of every month [1]. Desk arithmetic: that is four rings a year instead of twelve, or eight fewer ring changes a year for each woman. The opinion came under EU-Medicines for all, a procedure for medicines meant for use outside the EU, with experts from the WHO and regulators from Kenya, South Africa and Zambia taking part [1]. Fewer appointments is not a small thing; it is often the difference between a prevention plan and a prevention habit.

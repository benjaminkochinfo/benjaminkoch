A tanker sailing outbound through the Strait of Hormuz was hit on its stern by an unidentified projectile about 11 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, the UK Maritime Trade Operations centre said on Saturday [1]. It later reported a tanker hit in the engine room 17 nautical miles east of Khasab; whether both reports concern the same ship was unclear [1]. No casualties were reported; the environmental impact was unknown [1]. Iran's navy said a tanker caught fire after striking a mine in a restricted area east of the strait, an account Reuters could not verify [1]. A tanker near Khasab had already caught fire after an attack on Friday [1].

The strait carried nearly a fifth of global crude supplies before the Iran war [1]. Each incident report is a line in an insurer's ledger before it is a line in the news.

Of the 882 US-listed companies Morningstar covers, 824, or 93%, fell in the week to Friday; 58 rose [1]. Its US total market index lost 8.51% and is still up 4.96% for the year [1]. The fall had a shape. Growth stocks lost 10.06%, value stocks 6.75%; large caps 8.67%, small caps 7.24% [1]. Technology fell 11.98%, consumer defensives 2.03% and utilities 3.4% [1]. When nearly everything falls, look at what fell least: the companies selling toothpaste and electricity, whose invoices get paid in any weather. Nothing here is investment advice.

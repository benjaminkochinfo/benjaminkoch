Anthropic has closed its listing: 1,127,000,000 shares at $83.00, gross proceeds of about $93.5 billion, of which about $91.6 billion went to the company [1]. Alongside it, a private placement of Class N shares to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC Partners raised about $26.0 billion [1].

Desk arithmetic: roughly 22% of the new money Anthropic took in came from seven chip, memory and cloud companies rather than from the public offer [1].

That is the "circular" financing NPR's week-in-review flagged as a worry: Nvidia, it noted, owns shares in AI companies such as Anthropic and lends to them, while they buy its chips [2]. The shares rose 17% on debut and ended Friday near the issue price [2]. The invoice to watch is not the share price. It is what Anthropic spends with those seven names in the quarters ahead, line by line in its filings. Nothing here is investment advice.

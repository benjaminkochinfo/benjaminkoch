Polar maritime air has flowed into southern Germany from the north, the Deutscher Wetterdienst wrote in its Baden-Württemberg forecast at 07:00 CET on Sunday [1]. During Sunday it expects 1 to 5 cm of fresh snow in the Black Forest above 1,000 metres and on the Swabian Alb plateau above 600 metres [1]. Overnight into Monday the snow line in the Black Forest drops towards 600 metres, with 1 to 3 cm, locally 5 cm, also in Upper Swabia; with more snow on Monday in the southern Black Forest, totals on the Alb above 1,000 metres reach up to 15 cm [1]. Light to moderate frost is forecast everywhere in the night to Tuesday, with ice from snow or refreezing wet roads in the hills [1]. Check the tyres before you check the sky.

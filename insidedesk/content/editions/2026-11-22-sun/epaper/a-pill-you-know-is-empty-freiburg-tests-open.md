The psychosomatic clinic at Freiburg University Hospital wants to know whether a placebo can help when people know it contains no active ingredient [1]. Participants are adults under psychological strain who have sought professional help or an outpatient therapy place in the past six months and have not started therapy, or will not for at least four weeks [1]. One group takes open placebos, edible wafers of potato starch, water, oil and food colouring in seven named variants, for four weeks; the other gets them after a four-week wait [1]. Results come from online questionnaires [1]. "The search for a psychotherapy place can be very challenging," says project lead Stefan Schmidt [1].

No deception, no promise: a modest experiment aimed at the waiting room the system has not yet emptied.

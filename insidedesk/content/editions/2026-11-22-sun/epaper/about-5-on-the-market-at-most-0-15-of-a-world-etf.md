The week's record listing raised $107.3 billion; the shares were issued at $83, opened at $111.50 and left regular US trading on the first day at $97.17 [1]. If you hold a world-equity ETF, Stiftung Warentest's reading is calmer than the adverts that urged you to "be part of the IPO" [1]. Index weights count mainly the freely traded shares, and only about 5% of Anthropic's are; Vanguard estimates the company will make up at most 0.15% of its world-equity ETFs [1]. SpaceX, listed in June, was under 0.1% of the MSCI World on 13 November; MSCI's fast-entry rule could add Anthropic on 1 December at the earliest [1]. Desk arithmetic: on a €100 monthly savings plan, 0.15% is 15 cents. Nothing here is investment advice.

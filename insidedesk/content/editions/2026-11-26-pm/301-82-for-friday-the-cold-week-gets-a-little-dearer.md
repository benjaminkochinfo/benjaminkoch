Friday's day-ahead auction for Germany and Luxembourg averages €301.82 per megawatt hour, up from €291.77 for Thursday, according to Energy-Charts data from Bundesnetzagentur and SMARD [1]. Friday's quarter-hours range from €203.35 at 23:45 to €426.32 at 08:15 [1]. It is the highest daily average since 11 November.

[[chart:de-power-day-ahead-26nov-pm]]

The cause is unremarkable: high pressure and cold air over much of Germany, with light to moderate frost and persistent frost in the east and south on Friday, the German weather service reported [3].

Oil moved for other reasons. Brent was up $1.64, or 1.73%, at $96.25 a barrel on Thursday afternoon after Iran said mines struck two tankers in the Strait of Hormuz and the Houthis claimed an attack on Ras Tanura [2]. PVM's Tamas Varga sees a new range of around $88–97 for Brent [2]. WTI will have no settlement because of Thanksgiving [2]. The power price has a meteorological explanation; the oil price, for now, has a rumour and a range.

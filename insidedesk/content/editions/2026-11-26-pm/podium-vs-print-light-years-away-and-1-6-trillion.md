**The podium.** 'We are light years away from a solution to this problem,' Giorgia Meloni said in Brussels on Thursday [1]. Hungary's Péter Magyar put the chance of a deal at the summit at 'pretty much zero' and said a Christmas deal 'doesn't seem likely either' [1]. European Parliament president Roberta Metsola said MEPs would not rubber-stamp a deal [2].

**The print.** António Costa's draft is a seven-year budget of almost €1.6 trillion [1]. Seven 'frugals' — Austria, Belgium, Germany, Denmark, Finland, the Netherlands and Sweden — want it trimmed; 17 'Friends of Cohesion' reject further cuts [1]. Beside it sits the Commission's €45 billion loan for Ukraine, financed through joint debt, from which three countries want to opt out [1]. Friedrich Merz warned Germany could block the budget if countries pulled out of supporting Ukraine [1].

**The gap.** The speeches describe a chasm. The papers describe a negotiation over a number with a known size and two clear camps. Costa broke the summit into small groups, starting with the frugals [1]. That is what closing a gap looks like: dull, in rooms, line by line.

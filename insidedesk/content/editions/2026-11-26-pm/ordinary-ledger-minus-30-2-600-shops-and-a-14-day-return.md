**−30.2.** The NIM consumer climate, powered by GfK, rose 0.7 points to −30.2 for December, from a revised −30.9 [1]. Income expectations rose 1.8 points to −13.9, but are almost 14 points lower than a year ago; the propensity to buy edged up to −11.5 [1]. 'Für das bevorstehende Weihnachtsgeschäft sind das keine guten Vorzeichen,' said NIM's Rolf Bürkl: not good signs for Christmas trade [1]. Price expectations rose to 2.8, despite the fuel rebate [1].

**600 shops.** UK discounter Poundland went into administration on Wednesday, putting about 11,000 jobs and 600 stores at risk [2]. Its owner said that even after £100 million of cost savings the business remained 'vulnerable to inflationary pressures' [2]. The shops keep trading for now [2].

**14 days.** Temu committed to EU consumer authorities to make withdrawals, returns and refunds simpler, to show prices more clearly and to drop messages pressing shoppers to buy, the Commission said [3]. National authorities will monitor compliance [3]. On the eve of Black Friday, that is the useful entry: a right you can use, not a discount you are told to chase.

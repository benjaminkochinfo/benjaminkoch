Frankfurt's buyers went for the recently battered technology names, Infineon and Siemens Energy among them, wallstreet:online reported [2]. 'Griffen Anleger am Montag noch wegen der Rückkehr von Jensen Huang zu, kaufen sie jetzt wegen des Preises,' chief analyst Jochen Stanzl told Handelsblatt [1]. The real question, he said, remains whether the billions invested in AI pay off, especially if rates rise and a growing share is financed on credit [1].

Buying on price is the healthier motive. It means someone did the arithmetic instead of listening to a keynote.

This is reporting, not investment advice.

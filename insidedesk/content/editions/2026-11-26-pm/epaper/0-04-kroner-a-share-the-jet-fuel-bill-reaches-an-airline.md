Norwegian long-haul carrier Norse Atlantic cancelled all of Thursday's flights and said its board would file for bankruptcy unless short-term financing was secured urgently [1]. At 0.04 kroner a share it was worth about $9.7 million [1]. Euronews links the crisis to surging jet-fuel prices since the war in the Middle East [1]. Spain's Plus Ultra stopped flying on Tuesday, and airBaltic filed for Chapter 11 in September [1].

A war far away arrives as a fuel invoice. Airlines with thin margins pay it first, and passengers with booked tickets find out at the gate.

Besides the presidency, eurozone governments discussed two Executive Board seats on Thursday, Euronews reported [1]. France's Laurence Boone is expected to take responsibility for market operations from Isabel Schnabel, who leaves on 3 January 2027 for the IMF [1]. Germany's Markus Brunnermeier is in line to replace chief economist Philip Lane, whose term ends on 31 May 2027 [1].

In October it was Lane who proposed keeping rates unchanged [2]. A president chairs the committee. The chief economist writes the first draft of its thinking.

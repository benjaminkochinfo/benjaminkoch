Germany's Federal Office of Civil Protection and Disaster Assistance recommends buying food that people regularly eat and using up older supplies first, and storing non-perishables airtight in a cool, dry, dark place, Euronews reports [1]. The UK's Be Ready campaign suggests building up supplies over several shopping trips and checking expiry dates every six months [1]. In the EU, more than half of the nearly 58 million tonnes of food waste in 2024 came from households [1]. A reserve you rotate is preparedness. A reserve you forget is waste.

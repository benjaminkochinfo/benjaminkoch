ECDC estimates that infections with antibiotic-resistant bacteria cause more than 35,000 deaths a year in the EU/EEA, the OECD and Commission report notes [1]. OECD modelling puts the combined cost of resistance, from health spending to lost work, at an average of 11.7 billion euros (in purchasing-power terms) a year across the EU to 2050 [1]. Its policy packages, it says, generate savings that exceed their costs [1]. Prevention rarely gets a ribbon-cutting. It gets a smaller bill.

In Isabel Schnabel's market briefing to the October meeting, inflation fixings for 2026 and 2027 had shifted higher, peaking at 4.5% for January 2027 and staying above 2.5% until the first quarter of 2028, the account shows [1]. Market pricing pointed to a deposit-rate peak around 3% [1]. Diesel crack spreads stood above their levels after Russia's 2022 invasion [1]. The account is a list of prices the committee looked at.

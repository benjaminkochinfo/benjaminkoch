Commenting on Ukraine's attack on Russian ships in Libya, Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul said Germany supports Ukraine and its right to self-defence, but 'I strongly warn against allowing this conflict to spread geographically', France 24 reported, citing Reuters [1]. He spoke at a press conference in Berlin with his Romanian counterpart [1]. Support and restraint in the same sentence is not a contradiction. It is the job description of an ally.

High pressure and cold air dominate, with the remnants of an Italian low bringing snow to the south-east, the German weather service said [1]. Thursday brought 3 to 10 cm at the Alpine edge, up to 15 cm in places, easing by evening [1]. Overnight, light snow is expected from Saxony to southern Brandenburg and Thuringia, often little, 1 to 3 cm in eastern Saxony; there is light to moderate frost widely, and persistent frost in the east and south on Friday [1]. Black ice from re-freezing is possible in places [1]. Leave five minutes earlier; the gritting lorries have a schedule too.

A new fire broke out at the Kuibyshev refinery in Samara on Thursday morning, a day after it was struck, and Ukrainian forces hit the Subkhankulovo station, a hub of the Transneft trunk pipeline system in Bashkortostan [1]. A drone attack also caused a fire with serious damage at the Slavyansk ECO refinery in Krasnodar region, Reuters reported [2]. Pipelines and refineries are the war's quiet targets: each one changes what Russia can sell abroad.

The Bundestag approved the interior ministry's budget for 2027 on Thursday, BR24 reported, about one billion euros more than this year [2]. Presseonline put the total at €16.8 billion, with more for the federal police, the Federal Criminal Police Office and the domestic intelligence service [1]. Minister Alexander Dobrindt said the budget repositions Germany on security, with reforms of the intelligence service, the federal police and cyber defence [2]. The justice, economy, housing, agriculture and family budgets followed during the day; the whole federal budget is due for its final vote on Friday [1].

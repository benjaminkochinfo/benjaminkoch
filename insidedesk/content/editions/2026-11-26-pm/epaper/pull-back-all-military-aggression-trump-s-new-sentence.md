After talks with Nato secretary general Mark Rutte, Donald Trump wrote that in his discussions with Vladimir Putin in China he had 'spent a lot of time talking about the fact that I would like to see president Putin pull back all military aggression against territories throughout Europe', The Guardian reported [1]. What 'territories throughout Europe' means was left undefined, the blog noted, after incidents involving Russia in Estonia, Denmark and Poland [1]. A sentence is not a policy. The test is what follows it.

In a separate announcement, the US Trade Representative also proposed additional tariffs on goods from Brazil, including aircraft, over alleged inaction on US trade concerns, France 24 reported [1]. The Brazilian president's office called the measures 'unacceptable' and said Brasília would 'not yield to this type of threat' [1]. Two files in one week: the overcapacity probe for 14 economies, and a single-country case for the 15th.

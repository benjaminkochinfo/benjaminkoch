'No, I do not believe that we should "surrender human free will" to AI systems, and it's absurd to suggest that I do,' Anthropic's Dario Amodei wrote on Wednesday, after an Axios report [1]. 'We should never sacrifice legitimacy or self-determination at the altar of efficiency or security,' he added [1]. Euronews notes he had earlier said that in 'life-critical, safety-critical cases, we really want to turn it over' [1]. The useful test of such words is not the post. It is which decisions the products actually take away from you.

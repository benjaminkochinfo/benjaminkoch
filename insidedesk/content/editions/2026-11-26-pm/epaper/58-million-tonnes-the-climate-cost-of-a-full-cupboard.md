Food waste already accounts for up to 10% of global emissions, and food loss and waste generate nearly five times the emissions of aviation, Euronews reports [1]. The EU generated nearly 58 million tonnes of food waste in 2024, more than half from households [1]. Experts quoted warn that excessive stockpiling adds waste, shortages and price rises [1]. The most effective climate measure in many kitchens is a pen and a date on a tin.

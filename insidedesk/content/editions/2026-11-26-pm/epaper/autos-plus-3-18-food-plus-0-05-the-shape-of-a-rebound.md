Among the Euro Stoxx sector indexes, autos and parts rose 3.18%, technology 3.06%, basic resources 3.00% and banks 2.49% on Thursday, Boursorama's figures after the close show [1]. Food and beverages gained 0.05% [1]. Andreas Lipkow wrote that investors focused on the tech and car shares that had come under heavy pressure recently [2].

A rebound led by the most-sold sectors is a relief rally, not yet a change of view. The quiet number is food's 0.05%: nobody needed to buy the shelter back, because nobody had run from it.

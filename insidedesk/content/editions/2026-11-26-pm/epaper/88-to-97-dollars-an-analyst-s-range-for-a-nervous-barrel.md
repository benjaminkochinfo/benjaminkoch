Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two tankers exploded and caught fire after hitting sea mines north of Oman's Khasab port; one was a supertanker carrying more than two million barrels, according to the Guards [1]. Brent rose to $96.25 [1]. PVM's Tamas Varga said a perceived supply surplus and sticky inflation, which forces central banks to raise rates, could 'set a ceiling' on prices [1]. OPEC+ ministers meet on 29 November and may roll over current targets for the first quarter of 2027, two sources told Reuters [1].

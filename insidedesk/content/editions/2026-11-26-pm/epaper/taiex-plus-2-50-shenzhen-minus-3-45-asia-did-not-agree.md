Boursorama's closing levels show an Asia that read Wednesday's late Wall Street rebound in different ways [1]. Taiwan's Taiex gained 2.50% to 39,151.81, Hong Kong's Hang Seng 1.19% and Tokyo's Nikkei 225 0.86% [1]. The Shanghai Composite was flat, up 0.01%, while the Shenzhen Composite fell 3.45% [1]. One region, one night, three verdicts. That is what a market is supposed to look like.

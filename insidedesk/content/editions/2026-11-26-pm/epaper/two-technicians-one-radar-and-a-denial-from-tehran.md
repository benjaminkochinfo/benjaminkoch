Two technicians from Rheinmetall Italia were killed on Thursday in a drone attack in Kuwait while maintaining the detection antennas of the Skyguard air-defence system used by Kuwait's armed forces, Italy's foreign ministry said [1]. Kuwait's defence ministry said a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani national were injured at what it called a vital site [3]. Kuwait and Italy blame Iran; Tehran denies it, and Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi called the incident 'suspicious' and offered to work with both countries to determine the cause [2].

Giorgia Meloni said she learned of the deaths during the European Council meeting in Brussels [3]. Ursula von der Leyen said security in the Gulf 'is a security issue for us all' [2].

The men were not soldiers. They were doing maintenance, the dull work that keeps a radar honest. Wars reach the people who service the machinery long before they reach the communiqués.

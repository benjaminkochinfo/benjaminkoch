The NIM saving indicator fell 1.6 points to 15.6, its second decline in a row, which helped lift the consumer climate slightly [1]. Economic expectations, after six increases, fell 3.7 points to −1.6 [1]. The survey ran from 28 October to 9 November with about 2,000 interviews [1]. Households saving a little less while expecting a little worse is not a contradiction. It is what happens when prices have already eaten the cushion.

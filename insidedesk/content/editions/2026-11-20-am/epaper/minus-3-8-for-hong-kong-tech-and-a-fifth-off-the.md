Hong Kong's Hang Seng ended Friday 1.9% lower at 22,410, while its tech index fell 3.8% to 3,511 [1]. Moneycontrol reported that the intraday low took the index's decline from its 29 January peak to about 20%, the threshold commonly used to define a bear market [2]. The AI model developer Minimax fell 10.8% to a record low, bringing its weekly loss above 30%, and Z.AI lost 7.2% on the day [1]. Shanghai and the CSI300 posted their lowest closes since July 2025 [1].

A label like "bear market" is a convention, not a cause. The causes RTHK lists are concrete: stretched valuations, heavy capital spending on computing, and tighter US chip rules [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

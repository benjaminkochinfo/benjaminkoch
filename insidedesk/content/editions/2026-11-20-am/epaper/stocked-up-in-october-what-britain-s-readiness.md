Supermarkets in Great Britain reported an October uplift in emergency items such as UHT milk and tinned goods after the government's "Be Ready" campaign launched on 22 October, the Office for National Statistics said on Friday [1]. Food-store volumes rose as a result, and total retail volumes were up 0.7% on the month [1].

A readiness cupboard is the most ordinary form of insurance: a few days of food that keeps, water, batteries, the medicines you need. Bought once, rotated slowly, it is calm rather than panic. The ONS figure suggests many households treated it that way: a bump in one month, not a run.

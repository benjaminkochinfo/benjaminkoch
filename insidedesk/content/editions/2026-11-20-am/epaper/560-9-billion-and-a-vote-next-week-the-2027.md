The Bundestag is due to vote on the 2027 federal budget in its sitting week of 24 to 27 November, tagesschau reports [1]. After a session of more than 15 hours, the budget committee approved spending of about €560.9 billion, more than ever before, and net borrowing in the core budget of almost €123 billion, about €4 billion more than in the government's first draft [1]. With credit-financed investment from the special funds for infrastructure and the Bundeswehr, total new debt is likely to exceed €200 billion [1].

The headline is spending; the line to keep is borrowing. Spending can be cut next year. Interest on this year's borrowing cannot.

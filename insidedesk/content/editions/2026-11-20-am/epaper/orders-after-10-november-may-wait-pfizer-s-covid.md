Pfizer is experiencing a temporary supply shortage of its Covid-19 vaccine for people aged 12 and over because of higher-than-expected demand, North Carolina's immunisation programme told providers [1]. Orders placed on or after 10 November may be delayed for the next few weeks, affecting both public and private orders, and Pfizer expects to start filling delayed orders as more vaccine becomes available by the end of November [1]. The vaccine for children aged 5 to 11 is not affected [1].

Moderna's and Sanofi's vaccines for over-12s remain available without shipping delays [1]. Shortages are rarely solved by speeches; they are solved by having more than one factory on the order form.

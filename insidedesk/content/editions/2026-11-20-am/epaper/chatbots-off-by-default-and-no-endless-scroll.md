The European Commission's draft EU KIDS ACT would ban online services open to under-18s from using addictive, profiling-based recommendations, endless scrolling without a manual click, deceptive rewards, push notifications at night and unsolicited contact from strangers [1]. AI companions and chatbots would have to be switched off by default and must not simulate relationships in ways that create emotional dependence [1]. Large platforms would have to prove their services are "inherently safe" [1].

The Bundesrat's committees recommended welcoming the proposal, while asking to exempt operating-system providers and to scale duties for small forums and communities [1]. The design choices named in the draft are the useful part: they are things a parent can already check on a phone today.

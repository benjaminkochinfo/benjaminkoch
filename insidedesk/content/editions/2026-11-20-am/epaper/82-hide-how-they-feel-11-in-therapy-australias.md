More than 82% of Australians hide how they really feel from loved ones, according to research released by the insurer Bupa [1]. Anxiety (24%), insecurity (21%) and loneliness (21%) were among the hardest feelings to talk about [1]. While 85% believe talking therapy could be useful, only 11% are currently in therapy or receiving mental-health support, and 55% would be reluctant to speak to a therapist [1]. For 28% of those who have never tried it, a clearer idea of what happens in a session would make them more likely to consider it [1].

It is a company survey launched with a film series, so read it as a signal, not a census [1]. The useful finding is the last one: sometimes the barrier is not stigma but a missing description.

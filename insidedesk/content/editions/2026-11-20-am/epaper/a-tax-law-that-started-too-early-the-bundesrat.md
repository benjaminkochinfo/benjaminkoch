The Bundesrat on Friday approved changes to the 2026 fiscal equalisation law that also repair the recently passed tobacco tax law [1]. That law had unintentionally specified that its rules take effect the day after publication [1]. The correction restores the previous tax rates retroactively for 21 October to 31 December 2026; the increases now apply from 1 January 2027, and for cigars and cigarillos from 1 March 2027 [1].

The same law moves money between Berlin and the Länder. The federal government gets €250 million more from the fixed VAT amount after settling earlier refugee-related aid, and its VAT share rises by €625 million in each of 2026 and 2027 at the Länder's expense, which is how the Länder contribute to the fuel discount passed in September [1]. In return the Länder receive €750 million for the pact for the public health service [1].

A mistake caught, fixed and paid for in the open is not a scandal. It is the system working on a Friday morning.

Nvidia filed its quarterly 13F with the SEC at about 23:40 CET on Monday, the list of US-listed shares it held on 30 September [1]. It shows a company quietly trimming the stakes that made headlines when it bought them.

The Intel holding fell from 214,776,632 shares to 161,082,488, a cut of 53.7 million shares or a quarter [1]. The June count matched the stake Nvidia agreed in September 2025, a $5 billion purchase at $23.28 a share alongside joint product development [1]. Desk arithmetic: the remaining shares cost about $3.75 billion at that price and were valued at $19.37 billion on 30 September, roughly 5.2 times as much [1].

Nokia went further. Nvidia's 166,389,351 American depositary shares, issued to it in its $1 billion investment of October 2025, fell to 72,859,751, a 56% cut, worth $739 million at quarter-end [1]. The Synopsys stake fell 10% [1]. In the other direction, Nvidia added 7.7 million SpaceX Class A shares, a 6% increase, making SpaceX its largest line at $19.68 billion, 40.7% of a reported portfolio of $48.33 billion [1].

The market read it on Tuesday morning: Intel was more than 3% lower before the New York open and SpaceX 1.1% higher [2]. But read the form for what it is. A 13F gives no sale prices, no trade dates and no reasons, and Nvidia has not commented [1]. Partnership stakes can be sold while partnerships continue; you cannot tell from this filing which happened.

What it does show is that the paperwork arrived before the speech. Whatever is said on tonight's call, this cupboard was already photographed. Nothing here is investment advice.

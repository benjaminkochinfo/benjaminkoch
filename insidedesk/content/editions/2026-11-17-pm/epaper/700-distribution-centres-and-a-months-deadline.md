France's foreign minister Jean-Noël Barrot told EU colleagues he had brought evidence on those mainly responsible for a cyber attack, sponsored by Iran's Revolutionary Guards, on 700 supermarket distribution centres [1]. He asked for European sanctions against them "within a month" and said he had the support of "the vast majority" of member states, hoping for adoption at the next foreign ministers' meeting on 14 December [1]. The remarks, made in Brussels, were published on Tuesday [1].

For you: an attack on distribution centres is an attack on the dull machinery that fills shelves. The answer chosen is equally dull, and lasting: names, evidence and a date.

Nvidia has told customers that, for now, it will not sell its chips to those who rent more than 50% of their GPUs to Chinese companies, CNBC reported on Tuesday, citing two people with direct knowledge [1]. The company also warned buyers that the proposed Remote Access Security Act could expose both them and Nvidia to US "legal action" by treating cloud access like physical exports [1]. That bill passed the House of Representatives in January but not yet the Senate [1]. Nvidia did not respond to CNBC's request for comment [1]. The point for you is where control now sits: not only in a customs declaration but in a sales contract's customer clause, checked by the seller before the law obliges it to.

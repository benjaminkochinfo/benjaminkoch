At 21:22 CET, about 40 minutes before the New York close, the Dow Jones Industrial Average was down 1.76% at 50,698.54, the S&P 500 down 1.32% at 7,318.67 and the Nasdaq Composite down 1.19% at 24,216.97 [1]. MSCI's global index was 1.40% lower and Europe's FTSEurofirst 300 had closed down 1.78% [1]. These are snapshot figures; the settled 22:00 CET close had not been published when this went to press.

The selling was wide rather than deep in one place. The fall "is a reflection of overall investor nervousness", said Oliver Pursche of Wealthspire Advisors [1]. The dollar index eased 0.11% to 103.47, still near a 19-month high, with the euro at $1.1009; Brent was up 0.44% at $92.85 a barrel and spot gold down 0.21% at $3,848.13 an ounce [1]. Traders still price a Federal Reserve rate rise next month [1].

Nvidia reports after the bell, its first set of results since its chief executive resigned last week [1]. Nothing about them is known here. Two days of markets have priced the waiting; tomorrow's edition reads the invoice. Not investment advice.

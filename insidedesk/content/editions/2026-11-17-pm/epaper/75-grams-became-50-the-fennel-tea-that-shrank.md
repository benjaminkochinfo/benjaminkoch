Verbraucherzentrale Hamburg's "Mogelpackung des Monats" this time is Edeka's Gut & Günstig "Würziger Fenchel" tea [1]. The box still holds 25 bags, but each now contains 2 grams instead of 3, so the pack shrinks from 75 to 50 grams [1]. In many shops the price stayed at €1.19 or €1.29, which the consumer centre calculates as a hidden price rise of 50% per gram [1]. Where a shop cut the price from €1.39 to €1.29, it is still 39% dearer by weight [1].

The box, meanwhile, grew by about 75%, because each bag is now wrapped in paper [1]. Edeka cites "optimised product safety" and higher costs [1]. The defence for you is printed on the shelf label: the price per kilo.

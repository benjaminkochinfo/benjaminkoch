If you are planning panels on your roof, the most useful sentence of the week comes from a letter, not a law. SPD energy spokesperson Nina Scheer wrote to her MPs that installations of up to 25 kilowatts would keep a guaranteed feed-in payment under the reform of the Renewable Energy Act [1]. Grid operators could market the power of systems between 26 and 50 kW at the annual market value, and the maximum support for new solar would rise from 5.9 to 6.3 cents per kilowatt hour [1].

The CDU's Sepp Müller said compromises on parts depend on agreement on "the whole package" [1]. More than 20 economists warned in the FAZ that the solar and biogas support could add over €50 billion in costs [1]. The SPD aims to finish before the Bundesrat sits on 18 December [1]. Until then, a letter is a plan; the gazette is the invoice.

Indonesia's National Disaster Mitigation Agency, BNPB, said on Tuesday that about 10,000 families had been affected by flooding in Aceh as of Monday, especially in Aceh Tamiang and Aceh Singkil [1]. Heavy rain with strong winds is expected until 19 November in Southwest Aceh, South Aceh, Aceh Singkil and Subulussalam City [1].

The agency's explanation is a lesson in how weather connects: a cyclone in the Bay of Bengal moving away from Indonesia, a disturbance in the Indian Ocean west of Sumatra, and warm sea surface temperatures that add moisture, together raising the rain risk [1]. Its advice is the dull, useful kind: rain comes suddenly in the late afternoon and evening, so stay away from river and lake banks and follow the official weather service [1].

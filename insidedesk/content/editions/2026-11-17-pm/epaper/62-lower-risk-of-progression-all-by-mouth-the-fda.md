The US Food and Drug Administration approved Wilserdly (giredestrant) with everolimus on Monday for adults with ER-positive, HER2-negative, ESR1-mutated advanced breast cancer whose disease has come back or progressed on hormone therapy [1]. ESR1 mutations drive resistance in up to 40% of patients with hormone receptor-positive metastatic disease, the company says [1].

In the Phase III evERA trial, published in the New England Journal of Medicine, the combination cut the risk of progression or death by 62% against standard hormone therapy plus everolimus (hazard ratio 0.38) [1]. That is progression-free survival, the company's own release; read it as more time before the disease advances, not yet as longer life. The FDA is also reviewing the drug for early-stage use, with a decision expected by 30 November [1]. The practical gain is a regimen taken at home, without injections [1].

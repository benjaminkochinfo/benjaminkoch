Crude stocks in the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve fell to 276.4 million barrels last week, the lowest level since August 1982, Department of Energy data showed [1]. The drawdowns are part of a US agreement to release 172 million barrels from the reserve [1].

The timing matters. Oil is cheaper, Brent stood at $92.76 at 13:04 CET, down 21% this month, as Gulf exports recover [1]. OPEC+ meets on 29 November to set 2027 output policy, after an online OPEC meeting on Monday reviewed members' maximum sustainable capacity [1].

For you: an emergency reserve is an insurance policy, and this one has been partly cashed. Prices falling does not refill it; only purchases do. Nothing here is investment advice.

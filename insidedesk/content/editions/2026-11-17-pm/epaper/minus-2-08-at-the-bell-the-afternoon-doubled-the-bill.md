The DAX closed down 2.08% at 26,045.37 points; the MDax lost 1.25% to 30,147.34 [1]. At 15:40 CET, when our Markets note was written, it had been down 1.17% at 26,286.89, so the last stretch of trading took another 241.52 points (Desk arithmetic) [1]. The Stoxx 600 fell 11.59 points, or 1.75%, to 650.72, its largest one-day percentage fall since 20 March and its lowest close since 6 November [2]. European technology shares fell 3.9%, the steepest drop since 3 February, with ASML down 5.3% [3].

The single names kept their afternoon order. Deutsche Bank ended 6.0% lower after the reports built on leaked files; the bank said the matters lay many years back [1]. SAP lost 5.6%, its lowest level in four weeks [1]. Evonik rose 4.2% after Bloomberg reported that BASF is preparing a better offer [1]. Andreas Lipkow of CMC Markets pointed to a Fed official again stressing the need for further rate rises [1].

Nvidia's numbers come after the US close at 22:00 CET; nothing about them is known here yet. A sell-off before a verdict is a deposit, not a judgement. Not investment advice.

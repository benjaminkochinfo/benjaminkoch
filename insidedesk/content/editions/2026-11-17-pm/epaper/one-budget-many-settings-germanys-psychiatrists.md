Germany's psychiatric society DGPPN on Tuesday published a position paper, "Versorgung gestalten", co-signed by 20 clinic and professional associations and patient and family groups [1]. Its central ask: make global budgets for psychiatric clinics an option in regular care by amending the federal hospital rate ordinance [1]. A fixed budget lets a clinic move patients between ward, day clinic, outpatient and home treatment without the money deciding the setting [1].

People with severe and complex illness often find suitable help late, the society says, because responsibilities and funding are split [1]. Until budgets arrive, it wants looser rules for home-equivalent inpatient treatment, a review of outpatient needs planning and a scientific staffing tool for psychiatric hospitals [1]. Unglamorous, all of it. That is usually where care gets better.

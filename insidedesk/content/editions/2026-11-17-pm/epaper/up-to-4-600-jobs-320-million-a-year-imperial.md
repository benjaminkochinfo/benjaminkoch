Imperial Brands, the Bristol-based owner of Davidoff, John Player Special, Golden Virginia, Rizla and Blu vapes, said on Tuesday it will cut up to 4,600 jobs [1]. About 2,100 roles go this year; a further 2,100 to 2,500 are to follow by the end of 2030, out of a global workforce of about 25,000 [1].

The German line is concrete: the group will close its factory in Langenhagen, near Hanover, next year, and has sold a plant in Taiwan; together these account for 700 job losses [1]. Another 400 finance and procurement jobs in Kraków and Warsaw are to be outsourced to Capgemini [1].

Chief executive Lukas Paravicini called it a "transformation" and said the group is "fusing the organisation with more technologies", including AI, with at least £320 million of annual savings targeted by 2030 [1].

The accounts explain why. Revenue fell 1.5% to £31.7 billion in the year to 30 September and pre-tax profit 12.1% to £2.7 billion [1]. In key markets, prices up 5.1% offset volumes down 3.4% [1]. That is a business harvesting a shrinking crop: the smoker pays more for each pack, and there are fewer packs.

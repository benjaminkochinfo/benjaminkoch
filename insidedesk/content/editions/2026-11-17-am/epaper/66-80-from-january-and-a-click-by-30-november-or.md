The Deutschlandticket costs €66.80 a month from January 2027, up from €63 [1]. Desk arithmetic: €3.80 a month, €45.60 over a year.

**The detail that bites.** For subscriptions taken out with Deutsche Bahn, the price rise needs your active agreement. Without it, the subscription ends automatically at the end of the year, and DB needs the answer by 30 November 2026 at the latest [1]. Only the person who originally took out the subscription can agree, so if a parent or partner pays for your ticket, tell them [1].

**Two ways in.** In DB's subscription portal, log in, open the Deutschlandticket and use "Vertragsänderung zustimmen"; in the DB Navigator app, open the ticket, tap the three-dot menu below it and choose "Vertragsänderungen zustimmen" [1]. Anyone who ordered a new ticket from 15 October 2026 was told about the new price when ordering and needs to do nothing [1].

**Not everywhere the same.** The procedure applies only to DB subscriptions. Many local transport companies do it the other way round: the subscription simply continues and you must object if you do not want to pay more, as with Munich's MVG, according to the report [1]. If you miss the deadline, a cancelled DB subscription can still be reactivated at the new price until 31 December; after that it takes a new contract [1].

**For you.** Thirty seconds in an app now saves a ticket inspector's verdict in January. Check who issued your subscription, then check your inbox for the letter.

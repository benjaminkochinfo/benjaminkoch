Weipa, on Queensland's Cape York peninsula, reached 39.9°C on 10 November, its highest November temperature on record, according to ABC reporting compiled by Orbis Weather [1]. By 14 November the town had logged 39 straight days at or above 36°C [1].

It is not over. The Bureau of Meteorology expects a severe heatwave on Cape York with highs in the high 30s to low 40s through 19 November, and severe storms with hail over 5 cm and gusts above 125 km/h moving north across Queensland from 16 November, according to the same round-up [1].

Nvidia reports at about 22:20 CET. Options traders were pricing a 9.67% move in either direction afterwards, according to TipRanks figures cited by The Economic Times; UBS's Torsten Sippel put the implied move at roughly 7.8% [1][2]. The average absolute move after the last four reports was 4.78%, with reactions of +8.74%, -1.77%, -5.46% and -3.15% [1]. Analysts expect revenue of about $108.73 billion and earnings of $2.47 a share, against $96.22 billion and $2.22 last quarter [1]. It is the first report in 33 years without Jensen Huang, with Colette Kress as interim chief executive [1]. An implied move is a price for uncertainty, not a forecast of direction. Not investment advice.

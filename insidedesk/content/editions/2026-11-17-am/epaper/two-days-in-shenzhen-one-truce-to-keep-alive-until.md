The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation leaders' meeting runs on Wednesday and Thursday in Shenzhen, and the headlines will go to a first trilateral summit of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders on Thursday [1]. The economic business is plainer. The trade truce Donald Trump and Xi Jinping reached on the sidelines of last year's APEC summit in South Korea expires in January [1].

"The key overriding goal is to prevent another round of aggressive destabilising tariffs and trade wars rather than to produce any single major milestone," Dylan Loh of Singapore's Nanyang Technological University told AFP [1]. Lizzi Lee of the Asia Society Policy Institute calls "keeping the truce alive" the priority, with another Trump-Xi meeting expected at the G20 summit in Miami in December [1]. Canada and others are looking to broaden their trade partnerships to reduce dependence on the United States [1].

On artificial intelligence, the talks could build on the communication channel agreed in Washington in September. "The next step should be to agree on what constitutes a reportable incident, who has authority to communicate, how quickly notifications must occur," said Marina Zhang of the University of Technology Sydney [1].

Read the agenda for what it is: a maintenance schedule. A truce extended is a set of tariff rates that your suppliers' invoices do not have to change. That rarely makes a headline, and it is worth more than most that do.

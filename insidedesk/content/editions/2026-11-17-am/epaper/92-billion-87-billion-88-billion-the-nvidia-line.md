Nvidia reports quarterly results after the US close on Tuesday, after 22:00 CET, with Colette Kress on the call as interim chief executive [1]. Analysts expect revenue up 91% to $109.1 billion and earnings of $2.47 a share, according to LSEG [1]. The shares have fallen for six straight weeks, the longest run since 2012 [1]. Morgan Stanley has cut its 2027 and 2028 data-centre estimates by about 10% [1]. Less noticed: in its latest filing Nvidia said it had committed to $92 billion of supply and capacity for the rest of this fiscal year, $87 billion for the year to January 2028 and $88 billion for the year to January 2029 [1]. Desk arithmetic: $267 billion in all. A chief executive can change in a week; a purchase commitment cannot. Nothing here is investment advice.

Just over half of employees in Germany get a Christmas bonus: 52% of the roughly 36,000 people in the WSI institute's survey said so, BR24 reports [1]. In firms bound by a collective agreement the share is 78%; in firms without one, 42% [1]. The bonus is often paid with the November salary [1].

The spread is wide. Mid-grade print-industry staff in Schleswig-Holstein/Hamburg receive €5,366, against €1,658 in Bavarian hospitality and €250 in Bavarian agriculture [1]. A claim exists in three cases: a collective agreement, your own contract, or an employer who has paid it regularly for years without putting it in writing [1]. Tax and social contributions apply, so the payslip will show less than the headline [1]. Desk arithmetic: the dull institution of the collective agreement is worth 36 percentage points of probability in December.

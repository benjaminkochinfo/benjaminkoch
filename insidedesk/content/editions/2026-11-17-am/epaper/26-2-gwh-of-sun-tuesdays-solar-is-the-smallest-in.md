The forecast for German solar generation on Tuesday is 26.2 gigawatt hours, the lowest of the nine days from 9 November that SMARD shows [1]. Saturday 14 November was forecast at 140.8 GWh, Friday at 122.5 GWh [1]. Desk arithmetic: Tuesday's sun is worth less than a fifth of Saturday's.

The panels are expected to produce nothing before 07:00 and from 17:00 on Tuesday, with a midday peak of 4,667 megawatts between 12:00 and 13:00 [1]. That is not a failure of the panels; it is the season. The useful habit is to read the solar line as a calendar, not a verdict, and to let the wind and the gas plants do the November shift.

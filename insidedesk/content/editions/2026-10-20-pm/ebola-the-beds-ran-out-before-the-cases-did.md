The daily bulletin for 18 October, published on 19 October, reported 97 new confirmed cases and 41 deaths, above the seven-day average of 69.6 cases a day [1]. Since the outbreak began there have been 9,552 confirmed cases and 4,602 deaths, and 2,550 people have recovered [1]. No new health zone has been added since 16 October; the outbreak is in 66 zones across eight provinces [1].

**The capacity line.** Across the country, treatment centres held 895 patients in 1,461 beds [1]. North Kivu had 450 patients for 410 beds, and 91 of its hospitalised patients were being treated outside treatment centres [1]. Of the day's 41 deaths, 30 happened in the community rather than in a centre [1].

**The follow-up line.** Teams saw 76.9% of the 32,744 contacts they were meant to follow, below the 85% threshold the national public-health institute has set itself [1]. In Ituri fewer than three contacts in four were seen [1].

**The vaccine line.** 8,353 people have received the Ervebo vaccine since 26 August, but only 192 of them in North Kivu, according to the figures available on 17 October [1].

ECDC continues to assess the risk of importation into the EU/EEA as very low [2]. For Congo the risk is the gap between beds and patients. It is a logistics problem, which means it is a problem that can be solved with logistics.

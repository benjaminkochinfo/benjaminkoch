Haute-Corse is no longer on red. In its bulletin published at 19:00 on Tuesday, Météo-France lowered the département's rain and flooding warning to orange [1]. The forecaster's tally for the episode: 190 mm in one hour at Solenzara, more than 500 mm over the whole episode, and radar estimates of more than 700 mm [1]. Its reason for stepping down was one line: "Désormais, les intensités de précipitations relèvent du niveau orange" [1].

Three départements are still orange tonight: Haute-Corse, Corse-du-Sud for flooding, and Gard, where the banks of the Vistre remain at risk [1]. In the Bouches-du-Rhône, on yellow, franceinfo reports that a 75-year-old man was "emporté par les eaux" at Saint-Mitre-les-Remparts [1].

Orange is not the all-clear. Rivers keep rising after the rain stops, and the roads closed this morning reopen only when someone has checked them. A warning that goes down for a stated reason, at a stated hour, is worth more than one that simply fades.

The federal government has suspended its usually weekly cabinet meetings for the autumn school holidays, government spokesman Stefan Kornelius told the dts news agency on Monday [1]. He said cabinet planning to the end of the year was "vollgepackt", packed, and that work on cabinet business and legislation continued without interruption [1].

The pause itself is not new, Kornelius said [1]. The report notes that the Merz government held no such pause last year, and that in autumn 2024 the Scholz government held no cabinet meeting for two weeks running, shortly before that coalition broke up; the agency adds that it draws no connection between the two [1].

The constructive reading is the dull one. A cabinet meeting is the moment decisions are formally taken, not the moment work is done. The test of the pause is simple and checkable: how many drafts reach the cabinet table in the first sessions after the holidays.

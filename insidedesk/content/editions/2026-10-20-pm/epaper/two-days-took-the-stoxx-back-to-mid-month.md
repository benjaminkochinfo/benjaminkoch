The STOXX Europe 600 closed at 634.78 on Tuesday, down 0.75%, after 639.56 on Monday, down 0.80% [1]. On Friday it had closed at 644.74 [1].

Desk arithmetic: from Friday's close to Tuesday's, the index lost about 1.5%. That puts it back around its level of 13 October (635.01) and still above the 626.65 close of 1 October, the low of the month so far [1].

Reuters attributed the two-day fall to oil above $111, nervousness about AI spending ahead of results from large technology companies, and French bond yields after the weekend's riots [2]. The chip sector was the weakest part of the tape, with ASML and ASMI down almost 3% [2].

Two red days are a mood. A month's range is a context. Neither is advice.

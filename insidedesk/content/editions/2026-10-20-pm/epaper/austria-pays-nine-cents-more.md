German heating oil rose about 2.5 cents to €1.73 a litre on Tuesday; in Austria the litre rose only 0.3 cents, to €1.82, almost nine cents more than in Germany [1]. In Switzerland it rose almost half a rappen to CHF 1.75, just below this year's high, set in October [1]. Neighbouring markets move at different speeds because dealers buy, store and price differently. The national average is a guide, not a bill.

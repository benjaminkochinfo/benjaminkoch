An auction of long-dated Japanese government bonds drew record demand on Tuesday, and the yen strengthened 0.2%, Reuters reported [1]. Media reports said the Bank of Japan may raise its inflation outlook for the next fiscal year in forecasts due this month [1]. A record order book at an auction is the bond market's version of a full house. It says lenders still want to lend, at a price.

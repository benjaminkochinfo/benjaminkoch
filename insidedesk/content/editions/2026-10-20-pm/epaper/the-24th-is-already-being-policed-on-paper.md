In a telegram to prefects dated Monday and published by franceinfo on Tuesday, Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez asked them to ban protest demonstrations likely to disturb public order on Saturday 24 October, where dialogue with organisers has not created the necessary security conditions [1]. For marches that pose no such risk, he asked for routes away from public buildings and shopping streets, and for perimeter orders against gatherings at places hit last weekend [1].

The local orders followed. The Rhône prefect renewed the curfew in central Lyon from 21:00 to 06:00, saying the targeted measure had "contribué au calme" [1]. In Marseille a targeted curfew from 22:00 to 05:00 bars minors from shopping centres and parts of the city centre [1]. The Paris police prefecture banned carrying fuel and gas in individual containers until Thursday inclusive [1].

Monday night passed without major incidents, police said [2]. Orders with hours and perimeters can be checked against what happens. That is their merit.

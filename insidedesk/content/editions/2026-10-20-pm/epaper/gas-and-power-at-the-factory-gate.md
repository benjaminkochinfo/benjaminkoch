Oil products drove most of September's rise in German producer prices, but not all of it. Natural gas in distribution cost 15.0% more than a year earlier across all customer groups, and electricity 8.2% more [1]. District heating was 0.5% cheaper [1]. Energy as a whole was up 14.9% on the year and 5.8% on August [1]. The gas line is the one to watch as the heating season begins.

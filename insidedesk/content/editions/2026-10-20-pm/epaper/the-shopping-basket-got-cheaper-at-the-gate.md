Consumer goods, the category that ends up in shops, were 1.8% cheaper at the producer level in September than a year earlier, Destatis reported [1]. Durable consumer goods were 2.2% dearer, capital goods 2.4% [1]. Excluding energy, producer prices rose 0.1% on August [1]. The headline 6.7% is real. So is this. A shock that stays in the fuel bill is easier to carry than one that spreads into the trolley.

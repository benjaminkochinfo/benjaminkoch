France's digital minister Anne Le Henanff was due to meet social-media bosses on Tuesday, after saying on Monday that goods looted over the weekend were being sold online and calling on platforms to remove such listings [1]. The government also announced a meeting with the main social-media platforms as part of a series of emergency meetings, and an exceptional business council at the finance ministry [2]. The useful measure afterwards is not the communiqué. It is how many listings come down.

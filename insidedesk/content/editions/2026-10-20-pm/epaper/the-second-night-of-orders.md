Paris has renewed its curfew around Place de la République and Les Halles, from 23:00 on Tuesday to 06:00 on Wednesday, by order of the police prefecture [1]. At the prefecture's request the RATP is closing République, Châtelet-les Halles and Les Halles stations from 22:00; trains pass through without stopping [1]. Residents, people who must work there, emergency services and shopkeepers are exempt [1]. The prefecture said on X that the first night's curfew had restored "la paix publique" and that the measure would last "aussi longtemps que cela sera nécessaire" [1].

The orders also spread west. In Nantes the prefect banned gatherings in the city centre and around the Atlantis, Ruban Bleu and Océanis shopping centres from 21:00 to 06:00 until Saturday 24 October [2]. In Rennes, night gatherings in the centre are banned from 20:00 to 05:00 until Saturday morning; the prefect, Emmanuelle Dubée, said: "Ce n'est pas un couvre-feu" [2]. Marseille's curfew for minors runs to Friday 23 October [2].

A curfew with an end date and a list of exemptions is a rule people can follow and courts can review. That is the difference between an order and a mood.

Yields on ten-year French government bonds inched up to 4.955% on Tuesday, after their biggest one-day rise in about a month on Monday, Reuters reported [1]. An SEB economist said the weekend's protests had intensified concerns about French public finances, given the size of the austerity needed to cut the deficit [1]. The euro traded flat at $1.12 [1]. A second calm day in the bond market is not a verdict on France. It is the market waiting for the budget.

The Rhine gauge at Kaub read 21 cm on Tuesday morning, and little improvement is expected before Saturday, HeizOel24 reported [1]. Barges carry heating oil and diesel up the river to western and southern Germany, and low water makes those deliveries laborious [1]. The weather does not only show up on rain maps. Sometimes it shows up on a delivery note.

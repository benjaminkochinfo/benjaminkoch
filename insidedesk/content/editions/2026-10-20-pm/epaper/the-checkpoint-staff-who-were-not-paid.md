Congo's daily Ebola bulletin for 18 October lists the difficulties teams faced, province by province [1]. In Bas-Uélé, the Buta treatment centre is still under construction, there is no ambulance, 16 of 20 priority health facilities have no patient triage, and staff at an Isiro checkpoint are on strike because they have not been paid [1]. In Tshopo, seven patients have been lost to follow-up, six of them confirmed cases from Bafwasende, where the treatment centre has no food support [1]. In Ituri, blood is short and the Avakubi checkpoint is not working because of insecurity [1].

Safe burial teams carried out 106 burials on the day and tested 106 bodies [1].

None of these is a mystery of virology. They are a construction schedule, a vehicle, a ration and a payroll. That is why they matter: every one of them can be fixed by someone with a budget and a list.

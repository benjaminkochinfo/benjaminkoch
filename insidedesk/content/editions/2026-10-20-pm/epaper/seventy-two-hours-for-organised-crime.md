Justice Minister Gérald Darmanin said on TF1 that he had sent an instruction to every prosecutor's office in France to pursue the 17 October violence under the heading of organised crime [1]. That allows police custody to be extended from 48 to 72 hours and permits geolocation and phone taps, which he said would show where suspects were when shops were looted and town halls attacked [1]. A legal category is a procedure, not a verdict. Courts will decide whether it fits each case.

The suspect in the arson of Limoges town hall, a 34-year-old described as a known ultra-left activist, was arrested at dawn by the Raid police unit in a makeshift camp about 50 km from the city, franceinfo reported [1]. Separately, the domestic intelligence service DGSI told Libération and Le Monde that a report of rioters carrying automatic weapons was "complètement" false and that it had said nothing of the kind to journalists [1]. Arrests and denials are less exciting than rumours. They are more useful.

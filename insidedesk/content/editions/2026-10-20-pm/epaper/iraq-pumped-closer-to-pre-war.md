Iraq's oil minister Bassem Mohammed Khudair said October exports had so far averaged more than 3.4 million barrels a day, up from 2.65 million in September, HeizOel24 reported [1]. That brings the country back near its pre-war volumes [1]. The US also said it will allow the sale of sanctioned tankers so that more ships are available for oil transport [1]. Neither moved Brent much on Tuesday. Supply repairs are slow, and they rarely make headlines.

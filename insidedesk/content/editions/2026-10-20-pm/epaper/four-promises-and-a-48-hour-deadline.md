Vinted, Amazon, eBay, Leboncoin, Meta, TikTok and two e-commerce trade bodies met the commerce minister, Serge Papin, and the digital minister, Anne Le Hénanff, on Monday evening, the government said on Tuesday [1]. They made four commitments: closer cooperation with investigators, stronger monitoring, tighter moderation of listings with tell-tale signs such as abnormally low prices, new-goods bundles or security tags still attached, and warnings to buyers about the penalties for handling stolen goods [1]. They must also report figures on removed listings and referrals, with a first return due within 48 hours [1]. The communiqué is the promise. The first numbers will be the receipt.

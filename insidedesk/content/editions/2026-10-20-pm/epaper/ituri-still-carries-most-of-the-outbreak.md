Of 9,552 confirmed Ebola cases, 6,960 are in Ituri, with 3,217 deaths, and 2,084 in North Kivu, with 1,176 deaths, according to the bulletin of 18 October [1]. On the day, North Kivu reported 48 new cases and Ituri 42 [1]. Of 500 samples tested, 19.4% were positive, rising to 25.0% in Haut-Uélé [1]. Desk arithmetic: Ituri holds about 73% of all confirmed cases. A table by province is where a response decides where to send the next team.

Monday's ordinary ledger is short on purpose.

**Screen.** Brent at midday about $108.71, roughly a dollar below Friday's close, after the US waived new Graham Act duties on Russia-related energy trade [1]. WTI near $101.26 [1].

**Hedge.** Gasoil, the contract heating-oil dealers watch, about $1,447.50 a tonne — still above Friday afternoon's $1,413 — so most of Friday night's spike was still in the refined print [1].

**Doorstep.** HeizOel24: heating-oil prices in Germany and Austria "nearly unchanged"; Switzerland a bit softer [1]. Sunday night's tanke-günstig national average for a 2,000-litre order was €177.59 per 100 litres [2]; do not assume Monday erased that.

**Desk arithmetic (labelled).** If Brent falls one dollar and gasoil does not, a 2,000-litre household order should not be expected to fall in lockstep. The binding number remains the written quote for your postcode.

Nothing here is advice to buy, wait or switch. It is a reminder that the barrel and the invoice are different documents.

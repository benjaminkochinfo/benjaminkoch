The annex this evening is the Élysée's Monday statement on Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez, as reported by Reuters [1].

**What Nuñez said.** The police "lost control" in some cities. There had been a "lack of anticipation." He tendered his resignation "because I felt responsible for everything that happened" [1].

**What Macron refused.** Continuity of command for law enforcement "at this time." The resignation was declined [1].

**What Macron ordered instead.** Two deliverables, not one adjective. First: "take all necessary steps to ensure transparency over the failures in planning and intelligence that led to the violence." Second: "put in place new operational methods for security forces" as quickly as possible, including approaches to deal with looters [1].

**What sits beside it.** Lecornu's instruction to prefects for localized curfews [2]. Sunday's presidential address promising a prime-ministerial round table this week on schools and cost of living [3]. Opposition calls for Nuñez to be sacked [1].

**The reading.** A resignation refused without instructions is theatre. A resignation refused with two written tasks is a work order. Hold the ministry to the work order: a dated review, and operational methods that can be explained without a podium.

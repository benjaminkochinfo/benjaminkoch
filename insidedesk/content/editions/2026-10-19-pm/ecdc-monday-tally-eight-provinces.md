ECDC refreshed its Congo Bundibugyo page on Monday afternoon [1]. As of 18 October (data to 17 October) it reported 9,455 confirmed cases and 4,561 related deaths; 925 patients hospitalised in isolation; 2,530 recoveries among those who tested positive [1]. Since the previous ECDC update on 16 October (data to 14 October), an additional 178 confirmed cases and 83 deaths had been recorded [1].

Nord Ubangi is now listed: two confirmed cases from Wapinda health zone, bringing affected provinces to eight [1]. Ituri remains by far the heaviest burden (6,918 cases); 66 of 178 health zones across the eight provinces are affected [1]. About 80% of identified case contacts were under follow-up [1] — still short of the response's own 95% discipline that this morning's edition underlined from Congo's SitRep.

The constructive habit is unchanged: watch the follow-up rate and the laboratory throughput, not only the shaded map. An eighth province is serious. A contact seen today is how a ninth is avoided.

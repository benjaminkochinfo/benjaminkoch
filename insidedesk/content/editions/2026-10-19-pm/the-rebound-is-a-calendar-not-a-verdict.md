Wall Street's Monday was a partial undo of Friday's AI-led drop. The S&P 500 rose about 0.5% in early trade; Nasdaq futures had been up about 1%; chip names led [1][2]. Lower oil and a softer tone in long Treasury yields helped [1][2]. None of that settles the question Friday posed: whether hyperscaler spending, expected by some houses near $1.2 trillion of combined capex next year, will throw off enough profit to justify the borrowing [2].

The week's calendar is the print. Meta reports Wednesday; Tesla, Lam Research and Texas Instruments are also due; Netflix and GE Aerospace land Tuesday [2][3]. Swissquote's Ipek Ozkardeskaya put the ask plainly: investors will want evidence that AI is generating revenue and profit, "not another ambitious spending plan" [2].

A rebound ahead of those dates is permitted. Treating it as proof is not. Wait for the cash-flow line.

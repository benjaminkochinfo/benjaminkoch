France was not the only European capital of broken glass this weekend. In Milan, a second day of cost-of-living unrest brought fresh looting and more than 200 detentions, according to Euronews [1]. Turin and Bologna also saw clashes [1]. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni condemned the violence and convened a security meeting with police, carabinieri and intelligence chiefs on Monday [2].

The constructive comparison is institutional, not theatrical. Different countries, different interior ministries, the same demand: a public tally of arrests, injuries and shops, plus a dated plan for the next night. France published curfew instructions; Italy published a summit. Both are machinery. Neither is a slogan.

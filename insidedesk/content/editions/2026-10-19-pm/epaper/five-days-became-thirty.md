The most useful thing a minister said on Monday was a number of days. Serge Papin, the minister for small businesses, said the window for declaring damage to insurers would go from five days to thirty [1]. Five days is not long when your shutters are broken and your stock is gone. Thirty days gives you time to count it properly.

The rest of the toolkit is administrative too. The government said at least 3,000 businesses had been damaged or looted over the weekend, and that they could get short-time working support from Tuesday plus a dedicated support fund [1]. Françoise Gatel, the minister for local authorities, announced an exceptional fund to rebuild destroyed public buildings and a single point of contact at each prefecture, but gave no amount [1]. Casino's chief executive said nearly 50 of the group's stores had been attacked and 32 looted, and that none of its independent franchisees would go bankrupt [1].

None of this is a cheque yet. When a fund comes without an amount, it is still only an announcement. The amount, the eligibility rules and how fast money is paid out are the parts to watch. Until then, the thirty days is what has actually changed for a shopkeeper.

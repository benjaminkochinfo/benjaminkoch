Press release No. 371 of 19 October: real manufacturing order stock +0.5% on July after seasonal and calendar adjustment, +10.7% on August 2025, range unchanged at 9.0 months [1]. Capital-goods producers still show 12.6 months of work; intermediate goods 4.6; consumer goods 4.3 [1]. Automotive stock fell 0.4% on the month; computers, electronics and optical products rose 1.4% [1]. A backlog is the opposite of a protest video — dull, dated, and already sold.

European gas was quoted near €88.92/MWh on Monday, up about 1.6%, on concerns about Gulf LNG and the need to refill storage [1]. EU storage trackers citing AGSI+ recently showed fills around 74.5%, against a policy ambition of 90% in the early-winter window [1][2]. Oil can fall on a sanctions waiver while gas rises on a winter inventory gap. Households that heat with gas feel the second story first.

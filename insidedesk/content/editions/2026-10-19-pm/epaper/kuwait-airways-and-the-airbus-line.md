In Kuwait City on Monday, Airbus sales executive Benoit de Saint Exupéry and the Kuwait Investment Authority's Sheikh Saud Salem Abdulaziz Al Sabah signed a partnership that includes renewing the Kuwait Airways fleet [1]. Defence and AI agreements accompanied the visit; TotalEnergies and Engie chiefs travelled onward with Macron toward a Franco-Emirati business summit [1]. Receptions are easy to film. Fleet renewals are invoices with delivery slots. Prefer the second when judging whether a tour changed anything.

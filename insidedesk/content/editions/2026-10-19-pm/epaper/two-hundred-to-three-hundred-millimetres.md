Across the whole episode, expected cumuls on eastern Hérault and the Gard are of order 200–250 mm, locally 300 mm, on coast, plain and relief alike [1]. Hourly bursts of 60–80 mm are possible with gusts to 80 km/h [1]. Those figures are why orange exists. Stay out of underpasses; do not drive into water; follow the prefecture [1]. Rain totals are the weather's ledger. Behaviour is yours.

Two confirmed cases from Wapinda in Nord Ubangi brought the number of affected Congolese provinces to eight on ECDC's tally [1]. Sud Ubangi had already listed two cases; the northern neighbour is now on the same sheet [1]. Early, tiny counts are when contact follow-up either works or fails. Eighty percent of identified contacts under follow-up is progress and still short of a 95% operational target [1].

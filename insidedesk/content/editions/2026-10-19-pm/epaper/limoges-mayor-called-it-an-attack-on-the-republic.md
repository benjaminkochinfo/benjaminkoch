After flames took the roof and spire of Limoges's nineteenth-century town hall, Mayor Guillaume Guérin wrote that to attack the building was to attack the republic [1]. Strong words. They also name a function: a town hall is where permits, civil status and council votes live. Rebuilding it is not nostalgia; it is restoring a counter where ordinary life is stamped. Watch the reconstruction timetable as closely as the anniversary speeches.

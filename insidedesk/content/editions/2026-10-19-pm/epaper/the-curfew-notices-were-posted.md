This evening's lead asked whether the curfew notices would appear with clear hours. They did. The Paris police prefecture said a curfew order runs from 23:00 on Monday to 06:00 on Tuesday on the esplanade of the Place de la République and the streets that lead into Les Halles from the south [1]. Demonstrations are also banned in some Paris districts from 20:00 to 06:00 [1][2].

In the Nord, the zone prefect signed an order covering minors in Lille, Douai, Wattignies and Denain from 20:00 to 06:00 for the next two nights [1]. In Nice, the mayor's order keeps minors off central streets from 21:00 to 06:00 until Sunday [1][2]. Denain's mayor narrowed her own Sunday order, which had covered the whole town of about 20,000 people, to parts of the centre and to unaccompanied minors only [2].

Look at what the paperwork shows. These are small orders, limited by hour, street and age, and each has an end date. That is what makes them checkable. Tomorrow's tally of arrests and damaged shops in those streets, compared with the weekend, is the real test of whether they worked.

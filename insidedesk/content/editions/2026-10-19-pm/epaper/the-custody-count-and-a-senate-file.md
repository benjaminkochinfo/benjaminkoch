The weekend's arrest tally now has a court-side twin. Justice minister Gérald Darmanin said on TF1 that 2,513 people were taken into custody over the weekend and 67 jailed; he has told prosecutors to treat the cases as organised crime, which allows 72 hours of custody instead of 48 [1].

Lyon joined the curfew list: the Rhône prefect closed much of the Presqu'île, Place Bellecour included, from 21:00 to 06:00, with exemptions for residents and work [2]. Paris has banned undeclared gatherings from 20:00 on Tuesday to 06:00 on Wednesday around parliament, the Élysée and the big stations [1]. The Senate's law committee opened an inquiry into "the chain of events" [1].

Count the people held, date every restriction, then let parliament ask how it got this far.

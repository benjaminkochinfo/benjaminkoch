After the crisis meeting, Sébastien Lecornu said he had asked some local authorities to introduce curfews but dismissed putting all of Paris under such a measure [1]. AP reported his instruction to prefects for "very limited" and "localized" curfews [2]. That distinction matters. A national emergency declaration was urged by some on the right; Macron stopped short [1]. The tool chosen is the prefecture's timed order, which can be checked street by street. Hold each prefect to the notice they actually post.

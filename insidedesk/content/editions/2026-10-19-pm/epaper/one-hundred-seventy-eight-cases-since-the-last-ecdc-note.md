ECDC's Monday page states that since its 16 October update (data to 14 October), an additional 178 confirmed cases and 83 deaths were reported [1]. The cumulative print as of 18 October: 9,455 confirmed cases, 4,561 deaths, 925 in isolation, 2,530 recoveries among positives [1]. Pace matters as much as the stock. A weekday refresh that keeps showing triple-digit case increments is the opposite of a contained week.

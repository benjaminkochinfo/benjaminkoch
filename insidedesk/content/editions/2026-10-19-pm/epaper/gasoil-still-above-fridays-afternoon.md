HeizOel24's Monday midday note put ICE gasoil at about $1,447.50 a tonne, still above Friday afternoon's $1,413, even as Brent slipped to about $108.71 [1]. Heating-oil dealers hedge with gasoil, not with a Brent screenshot. That is why German and Austrian household quotes barely moved [1]. Until the refined contract gives back the spike, a cheaper barrel is a headline without a delivery discount.

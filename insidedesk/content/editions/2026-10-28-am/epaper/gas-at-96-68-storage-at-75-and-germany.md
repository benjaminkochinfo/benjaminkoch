The November TTF gas contract closed at €96.68 per MWh on Monday, up 3.02% on Friday's €93.85, and briefly touched €97.40 on Tuesday before easing, 3e-news reports [1]. The price is not the number to watch. Storage is. EU gas stores are about 75% full, the lowest level recorded for the season, and German stores only 61% [1]. Unplanned Norwegian outages and colder weather forecast across Europe add to the pressure, the report says [1].

In Bulgaria, the Balkan Gas Hub's day-ahead price for 27 October was €86.68 per MWh [1]. Desk arithmetic: about €10 below the Dutch benchmark. A full tank in October is the cheapest insurance policy a continent can buy. This year the premium is still being paid. Nothing here is investment advice.

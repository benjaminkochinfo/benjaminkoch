Jio Platforms, Mukesh Ambani's telecoms group, is set to debut on India's stock market on Wednesday after a record initial public offering, Bloomberg reports, into a market that has been unkind to India's big deals [1]. In Hong Kong, Suzhou Dongshan Precision Manufacturing slumped on its first day of trading after raising HK$9.9 billion, about $1.3 billion [1]. Anthropic has picked JPMorgan Chase to lead the US sale of its shares to individual investors, people familiar with the matter said [1].

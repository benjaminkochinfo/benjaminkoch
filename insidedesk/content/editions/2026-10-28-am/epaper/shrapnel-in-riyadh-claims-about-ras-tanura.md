Saudi Arabia said shrapnel from two Houthi ballistic missiles, shot down over Riyadh on Tuesday, caused material damage in the capital; no details of the damage were given [1]. The Saudi-led coalition said it also stopped three more Houthi missiles and four drones during the day, and later two more ballistic missiles [1].

The Houthis claimed attacks on the Ras Tanura refinery and on two military transport aircraft at Najran airport [1]. Saudi authorities had not confirmed the refinery strike, which Reuters describes as a claimed hit on the kingdom's biggest refinery, a 550,000-barrel-a-day plant [1]. Near the Bab al-Mandeb strait, both the Houthis and Saudi-backed coalition forces claimed gains in heavy fighting [1]. A Houthi official repeated that Egypt's military support for Saudi Arabia amounted to a "declaration of war" [1].

Read the morning in two columns. In one, what a government has stated: interceptions, debris, damage in Riyadh. In the other, what a combatant claims: a refinery hit, aircraft struck, ground taken. Markets already moved on the second column (oil rose overnight) [1], which is understandable and not the same as knowing. The useful question for today is a plain one: does Saudi Aramco or the energy ministry report any change in Ras Tanura's operations? Until then, a claim is a claim.

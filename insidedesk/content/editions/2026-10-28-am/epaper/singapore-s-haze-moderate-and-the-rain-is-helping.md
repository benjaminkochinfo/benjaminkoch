Singapore's National Environment Agency forecasts a 24-hour PSI of 60–90 for Wednesday, the moderate band [1]. At 6pm local time on Tuesday (11:00 CET), the 24-hour PSI was 62–76 and one-hour PM2.5 readings were 19–37 µg/m³, in the normal band; smoke haze was observed over Kalimantan and Sumatra [1]. The north briefly entered the elevated PM2.5 range on Tuesday afternoon before returning to normal [2]. On Thursday and Friday the winds are expected to swing to the east-southeast or south-southeast, which could carry smoke from southern Sumatra, but forecast showers "should help to lower the risk of haze", the agency said [1][2]. When hourly PM2.5 enters the elevated band, the agency advises healthy people to cut strenuous outdoor activity for the next hour, and vulnerable groups to avoid it [2]. Air quality is a regional problem with a very local instrument: the hourly reading on your phone.

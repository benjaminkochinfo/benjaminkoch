In August the US Food and Drug Administration approved an mRNA influenza vaccine, MFLUSIVA, for adults aged 50 and over [1]. It is a single intramuscular dose targeting two influenza A strains, H3N2 and H1N1, and one B strain [1]. A review published on Tuesday by NEJM Clinician sums it up in one line: the vaccine "offers modest benefit over standard-dose flu vaccine, with more side effects" [1]. The efficacy data behind the approval come from an international, industry-sponsored trial [1]. The technology's real promise, the review notes, is speed of manufacture, already used against Covid-19 and RSV [1]. For you, the practical news is unchanged: the best flu jab this autumn is the one you actually get.

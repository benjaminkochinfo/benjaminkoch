Euro-area consumers expect inflation of 3.1% over the next twelve months, up from 3.0%, according to the European Central Bank's Consumer Expectations Survey for September, published on Friday [1]. Perceived inflation over the past year rose to 3.6% [1]. Households expect their nominal income to grow 1.1% and their spending 3.7%; they say spending has already risen 5.3% over the past year [1]. Desk arithmetic: the expected spending line runs 2.6 points ahead of the expected income line. Three-year and five-year expectations held at 2.9% and 2.5% [1]. About 19,000 adults in eleven euro countries answered between 27 August and 28 September [1]. The ECB's Governing Council decides on rates on Thursday. A household's gap between what it earns and what it spends is the number no central bank can close for it, but it is the one each household can see first.

Turnout among Arab Israelis reached 80%, against 77.7% nationally, the Central Elections Committee's figures show [1]. On the partial count the Joint List has 12 seats and Ra'am four [1]. Ra'am's Mansour Abbas said he hopes the final results let the opposition and his party reach the 61 seats needed to govern [2]. Ra'am had been projected at around seven seats in the exit polls [2]; the partial count has it on four [1]. Arab-led lists accused Netanyahu of inciting hatred to mobilise his voters during polling day [2]. A record turnout is not yet a role in government. It is, though, a figure nobody can wish away in coalition talks.

A Ukrainian military source told Reuters that Russia hit Kyiv's Southern Bridge with four Iskander ballistic missiles; President Zelensky called the attack "a serious escalation" against Ukrainian logistics [1]. Energy, logistics and infrastructure in 10 other regions were also attacked overnight, he said [1]. Kyiv's mayor says the city plans to allocate 1 billion hryvnias, about $22 million, to build water crossings over the Dnipro, which divides the capital in two [1]. Poland, meanwhile, will host a maintenance centre for the Patriot air-defence system, the only one outside the US, its deputy defence minister said [1]. A water crossing is not a headline weapon. It is a line in a city budget that keeps people getting to work when a bridge cannot be trusted.

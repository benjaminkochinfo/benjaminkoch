Qatar's ruler and Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian met in Moscow on Tuesday and "reviewed developments in the regional situation, foremost among them the latest on the negotiations, the Strait of Hormuz, and energy supplies", the emir's office said [1]. Pezeshkian told Vladimir Putin the Middle East is "capable of resolving our problems" without the US [1]. In Washington, Donald Trump said the war could end "whenever I want it to", provided Iran declares it will never have a nuclear weapon [1]. Three capitals, three statements, no shared document. A communiqué you can read is worth more than a venue you can photograph.

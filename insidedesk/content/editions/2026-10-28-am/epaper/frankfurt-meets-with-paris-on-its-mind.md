The European Central Bank's Governing Council meets today and tomorrow, and a hold at 2.5% is widely expected, ING's economists write, with close to zero chance of a hike priced in [1]. The question is whether turmoil in French and other European bonds softens the message. ING thinks not, and expects the door to stay open for a December rise, which markets already price at more than 20 basis points [1]. There are no new staff projections at this meeting, so the wording carries the weight [1].

For your savings and loans in euros, a hold is not nothing. It means today's rates stay in place until the Council next moves. The decision is due on Thursday. Nothing here is investment advice.

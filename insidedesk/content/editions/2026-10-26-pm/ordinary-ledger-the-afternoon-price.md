The German average for 100 litres of heating oil stood at €174.58 on Monday afternoon, for a standard order, according to HeizOel24 [1]. That is €3.94 more than a week ago and €41.03 more than three months ago [1]. This morning the same page showed €174.34. Brent, meanwhile, fell more than 1.5% during the day [2].

**The ledger.** Desk arithmetic: a 3,000-litre delivery at the afternoon average costs about €5,237, roughly €7 more than at breakfast and about €1,231 more than three months ago. A detached house uses 2,000 to 3,000 litres a year, by the portal's rule of thumb [1].

**The lag.** A cheaper barrel does not make a cheaper tank on the same day. Taxes, the CO₂ levy, the dollar and dealers' margins sit between them, and the portal itself rates the moment to buy as "ungünstig", unfavourable [1]. An order is binding, and the price holds until delivery in two to four weeks [1].

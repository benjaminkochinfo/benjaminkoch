The Federal Reserve's committee meets on 27 and 28 October [1]. The ECB's Governing Council announces its decision on Thursday, 29 October. Both arrive on hold: the Fed's upper limit has been 4.00% since September, the ECB's deposit rate 2.50% since September [2].

[[chart:rates-fed-ecb-26pm]]

**The expectation.** Previews broadly expect the ECB to hold at 2.50%; one market-pricing tracker put the chance of an October rise at about 3%, and Morgan Stanley sees December, when new staff projections arrive, as the "natural moment" for the next move [3]. A forecast is not a decision. The chart shows only what was decided.

**The relief.** Moody's has kept France at Aa3, citing a diversified economy and strong public institutions despite rising debt and a fragmented parliament [4]. The French ten-year yield fell below 5% on Monday morning [4]. That is the most useful thing a rating agency can say about a country: the institutions still count.

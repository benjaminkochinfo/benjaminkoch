The Rhine gauge at Kaub read 55 cm at 16:30 on Monday, according to the federal waterways' PEGELONLINE service [1]. Desk arithmetic on the 15-minute readings: the mean so far today is about 53 cm, against 31.3 cm on Sunday and 24.5 cm on Saturday [1]. The service still classes the level as low against the long-term mean low water [1].

**The weather behind it.** The German Weather Service said on Monday morning that the remains of storm Manja were moving off over the Norwegian Sea, with gusts of around 70 km/h on the North Sea coast and up to 95 km/h on exposed summits such as the Brocken [2]. On Monday evening the warm front of the next storm, NAOMI, west of Ireland, reaches the north-west half of the country [2].

**Why it matters.** Kaub is the bottleneck gauge for Rhine shipping between the Upper and Middle Rhine. Every centimetre there is cargo a barge can carry instead of leaving at the quay. Rain upstream is, for once, a supply-chain story with a happy direction.

"The recovery is continuing," said Clemens Fuest, in the sentence every news agency used [2]. The ifo business climate rose for the sixth month in a row, to 90.2 points from 89.9, its highest since spring 2023, and above the 89.9 analysts had expected [1][2].

**The print underneath.** The climate is an average of two questions. Firms' expectations for the next six months rose to 91.6 from 90.4. Their assessment of business right now fell to 88.8 from 89.5 [1]. The headline rose on hope; the present slipped.

**Where it is real.** Manufacturing is the good news, and it is specific. Its balance turned positive, at +1.2, for the first time in more than three years; incoming orders rose and firms plan to expand production [1]. Services went the other way, to −1.2 from +0.7, with IT services the exception [1]. Trade was stuck at −17.2, construction improved to −15.5 [1].

**The cautious reading.** One bank analyst, Klaus Bauknecht of IKB, told tagesschau the survey shows no sign of a sustained investment upswing [2]. That is the right footnote. Orders are rising; capital spending is not yet in the numbers.

**The market.** The DAX closed up 0.63% at 25,341.54; the CAC 40 fell 0.36% and Spain's IBEX 0.73% [3]. Nothing here is investment advice.

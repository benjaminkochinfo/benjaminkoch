The Congolese bulletin for 24 October reports 88 new confirmed cases and 38 deaths, taking the outbreak to 9,964 confirmed cases and 4,841 deaths, a case fatality rate of 48.6% [1]. Over the past seven days the average was 72.7 cases and 40.0 deaths a day, against 68.1 and 32.1 the week before [1].

**Where.** Ituri reported 44 of the new cases, Nord-Kivu 39 and Haut-Uélé 5 [1]. No new health zone has been affected since 16 October; the outbreak stays in 66 zones across eight provinces [1]. WHO's latest update also lists imported cases in Kenya and Nigeria [2].

**The machinery.** Treatment centres hold 958 patients for 1,583 beds, but Nord-Kivu has 495 patients for 410 beds [1]. Teams saw 87.1% of contacts; 9,415 people have been vaccinated since 26 August [1]. Of 38 deaths, 26 happened outside a treatment centre [1], which is where the next infections usually start.

The good line, small but real: 2,670 people have recovered [1].

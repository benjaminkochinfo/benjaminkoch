Russia launched 140 drones and four missiles at Ukraine overnight; Ukraine's air force said 112 drones and three Kh-59/69 missiles were shot down [1]. Strikes injured people in Zaporizhzhia, Chernihiv and Izium, and damage to an energy facility forced emergency power cuts in the Khmelnytskyi region [1].

**The other direction.** In Russia, the Voronezh governor said more than 70 drones were shot down over the city; two people were killed and 17 injured, many on a commuter train hit by debris [1].

**The paper track.** Russia agreed last Wednesday to return to three-way talks with Ukraine and the US in November, in Abu Dhabi according to Moscow [1]. President Zelenskyy said on Sunday: "We are ready to talk," adding that territory must be settled at leaders' level [1]. On Friday, the two sides agreed a localised ceasefire around Oleshky in Kherson [1].

A ceasefire around one town is small. Small agreements are the ones that get kept, and kept agreements are the ones that get copied.

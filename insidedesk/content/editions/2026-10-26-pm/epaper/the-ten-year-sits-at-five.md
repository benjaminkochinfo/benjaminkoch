The US ten-year Treasury yield edged up to 5% by Monday midday, Dow Jones reported, while the French ten-year fell below 5% after Moody's held the country's rating [1]. The official H.15 series shows 4.96% for Thursday, 22 October, its latest value [2]. For once, Paris borrowed for ten years at about the same price as Washington. That is less a compliment to either than a reminder of what a decade of debt costs everyone. Nothing here is investment advice.

Audi plans to invest about $4 billion in its first US manufacturing plant, in Georgia, with around 4,500 jobs, according to Volkswagen [1]. Construction is due to start in 2027 and production in the early 2030s, with capacity for up to 200,000 premium SUVs a year for North American buyers [1]. Desk observation: a factory that opens in the 2030s is a bet on the market, not on any one administration. That is how trade policy is really answered.

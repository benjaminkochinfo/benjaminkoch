New York finished Monday higher. The S&P 500 rose 0.7% to 7,676.77 and the Nasdaq Composite 0.82% to 26,169.38, after Anthropic published its prospectus for a Nasdaq listing under the ticker "ANT" [1]. The filing shows $16.29 billion of revenue for the six months to June, against $1.16 billion a year earlier [1].

**The invoices beside it.** Meta said it plans to raise $95 billion for its AI investments, and Elon Musk said SpaceX has closed $40 billion of debt for AI chips [1]. Chipmakers rallied, yet the iShares semiconductor fund is still roughly 25% below its high [1]. Brent settled 0.8% lower at $115.81 a barrel [1].

**The other side of the board.** US-listed Brazilian shares paid for Sunday's result. Petrobras fell 21% before the open, and the iShares MSCI Brazil fund dropped nearly 20% overnight before paring the loss to 18% [1].

This evening's edition asked who pays for the buildings. Tonight's partial answer: new shareholders and lenders, in sums now written down. Futures put the chance of the Fed holding at 3.75–4% on Wednesday at about 90%, the same evening Alphabet and Microsoft report [1]. That is where the bills get read.

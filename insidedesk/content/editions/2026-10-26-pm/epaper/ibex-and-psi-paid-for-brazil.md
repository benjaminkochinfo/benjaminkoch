Dow Jones reported on Monday that Spanish and Portuguese shares were weighed down after the result in Brazil, where many of their listed companies earn money [1]. Spain's IBEX 35 closed 0.73% lower at 18,850.30 [2].

In Frankfurt the story was different. By midday Siemens Energy had gained 3.9% and led the DAX, followed by Hochtief at 3.3%; Infineon rose 2.7% and Aixtron 2.9% as the AI-related names recovered [1]. The DAX closed 0.63% higher at 25,341.54 [2].

The pairing says something useful. Markets react to elections in hours, and to order books over months. A turbine maker's gain is about contracts already signed; a bank's loss on Brazil is about a complaint not yet heard. Nothing here is investment advice.

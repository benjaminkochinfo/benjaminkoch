Flávio Bolsonaro's campaign had asked for longer voting hours in municipalities across five states, citing heavy rain, flooding and power outages, DW reports; Lula's campaign asked for a nationwide extension over voter transport problems, and the court granted that [1]. Weather does not take sides. It does test whether polling stations, roads and power lines can carry an election, and that is an infrastructure question long before it is a legal one.

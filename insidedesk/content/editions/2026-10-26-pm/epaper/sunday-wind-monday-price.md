German onshore wind generated 1,009,162 MWh on Saturday and 1,019,966 MWh on Sunday, against 242,281 MWh last Wednesday, according to SMARD [1]. The day-ahead price followed: €30.12 on Saturday, €13.12 on Sunday and €116.57 for Monday [1]. Desk arithmetic: Monday's average is almost nine times Sunday's. Wind is cheap when it blows and silent about tomorrow, which is why the grid still keeps everything else on standby.

Ursula von der Leyen called Lula's win a victory for "democracy and multilateralism", and António Costa congratulated him for the European Council [1]. Vladimir Putin sent a message through the Kremlin [1]; Mexico's Claudia Sheinbaum and Canada's Mark Carney posted their congratulations [2]. Donald Trump and Javier Milei had not reacted publicly by Monday, BBC Brasil reported [1]. Recognition is a diplomatic form, not a legal one: Brazil's courts, not foreign capitals, settle the complaint.

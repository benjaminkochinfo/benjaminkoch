Bayer said an interim analysis of the Phase III DASL-HiCaP trial showed a statistically significant 58% reduction in the risk of metastasis or death when darolutamide (NUBEQA) was added to hormone therapy and radiation, compared with placebo [1]. Interim means the trial continues and the full data will follow. A relative reduction is not a promise for any one patient; the number that matters to you is the one your doctor reads from the full paper.

HeizOel24 says a detached house uses about 2,000 to 3,000 litres of heating oil a year, about 15 litres per square metre, with 10 to 20% of it going on hot water [1]. Desk arithmetic at Monday afternoon's average of €174.58 per 100 litres: 2,000 litres cost about €3,492 and 3,000 litres about €5,237. Your own meter reading beats any rule of thumb. Nothing here is advice.

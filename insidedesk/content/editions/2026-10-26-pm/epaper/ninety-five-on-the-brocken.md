The German Weather Service forecast gusts of around 70 km/h on the North Sea coast on Monday, up to 85 km/h on the North Frisian Islands at first, and 80 to 95 km/h on exposed summits such as the Brocken and the Fichtelberg, easing through the day [1]. Overnight into Tuesday the wind on the North Sea picks up again and turns south, with gusts of 60 to 70 km/h, as the warm front of storm NAOMI arrives [1]. Two storms in a week: good for the turbines, a test for the roof tiles.

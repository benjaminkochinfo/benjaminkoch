The Houthis said on Monday they attacked pumping stations on Saudi Arabia's East-West crude pipeline and Riyadh airport [1]. Saudi Arabia did not immediately confirm the attacks or any damage [1]. The pipeline, which takes crude from the Gulf side to the Red Sea, was shut for a time in September after drone strikes on pumping stations and then resumed [1].

**The reserve.** In the US, the Energy Department said on Friday it had authorised a release of 400,000 barrels from the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, to cover a supply disruption at a Louisiana refinery [2]. That is a small, targeted loan, which is exactly what a reserve is for.

**The tank.** Saudi Arabia will start storing crude oil in Singapore from the second half of 2027, the two countries' energy ministers said on Monday, to make supply in the region more resilient [2]. Inventory near the customer is the oldest insurance in the trade.

**The socket.** In Germany, the day-ahead power price averaged €116.57 per megawatt hour for delivery on Monday, after €13.12 on storm-blown Sunday and €30.12 on Saturday [3]. The wind fell; the price remembered gas. Nothing here is advice.

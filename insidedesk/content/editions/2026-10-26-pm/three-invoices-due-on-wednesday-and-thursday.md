Alphabet and Microsoft report after the US close on Wednesday; Amazon and Intel after the close on Thursday [1]. Around 160 S&P 500 companies report this week, and about 83% of those that reported by Friday beat earnings expectations, according to FactSet data cited by CNBC [1].

**The receipt that came first.** Meta reported last week: revenue up 26%, but capital spending of $34.91 billion in the quarter turned free cash flow negative, and the shares fell nearly 7% [2]. That is the template for this week. The demand is real; the question is the bill.

**What analysts are watching.** For Alphabet, revenue growth of about 25% is expected, after last quarter's raised capex forecast in a "supply-constrained environment" [1]. For Microsoft, Azure's pace [1]. For Intel, analysts expect revenue growth of about 20.5% [1].

**At home.** In Monday's ifo survey, German IT service providers were the bright spot of an otherwise weaker services sector [3]. Data centres need electricians and invoices as well as chips. Nothing here is investment advice.

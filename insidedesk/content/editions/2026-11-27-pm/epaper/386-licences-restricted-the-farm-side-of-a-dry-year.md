There are 386 restrictions on abstraction licences in England, down from 431 a week earlier, the Environment Agency reports [1]. Seven navigation closures and four restrictions remain on the Canal & River Trust network [1]. Confirmed drought incidents total 440 this year, with no new ones in the last two weeks [1]. Every one of those numbers is a form someone filed. That is how adaptation looks up close.

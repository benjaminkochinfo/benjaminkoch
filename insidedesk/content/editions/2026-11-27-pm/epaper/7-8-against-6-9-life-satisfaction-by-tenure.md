Owner occupiers in England scored a mean of 7.8 out of 10 for life satisfaction in 2025-26, private renters 7.3 and social renters 6.9, the English Housing Survey reports [1]. Anxiety ran the other way: 2.7, 3.4 and 3.6 [1]. Private renters' life satisfaction rose from 7.1 in 2024-25 [1]. The survey warns that income, age and work may explain much of the gap [1].

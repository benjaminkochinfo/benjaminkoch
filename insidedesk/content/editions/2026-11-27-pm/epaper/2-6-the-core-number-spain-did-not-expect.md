Spain's flash core inflation, excluding unprocessed food and energy, fell five tenths to 2.6% in November, INE said [1]. On the harmonised measure core stood at 2.7% [1]. FinanzaOnline reports that economists had expected 3.2% for core, which is why the outlet says the slowdown goes beyond energy [2]. Prices overall fell 0.6% on the month [1].

A flash is an advance figure, confirmed or revised next month [1]. Read it as a direction, not a verdict.

In 2025-26, 94% of owner occupiers in England said they were satisfied with their accommodation, 82% of private renters and 75% of social renters [1]. Since 2019-20, satisfaction fell slightly among owners, from 95%, and among social renters, from 78% [1]. Across all tenures, life satisfaction averaged 7.5, against 7.7 before the pandemic [1].

Small declines measured carefully over years are more useful than large ones asserted loudly. These are the careful kind.

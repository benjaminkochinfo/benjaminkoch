Andreas Lipkow of wallstreet:online described Friday's German buyers as selective and unwilling to pay any price [1]. Technology and carmakers stayed in demand: the first, he wrote, on hopes the AI trend continues, the second as dividend stories [1]. He read the day's US trade and inventory data as hints of slower consumption and therefore of a cautious Fed [1]. The Census Bureau figures he referred to show wholesale inventories up 0.9% in October [2].

A selective buyer is a healthier market than an eager one.

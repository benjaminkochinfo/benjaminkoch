The Bundestag passed the 2027 federal budget on Friday in a roll-call vote, 317 to 260 with no abstentions [1]. Core spending is €556 billion, €31 billion more than this year [2]. Net borrowing in the core budget rises from €98 billion to €123 billion; with the two special funds for infrastructure and the Bundeswehr, new federal debt comes to more than €210 billion, a sum topped only in 2021 [1].

The accompanying law is the part you will notice. The electricity tax falls to the EU minimum for all consumers in 2027 [1]. Basic-income rates are frozen until mid-2028, so a single adult keeps €563 a month [2]. Taxes on spirits rise at the turn of the year, and a levy of 25, 30 or 35 cents a litre on sugary drinks starts in 2028 [2].

Finance Minister Lars Klingbeil warned that the 2028 budget will be harder still; his ministry expects a gap of about €25 billion [2]. "We will not get around asking something of people," he said [1].

A budget is a promise with a date on it. The harder date is 2028.

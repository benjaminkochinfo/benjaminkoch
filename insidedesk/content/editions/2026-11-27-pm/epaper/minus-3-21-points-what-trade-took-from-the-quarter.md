Trade subtracted 3.21 percentage points from US GDP growth in the third quarter, the government reported on Wednesday; the economy still grew at a 4.3% annualised rate, carried by consumer spending and AI-related equipment investment [1]. Trade has not added to growth for four straight quarters [1]. Inventory investment added 1.64 points in the third quarter [1].

The arithmetic is simple and stubborn: what a country buys abroad does not count as its own output, however modern the machine.

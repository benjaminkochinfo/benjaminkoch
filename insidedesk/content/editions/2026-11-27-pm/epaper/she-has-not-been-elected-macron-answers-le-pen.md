Marine Le Pen, frontrunner in France's presidential race, called the budget talks 'underhanded political manoeuvring to thwart the democratic will of the French people', the BBC reports [1]. Last month she said she would significantly reduce French aid to Ukraine and EU contributions if elected [1]. Emmanuel Macron replied in Brussels that it is 'an odd kind of arrogance' to act as if she were already in charge, 'because, as things stand, she has not been elected' [1].

Many governments want the budget locked in before the 2027 elections [1]. That is the reason a seven-year plan is being argued about in a hurry.

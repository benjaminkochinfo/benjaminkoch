Talks on the EU's 2028–2034 budget ended after 12 hours on Thursday, with four countries walking out after several hours, the BBC reports [1]. The European Council has called a two-day summit for 17 and 18 December [1]. Each country effectively holds a veto [1]. A missed deadline in Brussels is a scheduling fact, not a collapse.

Ukraine launched nearly 500 strikes at Russian targets more than 500km away in 2026, more than in the first four years of the war combined, according to AFP analysis of ACLED data to 20 November, the Guardian reports [1]. Some targets were 1,500km from both sides [1]. Ukraine's foreign minister expressed 'regret' over 'unintended consequences' of an attack on Russian ships in a Libyan port [1]. Range brings reach, and with reach comes responsibility for where the debris falls.

Dark patterns are interface tricks that nudge people into choices against their interests. Singapore's CCS says AI can personalise them, for example false scarcity messages tailored to a consumer's online behaviour, and makes it easier to build websites that use such tactics [1]. The regulator admits the covert nature of some dark patterns 'makes them difficult to detect and address' [1]. In 2025 it found a car-detailing workshop had used ChatGPT to generate reviews for customers without their knowledge [1].

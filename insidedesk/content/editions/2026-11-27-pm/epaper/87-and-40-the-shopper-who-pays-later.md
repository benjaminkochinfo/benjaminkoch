Deloitte's survey of 1,200 consumers found 87% planned to buy during the Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales, up from 82% in 2025, CNBC reports [1]. Shoppers expect to spend an average of $624, similar to last year [1]. Some 40% of Generation Z shoppers plan to use buy now, pay later [1]. Deloitte's Brian McCarthy described people 'feeling the squeeze on prices' but 'still wanting to protect the holidays' [1].

Instalments are not a scandal. They are a bill with a later date. Write the date down.

Gavi said on Friday it has received a grant of up to $214 million from GiveWell to sustain and expand malaria vaccination in Africa [1]. If approved by Gavi's board in December, it would restore support to 85% of eligible populations in moderate- and high-transmission areas, after funding limits had cut planned support to 70% [1]. Sabin Vaccine Institute analysis finds three-dose coverage at 70% on average where the vaccine is being rolled out, and first-dose coverage at 80% [1]. Malaria vaccination is now routine in 25 African countries [1].

In 2024 there were an estimated 610,000 malaria deaths, around 74% of them children under five [1]. A grant is paperwork. Here the paperwork is doses.

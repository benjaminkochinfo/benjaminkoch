Brent crude futures traded at $96.60 a barrel on Friday afternoon, after four sessions of gains, and touched their highest since 9 November, Reuters reports via RTÉ [1]. WTI was at $85.14 [1]. Iran has continued to attack ships in the Strait of Hormuz, and Yemen's Houthis have struck Saudi infrastructure, including Ras Tanura [1]. ANZ analysts think Brent can push above $100 without a major new escalation, but that 'increasingly requires evidence of constrained deliverable supply' [1].

OPEC+ meets on Sunday [1]. An analyst's 'can' is not a price. It is a condition, and conditions are worth reading.

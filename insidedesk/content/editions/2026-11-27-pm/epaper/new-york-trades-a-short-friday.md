US markets reopened on Friday after Thursday's holiday for a session that ends early, at 19:00 Berlin time. After Europe's close, the S&P 500 was up 1.27% at 7,274.08 on Boursorama's board, after 7,182.78 on Wednesday [1]. Tokyo's Nikkei 225 had closed 2.11% higher at 58,711.11 [1].

These are intraday levels, not closing prices. Half-day sessions are thin; the move means less than the number suggests.

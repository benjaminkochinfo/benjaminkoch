Adobe expects Cyber Monday to remain the biggest online shopping day, with sales up 6.2% to $15.1 billion, Anadolu Agency reports [1]. Over the five days from Thanksgiving it forecasts $47.5 billion online, up 7.4% [1]. The NRF expects November-December holiday retail sales of $1.013 trillion, up 4.2% [1]. Oxford Economics expects 2.2% growth after inflation [1]. The gap between those two growth rates is the price level.

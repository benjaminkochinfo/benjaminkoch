Russia has carried out more than 1,000 strikes on Ukraine's energy system since September, targeting generation and high-voltage substations, Euronews reports [1]. After the attack on 20 November Ukraine halted electricity exports, and imports reached their highest level since mid-June, according to the Centre for Economic Strategy [1]. On Friday engineers in Dnipropetrovsk carried out emergency shutdowns after voltage fell in the high-voltage grid [1]. Europe's interconnectors are now part of Ukraine's air defence.

Wall Street's shortened Friday session closed at 19:00 Berlin time. The S&P 500 ended 1.6% higher at 7,297.49, the Nasdaq Composite gained 2.31% to 24,129.50 and the Dow added 591.99 points, or 1.18%, to 50,774.94 [1][2]. Over the week the Dow and the S&P 500 both rose more than 5% and the Nasdaq more than 6%, CNBC reported [1].

A strong week after a hard month is a fact about prices, not a verdict on the month. The invoices behind the AI trade arrive on their own schedule.

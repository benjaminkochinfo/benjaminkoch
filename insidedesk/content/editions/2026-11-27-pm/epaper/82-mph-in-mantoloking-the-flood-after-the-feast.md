Heavy rain and high winds swept the US east coast on Thanksgiving, cutting power to nearly a million customers at the peak and leading to water rescues from New York to Virginia, the Guardian reports [1]. New Jersey saw flooding at levels not seen since Hurricane Sandy in 2012; the strongest gust, 82 mph, was measured in Mantoloking [1]. Macy's postponed its parade for the first time [1]. St Louis had 7.3 inches of snow, and freeze warnings cover parts of the Deep South [1].

'The bulk of the storm impacts are behind us, but tidal flooding will continue today,' the National Weather Service said on Friday [1]. Clean-up is the part of weather that never trends.

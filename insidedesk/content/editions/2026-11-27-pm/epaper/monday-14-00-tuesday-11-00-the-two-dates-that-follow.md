Destatis lists its preliminary November consumer prices for Monday 30 November, published 'im Laufe des Tages' [2]. FinanzaOnline puts the time at 14:00 and reports that economists expect German harmonised inflation to slow to 2.7% from 2.9% [1]. Italy's Istat reports the same morning, starting from 5.6% on the harmonised measure in October [1]. The euro-area flash follows on Tuesday 1 December, from 4% in October, with expectations of 3.9% to 4% [1].

Expectations are not data. On Monday you will have data.

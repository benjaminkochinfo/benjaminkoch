Just over a third of two- and three-year-olds in England have had a flu vaccine, UKHSA figures show, and uptake among young children and over-65s is lower than at the same point last year, the BBC reports [1]. School-age children who missed the nasal spray at school can get it at some pharmacies from 1 December [1]. Children carry flu home. A spray in December protects a grandparent at Christmas.

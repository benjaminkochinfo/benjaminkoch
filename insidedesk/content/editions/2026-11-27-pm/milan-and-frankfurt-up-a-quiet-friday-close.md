Europe ended Friday the way it started Thursday: up. Boursorama's levels after the cash close show Milan's FTSE MIB up 1.89% at 49,275.60 and Madrid's IBEX 35 up 1.72% at 19,386.60 [1]. The Euro Stoxx 50 gained 1.18%, Amsterdam's AEX 1.24%, London's FTSE 100 0.84% and the DAX 0.86% to 25,431.29 [1]. Paris rose 0.58% and Zurich's SMI 0.58% [1].

[[chart:europe-friday-27pm]]

Andreas Lipkow, writing for wallstreet:online, described investors as willing to buy German blue chips, 'allerdings weiterhin sehr selektiv und nicht zu jedem Preis' — very selectively, and not at any price [2]. Technology and carmakers stayed in demand [2]. He pointed to Friday's US data: a wider goods trade deficit and stronger than expected wholesale inventories, which he reads as early signs of softer consumer momentum [2].

The level is the dull fact. The DAX ended Thursday at 25,215.52 and stood at 25,431.29 after Friday's close, by Boursorama's count [1]. The week ends with Milan and Madrid at the top of the table and every main index in the black. Breadth is a better sign than height.

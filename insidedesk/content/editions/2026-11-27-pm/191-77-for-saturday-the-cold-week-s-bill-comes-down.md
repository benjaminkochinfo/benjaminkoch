Friday's day-ahead auction set Saturday's German-Luxembourg electricity price at an average of €191.77 a megawatt hour, according to Energy-Charts data from the Bundesnetzagentur's SMARD platform [1]. Friday itself averaged €301.82, the cold week's dearest day [1]. Saturday's quarter-hours run from €138.91 at 23:45 to €232.03 at 17:30 [1].

[[chart:de-power-day-ahead-27pm]]

The fall is mostly the calendar. Offices and factories close, demand drops, and the expensive gas plants at the top of the merit order are needed less. It is not a turn in the energy shock. Brent crude futures traded at $96.60 a barrel on Friday afternoon and were set to snap a three-week losing streak, Reuters reported via RTÉ, after attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz and on Saudi infrastructure [2]. Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery temporarily halted some operations after a projectile struck it on Thursday, killing a Nepali worker [2].

So the two numbers point in different directions for different reasons. Oil prices a war; Saturday's power prices a weekend. You pay both, but only one of them you can shift by running the washing machine at night.

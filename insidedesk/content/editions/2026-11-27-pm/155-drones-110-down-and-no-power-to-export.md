Since Thursday evening Russia launched 155 strike drones at Ukraine, 51 of them jet-powered, and Ukrainian air defences shot down 110, Euronews reports, citing the air force [1]. Prime Minister Sergey Koretsky said strikes hit the railway, the energy sector, civilian enterprises and homes in nine regions [1]. Two people were killed at an enterprise in Nizhyn, an 86-year-old woman in Izium and one person in Kryvyi Rih [1].

The figure behind the night is in the grid. After the attack on 20 November Ukraine completely halted electricity exports, and imports reached their highest since mid-June, according to the Centre for Economic Strategy [1]. Since September Russia has carried out more than 1,000 strikes on the energy system, including high-voltage substations [1]. Ukrainian drones in turn struck a Rosatom heat and power plant and a synthetic rubber plant in Yefremov, which Kyiv says makes solid rocket fuel components [1].

Zelenskyy said on Thursday night that Russia was preparing 'a new mass strike' and asked people to heed missile alarms [2]. Donald Trump wrote that he had asked Vladimir Putin to pull back 'all Military aggression against territories throughout Europe' [2]. The warning and the request arrived on the same night. Only one of them came with a count.

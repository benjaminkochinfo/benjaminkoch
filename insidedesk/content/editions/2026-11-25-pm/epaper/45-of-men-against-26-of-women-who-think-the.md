Men with depression use AI for mental health problems more often than women (41% against 35%), according to the 2026 Deutschland-Barometer Depression [1]. 45% of men think the AI helpful enough to do without professional treatment, against 26% of women, and men report twice as often that they felt worse afterwards (16% against 8%) [1]. 40% of all users have come across false information [1]. Foundation chair Ulrich Hegerl says there are no good studies proving such models safe or effective for depression; the right places remain psychiatrists, psychotherapists and family doctors [1]. In Germany the Info-Telefon Depression is 0800 33 44 5 33 [1].

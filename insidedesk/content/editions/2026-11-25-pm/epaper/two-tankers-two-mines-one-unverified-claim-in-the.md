The navy of Iran's Revolutionary Guards said in a statement carried by state media that two oil tankers attempting to pass through what it called an unauthorised route in the Strait of Hormuz struck naval mines, causing explosions and fires in their engine rooms [1]. It said the ships had tried to cross undetected, near the southern and eastern shores of the strait, and warned crews sailing with navigation systems switched off not to trust 'the lies of American officials' [1]. The claim could not be independently verified [1]. The tankers were not named.

The statement fits a pattern of pressure on shipping since the war began in February, and it came the same day as the Houthi attack claim against Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura [2]. Two unverified claims do not make one verified fact. Insurers, which set war-risk premiums by voyage, will wait for ship names and port reports, and so should you.

Attorney General Todd Blanche signalled on Wednesday that he would not investigate Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang; asked whether he had, he replied 'For what?' and called Nvidia 'a good corporate citizen' [1]. Donald Trump told the New York Post on Tuesday he would be willing to pardon Huang but did not think it necessary [1]. The consumer group Public Citizen has asked for an inquiry into suspiciously timed prediction-market bets on Huang's reinstatement, including one account that made $128,000 [1]. Lawmakers of both parties are less relaxed. Courts and committees are slower than press gaggles, and they usually last longer.

The national average for a kilowatt hour at a public EV charging station in the US stayed at 42 cents in AAA's latest weekly report, while a gallon of regular fell 18 cents in a week to $3.72 [1]. Hawaii is the dearest state for public charging at 55 cents, Kansas the cheapest at 32 [1]. By Wednesday regular stood at $3.5866 [1]. Pump prices follow crude within days; charging prices follow electricity contracts and station costs, which move slowly in both directions. Slow prices are dull. Dull is easier to budget.

Wall Street fell on Wednesday under the weight of AI stocks, as the enthusiasm over the return of Jensen Huang to Nvidia faded further, AP reported; its headline at 17:12 Berlin time had the S&P 500 down 2.7% [1]. Worries about high inflation dragging on the economy added to the selling [1].

The day's data gave both sides something. Growth in the third quarter was revised up to 4.3% annualised, but October's PCE prices were 3.8% higher than a year earlier [2]. A market analyst in Frankfurt called it not the data package investors had wanted, and noted the gap between the mood on the trading floor and the real economy, where the AI investment is already visible in growth [2].

The session runs until 22:00 Berlin time and closes early on Friday after Thursday's holiday. A morning figure is not a close; read it as weather, not climate.

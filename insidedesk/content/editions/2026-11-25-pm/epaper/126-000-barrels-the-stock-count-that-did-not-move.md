US crude stocks rose by 126,000 barrels in the week to 20 November, according to American Petroleum Institute figures cited by Reuters; eight analysts had expected a rise of about 1.5 million [1]. In another week, a smaller-than-expected build would be a story. On Wednesday Brent rose to $93.60 a barrel at 16:16 Berlin time on the Houthi claim against Ras Tanura instead [2]. The official government count usually follows the API figures. Inventories are the dullest number in oil, and the one that decides whether fear lasts longer than an afternoon.

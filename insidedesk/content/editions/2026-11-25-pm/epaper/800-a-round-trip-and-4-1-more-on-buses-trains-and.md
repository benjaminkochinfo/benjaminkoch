Round-trip domestic flights booked for Thanksgiving cost $800 on average, 17% more than last year, according to AAA's booking data [1]. AAA expects 5.96 million people to fly over the holiday period, 0.2% fewer than in 2025 [1].

The only growing mode is the slow one. AAA projects 2.6 million Americans will take buses, trains and cruises, 4.1% more than last year [1]. Nearly 90% of holiday travellers still go by road, and last Thanksgiving AAA answered more than half a million breakdown calls for flat tyres, dead batteries, fuel and lockouts [1]. Hertz expects Tuesday and Wednesday to be its busiest pick-up days, with Orlando, Phoenix, Las Vegas, Atlanta and Denver in highest demand [1].

Desk arithmetic: a family of four flying round trip at the average fare pays about $3,200, roughly $465 more than a year ago. That is the kind of figure that turns a flight into a nine-hour drive.

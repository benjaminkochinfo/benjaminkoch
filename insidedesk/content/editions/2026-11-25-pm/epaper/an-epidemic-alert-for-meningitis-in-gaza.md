Gaza's health ministry declared an epidemic alert on Tuesday over a surge in meningitis, warning that hundreds of thousands of people are at risk [1]. Overcrowded shelters, damaged water systems and a health service working with little are the conditions in which such infections spread. Israeli strikes continued on Wednesday, including one that killed a senior Hamas security official with his wife and two children, according to Gaza's interior ministry and medics [2]. Vaccination, clean water and functioning clinics are the remedy; all three need access more than speeches.

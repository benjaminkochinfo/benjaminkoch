Carles Puigdemont, the former Catalan leader, said on Wednesday he would return to Spain on 12 December, after this weekend's national election, ending nearly a decade of self-imposed exile in Belgium [1]. Spain's Supreme Court applied the amnesty law earlier this week and shelved the criminal case against him; he plans to present a renewed independence proposal on 17 December [1]. Spaniards vote on Sunday in a snap election triggered by protests over the cost of living [2]. An amnesty is a law, not a reconciliation. Elections decide what follows.

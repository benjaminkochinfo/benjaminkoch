The World Food Programme warns that the war in Ukraine is disrupting grain shipments and putting food aid for 7.3 million people at risk [1]. 'Grain is available, but it is not reaching the people who need it', said Luke Lindberg of the WFP; silos in Ukraine and Poland are full, while families in Somalia, Sudan and South Sudan go hungry [1]. Yemen, he said, imports 90% of its food [1]. Odesa, Ukraine's grain gateway, was hit again overnight. The shortage is in shipping and safe passage, not in the fields.

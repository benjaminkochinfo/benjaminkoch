Italy has told Ukraine it was wrong to attack Russian ships in Libyan waters and warned Kyiv not to spread the war to new areas, Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani told Corriere della Sera [1]. 'It was a mistake to violate Libya's sovereignty', Tajani said, adding that Rome had received no advance warning [1]. A Ukrainian drone struck a Russian ship at Benghazi, killing two Libyan naval officers, and the Libyan National Army has vowed to retaliate [2]. Allies can agree on the war and still disagree on its map. Saying so in public is how alliances keep working.

The Deutscher Wetterdienst's weekly hazard outlook, issued at 11:00 on Wednesday, expects snow in south-eastern Bavaria on Thursday: 5 to 10 centimetres, locally up to 15, in 12 to 18 hours [1]. Light drizzle with glaze cannot be ruled out locally in the south and east [1]. Friday and Saturday bring no notable weather [1].

The next risk comes from the other end of the country. On Sunday evening and into the night to Monday the north-west and north face an increased risk of slippery roads; freezing rain with glaze is possible on some stretches but rated of low probability, and may linger on Monday morning in the north [1]. Tuesday is expected to be quiet [1].

It is a reminder that winter hazards are not only about amounts. Five centimetres of snow in Passau is routine; a millimetre of freezing rain in Bremen, on a Monday morning commute, is not. The same cold week sits behind Thursday's power auction, which averages €291.77 a megawatt hour (see the lead). The forecast is the cheapest piece of infrastructure the country owns. It is worth reading before Sunday.

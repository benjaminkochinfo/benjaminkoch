If your house heats with oil, the ledger this morning has one line that matters, and it is quieter than the headlines.

**The price.** FastEnergy's national average for standard heating oil, for a 3,000-litre order including delivery and VAT, was €174.23 per 100 litres at 05:10 this morning [1]. That is down 83 cents on yesterday and 78 cents on a week ago [1]. Further back the picture is less kind: €165.54 a month ago, €133.17 three months ago and €95.90 a year ago, a rise of 81.68% over the year [1].

**The map.** Where you live matters almost as much as when. FastEnergy's state table put Hamburg lowest this morning at €165.92 and Hesse highest at €178.97 [1]. Berlin, Bremen and Brandenburg each fell by around €2.40 to €2.50 overnight; Saarland and Thuringia did not move [1]. The company puts the regional gaps down to supply routes, competition between dealers and local demand [1].

Desk arithmetic: the €13.05 per 100 litres between Hamburg and Hesse is about €390 on a 3,000-litre order.

**What's in the litre.** Most of the price is not the dealer's. In FastEnergy's breakdown, purchase costs are about two-thirds, VAT about 16%, the CO₂ charge about 8.6% and the fixed oil tax 6.14 cents a litre; the dealer's own margin and costs are 2.48% [1]. That is why a move in crude never passes through to your bill one for one.

**What you can do.** The advice is unglamorous: watch the market, avoid emergency orders, order a larger amount if you can, and consider a joint order with neighbours [1]. Ordering together spreads the delivery cost; a gauge checked now avoids the phone call in January. Nothing here is investment advice.

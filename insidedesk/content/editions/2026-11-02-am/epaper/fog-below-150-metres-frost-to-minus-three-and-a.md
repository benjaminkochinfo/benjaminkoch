High pressure and ageing polar air set Germany's weather this week, the Deutscher Wetterdienst wrote at 05:00 this morning, with a weak front from the North Sea bringing a little rain to the northwest [1]. Nothing dramatic is forecast, and quiet weeks are when the small preparations get done.

This morning, the centre and south have patchy dense fog, with visibility below 150 metres, and light frost down to -3 degrees across much of the southern half [1]. Today's highs run from 4 to 12 degrees, with little wind except on the North Sea, where gusts around 55 km/h from the north to north-east reach the East Frisian Islands into Tuesday night [1]. On the High Black Forest summits, easterly gusts of 60 to 75 km/h are possible [1].

Tuesday stays cloudy, with drizzle from the west across the northwest to the north-east and highs of about 4 degrees on high ground in the south and 11 on the North Sea [1]. Wednesday is dry and murky at the Alpine edge, at 5 degrees, and up to 10 elsewhere [1]. Thursday brings stubborn fog first, then sunny spells; on the night into Friday, light frost of 0 to -4 degrees is widespread away from the coast and the far north [1].

So: a scraper in the car, dipped headlights in the fog, and a radiator that is bled before the cold arrives rather than after. The forecast is the DWD's; the windscreen is yours.

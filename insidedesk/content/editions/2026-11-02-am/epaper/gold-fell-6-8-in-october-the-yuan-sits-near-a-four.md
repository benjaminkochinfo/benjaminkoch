The euro was flat at $1.1156 early on Monday and the dollar was barely changed at 157.62 yen, Reuters reported [1]. The action was elsewhere.

Gold bounced 0.6% to $3,898 an ounce after falling 6.8% in October, Reuters reported. It had hit a one-year low amid talk that stressed investors were selling to meet margin calls [1]. A safe haven gets sold when people need cash quickly, because it is the easiest thing to sell.

The Chinese yuan stayed near a more than four-year high against the dollar, Investing.com reported, helped by exporters converting foreign-currency earnings into yuan [2]. That is an exchange rate moved by invoices being paid, not by speeches.

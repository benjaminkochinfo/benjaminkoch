European natural gas fell to €102.5 per megawatt hour on Monday as diplomatic efforts in the Middle East eased fears of further escalation, Trading Economics reported [1]. Iran and Qatar held talks in Doha for a third consecutive day [1].

The price is the noisy part. The quieter number is Europe's gas storage: 74.62% full as of 1 November, Trading Economics said, a historically low level going into winter [1]. LNG supplies from the Persian Gulf remain severely disrupted while shipping through the Strait of Hormuz is threatened, and withdrawals have already begun in several countries as temperatures fall [1].

A tank does not react to a headline. It fills or empties one cargo and one cold night at a time, and that is what you can check each morning. Nothing here is investment advice.

After three years of price cuts, Stadtwerke Hamm is raising its prices from 1 January 2027, blaming higher procurement costs, especially for gas [1]. In the basic gas tariff, the unit price rises by 2.68 cents per kWh and the standing charge by €29.75 a year, gross [1]. For a household using 20,000 kWh, that is €47.15 more a month, or 23.54% [1].

The same household moving to one of the utility's special gas products would pay only €12.31 a month more than before [1]. Electricity in the basic tariff rises by 3.87 cents per kWh plus €23.80 a year; at 3,000 kWh that is €11.66 a month, or 12.51% [1].

Desk arithmetic: the gap between staying and switching on gas is €34.84 a month, about €418 a year. The cheapest tariff is often the one next door, at the same company.

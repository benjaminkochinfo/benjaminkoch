Since Sunday, 1 November, Latvia has presided over the UN Security Council, for the first time in its history [1]. The presidency rotates monthly in English alphabetical order; Greece had October, Liberia takes December [1].

Its ambassador, Sanita Pavļuta-Deslandes, says about 90% of the Council's agenda is mandated and regular meetings, leaving room for "one or two signature events" [1]. Latvia's are Ukraine and technology: the Council's new informal expert group on technology, peace and security, set up on 23 October at Latvia's and Greece's initiative, holds its first meeting this month [1]. On 3 November Latvia also presides over the election of judges to the International Court of Justice, and a vote on extending the Yemen sanctions regime is expected [1].

The Council has adopted 19 resolutions this year, the ministry says [1]. Small states do not win arguments at the UN by volume. They win them by chairing the meeting well.

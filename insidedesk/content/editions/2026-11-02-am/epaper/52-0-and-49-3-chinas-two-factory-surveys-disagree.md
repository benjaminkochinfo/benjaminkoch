The RatingDog China manufacturing PMI read 52.0 for October, just under September's 52.1 and in line with forecasts, Investing.com reported. It is the 11th month in a row above the 50 line [1]. Output grew at its fastest pace since April [1].

The official NBS index fell to 49.3 from 50.1, missing forecasts of 49.9 [1]. The two surveys cover different firms. The official one leans towards large and state-owned manufacturers; RatingDog has more small and private ones [1].

One detail matters more than either headline. Input costs rose for a 16th month, but selling prices fell for the second time in three months as competition pushed firms to discount [1]. Final October factory readings from India, Australia, the UK and the US are due later on Monday, NDTV Profit noted [2].

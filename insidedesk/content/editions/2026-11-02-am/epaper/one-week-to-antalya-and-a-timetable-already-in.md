The UN climate conference, COP31, runs in Antalya from 9 to 20 November, the United Nations says [1]. The leaders' summit is on 11 and 12 November [2]. The detailed session programme is still to come, but the Turkish presidency has printed a theme for each day [2].

Monday 9 November is food, agriculture and health; Tuesday energy and transport; Wednesday zero waste and methane; Thursday resilient cities and disaster risk management; Friday 13 November finance, trade and carbon markets [2]. The second week moves through science and industry, then oceans and land use, and the last themed day, 19 November, is "İmece: Enhancing Implementation" – capacity building, investment-ready projects and access to finance [2].

Read the last line twice. A conference that ends on implementation is admitting where the gap is. Judge it in a fortnight by projects with a budget, not by the leaders' paragraphs.

Apple publishes its fiscal fourth-quarter results today at 1:30pm Pacific time, which is 22:30 in Berlin now that both sides have left summer time; the call with new chief executive John Ternus and finance chief Kevan Parekh follows at 23:00 [1].

The quarter ran from 28 June to 26 September and includes only launch-week sales of the iPhone 18 Pro [1]. On the previous quarter's call, Parekh guided to total revenue growth of 9 to 11%, with iPhone growth in the mid-teens despite currency headwinds and supply constraints [1]. On last year's $102.5 billion, MacRumors puts that at $111.7 billion to $113.7 billion [1]. Watch for what management says about memory pricing and supply [1].

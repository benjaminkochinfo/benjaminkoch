Bulgaria held the second round of its presidential and vice-presidential election on Sunday. By midnight, Sofia time, the Central Election Commission had begun publishing preliminary results, with 52.42% of polling-station protocols processed by the district commissions [1]. On that count, Iliana Iotova and her running mate Kiril Valchev had 57.95%; Andrey Gyurov and Georgi Kandev had 39.01% [1].

The third line is the one other countries lack: "I do not support any candidate", at 3.04% [1]. It is a small, formal way to turn up and say no, and it is counted like everything else.

These are preliminary figures on just over half the protocols, not a result. For you, the habit is the same as in America this week: read the share counted before the share won.

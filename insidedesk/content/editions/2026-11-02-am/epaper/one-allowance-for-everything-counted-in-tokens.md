The most consequential product change of the weekend arrived as a help-centre article. "Starting in December," OpenAI writes, the usage allowance on Plus, Pro and Business plans "will be shared across ChatGPT and Codex", as the agent features of its Work mode move into the main ChatGPT experience [1].

Usage will be measured in tokens, the units a model reads and writes, and how fast you spend them depends on the model and the task: more advanced models and complex tasks use more [1]. Chat with the Instant models will not count against the allowance [1]. Your remaining balance and reset times will sit under Settings → Usage; when it runs out, you can wait, upgrade, or buy credits [1]. OpenAI says it expects most users "won't need to wait for their usage to reset more often than they do now" [1].

The Decoder reads it as the end of flat-rate prompting for heavy users, noting that OpenAI recently doubled limits on its pricier plans and that users currently choose between two operating modes and 11 model settings [2]. Anthropic has already combined chat and "Cowork" in Claude, it adds [2].

The constructive reading is the boring one. A meter is not a betrayal; it is an invoice you can read. Once the December date is set, check Settings → Usage after a normal week before deciding whether you need a bigger plan.

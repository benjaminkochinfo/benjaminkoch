On Sunday Serbia's Republic Electoral Commission published results from all 8,392 polling stations in the snap parliamentary election of 25 October, after voting was repeated at 53 of them [1]. The Student List won 53.01% of the vote; "Aleksandar Vučić – United Serbia" won 38.98% [1]. Ivica Dačić's list took 2.13% [1]. Turnout was 4,444,310 voters, or 68.89% of the register [1].

The rerun was small and specific. At 31 polling stations local commissions had ruled that the result could not be established; at 22 the vote had been annulled [1]. In all, 24,438 citizens were entitled to vote again, from 07:00 to 20:00 [1]. At one station in Zemun the reason was plain arithmetic: the number of voters who signed the register did not match the number of ballots, Tanjug reported [1].

Desk arithmetic: the preliminary count, from 8,338 stations, had the Student List on 53.04% and the Vučić list on 38.97% [1]. A week of complaints, annulments and reruns moved each by three hundredths of a point or one hundredth.

That is what a working count looks like: disputes settled station by station, in public, by the commissions the law names, before anyone sits in parliament. The seat allocation follows from these totals; the commission's own seat figures are the ones to wait for.

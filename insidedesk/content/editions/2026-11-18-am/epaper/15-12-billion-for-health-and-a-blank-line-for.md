The 2027 federal health budget is set at €15.12 billion, about €6.65 billion less than this year's €21.77 billion, G+G reports [1]. The manufacturer discount on patented medicines is due to rise from 7% to 15.5% from 2027; an extended "location clause" is meant to relieve firms that research and employ in Germany, at an estimated €750 million to €1.3 billion [1]. For now the economy ministry's budget shows only an empty line for it [1]. "Instead of clear funding there is so far only an unbudgeted budget heading," the Pharma Deutschland association said [1].

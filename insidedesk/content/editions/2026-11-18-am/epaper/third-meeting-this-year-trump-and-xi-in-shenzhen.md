Leaders of the 21 APEC economies meet in Shenzhen today and tomorrow, with China hosting for the first time in 12 years, CNA reports [1]. Donald Trump is due to meet Xi Jinping separately, their third meeting this year, and on Thursday the two are to sit down with Vladimir Putin in what China's foreign ministry calls a "China-Russia-US heads of state meeting", the first of its kind [1].

The trilateral will get the pictures. The bilateral has the invoices. After the leaders met in Beijing in May, the White House said China had committed to buy at least $17 billion of American farm goods a year on top of soybeans [1]. China bought $7.2 billion of non-soybean US farm goods between January and September, according to a Reuters analysis cited by CNA [1]. The trade truce was extended on 9 November and runs until 10 January [1]. "I think best case, the truce renewal continues beyond Jan 10," said Drew Thompson of Singapore's RSIS [1].

China wants a Shenzhen declaration and a roadmap for a Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific, an idea first endorsed in 2006 [1]. A US official called it "more an agenda than it is a kind of destination" [1]. Watch the purchase figures, not the handshake.

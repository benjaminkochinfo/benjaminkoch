India's foreign ministry said 21 of the 24 Indian nationals aboard the Panama-flagged LPG tanker Tara Gas, attacked in the Strait of Hormuz on 16 November, had reached the Omani port of Khasab safely; two injured crew were in hospital and one Indian national remained missing, CNBC TV18 reports [1]. Iran's Revolutionary Guard claims six tankers have caught fire after striking mines in the strait, a figure no maritime authority has confirmed, Straits.live notes [2]. Seafarers carry the risk that markets average away.

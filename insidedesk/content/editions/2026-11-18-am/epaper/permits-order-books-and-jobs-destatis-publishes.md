Destatis's weekly preview lists three releases for today: building permits, manufacturing order stocks and quarterly labour-market statistics [1]. They arrive a day after ZEW analysts cited "increasing manufacturing orders" as a support for Germany's recovery, while their construction outlook moved deeper into negative territory [2]. Today's permits and order-stock figures are where that claim can be checked against the paperwork. Expectations are a survey; an order book is a file.

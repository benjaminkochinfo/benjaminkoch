The Bundestag's budget committee ended its final session before dawn on 13 November after more than 15 hours, approving 2027 spending of €560.9 billion and net borrowing of almost €123 billion in the core budget, G+G reports [1]. The committee dealt with more than 1,500 motions, said CDU/CSU spokesman Christian Haase [1]. Two items stayed open: funding the electricity-tax cut for everyone, which the coalition committee worked on overnight [2], and a "location clause" for pharma companies, for which the economy ministry's budget so far shows only an empty line [1]. An empty line is honest. It is also a bill not yet addressed.

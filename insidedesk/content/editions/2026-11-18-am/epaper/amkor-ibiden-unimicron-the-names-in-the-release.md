Nvidia's results release lists plans with supply-chain partners Amkor Technology, Ibiden, King Yuan Electronics, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, Siliconware Precision Industries and Unimicron "to scale critical manufacturing capabilities" [1]. These are packaging, test and substrate companies, mostly in Taiwan, Japan and Korea. On the call, Kress said that "advanced packaging or memory" was "coming together" with chip supply [2]. If AI demand has a physical limit this year, it lives in their factories, not in a keynote.

Japan's October exports of semiconductor-manufacturing equipment rose 49.7% on a year earlier, NDTV Profit reports, as total exports reached a record ¥11.77 trillion [1][2]. That is the upstream end of the AI build-out: tools sold to chipmakers in Taiwan, Korea, China and the US months before the chips themselves are sold. It is also the part most exposed to export licences, which is why the figure is worth tracking month by month rather than celebrating once.

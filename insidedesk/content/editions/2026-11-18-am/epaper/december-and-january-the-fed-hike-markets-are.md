The Federal Reserve held its benchmark rate at 3.75–4.00% last month, and futures now price a 25-basis-point rise in December and another in January, Reuters reports, citing CME FedWatch [1]. Economists expect core PCE inflation, the Fed's preferred gauge, at 3.2% in the year to October [1]. Higher US rates tend to lift the dollar and tighten conditions well beyond America, including for European companies borrowing in dollars. Nothing here is investment advice.

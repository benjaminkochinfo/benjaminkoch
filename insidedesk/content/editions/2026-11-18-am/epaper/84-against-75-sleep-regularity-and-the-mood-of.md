Researchers led by UC San Francisco and UC San Diego analysed about 23,000 participants who wore a smart ring for an average of 154 days, UCSF reports [1]. On a 0–100 Sleep Regularity Index, people with few or no depressive symptoms averaged 84; those with severe symptoms, 75 [1]. Roughly, the first group's bed and wake times shift by about an hour a day, the second's by about an hour and a half, said first author Severine Soltani [1]. Senior author Ashley Mason notes irregular sleep may be a cause or a symptom of poor mental health [1]. The study was funded partly by the ring's maker, and Mason has consulted for it [1].

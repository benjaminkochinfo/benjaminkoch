Item 126 of the first COP31 draft cover decision welcomes an "Antalya Compact for Energy Efficient Buildings" aiming to cut the operational energy intensity of the global building stock by at least 25% by 2035 against 2020 levels [1]. The draft also proposes electrification goals of "35% by 2035", Chris Bowen said, "taking into account national circumstances" [2]. Buildings are where climate policy meets household budgets: insulation, heat pumps, windows. The fossil-fuel paragraph will make the headlines; the buildings paragraph is the one most likely to reach your radiator.

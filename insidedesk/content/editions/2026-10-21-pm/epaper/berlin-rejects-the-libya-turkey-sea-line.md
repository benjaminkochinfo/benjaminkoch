Libya's House of Representatives has approved the memorandum with Turkey that draws maritime zones in the eastern Mediterranean, a Greek reporter noted at Berlin's government press conference [1]. The foreign office said it had taken note and agreed with the EU Commission and Council presidents [1]. Turkey and Libya must respect EU members' sovereign rights, and sea boundaries must follow international law and involve all coastal states, it said [1]. Asked about sanctions, the spokeswoman said that was getting ahead of things; Berlin rejects the memorandum and will discuss next steps in the EU [1]. Sea lines matter because gas fields follow them.

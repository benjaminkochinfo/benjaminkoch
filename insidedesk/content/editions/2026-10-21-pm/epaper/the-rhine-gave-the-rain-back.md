The Rhine gauge at Kaub read 11 cm at 16:15 on Wednesday, according to the federal waterways administration [1]. It had risen from 7 cm on Sunday afternoon to 21 cm early on Tuesday, then fell back through Wednesday [1]. The lowest low-water level in the station's record was 25 cm, on 22 October 2018 [1]. Desk arithmetic: today's reading is 14 cm below that. A gauge reading is measured from a fixed gauge zero, not from the riverbed, so it is not the depth of the shipping channel.

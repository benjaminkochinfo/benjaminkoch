Large demonstrations are announced in Germany for 31 October [1]. On Wednesday the interior ministry drew a line between peaceful school pupils' protests and a violent extremist scene that may use them [1]. Federal and state agencies are sharing information, including with France and Italy, the ministry said [1]. The domestic intelligence service is watching relevant websites and social media; the federal police can help state police and turn back people at the border who intend to join violence [1]. It gave no estimate of numbers [1]. Peaceful protest is the default; policing should be built for that.

Germany's justice and finance ministries published a joint discussion paper on insurance against natural hazards on Wednesday [1]. The proposal: new residential building policies would only be offered with cover for floods and other natural hazards, and existing policies would be switched over on a fixed date [1]. The justice ministry said 98% of residential buildings already have building insurance, so extending those policies would cover most homes [1]. Different deductibles may be offered [1]. Länder and associations will now be consulted [1]. It is a default, not a mandate; defaults tend to work.

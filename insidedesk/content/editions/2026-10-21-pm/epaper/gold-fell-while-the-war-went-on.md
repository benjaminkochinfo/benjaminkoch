Spot gold slipped 0.2% to $4,118.18 an ounce early on Wednesday, its fourth daily fall, leaving it about 26% below its January record and about 22% lower since the war began [1]. The reason given is oil: expensive crude keeps inflation and further Federal Reserve rises in view, and gold pays no interest [1]. Futures put roughly a 16% chance on a Fed increase at the 27–28 October meeting [1]. A safe haven is only safe against the risk it was bought for.

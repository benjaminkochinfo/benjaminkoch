A litre of heating oil cost €1.74 on the German average on Wednesday morning, a good half cent more than on Tuesday, according to the trader HeizOel24 [1]. Hamburg was cheapest at €1.70 and Rhineland-Palatinate dearest at €1.78 [1]. Desk arithmetic: on a 3,000-litre delivery, that eight-cent gap is €240. Gasoil, the exchange price behind heating oil and diesel, was about $30 a tonne above Tuesday evening [1]. Austria averaged €1.83 [1]. Nothing here is advice.

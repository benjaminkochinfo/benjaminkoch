At GTC Berlin on Wednesday, Nvidia announced the OpenShell Reference Architecture, a design for building, containing and monitoring AI agents on company infrastructure [1]. It combines its open-source OpenShell runtime with BlueField data-processing units running Nvidia's Sentry software for "out-of-band monitoring of agent behavior and in-silicon policy enforcement" [1]. Nvidia names Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft, OpenAI, SAP and Siemens among those working with it [1]. "Safety and control must be built into the computing architecture," Jensen Huang said [1]. It is a company's own claim; the test is an audit log, not a keynote.

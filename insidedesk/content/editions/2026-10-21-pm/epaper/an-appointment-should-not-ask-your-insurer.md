Health minister Carsten Linnemann plans to present a primary-care reform "probably in November", with GP-first referrals and an appointment guarantee [1]. On Wednesday, CSU and SPD politicians said public or private insurance should no longer decide who gets a specialist slot first [1]. The statutory insurers' federation called need-based booking "overdue"; the private insurers' association said its members are about 10% of patients and cannot cause the waits of the other 90% [1]. Depending on the study, publicly insured patients wait 36 to 41 days on average [1]. Part of the fix may sit in the booking software: Holetschek suggested online portals stop asking [1].

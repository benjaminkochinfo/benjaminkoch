EU gas storage stood at 74.62% full on Wednesday, 15.9 percentage points below the five-year average of 90.5% for the date, according to GasRadar's daily briefing [1]. The trend is flat, at about 0.01 points a day, as injection season winds down [1]. Poland is 98.7% full and Portugal 95.5%; Germany is at 61.2% and the Netherlands 64.7% [1]. The front-month Dutch TTF contract fell 3.38% to €85.06 per megawatt hour, the briefing said, after a rally from €73.51 on 5 October [1]. A storage figure is the dullest number in energy, and the one that decides how a cold February goes.

Speaking at an officer graduation ceremony in the Negev on Wednesday, Benjamin Netanyahu said he had directed the Israeli army, the Mossad and the security services to topple the government in Tehran [1]. "There is only one way to remove this threat once and for all," he said [1]. CBS News notes that he did not say how [1].

The same report carries the other side of the day. Iran's foreign minister, Abbas Araghchi, said indirect talks with Washington over a seven-day ceasefire proposal were continuing through mediators including Pakistan and Qatar, and that the US wanted to add clauses [1]. "The negotiating table remains in place," he said [1]. Citing the Financial Times, CBS also reports that President Trump blocked a planned multi-day Israeli strike operation for the weekend of 10 October [1]. And Egypt's president said Cairo's proposed military support for Riyadh was to "defend Saudi territory" and did not include an attack on Yemen [1].

A sentence at a graduation is a speech. A mediator's flight schedule, a clause under negotiation and a strike that was called off are the machinery. Tonight the machinery is still running, slowly, and that is the part to watch. Nothing here is advice.

Germany's Federal Joint Committee (G-BA) on Friday updated the requirements for its disease-management programme for rheumatoid arthritis, the first scheduled revision, based on an IQWiG review of 16 evidence-based guidelines [1]. Glucocorticoids are to be tapered off within three months instead of six [1]. Coordinating doctors will check vaccination status at least once a year, record smoking status at every consultation, and recommend moderate endurance and strength training; special diets are out for lack of evidence [1]. Interstitial lung disease gets its own section [1]. The health ministry still has to review the decision; contracts then have a year to adapt [1].

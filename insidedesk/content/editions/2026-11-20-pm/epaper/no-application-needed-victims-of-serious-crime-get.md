The Bundesrat on Friday cleared a law on psychosocial support for victims of violent and sexual crimes through criminal proceedings [1][2]. Children, adolescents and people with disabilities who are victims of serious crimes are to receive this support automatically, without having to apply [1]. Adult victims of a serious crime get a right to free support without first proving they are especially vulnerable, and victims of domestic violence get both free psychosocial support and free legal representation [1]. Police, prosecutors and courts must tell victims the support exists, and pay for the companions rises [1].

Psychosocial trial support is non-legal: information and steadiness through a criminal case, with help on understanding the proceedings [1]. Since 2017 only minors and particularly vulnerable adults had a claim [1]. Removing the paperwork matters most for people least able to fill it in.

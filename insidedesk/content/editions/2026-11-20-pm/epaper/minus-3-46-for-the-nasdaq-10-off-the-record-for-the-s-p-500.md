After Frankfurt had closed, New York kept selling. At 19:34 CET the S&P 500 stood at 7,025.36, down 2.10% on the day, the Nasdaq Composite at 23,052.54, down 3.46%, and the Dow at 48,769.79, down 0.67% [1]. That puts the Nasdaq 0.82% below where it began the year [1]. CNBC reported both the S&P 500 and the Dow more than 10% below their closing records [2]; as AP notes, the S&P 500 only enters what professionals call a correction if it closes there [3].

Anthropic, up 17% on its debut on Thursday, was down about 12%; AMD fell 11%, Nvidia more than 7% and Micron more than 12% [2]. Walmart, Costco, Johnson & Johnson and Procter & Gamble rose [2]. The VIX volatility index stood at 31.98 [1]. In Chile, Fed Governor Lisa Cook said waiting longer to raise rates "risks getting behind the curve" [2].

Ten per cent is a round number, not a verdict. The closing bell rings at 22:00 CET; until then these are afternoon prices, not settled ones. Not investment advice.

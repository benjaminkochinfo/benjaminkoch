Austria's Foreign Ministry said on Friday that Russia alone is responsible for damage to the Austrian embassy in Kyiv during Thursday's attack [1]. Russia's embassy in Austria had written on X that Moscow had repeatedly warned foreign nationals, including diplomats, about staying in Ukraine, and that "full responsibility" for any consequences "lies entirely with the Austrian side" [1].

Vienna's answer: "evacuation recommendations in no way absolve Russia of its obligations under international law," and "responsibility for the consequences of Russian air strikes lies solely with the Russian aggressor", a point made to the Russian ambassador when he was summoned [1]. A projectile struck directly in front of the building; no staff were hurt, and extra security was ordered [1]. The Dutch embassy was also damaged in the same attack [1].

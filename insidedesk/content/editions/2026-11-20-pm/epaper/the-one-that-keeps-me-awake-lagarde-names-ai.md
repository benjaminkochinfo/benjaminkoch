The greatest immediate challenge for finance may be cyber threats supercharged by increasingly sophisticated AI agents, ECB president Christine Lagarde said at the Frankfurt European Banking Congress on Friday, Reuters reported [1]. "The one (worry) that keeps me awake, and many of you awake as well ... is AI applied together with cyber threat," she said, adding that policymakers had not paid enough attention to it [1].

Attackers are often anonymous and hard to track, she said, and rely on dubious funding, often from "toxic states" with an interest in disrupting the world [1].

It is a telling choice for a central banker whose day job is inflation. A rate decision can be corrected at the next meeting. A bank that cannot open its systems on a Monday morning cannot be fixed by a forecast. For you, the practical version is old advice: two-factor log-ins, and no payment approvals over the phone.

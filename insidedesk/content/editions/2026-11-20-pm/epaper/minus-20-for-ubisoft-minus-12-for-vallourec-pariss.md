Ubisoft lost 20% after reporting half-year sales down 18% and forecasting lower net bookings for the holiday quarter, when Take-Two's "GTA VI" will compete for the same players [1]. The group kept its annual targets, which some analysts say pushes more of the effort into the fourth quarter; others point to balance-sheet risk from heavy cash burn [1]. The steel-tube maker Vallourec fell 12% after a lower quarterly gross operating profit and a year-end outlook below market expectations, and the caterer Elior 5.8% on a margin outlook Citi reads as about 3% under consensus [1]. On a day of sector-wide selling, these were company-specific prices.

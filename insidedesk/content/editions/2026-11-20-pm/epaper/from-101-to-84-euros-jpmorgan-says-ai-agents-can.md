JPMorgan cut Scout24, the operator of ImmobilienScout24, from "Overweight" to "Neutral" on Friday and lowered its price target from €101 to €84 [1]. The analyst Marcus Diebel wrote that his own test cases in property search showed agentic AI already offering convincing, sometimes much better functions, including going straight to estate agents' websites (translated) [1][2]. He expects pressure first on high-margin advertising and in negotiations with agents, later possibly on listings and user traffic [1]. The shares fell more than 5% in early Xetra trading [2]. A forecast, not a fact yet; but it is the clearest description so far of how AI could hurt a business without anyone buying a single chip.

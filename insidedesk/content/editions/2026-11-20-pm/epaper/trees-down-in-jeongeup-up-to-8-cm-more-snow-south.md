Overnight snow brought trees down in Jeongeup, North Jeolla Province, on Friday, with firefighters clearing roads [1]. The Korea Meteorological Administration expects 3 to 8 cm more in parts of North Jeolla through early Saturday, possibly at 1 to 3 cm an hour, enough for heavy snow advisories [1]. Saturday morning lows range from -8°C to 3°C, below seasonal averages: -5°C in Seoul and Incheon, -7°C in Chuncheon [1]. The KMA warns that rain and snow lying since Wednesday could freeze into ice, while dry-weather advisories in eastern Gangwon raise the risk of wildfires [1]. On Jeju Island, gusts of up to 70 km/h, and 90 km/h in the mountains, are expected through Saturday morning [1].

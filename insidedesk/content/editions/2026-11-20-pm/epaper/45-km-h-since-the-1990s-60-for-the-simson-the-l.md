The Bundesrat on Friday adopted a resolution from Saxony and Thuringia calling for mopeds and other light vehicles to be allowed 60 km/h [1]. Today's 45 km/h is not a traffic-law limit but a design speed tied to an EU vehicle classification in force since the 1990s; faster vehicles in those classes simply get no type approval [1].

The Länder argue that brakes, tyres, chassis and lights have improved, and that a faster moped would be a cheap option for longer trips in rural areas with thin public transport [1]. Their evidence from the road: certain small motorcycles from the former GDR are still allowed 60 km/h [1]. Because the rule is European, Berlin is asked to push for changes to classes L1e-B, L2e and L6e and to the licence rules [1]. The government must consider the resolution but is free to ignore it [1].

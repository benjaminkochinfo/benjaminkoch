Germany imported just under 51,100 tonnes of fireworks in 2025, slightly more than the 50,900 tonnes of 2024, the statistics office reported; 50,100 tonnes, or 98%, came from China [1]. In 2024 the share was 99% [1]. Over the past twenty years China has always supplied more than 90% of the imported quantity [1].

Desk arithmetic: 51,100 tonnes is about 610 grams for each of Germany's roughly 84 million residents, though of course most people buy none and a few buy a great deal. The office notes that sales were banned during the pandemic years and that wider restrictions on private fireworks keep being debated on safety and environmental grounds [1].

You need not take a side in that debate to notice the supply chain. A New Year's custom that feels local depends, almost entirely, on one shipping route and one exporting country. That is not a reason for alarm, but it is the kind of dependency that is easier to see on a dull Tuesday in November than on 31 December.

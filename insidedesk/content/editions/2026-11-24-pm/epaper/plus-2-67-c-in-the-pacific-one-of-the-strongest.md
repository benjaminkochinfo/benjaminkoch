The weekly relative Niño3.4 index was +2.67°C for the week ending 22 November, down from the event's peak so far of +2.88°C, Australia's Bureau of Meteorology says; the event is tracking as one of the strongest on record [1]. Most models see the peak in December or January, with El Niño conditions into the southern autumn of 2027 [1]. October was the warmest October on record for global sea surface temperatures [1]. A forecast this far ahead is a planning tool for farmers and insurers, if they use it.

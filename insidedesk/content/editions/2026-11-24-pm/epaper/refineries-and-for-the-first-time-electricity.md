Under the Commission's proposed 22nd package, no European company could help Russia build, operate or repair its energy infrastructure, including refineries, and for the first time the ban would cover electricity services [1]. The clause lands as Ukrainian strikes knock out Russian refining capacity; the Novoshakhtinsk refinery in Rostov region suspended operations after a strike on 23 November [1]. Repairs need parts and engineers. That is where a ban bites slowly but surely.

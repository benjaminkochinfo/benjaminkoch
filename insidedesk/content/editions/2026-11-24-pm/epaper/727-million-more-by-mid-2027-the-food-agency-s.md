The World Food Programme's executive director, Luke Lindberg, warned that chokepoints linked to the wars in the Middle East and Ukraine could raise the agency's costs by $727 million by mid-2027, threatening food aid for 7.3 million more people [1]. He urged diplomats to get 10 million tonnes of Ukrainian and Russian grain already in silos to people who need it [1]. Freight is the least visible line in a food budget, and the one that moves first.

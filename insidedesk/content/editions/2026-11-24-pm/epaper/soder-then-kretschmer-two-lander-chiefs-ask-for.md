After Bavaria's Markus Söder (CSU), Saxony's Michael Kretschmer (CDU) also wants the federal government and the states to offset cold progression, the tax creep when a pay rise that only matches inflation lifts your tax rate [1]. The government points to its income tax reform, with higher child benefit and a higher basic allowance, and says the debate is closed [1][2]. The Länder share the income tax, so they have a stake in every bracket.

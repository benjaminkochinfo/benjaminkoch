In WHO/Europe's new health equity report, the poorest working-age men have four times the level of risk of clinical depression; more than a third of the least affluent 16- to 24-year-olds face severe housing-cost overburden, against under 1% of the most affluent [1]. The report lists the chance "to learn and stay connected to others" among five foundations of health [1]. Rent is a mental-health variable. It rarely appears in the clinic.

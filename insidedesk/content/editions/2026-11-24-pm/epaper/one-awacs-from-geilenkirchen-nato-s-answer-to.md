NATO is moving an AWACS surveillance aircraft to Poland after three Russian cruise missiles crashed on Polish territory, according to Polish prosecutors, Poland's deputy defence minister Zalewski announced after a NATO meeting in Brussels [1]. The aircraft are based in Geilenkirchen in North Rhine-Westphalia [1]. An answer that watches before it shoots is an answer that keeps control of the escalation.

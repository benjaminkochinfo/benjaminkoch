The government had planned taxes on sugary drinks and plastic packaging; the CDU/CSU and SPD have now postponed them, ZDF reported [1]. Cuts to housing benefit and to the child supplement for low-income families were also averted [1]. Relief is to come from an income tax reform with higher child benefit and a higher basic allowance [1]. What a budget does not do is often the part you notice at the till.

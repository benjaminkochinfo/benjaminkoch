The iShares Semiconductor ETF is still more than 42% below its June highs, Wolfe Research noted; its chief strategist Chris Senyek called Monday's move "the start of a semi bear market rally" and said Wolfe still believes chips peaked in June [1]. He prefers the hyperscalers to the chipmakers [1]. One note is not a forecast you should act on. It is a useful reminder that a 31% day can sit inside a long fall.

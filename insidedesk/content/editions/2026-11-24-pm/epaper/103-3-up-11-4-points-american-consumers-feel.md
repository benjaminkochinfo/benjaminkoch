The Conference Board's consumer confidence index rose 11.4 points to 103.3 in November, from 91.9 in October; the present-situation index rose to 132.8 and expectations to 83.6 [1]. "Potentially due to lower uncertainty following the midterm elections," said its economist Fergus McCormick [1]. 30.8% of consumers called jobs "plentiful", up from 26.2% [1]. Mood is not spending, but it is usually where spending starts.

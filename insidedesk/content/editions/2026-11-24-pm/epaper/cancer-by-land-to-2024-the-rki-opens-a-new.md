The Robert Koch Institute's cancer registry centre and the German cancer registries association have launched an online report with data up to 2024, updated every year, comparing incidence and mortality between the Länder on maps and over time [1]. It covers new cases, deaths, survival, tumour stages and histology, and all indicators can be downloaded [1]. The next full "Krebs in Deutschland" report follows at the end of 2027 [1]. Open data is a public good that needs no press conference.

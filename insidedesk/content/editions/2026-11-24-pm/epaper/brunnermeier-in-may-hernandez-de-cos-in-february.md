Pablo Hernández de Cos is on track to take over from Christine Lagarde in February; Markus Brunnermeier is likely to become chief economist when Philip Lane leaves on 31 May, and Laurence Boone is expected to run market operations [1]. All nominations are subject to confirmation [1]. Monetary policy is set by a 27-member council, not by three people. The handovers are staggered, which is how continuity is built into a central bank.

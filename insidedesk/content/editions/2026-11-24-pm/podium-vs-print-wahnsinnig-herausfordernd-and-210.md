**The podium.** Lars Klingbeil opened the final budget debate by defending record spending on investment and security; a €34 billion gap had had to be closed, he said [2]. "Das ist wahnsinnig herausfordernd", enormously challenging, he said, and he understood parliament's irritation at a finished budget being reopened [1].

**The print.** The core budget plans almost €561 billion of spending and almost €123 billion of net borrowing [2]. Add €57.8 billion of credit for infrastructure and €29.6 billion for the Bundeswehr from special funds and new debt comes to about €210 billion [3]. Desk arithmetic: that is about 32% of combined spending of roughly €648 billion, hence Lisa Paus's "every third euro" [3].

Parts of it go back to the committee on Wednesday to absorb the electricity tax cut, before Friday's vote [4]. The speech says what the money is for; the print says where it comes from.

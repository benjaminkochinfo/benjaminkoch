Egypt's parliament on Monday delayed a vote to authorise the military to support Saudi Arabia against Yemen's Houthis [1]. Britain said on Tuesday it would send Typhoon jets to help defend Saudi Arabia, in a "defensive role" only [1]. Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi said any Egyptian intervention would "further complicate the situation", and that neither Riyadh nor the Houthis had answered Tehran's offer to mediate [2]. A postponed vote is not a refusal. It is a parliament taking its time, which is what parliaments are for.

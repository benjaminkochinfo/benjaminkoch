3M said it is issuing a warrant for 3.2 million shares to an Amazon subsidiary, after Amazon spent $6 billion on "optical connectivity and other data center products" [1]. 3M's shares rose 5.8% on Tuesday after its sales and profit beat expectations and it raised guidance [1]. The AI build-out is mostly written about in chips. A lot of the money goes on cables, connectors and cooling, sold by companies that have been making such things for decades.

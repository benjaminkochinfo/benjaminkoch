The November contract for US West Texas Intermediate crude expired on Tuesday at $101.79, down $1.87, after touching $110.48, its highest since April for a nearby contract [1]. The more active December contract settled 72 cents lower at $97.82 [1]. Eight analysts polled by Reuters expect US crude inventories to have fallen by about 800,000 barrels in the week to 16 October [1]. The official weekly count comes from the Energy Information Administration. Wait for it before drawing conclusions from the poll.

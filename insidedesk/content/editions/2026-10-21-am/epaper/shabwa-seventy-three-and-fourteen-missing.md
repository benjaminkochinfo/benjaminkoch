Local authorities raised the death toll from Monday's Houthi missile strike on the al-Alam camp in Shabwa to 73, with 14 people missing [1][2]. Rashad al-Alimi, head of Yemen's Presidential Leadership Council, formally asked the UN to designate the Houthis a terrorist organisation [1]. Britain said its Typhoon jets would help protect Saudi Arabia in a "defensive role" and would not strike Yemen [1]. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a ballistic missile fired towards Riyadh on Tuesday [1].

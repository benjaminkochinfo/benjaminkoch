Shares of Braze, a company that sells software for sending real-time messages to customers, rose by nearly 52% on Tuesday after Salesforce agreed to buy it for $5.66 billion [1]. Cboe's market note describes the purchase as the latest in a series of deals Salesforce has made to step up its move into AI [1]. Salesforce's own shares ended flat [1].

The day's other large moves also came from documents rather than moods. Equifax was the S&P 500's best performer, up 11.33% [2]. Hasbro rose 11.20% to a five-year high after its results [2]. General Motors was the worst, down 6.84% [2].

A takeover price is the purest kind of market information. Someone has signed a document saying what they will pay for the whole company. You can still argue about whether it is a good price, but at least there is a number to argue about.

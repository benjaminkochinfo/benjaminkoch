The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit meets in Shenzhen on 18 and 19 November, and the United States, China and Russia are all members [1]. That is the whole factual basis of the three-way summit now being discussed.

President Donald Trump has said he is open to such a meeting [2]. The Kremlin's spokesman Dmitry Peskov said the topic of a "hypothetical meeting" had been mentioned, but that "no substantive proposals or agreements" on the meeting or its agenda had been put forward by anyone [1]. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said he did not know "if anything has been finalised" [1]. China's foreign ministry said it had no information [1].

Ukraine's foreign minister Andriy Sybiga objected that a Russia–China–US meeting "would do only the opposite" of ending the war [1]. Peskov, for his part, said a final settlement is impossible without someone from Kyiv prepared to take responsibility [1].

So there is a venue and a date, but no invitation, no agenda and no confirmation from Beijing. If an agenda is ever published, that is when to start reading it.

Unknown persons have published hundreds of thousands of emails said to come from accounts of the AfD, according to Der Spiegel [1]. The party confirms an attack but plays down its importance [1]. Whatever is in the files, the useful questions are procedural ones: who verifies that the material is authentic, which authority investigates the intrusion, and whether the data-protection rules for third parties named in the emails are followed. A hack of a party is a crime against a democratic institution, whichever party it is.

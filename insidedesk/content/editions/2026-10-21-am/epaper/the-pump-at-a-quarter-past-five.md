At 05:14 on Wednesday, the national average at open stations was €2.144 for Super E5, €2.087 for Super E10 and €2.239 for diesel, based on reports to the official fuel-price transparency office [1]. The E10 average covered 8,119 stations [1].

**Where.** For E10, Berlin was cheapest at €2.017 and Hessen dearest at €2.143; Hamburg, at €2.051, and Baden-Württemberg, at €2.056, were also near the bottom [1]. Diesel ran from €2.185 in Berlin to €2.264 in Hessen [1]. Desk arithmetic: the 12.6-cent E10 gap between Berlin and Hessen comes to €6.30 on a 50-litre tank.

**Which brand.** Among the larger chains, HEM averaged €2.046 for E10 and AGIP ENI €2.155 [1]. Desk arithmetic: that is almost 11 cents a litre, or about €5.45 on 50 litres, for the same fuel standard.

**Compared with yesterday.** Tuesday's snapshot at 05:12 put E10 at €2.089 [2]. Two-tenths of a cent is not a trend. It is a price holding near €2.09 while Brent hovers near $110.

These are early-morning figures, and prices change through the day. The point of the snapshot is not to tell you where to fill up. It is that the data exist, are public, and are fresh every few minutes.

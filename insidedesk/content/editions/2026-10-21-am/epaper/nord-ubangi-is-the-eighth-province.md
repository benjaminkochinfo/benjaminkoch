Nord-Ubangi has reported two confirmed Ebola cases, including one death, in one of its 11 health zones, the ECDC says [1]. Spain's health ministry names the zone as Wapinda [2]. That makes eight affected provinces and 66 of 178 health zones nationally [1]. Ituri remains the most affected province, with 6,960 cases [1]. A new province with two cases is a warning to act early, while the numbers are still small enough to trace every contact.

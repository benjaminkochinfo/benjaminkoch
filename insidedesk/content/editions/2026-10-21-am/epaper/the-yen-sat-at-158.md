In early Wednesday trading the yen was little changed at 158.29 per dollar and the offshore yuan held at 6.6746 [1]. Long-dated US Treasuries outperformed in New York on Tuesday, which weighed on the dollar [1]. CNBC TV18 notes that oil has driven Treasury yields for much of the year, but that bond investors largely ignored crude last week as AI-linked stocks sold off [1]. A currency that does not move on a 1.76% equity rally is telling you that the rate gap, not the mood, is setting its price.

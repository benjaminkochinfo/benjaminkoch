Market experts quoted by CNBC TV18 call it the "mother of all IPOs", expected to be the largest in India's history [1]. The same report says the Kospi's 3.62% rise came as Seoul recovered from one of its worst IPO debuts on record [1]. An order book tells you how many people want shares at the offer price. Only the first trade tells you what they are worth to everyone else.

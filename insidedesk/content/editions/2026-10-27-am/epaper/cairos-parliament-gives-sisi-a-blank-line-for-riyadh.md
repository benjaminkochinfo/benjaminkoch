In a closed session on Monday, at least two-thirds of Egypt's parliament authorised President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi to take "all necessary measures" to support Saudi Arabia's defence, according to an official statement [1]. The vote tally and the type of support were not disclosed [1]. A committee deputy head told Reuters Egyptian forces would go to Saudi Arabia only at Riyadh's request; none has been announced [1]. The concern behind it is the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, the Red Sea's southern gate and the road to the Suez Canal [1]. The same day, Saudi, Turkish and Pakistani officials met Iran's president in Tehran and agreed to keep talking [1]. An authorisation is not a deployment. Watch for the request, not the speeches.

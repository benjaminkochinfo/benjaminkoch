Typhoon Champi has been declared the strongest storm of the year so far, after sustained winds of 185 mph (about 300 km/h) last week [1]. From late Thursday to early Saturday it battered Taiwan and Japan's southern islands, with gusts of up to 141 mph and more than 620 mm of rain in 48 hours in parts of Yilan County [1]. One person died and 527 were injured; more than 288,000 homes lost power at one point, and most have since been reconnected [1]. On Monday it was a weakening tropical storm south-west of mainland Japan [1]. The strongest storm of the year, and the death toll is one.

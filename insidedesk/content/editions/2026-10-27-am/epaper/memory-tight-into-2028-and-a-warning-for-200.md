Two sentences from Monday sit side by side. Bank of America analyst Vivek Arya said he had become more positive on the memory industry after meetings with Micron's management indicated that supply could remain tight in 2027 and 2028, NDTV Profit reported [1]. Micron shares gained 2% [1].

On the same day Elon Musk warned that more than 200 companies in specialised cloud computing, the so-called neoclouds that rent out chips, faced potential failure [1]. CoreWeave and Nebius rose anyway, 4.7% and 2% [1].

Both can hold. Scarce memory is good for the factory that makes it. A crowded field of landlords renting the same chips is a different business. Shortages reward makers; gluts punish middlemen. Nothing here is investment advice.

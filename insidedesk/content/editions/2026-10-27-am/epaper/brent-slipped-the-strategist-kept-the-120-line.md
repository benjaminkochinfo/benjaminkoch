Brent crude futures eased 0.7% to $114.95 a barrel in Asian trade on Tuesday, drifting away from a six-month high as investors weighed the chance of a resolution to the US-Israeli war with Iran, Reuters reported [1]. Gold eased a touch to $4,013.70 an ounce [1].

Nick Twidale of ATFX Global said supply remains the key concern, and that any development threatening physical exports could see Brent retest its recent highs and potentially push back towards $120 [1].

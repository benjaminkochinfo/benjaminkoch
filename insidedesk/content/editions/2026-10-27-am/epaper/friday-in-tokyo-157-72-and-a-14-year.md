Currency markets were muted on Tuesday ahead of three central bank decisions this week: the Fed, the ECB and the Bank of Japan, all expected to stand pat [1]. The yen, described by Reuters as the haven of choice, was at 157.72 per dollar [1].

The Bank of Japan decides on Friday. It is widely expected to declare victory in a nearly 14-year battle to reach its 2% inflation target and to stress the risk that prices could overshoot, signalling a possible rate rise as soon as December [1].

Fourteen years is a long time to aim at one number. When a committee finally says it has hit it, the sentence that follows matters more than the claim. Nothing here is investment advice.

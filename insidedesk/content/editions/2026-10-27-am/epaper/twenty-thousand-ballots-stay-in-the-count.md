The 9th US Circuit Court of Appeals on Monday denied the administration's request to sequester roughly 20,000 mailed ballots it says went to "likely" non-citizens; it has not said publicly how it built that list [1]. The majority said lower courts had not been given enough time, and Judge Gabriel Sanchez wrote that the request asked a federal court "to dictate to a state legislature a matter squarely within the state's traditional domain" [1]. A lower-court hearing is scheduled for Tuesday; the midterms are on 3 November [1]. The Justice Department has sued 30 states and Washington, DC, for full voter-roll access and has lost 26 of those cases so far [1]. Elections are run by clerks with lists and deadlines. Courts keep sending the question back to them.

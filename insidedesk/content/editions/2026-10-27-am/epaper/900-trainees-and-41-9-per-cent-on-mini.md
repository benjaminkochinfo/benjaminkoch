At the end of 2025, 900 people were in dual training as a Bestattungsfachkraft, a funeral specialist, a record and more than twice the 430 of 2015, Destatis reports [1]. Women make up 61% of the trainees, up from 46% ten years earlier [1]. About 32,000 people worked in the trade's roughly 4,200 firms in 2024, with turnover of €2.37 billion [1]. But 41.9% of the jobs were marginally paid, against 21.1% across the crafts as a whole; Destatis notes that second jobs are now counted too [1]. A payroll with that many mini-jobs leans heavily on part-time hands. The trainees are the good news. The contracts they get afterwards are the next thing to count.

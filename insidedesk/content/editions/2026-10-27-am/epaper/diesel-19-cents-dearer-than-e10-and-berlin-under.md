At 05:15 CET on Tuesday the national average across open filling stations was €2.072 a litre for Super E10, €2.128 for E5 and €2.264 for diesel, according to data from the Bundeskartellamt's fuel transparency unit, MTS-K, as published by kraftstoffbilliger.de [1]. That is an early-morning snapshot, not a daily average, so it will move during the day.

**The gap.** Desk arithmetic: diesel costs 19.2 cents a litre more than E10. A 50-litre tank of diesel comes to about €113.20 at that average, the same tank of E10 to about €103.60.

**The map.** Berlin had the cheapest E10 of the 16 Länder at €1.998, North Rhine-Westphalia the dearest at €2.120 [1]. Desk arithmetic: 12.2 cents a litre apart, or about €6 on a 50-litre tank. For diesel the range ran from €2.229 in Berlin to €2.289 in Hesse [1].

**The brand.** Among the larger chains, HEM's average E10 price was €2.033 and AGIP ENI's €2.132; for diesel, HEM's was €2.227 and AGIP ENI's €2.356 [1]. Desk arithmetic: 12.9 cents a litre of diesel between those two brand averages.

The public price register was set up so that a phone can do what a driver once could not: compare every station before turning the key. That is a dull institution, and it works. Nothing here is advice.

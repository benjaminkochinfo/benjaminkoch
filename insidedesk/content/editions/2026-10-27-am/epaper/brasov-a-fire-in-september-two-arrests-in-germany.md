On 14 September a fire broke out on an industrial site in Brașov, right next to CARFIL SA, a subsidiary of Romania's state arms company ROMARM [1]. On Monday, Romania's organised-crime prosecutors (DIICOT) and intelligence service (SRI) said they had documented a sabotage network "coordinated from the Russian Federation" behind it [1].

Three suspects are named by nationality only: two Belarusians and one Ukrainian, all living in EU countries [1]. Two were located and placed in pre-trial detention in Germany "while preparing to carry out similar acts"; a European arrest warrant for the third, located in Poland, was issued on Monday [1]. Romania is seeking extradition of all three [1]. Russia has repeatedly denied such accusations [2].

The SRI says the network's targets were not limited to Romania and included state and private arms factories across the region [1]. Its description of recruitment is the useful part for you: it often starts on social media with money for a "seemingly harmless task", such as photographing a site, and escalates [1].

The Guardian notes the context: at least eight cargo ships hit off Romania and Bulgaria in three weeks, with some 34 sailors reported dead or missing [2].

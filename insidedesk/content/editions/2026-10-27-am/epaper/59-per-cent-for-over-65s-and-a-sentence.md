A Moderna-funded study presented at IDWeek found that its newer COVID-19 vaccine, mRNA-1283, was 59.3% effective against COVID-related hospitalisation among 233,072 vaccinated US adults aged 65 and over in the 2025–26 season, and 66.9% in those aged 75 and over [1]. Pfizer-BioNTech's vaccine, measured against its own unvaccinated group, showed 48.3% [1]. The lead author, a Moderna employee, said the results "should not be interpreted as evidence of superiority" [1]. Each vaccine was compared with its own unvaccinated group, not head to head [1]. Take her at her word. The finding that counts is that a fresh autumn dose still added protection in people who were mostly vaccinated the year before [1].

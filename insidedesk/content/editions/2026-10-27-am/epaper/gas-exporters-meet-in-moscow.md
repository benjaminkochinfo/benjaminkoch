Iran's President Masoud Pezeshkian has arrived in Russia for the eighth summit of the Gas Exporting Countries Forum, Iranian state broadcaster IRIB reported, and is due to speak and to meet Vladimir Putin [1]. Iran's acting oil minister, Hamid Bovard, said the agenda includes protecting energy infrastructure during wars and crises, more gas trade among exporters and a common gas market [2]. The forum has 12 full members, among them Qatar, Algeria and the UAE, holding about 70% of the world's proven gas reserves, by its own figures [2]. A declaration is a paper; Europe's gas bill is decided by the LNG cargoes that actually sail.

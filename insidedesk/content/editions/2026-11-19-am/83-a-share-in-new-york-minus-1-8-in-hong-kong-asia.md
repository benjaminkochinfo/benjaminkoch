Anthropic priced its shares at $83 overnight, above its marketed range, raising more than $80bn and valuing the company at about $1.5 trillion, AFP reports; the stock debuts later on Thursday [1]. Futures on Wall Street's three main indexes rose on the news, the Nasdaq's by around 1% [1]. Asia did not follow.

At the Tokyo lunch break, about 03:30 CET, the Hang Seng was down 1.8% at 22,885.38, the Kospi down 1.1% at 5,013.46 and the Shanghai Composite down 0.8% at 3,560.01; only the Nikkei 225 edged up, 0.2% to 59,140.87 [1]. Newsquawk had Sydney's ASX 200 down 0.9% after weak Australian jobs data, and said Hong Kong's platform stocks were pressed by Tencent's results [4]. AFP reported Seoul and Taiwan, home of SK hynix and TSMC, "sharply down" [1].

[[chart:asia-thursday-19nov]]

Wall Street's Wednesday was a split ledger. The Dow fell 1.1% to 50,198.17, the S&P 500 lost 0.2% to 7,323.63 for a fifth straight drop, and the Nasdaq rose 0.4% to 24,429.88 [2]. Nvidia closed at $150.96, down 2.9%, after results that beat on paper [3]; AMD rose 5.31% and Broadcom 3.65% on the same day [3].

Desk arithmetic: between Tokyo's gain and Hong Kong's fall lies a gap of 2.0 percentage points, in markets that trade the same AI story. The difference is in the bills: Hong Kong is digesting a quarterly report, Tokyo a governor's speech. Euro Stoxx 50 futures pointed 0.1% higher at the European open, per Newsquawk [4].

One listing does not change what the rest of the market owes in rates and earnings. Read the debut price, then read the minutes. Nothing here is investment advice.

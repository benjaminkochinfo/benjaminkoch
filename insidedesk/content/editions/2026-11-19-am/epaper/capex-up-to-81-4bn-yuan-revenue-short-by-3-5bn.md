Tencent's third-quarter revenue rose 8% to 207.3bn yuan, below an LSEG consensus of 210.8bn, Reuters reports [1]. Net profit rose 0.8% to 63.61bn yuan, ahead of the 59.46bn expected [1]. Capital expenditure was 81.4bn yuan, up from 52.8bn in the June quarter [1]. Desk arithmetic: the revenue shortfall against consensus is 3.5bn yuan; the quarter-on-quarter rise in capex is 28.6bn.

Fintech and business services, which include the cloud unit, rose 9% to 63.7bn yuan, with AI-related cloud demand a key driver [1]. Gaming slowed: value-added services grew 3%, with international games down 9% [1]. On Thursday morning Newsquawk tied Hong Kong's slide to the results, which pressured platform stocks across the market [2]. Nothing here is investment advice.

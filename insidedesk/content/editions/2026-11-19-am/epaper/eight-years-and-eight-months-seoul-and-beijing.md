South Korea and China reached a substantive conclusion to services and investment talks under their free trade agreement, after a Lee–Xi summit in Shenzhen, Seoul's industry ministry said on Wednesday [1]. The talks began in March 2018 and took eight years and eight months [1].

China will grant South Korea most-favoured-nation treatment in film and gaming and apply non-discrimination in screening online games and films [1]. Korean travel agencies in 23 designated Chinese regions may arrange overseas trips for Chinese nationals [1]. Korean law firms may open offices in more locations; Seoul pledged wider access in legal services, distribution and audiovisual [1].

"Substantive conclusion" is the ministry's phrase, not a date of entry into force. Read the signed text when it comes; that is where market access lives.

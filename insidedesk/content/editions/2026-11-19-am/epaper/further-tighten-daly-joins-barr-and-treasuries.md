San Francisco Fed president Mary Daly said the Fed will "have to further tighten interest rates" to be fully confident inflation is on a gradual path back to 2%, AFP reports [1]. Governor Michael Barr warned more tightening could be appropriate [1]. CNBC TV18, citing Bloomberg, described Daly as urging a measured approach [2].

The bond market did not panic: US Treasuries rose for a third day, CNBC TV18 reports [2]. The euro stood at $1.0966 in AFP's morning snapshot, from $1.0977 on Tuesday [1].

For you, the useful distinction is between the direction, which officials keep stating, and the size and timing, which they keep reserving for the data. Nothing here is investment advice.

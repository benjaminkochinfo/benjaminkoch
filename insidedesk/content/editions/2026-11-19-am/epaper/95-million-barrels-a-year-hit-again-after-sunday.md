Ukraine struck two oil refineries in Russia's Krasnodar region overnight into Wednesday, Ukrainian officials said [1]. The general staff said the strikes hit storage tanks at the Ilsky refinery and a processing unit at the Afipsky refinery, both also targeted on Sunday night [1]. Together the two plants can process about 95 million barrels of oil a year and supply Russian forces in southern and eastern Ukraine, it said [1]. Desk arithmetic: that is roughly 260,000 barrels a day.

The traffic ran both ways. Russian drones hit energy facilities, businesses and homes in five regions, prime minister Serhii Koretskyi said; a strike on Odesa killed four people, and a drone attack on a market in the Kharkiv region wounded 12, including an 11-year-old boy [1]. A refinery takes months to repair. A market stall should never have to.

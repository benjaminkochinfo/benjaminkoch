Palo Alto Networks has agreed to buy Applied Compute, a platform for post-training and running open-weight AI models, in an all-stock deal valued at $4.5 billion, subject to adjustments [1]. The company expects to close in its fiscal third quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory clearance [1]. Applied Compute's chief executive, Yash Patil, would become co-chief AI officer [1].

The thesis is stated plainly: "Every enterprise is going to run a mix of open and closed models, and every one of those models needs to be secured," said chief executive Nikesh Arora [1]. The deal lets customers tune models on their own data in public or sovereign clouds [1]. Read it as a bet on the cost-conscious buyer: the firm that would rather own a smaller model than rent a bigger one, and still wants a guard on the door.

The Federal Constitutional Court has rejected the election challenge brought on behalf of the BSW as inadmissible, in a unanimous decision of its Second Senate published on Wednesday [1]. Under the official final result of the February 2025 Bundestag election the party took 4.981% of valid second votes; it was 9,529 votes short of the 5% threshold and therefore of seats [1]. Never has a party failed the threshold so narrowly, tagesschau notes [2].

The complaint, brought by four voters including Sahra Wagenknecht, argued that BSW votes had been wrongly declared invalid and mixed up with those of the similarly named Bündnis Deutschland, listed next to it on the ballot, and asked for a "nationwide recount" [1][2]. The court's answer is about method rather than sympathy. Hints at possible errors, "anomalies" in precinct results and a multitude of unproven suspicions do not amount to a concrete fact pattern showing an error that changed seats [1]. A close result, the judges added, does not lower that bar [1].

The detail that settles it is a small number. The sworn statements submitted from voters who said they had backed the BSW in districts recording no BSW votes added up to 146 second votes in total [1]. Even if every one of them were credited to the party, the court said, it would plainly not affect the threshold [1]. Desk arithmetic: 146 is about 1.5% of the 9,529 missing.

Wagenknecht called it the "election review of a banana republic" [2]. Macit Karaahmetoğlu (SPD), who chairs the Bundestag's election review committee, welcomed "clarity" on the recount question, and the Union's legal spokesman Martin Plum said the procedure itself needs reform because it takes too long [2].

Both things can be true. The process that took until November 2026 to close a February 2025 question is slow, and that is worth fixing. But the answer it gave is the right kind of answer for you as a voter: a result stands until someone shows, with evidence, where it went wrong. That is not a banana republic. It is a filing system that works.

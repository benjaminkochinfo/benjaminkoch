Vladimir Putin used his press conference after meeting Donald Trump and Xi Jinping in Shenzhen to close a door. "After the strikes on the Crimean Bridge, we will not negotiate," he said, adding that Russia's response would be "far more painful than Kyiv's attack on the bridge" [1]. He claimed he had been considering local ceasefires until the road bridge was destroyed [1]. The bridge has now been hit three times in a week, the latest attack overnight against the rail crossing, which Ukraine's military said was carried out with domestically made Neptune missiles; train services were halted [1].

On the same afternoon a series of explosions hit Kyiv. City authorities said a 26-year-old man was killed and five people were injured, and a projectile struck behind the Dutch embassy, causing minor damage but no injuries, according to the Dutch foreign minister Tom Berendsen [1].

The other room was in Berlin. Volodymyr Zelenskyy arrived for the meeting of the coalition of the willing saying "more than 40 partners" would be represented, including the US president's team, and that Ukraine's priorities were air defence, energy and financial support, the diplomatic track and security guarantees [2]. The US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner briefed Friedrich Merz and his national security adviser Günter Sautter on the China talks; Kushner called the meeting "very productive" and said they were preparing "for the next round of negotiations" [2].

Two pictures, then, as the Guardian put it: three leaders in Shenzhen, a crowded family photo of allies in Berlin [2]. Neither picture ends a war. But only one of them comes with a shopping list of interceptors, power-grid repairs and budget lines, and that is the list worth following. If you want to know where this goes, read the delivery schedules, not the press-conference adjectives.

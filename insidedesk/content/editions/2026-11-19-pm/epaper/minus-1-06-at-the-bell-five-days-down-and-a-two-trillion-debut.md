The DAX closed Thursday down 272.43 points, or 1.06%, at 25,463.81, its fifth losing day in a row and its lowest close since 26 October [1]. Over those five sessions it gave up 4.89% [1]. The MDax lost 1.25% to 29,505, the TecDAX 0.7% and the SDAX 0.7% [2][3]. The Bund future fell 43 ticks to 121.55 [2].

Evonik settled 16% lower at €18.24 after BASF dropped its takeover attempt; BASF itself lost 1% [3]. SAP fell 3.56% and Fresenius Medical Care 4.77%, while Infineon and Siemens Energy edged up [2][4].

Anthropic's first Nasdaq price came later, at $111.50, about 34% above the $83 issue price, for a market value of around two trillion dollars [4]. Before it printed, the Nasdaq 100 had already turned negative [3][4]. The Philadelphia Fed's prices-paid gauge jumped to 58.2 from 43.2, which Dow Jones said kept rate worries alive [2].

A listing is a single price on a single afternoon. The Bund future and a prices index are the slower bills that arrive afterwards. Not investment advice.

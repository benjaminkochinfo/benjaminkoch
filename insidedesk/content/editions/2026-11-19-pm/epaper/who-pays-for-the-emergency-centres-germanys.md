Germany's hospital congress, the Deutscher Krankenhaustag, ended on Thursday after four days and about 2,000 visitors, with one question louder than the rest: who pays for the integrated emergency centres (INZ) that the emergency-care reform creates [1]? The reform was due to be discussed in the Bundesrat the following day [1].

The hospital operators' position, as the German Hospital Federation (DKG) sums it up, is that the reform cannot take effect in its current form while INZ financing is unresolved; how regional committees assign the centres also remains open [1]. The DKG wants a binding settlement, either a protocol note or a mediation procedure, and if mediation is needed it should finish this year, because financial help for hospitals is tied to the reform [1].

A reform is a promise; a financing line is the contract. Watch which of the two the Länder sign.

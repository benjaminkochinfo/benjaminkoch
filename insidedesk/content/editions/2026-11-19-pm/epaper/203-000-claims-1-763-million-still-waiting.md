Initial US claims for unemployment benefits rose 1,000 to 203,000 in the week to 14 November, the Labor Department said at 14:30 CET; economists polled by Reuters had expected 197,000 [1]. The more telling line was continuing claims, up 70,000 to 1.763 million in the week to 7 November [1]. Before seasonal adjustment, Michigan added about 39,000 of the 43,000 national increase in people collecting benefits [2]. A year earlier, the same week saw 222,000 new claims [2].

Layoffs remain low; finding the next job is what takes longer. For the Fed, which held rates at 3.75%–4.00% in October, a steady labour market leaves inflation as the main argument [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

From March to October, OpenAI's estimated success rate for agents completing research tasks that take a human two to four hours rose from 23% to 46%, and for four-to-eight-hour tasks from 13% to 35%, according to an internal report published on Wednesday [1]. The spread by task is the real finding: 79% for monitoring training runs, 35% for launching them, 25% for writing inference and serving code [1]. OpenAI itself calls the uplift "jagged" and says humans remain central [1].

At API list prices, the median researcher's daily agent use reached $2,400 in October, up 62% from September; at the 90th percentile, $23,000 [1]. These are self-reported internal measurements, not an audit. Still, a lab publishing its own meter readings is a habit worth encouraging; the next useful step is someone else reading the meter.

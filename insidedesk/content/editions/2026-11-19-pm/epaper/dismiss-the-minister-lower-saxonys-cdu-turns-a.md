The CDU in Lower Saxony's state parliament has asked the minister-president to dismiss justice minister Kathrin Wahlmann (SPD) [1]. "We call on the minister-president to remove the justice minister from office," said Carina Hermann, the group's parliamentary manager, at a hastily called press conference on Wednesday [1].

The trigger, according to the CDU, is an untrue answer from Wahlmann's ministry about the handling of investigation files in the case of a prosecutor convicted of corruption; the minister acknowledged it in the legal affairs committee on Wednesday and said she had never seen the reply herself, which her new state secretary had signed while several staff were on leave, the CDU said [1]. Wahlmann has also faced criticism over costly official trips and the rollout of electronic files [1].

Ministers rarely fall over a single letter. They fall over whether anyone can show who read it.

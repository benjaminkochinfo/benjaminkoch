The reform of the Renewable Energy Act must be finished before the end of the year to comply with EU state-aid rules; a final decision is expected by mid-December [1]. Coalition energy experts appear set to keep feed-in tariffs for rooftop systems up to 25 kilowatts, which the economy ministry had wanted to end, and to widen biogas support [1][2]. The Union still ties the deal to a grid package, so there is no overall agreement yet [2].

BSW Solar cites an EWI analysis finding that a typical new family-home system needs support of only 0.8 cents per kilowatt-hour [1]. The BDI's Holger Lösch warned that without cost discipline the electricity-tax cut becomes "a bridge to nowhere" [1]. The DNR's Kai Niebert said about 1.5 million hectares already grow energy crops for biogas [1]. Every side has a figure. The deadline is the only one that binds.

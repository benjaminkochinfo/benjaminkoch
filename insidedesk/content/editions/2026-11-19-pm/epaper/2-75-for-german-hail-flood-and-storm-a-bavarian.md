Versicherungskammer Bayern (VKB), a German public-law insurance group, has secured a €200 million catastrophe bond covering earthquake, hail, flood and windstorm losses in Germany for three years from 1 January 2027 [1][2]. It had first sought €175 million [1].

The price tells the story. Investors will receive a risk spread of 2.75% a year, against initial guidance of 3% to 3.25%, on notes with an expected loss of 1.68% [1]. The bond it replaces, issued in 2023, paid 5% on an expected loss of 0.81%; Artemis puts the multiple at almost 6.2 times expected loss then and about 1.64 times now [1]. The new cover also attaches lower down, so it pays out sooner [1].

Cheap reinsurance does not make hail milder. It means capital is plentiful this winter, and that is the time to buy protection, not to cancel it.

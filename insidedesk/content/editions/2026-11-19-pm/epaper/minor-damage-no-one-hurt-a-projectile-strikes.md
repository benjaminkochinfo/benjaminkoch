"This afternoon, a projectile struck the area behind the Dutch embassy in Kyiv," Dutch foreign minister Tom Berendsen said on Thursday; staff were unharmed and the building suffered minor damage, and officials are investigating what happened [1]. Ukraine's foreign minister Andrii Sybiha said Russian attacks on Kyiv had "once again damaged a diplomatic mission" and urged condemnation [1].

Dutch media stressed it was not clear whether the embassy was the target [1]. The timing is awkward all the same: on Wednesday the Russian embassy in the Netherlands had asked whether The Hague was "openly joining the conflict", after Dutch-headquartered Destinus said its missiles were used against the Kerch bridge [1].

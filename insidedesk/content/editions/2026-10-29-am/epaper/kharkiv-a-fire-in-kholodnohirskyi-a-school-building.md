Russia attacked Kharkiv with strike drones overnight, including jet-powered ones, according to Ukraine's air force [1]. The mayor, Ihor Terekhov, reported a hit in the Kholodnohirskyi district at about 03:17 local time, 02:17 in Berlin, followed by a fire [1]. At 04:40 he reported a drone strike in the Saltivskyi district and later specified that an educational institution had been hit [1]. No casualty figures had been given when RBC-Ukraine published.

It was the city's second drone night in three: late on 27 October, apartment blocks, private houses and a production building burned [1]. Strikes at night on a school building leave a daytime question for the city's administrators: where the pupils learn this morning. Kharkiv has had years of practice in answering it.

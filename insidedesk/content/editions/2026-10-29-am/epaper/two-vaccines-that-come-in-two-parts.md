Australia's Therapeutic Goods Administration has reminded health professionals to follow each vaccine's preparation instructions after reports of administration errors with Infanrix Hexa and Act-HIB [1]. Both come in two parts. The liquid in the syringe must be added to a vial holding a freeze-dried pellet or powder, the Hib component, and dissolved before injection [1]. Skip that step and the injection does not contain what the label promises. The product leaflets have been updated to make this clearer, and errors should be reported to the regulator [1].

It is the dullest kind of safety notice, and the most useful: a label rewritten so the next tired nurse gets it right. If your child had these vaccines and you are unsure, ask your clinic; that is what the records are for.

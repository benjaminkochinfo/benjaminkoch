Revenue is the number in the headline. Cash is the number that pays for the next datacentre. On Wednesday, Microsoft reported free cash flow of $18.63 billion for its September quarter, down 27% on a year earlier [1]. Alphabet, reporting the same afternoon, had more than $9 billion of negative free cash flow in the quarter, and Meta said last week that its cash flow had turned negative for the first time [1]. Alphabet's cloud did not lack customers: Google Cloud revenue was $30.7 billion, up 103% [2].

The pattern is simple. The buyers are there; the building is expensive, and it is paid for before the bills come back in. Watch the cash line, not the growth line. Nothing here is investment advice.

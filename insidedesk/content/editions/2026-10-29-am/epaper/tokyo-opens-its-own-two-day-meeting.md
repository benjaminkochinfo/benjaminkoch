The Bank of Japan's two-day policy meeting begins today and ends on Friday 30 October with a decision and statement, NDTV Profit reports; the market expects the benchmark rate to stay at 1.25% [1]. By the Tokyo lunch break the Nikkei 225 was up 0.3% at 64,131.28, and the dollar bought 157.64 yen, down from 158.03 on Wednesday, AFP reports [2]. Wall Street had closed higher after the Fed's hold, the S&P 500 up 0.28% for a third straight gain [1]. Chip-equipment and memory names split: Kioxia and Advantest rose, Tokyo Electron and SoftBank fell [2]. The decision comes tomorrow. Until then, a quiet night for the yen is just that: a quiet night.

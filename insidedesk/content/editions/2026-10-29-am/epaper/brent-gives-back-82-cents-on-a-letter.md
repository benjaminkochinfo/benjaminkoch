Brent crude fell 82 cents, or 0.7%, to $114.50 a barrel by 02:32 CET, and US WTI fell $1.04 to $101.74, CNBC reports [1]. On Wednesday Brent had risen 1.3% and WTI 3% [1]. The trigger was a Wall Street Journal report that US Vice President JD Vance had written to Mohsen Rezaei, head of Iran's Security Council, to revive talks [1]. "However, the decline remains limited," said Yuki Takashima of Nomura Securities, pointing to Houthi attacks and fears of US strikes after the 3 November midterms [1]. AFP's later reading had Brent at $114.32 [2]. A letter moves a price by cents. Barrels move it by dollars. Nothing here is investment advice.

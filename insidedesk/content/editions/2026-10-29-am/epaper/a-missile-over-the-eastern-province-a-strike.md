The Saudi-led coalition fighting Yemen's Houthis said it destroyed a ballistic missile fired by the group towards the kingdom's eastern region early on Thursday, Reuters reports [1]. On Wednesday the Houthis attacked Aden airport, Yemeni government forces said [1]. That followed the Houthis' claim on Tuesday to have hit Saudi Arabia's biggest oil refinery, a 550,000-barrel-a-day plant, which Saudi authorities had not immediately confirmed [1]. Oil traders weighed the attacks against signs of renewed diplomacy with Iran, and Brent still fell 0.7% to $114.50 [1]. An intercepted missile makes no headline in the price. A confirmed hit on a refinery would.

The European Central Bank announces its decision at 14:15 CET today, 13:15 GMT [1]. Newsquawk's preview expects the deposit rate to stay at 2.50%, where September's quarter-point rise took it, and sees a second rise in a row as unlikely, though not impossible [1]. Markets, it says, price only a basis point or two of tightening for today and about 20 basis points by the end of 2026 [1].

The pull in two directions is clear. Dutch gas (TTF) has come within a few euros of €100 per megawatt-hour, well above the €75 in the ECB's adverse scenario [1]. Against that sit France's bonds, which the preview expects to dominate the questions [1]. Desk note: the decision is the ECB's to announce; this is a preview, not a guess.

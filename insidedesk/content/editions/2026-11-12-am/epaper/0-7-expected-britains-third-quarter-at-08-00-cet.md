At 07:00 UK time, 08:00 CET, the Office for National Statistics publishes its first estimate of UK GDP for July to September [1]. Markets expect growth of 0.7% on the quarter, after 0.5% in the second quarter, and an annual rate of 1.9%, FXStreet reports [2]. Monthly figures for August beat expectations with growth of 0.1%, helped by factory output up 0.8% [2].

The context is rates: the Bank of England raised its rate last week for the first time in three years, and Deputy Governor Clare Lombardelli said on Tuesday she "would not rule out" a rise in December [2].

For you: a first estimate is a first draft; the ONS revises. If you hold sterling or a UK mortgage, the figure matters mainly for what it does to the Bank's next decision. Nothing here is investment advice.

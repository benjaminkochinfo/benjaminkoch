Myanmar's military-led government is urging the United States to step up re-engagement and rapidly increase investment in the country's rare-earth mines, a sector now dominated by neighbouring China, Reuters reports [1]. The US State Department outlined a plan to expand its influence and potentially gain access to Myanmar's rare-earth deposits in a strategy document released last week, after months of secretive talks with Myanmar officials [1].

Rare earths are where geopolitics meets the purchase ledger: magnets for motors, turbines and missiles. Diversifying supply is sensible. Doing it through a war zone is a different calculation, one that involves mine safety, sanctions law and who actually controls the ground. A strategy document is a start. The test is whether any contract that follows can be audited.

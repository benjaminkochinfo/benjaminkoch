Iran has attacked ship navigation systems on the Omani island of Didamar in the Strait of Hormuz, three sources with knowledge of the matter told Reuters [1]. Iranian state media said on Tuesday that the Revolutionary Guards had destroyed "enemy navigation systems" there; Reuters confirmed smoke rising from the island in video footage but could not establish what was burning or whether any systems were destroyed [1].

The impact on commercial shipping is likely to be limited, two shipping sources and a maritime security source said, one of them because other systems are in place elsewhere [1]. That is the quiet value of redundancy: a second beacon, a second chart, a second crew trained to use both. Nobody notices it until the first one is gone.

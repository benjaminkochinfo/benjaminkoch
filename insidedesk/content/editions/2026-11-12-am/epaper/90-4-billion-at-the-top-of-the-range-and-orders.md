Anthropic has drawn orders for several times the shares on offer in its initial public offering, Bloomberg reports, mostly from large anchor-style expressions of interest; the terms can still change [1]. The company launched its roadshow on Tuesday with a range of $70 to $78 a share for 825 million Class A shares, which would raise up to $64.35 billion before costs at the top [1].

**The second cheque.** Anthropic has also agreed to sell $26 billion of stock at the IPO price in a private placement to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC [1]. Together the two could raise as much as $90.4 billion, which Bloomberg says would be the largest stock market debut on record [1]. The fully diluted valuation aimed at is roughly $1.4 trillion, below the roughly $1.5 trillion investors expected a week ago, according to The Wall Street Journal [1].

**The calendar.** Pricing is planned for 18 November and trading for 19 November, on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT; Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley lead, with 20 further banks [1]. A portfolio manager quoted by Bloomberg on Thursday called the reception of the listing a more important near-term driver of AI sentiment than Nvidia's boardroom [2].

**For you.** Oversubscription describes demand at a price, not value after it. Note who buys in the placement: the suppliers. Nothing here is investment advice.

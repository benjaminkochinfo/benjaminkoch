OpenAI generated $10 billion of revenue in the three months to September, its first quarter at that level, and an operating loss of $10.9 billion, according to Bloomberg, which says the figures were shared with investors on a call on Tuesday [1]. Revenue rose about 50% on the second quarter and was nearly three times the year-earlier level; the adjusted operating margin improved to −109% from −150% [1]. OpenAI declined to comment [1].

Rival Anthropic posted revenue of roughly $17 billion for the quarter, though the two companies have historically counted revenue differently [1].

For you: in a young industry, the useful line on the invoice is the margin, not the headline. It is moving the right way, from a long way below zero. Nothing here is investment advice.

A nor'easter developing off the US East Coast is expected to bring rain and increasing wind to much of the Northeast on Thursday, with wet snow late over the hilly interior, weather.com reports [1]. The best chances of accumulating snow are in the Green and White Mountains, the Berkshires, the Adirondacks and Catskills, the Appalachians from West Virginia to western North Carolina, and parts of western upper Michigan; for many of these places it would be the season's first snow [1]. Gusts of up to 30 mph are possible [1].

The colder air reaches far south: by the weekend, the first freeze of the season could come to much of the Carolinas, northern and central Georgia and northern and central Alabama [1]. The forecasters stress it is not expected to be a high-impact storm, with only minor coastal flooding at high tide [1]. A storm that is forecast, sized honestly and called modest is also news, of the reassuring kind.

The tax on heavily sweetened drinks will start on 1 January 2028 instead of 1 July 2027 as the cabinet draft had it, and will be levied in three tiers by sugar content rather than two, Stern reports from coalition circles [1]. It is to cover colas, lemonades, iced teas, energy drinks and fruit-juice drinks with added sugar; the CDU/CSU had pushed for the later date to give manufacturers time to change recipes [1]. The soft-drinks industry association said a third tier would burden consumers further [1]. The tax was agreed as part of the law to keep health-insurance contributions stable [1]. The planned tax on plastic packaging will not be levied next year [1].

Six months is a recipe change, not a reprieve. If you want to know whether it works, watch the labels, not the speeches.

Mohammed Ali al-Houthi, a member of the Houthis' political bureau, said on Tuesday that the Egyptian parliament's decision to authorise military support for Saudi Arabia amounted to declaring "war against the Republic of Yemen" [1]. His Telegram statement mostly answered Monday's visit to Tehran by the Saudi defence minister, Pakistan's army chief and Türkiye's intelligence chief, who met Iranian leaders to try to de-escalate [1]. He said the group was "not afraid of any alliance" [1]. Since the Houthis stepped up attacks on Saudi Arabia this month, neither Pakistan nor Türkiye, Riyadh's partners in the Mecca defence pact, has announced strikes on the group [1]. For now the words are louder than the orders, and the diplomats are still travelling.

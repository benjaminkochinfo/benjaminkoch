A week before the US midterms, the Brennan Center has read the end-of-September filings in four close governor races [1]. In Wisconsin, three donors, Diane Hendricks and Richard and Liz Uihlein, accounted for 92% of Republican Tom Tiffany's fundraising in the prior quarter [1]. State law caps individual gifts to statewide candidates at $20,000 but not gifts to parties, which may pass on unlimited sums: the three gave nearly $60 million to the state Republican Party, which transferred nearly $35 million to Tiffany [1]. Democrat David Crowley has raised about $12 million, $3 million of it from his party [1]. The Brennan Center calls the party route a loophole; it is a legal one, and the cap and the exemption are both Wisconsin law [1]. The useful fact is that you can see it at all, because the filings are public.

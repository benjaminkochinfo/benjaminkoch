America's harvest is a weather story told in percentages. By Sunday, 79% of US soybeans were harvested, two points ahead of the five-year average of 77%, after a very late start in September [1]. Corn was 60% harvested, six points behind the average of 66%; in Iowa, the largest producer, only 47% is in, against a usual 67% [1]. Of the corn still standing, 56% is rated good to excellent [1]. DTN's meteorologist expects scattered showers and mostly above-normal temperatures this week, with "minor" disruptions to fieldwork and possible tropical moisture reaching Texas on Friday [1]. Nobody in that report is predicting a shortage. They are predicting a lot of long days on a combine.

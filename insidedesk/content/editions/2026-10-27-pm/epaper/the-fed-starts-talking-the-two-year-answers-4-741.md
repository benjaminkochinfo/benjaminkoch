The Federal Reserve's two-day meeting began on Tuesday, with the decision due on Wednesday. The two-year Treasury yield rose 3 basis points to 4.741%, CNBC reports, and fed funds futures price about a 90% chance that the range stays at 3.75%–4% [1]. Traders are pricing an increase in December, Reuters says [2].

The two-year is the number to watch: it moves with what the market expects the Fed to do next, not with what the Fed says today. Chairman Kevin Warsh's press conference follows the decision, and investors will parse it for hints on whether the Fed hikes again before the end of the year [1]. The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, PCE, is due on Thursday [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

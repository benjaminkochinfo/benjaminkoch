Brent crude futures were down 1.7% at $113.88 a barrel at 16:10 CET on Tuesday, and US WTI 1.7% lower at $100.05, CNBC reports; it was the second falling session in a row [1]. Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei met the country's top national security official, Mohsen Rezaei, to discuss Tehran's conditions for ending the war with the United States, and said the state of "neither war nor peace" needs to end, according to a post on his account [1]. Tehran described Monday's talks with Saudi, Turkish and Pakistani officials as a step towards regional security cooperation, according to the state outlet Tasnim [1]. Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia kept earlier losses limited [1]. Talk lowers the price by a dollar or two; a reopened sea lane would lower it by much more. Nothing here is investment advice.

The Entsorgungsverband Saar, which handles waste and wastewater for Saarland households, plans to raise waste fees by almost 8% in both 2027 and 2028, Saarländischer Rundfunk reports [1]. The managing director, Holger Schmitt, cites the carbon price, pay rises for refuse workers and higher logistics costs [1]. For an average household with a 120-litre bin emptied ten times a year, that means almost €10 more next year and almost €11 more in 2028 [1]. The wastewater fee rises 2.5%, about 36 cents a head a month, or roughly €4.50 a year [1]. These are proposals: the municipal councils vote, and the result is due at the association's assembly in December [1]. A fee rise that passes through elected councils, with the reasons on the table, is how a public service should send its bill.

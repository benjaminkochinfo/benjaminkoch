Spot gold eased 0.1% to $4,010.96 an ounce on Tuesday, Reuters reports [1]. The euro slipped 0.2% to $1.114, near the May low it touched two weeks ago [1]. Neither moved much, and that is the information: markets are waiting for the Fed on Wednesday, the ECB on Thursday and the Bank of Japan, all three expected to hold [1].

"We remain medium-term bullish on the 'picks and shovels' trade," said Mohit Kumar of Jefferies, while admitting that "a number of companies will not remain profitable and even go bust" [1]. A forecast that names its own losers is more honest than most. Nothing here is investment advice.

About half of US adults now use AI chatbots, but only 8% say they are using them for information about this year's elections, according to a Pew Research Center survey of 5,273 adults taken from 12 to 18 October [1]. Among those who do, 76% look up where candidates stand, 71% check the accuracy of something they heard or read elsewhere, and 40% ask for suggestions on how to vote [1]. Use is higher under 50 (11%) than over 50 (4%) [1]. The reassuring detail is the second figure: most people who ask a machine about an election are fact-checking, not outsourcing the choice. The question for the tools is whether their answers are worth checking against.

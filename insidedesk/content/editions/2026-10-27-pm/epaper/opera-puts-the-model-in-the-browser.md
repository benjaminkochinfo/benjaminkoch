Opera has introduced WebAI, a new web standard that lets AI models run directly in the browser rather than in the cloud, TechCrunch reports [1]. It works with open-weight models such as Google's Gemma and, on Macs, Apple's on-device foundation model [1]. An early preview is in Opera One, with Opera GX and Opera Neon to follow; users must opt in [1]. "Users don't need AI subscriptions, the information the local AI handles stays on their own device," the company said [1]. The trade-off is your own hardware: performance depends on the machine [1]. A model on your own disk is less exciting than one in a data centre. It is also one fewer place your text has to travel.

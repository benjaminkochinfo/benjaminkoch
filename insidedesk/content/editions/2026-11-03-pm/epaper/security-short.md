Defence spending gets debated in speeches but approved in files. On Wednesday, 4 November, the Bundestag budget committee has seven so-called €25-million proposals before it, all funded from the defence budget, Einzelplan 14 [1].

The list is practical. It includes another change to the Eurofighter production contract, which currently covers 38 Tranche 4 and 20 Tranche 5 aircraft. There are further night-vision attachments for rifles from an existing framework deal, and troposcatter radios that link network nodes without line of sight [1]. There is also an order under the 2025 framework for protected mobile Role 2B medical units (eight already firmly ordered, for delivery from 2030), DM58 training rounds for the Puma, SPIKE-ER2 missiles bought specifically for the H145M light attack helicopter, and a follow-on purchase of something called PAYLOAD 1, about which ES&T says nothing is known [1].

The bigger number is the one in the queue. The committee has decided 62 such proposals so far this year, these are numbers 63 to 69, and the ministry plans 38 more before the year is out, across three remaining sessions. That works out at more than a dozen per sitting [1].

Through all of this, the committee is also preparing the final session on the 2027 federal budget, before the budget week that begins on 24 November [1]. Ammunition and radios rarely make the news. They are still where the readiness is decided.

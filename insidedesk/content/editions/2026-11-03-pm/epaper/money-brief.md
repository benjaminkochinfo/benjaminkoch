The euro traded at about $1.1178 in early afternoon, slightly lower, as the dollar firmed ahead of the US count [2]. ING's strategists expect any move from the midterms to be short-lived; the dollar, they write, still answers mainly to the Fed [1].

The more durable story is at home. French spreads did not benefit from Monday's lower oil price, which ING reads as a sign that levels above 150 basis points "are the new normal now", leaving the euro with a lingering risk premium [1]. ECB speakers since last week's meeting have sounded hawkish, ING adds [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

AMD reports after New York closes tonight. Analysts expect earnings of $1.97 a share on revenue of $13 billion, against $1.20 and $9.2 billion a year ago, according to Bloomberg consensus [1]. Data centres should bring in $8.19 billion, up 88%; gaming is expected to fall 49% to $655 million as memory shortages push up prices [1].

The shares rose more than 4% early on Tuesday [1]. Last week Intel beat on the quarter, gave a fourth-quarter range of $15–16 billion against $17 billion expected, and fell nearly 29% [1]. That is the bar: the forecast for the next quarter, not the result for the last one. Nothing here is investment advice.

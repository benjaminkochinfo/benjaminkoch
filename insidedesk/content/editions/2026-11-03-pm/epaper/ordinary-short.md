If your house runs on an oil tank, Tuesday brought a small mercy: a litre of heating oil cost between €1.70 (esyoil) and €1.76 (Tecson), about a cent less than on Monday [1]. Brent stood at $106.80 a barrel and gasoil at $1,433.25 a tonne that morning, according to HeizOel24 [1].

The comparison that matters is the calendar. At the end of October 2025, the Morgenpost notes, a litre cost roughly 80 cents less than today [1]. On esyoil's mid-month averages, the price was €0.92 in mid-February 2026, jumped to €1.34 by mid-March, and has sat at €1.69 to €1.73 since mid-September [1].

esyoil's market commentary does not promise relief. "An end to the high price level is not in sight," it says, and it suggests that anyone who must buy orders only part of a tankful, hoping to top up more cheaply next year [1]. That is a dealer's view, not a forecast to bank on.

Desk arithmetic: 1,000 litres at €1.70 is €1,700; at last November's mid-month €0.95 it would have been €950. The tank is the same size. Only the invoice grew. Nothing here is advice.

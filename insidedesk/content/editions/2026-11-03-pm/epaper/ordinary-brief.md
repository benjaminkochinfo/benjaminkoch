On Tuesday the cheapest electricity tariff for a new customer cost 32.02 cents per kWh on the national average, StromAuskunft reports, up 7.20 cents, or 29%, on a year ago and the highest since May 2023 [1]. Basic supply (Grundversorgung) moved the other way: 40.39 cents, down from 45.47 a year ago [1].

So the gap between the two has shrunk from 20.65 cents to 8.37 cents per kWh [1]. For a household using 3,500 kWh a year, switching from basic supply to the cheapest tariff would still save about €293 a year on the site's figures, with Thuringia the outlier at €711 [1].

The slow tariff absorbed the shock; the fast one passed it on. Neither is a scandal. It is how a price guarantee and a monthly reprice behave in the same storm. Nothing here is advice.

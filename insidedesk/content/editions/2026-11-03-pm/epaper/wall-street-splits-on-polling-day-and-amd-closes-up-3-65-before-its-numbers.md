New York closed at 22:00 Berlin time with the benchmarks pointing different ways. By Investing.com's tally at the close, the S&P 500 rose 0.33% and the Nasdaq Composite 0.84%, while the Dow lost 0.46% [1]. Falling shares still outnumbered rising ones on the New York Stock Exchange, 1,616 to 1,177 [1]. A handful of large names carried the index; most of the board did not follow.

Microsoft led the Dow, up 3.01%, with Amazon up 2.69% and Nvidia up 2.38% [1]. At the other end, Merck fell 3.22% and Home Depot 2.80% [1]. In the wider S&P 500, Gartner dropped 12.80% and Sysco 9.30% after their results [1]. Each move was a company's own invoice, read on the day it arrived.

AMD, the report this evening's Markets brief was waiting for, closed at $483.90, up 3.65% [2]. Its results were not yet out when this was written. So the shares went into the numbers higher, which raises the bar the brief described: the guidance for next quarter, not the result for the last one. Americans are still voting tonight, and prices will be marked again tomorrow. Nothing here is investment advice.

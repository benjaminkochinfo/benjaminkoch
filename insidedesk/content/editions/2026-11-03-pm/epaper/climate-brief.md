Winter knocked early on Tuesday. The Deutscher Wetterdienst issued level-one frost warnings for several Bavarian districts from midnight, in places until 06:00, with temperatures around −1°C, the Augsburger Allgemeine reported [1]. Baden-Württemberg, Thuringia and Saxony were also covered by the frost warning [1].

Daytime brought little relief: dense, high-fog-like cloud over Swabia, Upper Bavaria and the Danube, with highs of only 3°C in the Allgäu to 8°C on the lower Danube [1]. Where skies clear, light frost is possible locally, and freezing wet roads can turn slippery in higher Alpine valleys [1].

The useful advice is the dull one: leave a few minutes early to scrape the windscreen, and drive gently on damp, cold roads [1].

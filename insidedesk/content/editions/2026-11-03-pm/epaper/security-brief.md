Drone activity near Jersey Airport on Friday evening kept four planes from landing. One flight was cancelled and three were diverted back to the mainland. On Saturday evening, despite a police appeal that morning, five more flights were disrupted, including one from Funchal that ended up in Paris [1].

Jersey says "additional measures" are now in place, without giving details for operational reasons. Police are trying to identify those responsible, and under Jersey's aviation law, recklessly or negligently endangering an aircraft can bring up to five years in prison [1].

The sensible response came from next door. Guernsey Airport says it is reviewing its response planning and risk management with the relevant authorities [1]. It is a modest step, but it is how airports are meant to learn from each other.

The evening's Markets note left the DAX at 16:08, down 0.27%. Xetra has since closed it 0.31% lower at 25,900.91, after a day that ran from 25,677.86 to 26,037.28 [1]. It crossed 26,000 and could not stay there. The Stoxx Europe 600 closed 0.58% lower at 645.66, with miners leading the fall on weaker copper [2].

Bayer kept most of its good day, not all of it. The shares had been up more than 6% in the afternoon; Reuters has them closing 3.6% higher after the raised profit forecast [2]. The rest of the board read like a ledger of separate invoices. Zalando fell 6.6%, the worst in the Stoxx 600, as a weaker gross margin and a net loss outweighed slightly better sales [2]. Lufthansa rose 3.9% on results analysts called broadly resilient, even with a trimmed profit forecast [2]. Philips gained 4.8% on a preliminary agreement with the US Justice Department to end investigations linked to its 2021 device recall [2]. Adidas lost 2.8% on a downgrade to "sell" [2].

Brent was last down 1.2%, still above $100 a barrel [2]. So the close says what the afternoon said, only more quietly: Europe waited for America, and in the meantime marked each company on its own paperwork. Nothing here is investment advice.

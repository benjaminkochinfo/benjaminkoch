The Saudi-led coalition in Yemen said on Sunday that two drones and a ballistic missile heading for Riyadh were "intercepted and destroyed", as were 10 ballistic missiles launched towards the kingdom's southern region and a cruise missile aimed at Khamis Mushait [1]. The Houthis said they had "carried out seven qualitative military operations", naming Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport and Saudi Aramco facilities in Yanbu among their targets [1]. Saudi Aramco did not immediately respond to CNBC [1].

The airport said it was resuming operations on Sunday, using Terminal 1 for domestic flights and Terminal 2 for international routes [1]. It had suspended operations on 10 October after a strike that killed at least 13 people and injured around 300, the deadliest attack on Saudi Arabia in more than a decade [1].

The US ordered non-emergency government staff and their families to leave Saudi Arabia last week [1]. Reuters reports the Houthis said they attacked the airport and oil facilities again on Saturday [2].

What you can take from it: the interceptions worked on Sunday, and an airport reopened under fire. Both are the work of people doing shifts. Neither guarantees next week.

Many voters in São Paulo were angry at an apparent attempt by pro-Bolsonaro politicians to derail Sunday's vote by sabotaging public transport in Lula-leaning areas, The Guardian reported; it said the alleged attempt failed [1]. Abstention fell to 19.21% from 21.08% in the first round [1]. Flávio Bolsonaro's father, the former president, is serving a 27-year sentence for plotting a coup [1].

Order on election day is buses running and polling stations open until the count. Both held.

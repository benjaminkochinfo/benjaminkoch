An oil spill from a tanker was detected off the coast of Umm Al Quwain in the United Arab Emirates, the country's Ministry of Climate Change and Environment said, according to Reuters [1]. The ministry did not disclose the cause, and the vessel had not been identified [1].

It is a small item in a crowded week, and it should stay a fact until there is more. Iran has claimed control over the Strait of Hormuz since the war began and has regularly targeted vessels crossing without its authorisation; the US maintains a counter-blockade of Iranian ports [2]. In waters like these, a spill can be an accident, a collision or an attack. The ministry has not said which, and neither should anyone else yet.

Desk view: the first job after a spill is containment booms and samples, not attribution. Coastal agencies that publish quickly and say what they do not know are doing it right.

The ECB's calendar for this week lists the survey on the access to finance of enterprises (SAFE) and the autumn release of annual government finance statistics on Monday at 10:00, then monetary developments in the euro area and the euro area Bank Lending Survey on Tuesday at 10:00 [1]. On Tuesday evening Christine Lagarde and the Governing Council have an informal dinner with EU Trade Commissioner Maroš Šefčovič in Frankfurt; no text will be published [1]. The monetary policy meeting follows on Wednesday and Thursday [1].

The surveys are where a rate rise shows up as a refused loan or a dearer one. Read them before the press conference, not after.

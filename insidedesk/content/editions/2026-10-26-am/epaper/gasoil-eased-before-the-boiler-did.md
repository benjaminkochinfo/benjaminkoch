At 05:18 on Monday, gasoil, the wholesale middle distillate behind both diesel and heating oil, stood at $1,468.25 a tonne, down 0.73% [1]. The German heating oil average moved 0.03% lower [1]. HeizOel24's weekly market note says Russian diesel deliveries have resumed under the agreement with the US and that IEA countries want to release their remaining emergency reserves faster, diesel included; that pulled gasoil down towards the end of last week [1].

Reserves released are stocks drawn down. They buy time, not supply.

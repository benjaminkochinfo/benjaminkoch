As of 22 October, 4,588 confirmed measles cases were reported in the United States in 2026, from 47 jurisdictions; 96% are outbreak-associated, and 43 new outbreaks have been reported this year [1]. The 2025 total was 2,289 [1]. Kindergarten MMR coverage fell from 95.2% in 2019–20 to 92.4% in 2025–26, leaving about 280,000 children at risk [1]. Two doses are 97% effective [1].

Onshore wind generated 1,009.2 GWh in Germany on Saturday and 1,019.9 GWh on Sunday [1]. Lignite plants, which made 158.9 GWh on 17 October, made 92.1 GWh on Sunday [1]. Desk arithmetic: lignite output fell by about two fifths. The DWD expects stormy gusts on the North Sea on Wednesday and then calm high pressure [2].

A storm costs roofs and trees. It also, for two days, ran the country's power on air.

The 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China began its fifth plenary session in Beijing on Monday morning, Xinhua reported; Xi Jinping delivered a work report and explained a draft decision on "strengthening full and rigorous Party self-governance with perseverance" [1]. The meeting runs until 29 October [2].

The order has changed. A fifth plenum usually lays out the next five-year plan, but the fourth plenum in October 2025 already adopted the recommendations for the 15th Five-Year Plan, so this one turns to supervision, assessment and accountability of officials [2]. It comes about a year before the 21st Party Congress [2].

For the outside world the economic signal is indirect. Chinese blue chips fell 1.0% on Monday and have fallen for 11 weeks straight on worries about domestic demand [3]. A meeting about how officials are graded will not change that this week. It may change, over years, which local projects get approved, and that is where demand comes from. Watch the communiqué on Thursday.

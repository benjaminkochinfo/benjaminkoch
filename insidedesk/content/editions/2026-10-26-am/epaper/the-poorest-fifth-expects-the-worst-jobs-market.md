In the ECB's September survey, households in the bottom income quintile expected unemployment of 13.6% in a year; those in the top quintile expected 9.7% [1]. The bottom fifth expected mortgage rates of 5.7% in a year, the top fifth 4.4% [1]. Lower-income respondents also reported higher inflation perceptions and expectations; younger people, aged 18 to 34, reported lower ones than older people [1].

Expectations are not a forecast. They are a description of where you stand. People closer to the edge see the edge more clearly.

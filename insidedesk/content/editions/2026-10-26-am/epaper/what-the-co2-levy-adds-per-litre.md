Germany's CO₂ price for heating and transport fuels sits in a fixed corridor of €55 to €65 per tonne in 2026 and 2027, before moving into the EU's ETS 2 from 2028 [1]. HeizOel24's table converts that into 17.4 to 20.6 cents per litre of heating oil, 17.3 to 20.5 cents for diesel, 15.5 to 18.3 cents for petrol and 13.2 to 15.5 cents per cubic metre of natural gas [1]. VAT of 19% is charged on top of all of it [1].

Desk arithmetic: on a 3,000-litre heating oil order, the levy is between about €522 and €618 before VAT.

HeizOel24, a German price-comparison portal, runs a standing reader poll: buy now, or wait? On Monday morning 82% said wait and 18% said buy, with the price at €174.34 per 100 litres [1]. One commenter urged everyone to buy fast because the Saudis were under attack; another quoted Gadamer on the other side possibly being right [1].

Desk view: waiting feels like control when prices are high, but it is often a bet that the recent past will reverse. The calm alternative is to know your tank level and how many weeks of heat it holds. A deadline turns a mood into a plan. Nothing here is advice.

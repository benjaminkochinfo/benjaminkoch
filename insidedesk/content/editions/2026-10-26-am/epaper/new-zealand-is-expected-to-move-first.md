The Reserve Bank of New Zealand publishes its Monetary Policy Review and official cash rate on Wednesday 28 October at 14:00 New Zealand time [1]. Reuters says it is "widely expected" to raise rates [3]; ING expects a quarter-point rise, citing inflation risks [2]. Desk arithmetic, from the expected 25 basis points: 2.75% to 3.00%.

Small central banks often move before big ones because their currencies and import bills feel the oil shock first. New Zealand imports its fuel and has a currency that weakens when the world is nervous. Its committee is the early sentence in a paragraph the larger ones will finish later. JPMorgan's Bruce Kasman expects Canada to start tightening and first moves from the UK and Sweden next month [3]. Nothing here is advice.

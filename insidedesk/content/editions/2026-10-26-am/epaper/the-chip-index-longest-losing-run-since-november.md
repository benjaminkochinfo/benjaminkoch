In early trade on Monday Tokyo Electron fell 3.24% and Advantest 1.67%, while SoftBank Group rose 0.35% and Samsung Electronics fell 0.70% [1]. The Nikkei 225 was down 0.45% and the broader Topix up 0.32% [1]. About 550 of the roughly 1,200 companies in the MSCI Asia Pacific index report results this week [1].

When the broad index rises and the chip-tool makers fall, the market is not leaving Japan. It is leaving one bet.

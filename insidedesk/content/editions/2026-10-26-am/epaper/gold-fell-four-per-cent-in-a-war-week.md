Gold was "a fraction firmer" at $4,001 an ounce early on Monday, having fallen more than 4% last week, Reuters reported [1]. In the same week Brent rose 6.2% [1]. The yen did the hiding instead: it gained around 0.8% last week and held at 157.00 per dollar [1].

A non-interest-bearing metal competes with a ten-year Treasury paying about 5%. When yields are that high, holding gold has a visible cost, war or no war. Safe havens are relative prices, not feelings. Nothing here is advice.

National averages hide the strain. Congo's Ebola treatment centres held 900 patients for 1,619 beds on 23 October, 56 per 100 [1]. Nord-Kivu was beyond capacity: 447 patients for 410 beds, with 107 of them treated outside the centres and beds for suspected cases 91.7% occupied [1].

The bulletin's list of difficulties is specific and sobering. In Ituri blood is short and 11 patients in Mandima were not fed; in Haut-Uélé a confirmed patient escaped from the Boma Mangbetu centre and vaccination has stopped; in Tshopo a nurse has been infected [1]. Nord-Kivu reported 29 of the day's 60 new cases and Ituri 28 [1].

The vaccine in use, Ervebo, was made for a different strain, and Congo has begun a trial as the outbreak continues [2]. Desk view: an outbreak response is a logistics chain. Beds, food, blood, transport. The places where the chain breaks are named in the bulletin. That is where money helps most.

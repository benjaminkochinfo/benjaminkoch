About a third of S&P 500 companies report this week, Reuters notes, including the hyperscalers Alphabet, Microsoft and Amazon [1]. Consensus on Microsoft is $90.6 billion of revenue and adjusted earnings per share of $4.73; on Amazon, $202 billion and about $1.97; on Alphabet, $128 billion and $3.00 [1]. Results from Intel and Sandisk "also could influence the AI trade" [1].

The other side of those invoices is already visible. A closely watched US chip index extended its losing run on Friday to the longest since November, and the regional Asian benchmark is down more than 6% this month, CNBC-TV18 reported [2]. It describes the year-long question plainly: whether profits justify the sums being poured into AI [2].

Desk view: revenue tells you who is paying. Capital expenditure tells you who is being paid: the chipmakers, builders and utilities. Read both lines. Nothing here is advice.

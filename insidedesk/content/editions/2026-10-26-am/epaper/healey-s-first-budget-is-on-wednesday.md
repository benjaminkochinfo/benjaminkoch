John Healey, Britain's Chancellor, delivers his first Budget in the Commons on Wednesday 28 October at about 12:30, with the Office for Budget Responsibility's economic and fiscal forecasts [1]. In July he promised it would be "built on fiscal discipline" and would "meet our fiscal rules" [2]. Reuters notes the backdrop: rising global borrowing costs and high energy prices from the Iran war; sterling held at $1.3346 on Monday [3].

The speech will be about postcodes. The OBR's tables will be about gilts. Read the tables.

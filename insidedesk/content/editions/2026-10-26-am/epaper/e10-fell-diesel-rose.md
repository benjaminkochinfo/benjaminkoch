In ADAC's latest weekly comparison, a litre of Super E10 cost €2.110 on the German average, 4.1 cents less than a week earlier, while diesel rose 0.8 cents to €2.255 [1]. ADAC says most of the fuel tax cut now seems to be reaching the pump for petrol, and that rising heating oil demand at the start of the cold season is weighing on diesel [1].

Desk arithmetic: a 50-litre tank of E10 costs about €105.50 at that average, and the same tank of diesel about €112.75.

ADAC's practical observation is about the clock, not the market: prices are lowest shortly before 12:00, rise sharply at noon and then fall slowly [1]. Its app compares prices at more than 14,000 stations [1]. The next weekly comparison is due this week. Nothing here is advice.

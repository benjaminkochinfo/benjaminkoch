A hundred litres of heating oil cost €174.34 on the German average on Monday morning, for a 3,000-litre order, according to HeizOel24 [1]. That is 0.03% less than the day before, €3.70 more than a week ago and €40.79 more than three months ago [1]. The three-year range runs from €85.59 on 1 June 2025 to €179.02 on 16 September 2026 [1].

**The ledger.** Desk arithmetic: a 3,000-litre delivery costs about €5,230 at today's average, some €1,224 more than the same order three months ago and about €140 below the September high. The portal's own rule of thumb: a litre of heating oil gives about 10 kWh, so today's price works out at about 17.4 cents per kWh of heat [1].

**The exchange.** At 05:18, Brent stood at $114.40 a barrel, down 0.97%, and gasoil, the wholesale product, at $1,468.25 a tonne, down 0.73% [1]. Only about half of what you pay comes from the oil itself; energy tax, the CO₂ levy and VAT make up much of the rest [1].

**The mood.** In HeizOel24's reader poll, 82% say they would wait and 18% would buy [1]. On Friday the "wait" side stood at 90%, by this desk's earlier reading of the same poll. A poll is not a forecast, and an order, once placed, cannot be cancelled; the price holds until delivery, usually two to four weeks [1]. Nothing here is advice.

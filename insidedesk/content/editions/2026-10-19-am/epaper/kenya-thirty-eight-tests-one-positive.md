"Today, 38 people were tested, and one tested positive for Ebola," Kenya's health director-general, Patrick Amoth, said on Sunday [1]. The patient, a 49-year-old attendant who cleaned utensils and linen, was likely exposed to the country's first case and was tested after developing symptoms in quarantine [1]. The other six living patients were stable on supportive treatment; the first patient died on 5 October [1]. A case found inside quarantine means the chain was already on paper.

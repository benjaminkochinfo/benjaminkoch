In Sunday's address Emmanuel Macron traced the protesters' fuel-price worries to the conflict in the Gulf, "auquel la France n'a pas pris part", in which France took no part [1]. He said he would continue his international efforts "dès demain matin", from Monday morning, by travelling to the Gulf states to secure supplies, and that the government had agreed decisions with fuel distributors so that its efforts show up in prices [1]. A supply contract signed in a Gulf capital is the kind of answer a pump understands.

Vladimir Putin has proposed talks with Donald Trump and Xi Jinping next month, according to a Wall Street Journal report cited by CNBC TV18; Moscow confirmed that discussions on the idea were under way, while a senior US official told the paper that Trump had not agreed [1]. The same report notes that US lawmakers have introduced a bill to block the Russian fuel purchases [1]. A proposal with no date, no venue and one signature is a podium. Watch for the second signature.

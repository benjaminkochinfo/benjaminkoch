Sunday's Ukrainian strikes on St Petersburg set the oil terminal and the Ruchi depot on fire, and the companies confirmed that a logistics centre of the retailer Ozon and a data centre operated by VK were also targeted [1]. In a war fought partly over energy, compute is joining the list of infrastructure that needs air defence, fire suppression and, above all, redundancy. The last one is the cheapest, and it is a design decision made years before anyone fires anything.

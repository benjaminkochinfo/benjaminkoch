A cut-off low forming off the Carolinas over unusually warm water is expected to meander for several days, pushing strong winds against the coast [1]. Several days of at least minor to moderate coastal flooding, with breaking waves of 8–13 feet, are expected from North Carolina's Outer Banks to Delmarva and southern New Jersey; non-residents of the Outer Banks have already been ordered to leave [1]. Storms without names still send invoices, mostly to beaches, roads and insurers.

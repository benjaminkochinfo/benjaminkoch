Pedro Sánchez's emergency housing legislation, meant to protect tenants, was defeated by right and far-right parties in Congress, and he called an early election for 29 November, saying he needed a "broader progressive majority" [1]. Some housing decrees could still pass through Congress's permanent council, which sits while parliament is dissolved [1]. Both the Socialists and the People's Party promise to build more homes [1]. An election is the slowest institution there is, and the most legitimate one.

Two numbers in Sunday's address speak to how people feel rather than what they pay. The education ministry's consultation platform has gathered more than 100,000 contributions, on top of meetings in academies and schools [1]. And France is six months from its presidential election, which Macron repeated twice, "À six mois" [1]. Feeling unheard is one of the strongest fuels of anger. A published count of who was heard, and a vote with a date, are its slowest and surest remedies.

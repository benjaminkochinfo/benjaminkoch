The yen stood at 157.89 per dollar early on Monday [1]. Bank of Japan data showed the dollar averaging ¥156.41 during Tokyo trading hours in September, after ¥158.74 in August [2]. The bank raised its policy rate to 1.25% in September and next meets on 29–30 October [2]. A rate rise that leaves the currency weaker than the month it was made in is a reminder that a currency prices the gap between two central banks, not one. Japan's September inflation on Friday is the next print.

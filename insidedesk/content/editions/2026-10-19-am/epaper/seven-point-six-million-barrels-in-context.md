Under the deal Trump announced, Russian diesel exports to the United States and others start at about 2.3 million barrels this month and rise to about 7.6 million barrels a month [1]. Desk arithmetic: 7.6 million barrels over 30 days is roughly 250,000 barrels a day. For scale, the International Energy Agency agreed last week to release more than 20 million barrels of diesel from stocks [2]; the full monthly deal is a little over a third of that one-off release. Useful at the margin, and a margin is where diesel prices are set.

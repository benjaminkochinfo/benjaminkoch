The interior ministry said almost 1,000 police officers and gendarmes were injured on Saturday, seven seriously [1]. In Saint-Étienne a 17-year-old lost his right hand to a crowd-control grenade; in Mulhouse a 17-year-old girl lost an eye, and a police investigation has been opened [1]. In Limoges the mayor said municipal police, "although present in number", could no longer hold back rioters at about 3.30pm because they had run out of ammunition [1]. Order is a supply chain too, and it owes the public a review of both its shortages and its injuries.

Shares in Adecco, the Swiss staffing company, rose nearly 15% on Thursday morning after third-quarter profit beat forecasts and permanent recruitment returned to growth [1]. In the same report, B&M European Value Retail jumped 17% on first-half sales, margins and profit, while Wizz Air fell nearly 1% after warning of higher costs and reporting first-half profit down by two-thirds [1].

Staffing firms see the labour market before the statistics do: an employer asks for a permanent hire only when it expects the work to last. One quarter at one company is not a trend. It is, however, a payroll decision someone actually made, which is worth more than a forecast about one. Nothing here is investment advice.

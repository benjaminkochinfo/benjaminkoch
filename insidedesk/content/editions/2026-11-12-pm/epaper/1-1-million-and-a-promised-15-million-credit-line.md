Romanian prosecutors said on Thursday they had indicted Călin Georgescu, the pro-Russian former frontrunner in the country's cancelled 2024 presidential election, on charges of orchestrating a €1.1 million fraud scheme, Reuters reported [1]. Prosecutors say he ran an organised crime group that led a Romanian businessman to hand over €1.1 million in the belief they could get him a credit line of up to €15 million from a foreign financial institution [1]. Georgescu has been in pretrial detention since late September; his wife, Cristela Georgescu, denied the charges on his Facebook account and called them politically motivated [1]. An indictment is an accusation, not a verdict. The point worth noticing is the venue: the claim will now be tested by a court, with evidence and a defence, which is exactly where it belongs.

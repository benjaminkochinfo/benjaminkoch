The Dutch TTF front-month contract, Europe's gas benchmark, rose 2% to €88.42 per megawatt hour early on Thursday, its third straight daily gain since touching a one-month low earlier in the week [1]. Britain's NBP contract rose 1.7% to 219.00 pence per therm [1]. In the same hours Brent crude fell $1.47, or 1.58%, to $91.29 a barrel [1].

The split has a plain explanation: the heating season has started, and traders are pricing near-term supply security ahead of any long-term relief from talks over Ukraine or the Gulf [1]. A ceasefire would matter for next year's contracts; this month's gas still has to be in the pipes.

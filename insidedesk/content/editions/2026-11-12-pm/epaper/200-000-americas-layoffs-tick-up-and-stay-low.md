New claims for US unemployment benefits rose by 9,000 to 200,000 in the week ending 7 November, the Labor Department said on Thursday [1]. The previous week was revised up by 2,000, to 191,000, and the four-week moving average was unchanged at 195,250 [1].

Desk arithmetic: in the four weekly reports since mid-October, the headline number has moved between 189,000 and 200,000 [1]. A single week's rise is noise; a flat four-week average is the signal. Employers are not, on this evidence, letting people go in larger numbers.

For you, that matters through the dollar and through the Federal Reserve, which is weighing how tight the labour market still is. Nothing here is investment advice.

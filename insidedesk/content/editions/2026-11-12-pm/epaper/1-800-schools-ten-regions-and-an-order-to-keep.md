Some 1,800 schools in at least ten Italian regions were targeted with fresh bomb threats on Thursday, alongside the prime minister's office, the Guardian reports [1]. Unlike on previous days, the government told local authorities and head teachers to keep classes running unless there were credible grounds for alarm; several schools still evacuated as a precaution, Ansa reported [1]. Deputy prime minister Antonio Tajani called the hoaxes "a dangerous hybrid warfare campaign" aimed at Italy for backing Ukraine, and said: "we will not be intimidated" [1]. Rome prosecutors' anti-terrorism team is investigating [1]. The constructive part is the instruction itself. A hoax works only if it empties the building; a school that triages the threat and keeps teaching has taken away the reward.

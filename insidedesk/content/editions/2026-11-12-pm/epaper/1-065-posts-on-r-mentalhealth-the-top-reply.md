Researchers at Flinders University examined 1,065 posts in Reddit's r/mentalhealth community and the highest-ranked visible reply to each, comparing the advice with international and Australian clinical guidance, in the Journal of Medical Internet Research [1]. The most common recommendation for both depression and anxiety was to seek professional help [1]. Where replies fell short was in crisis: some "were less clear about how urgently they needed it or what they should do next," says senior author Peivand Bastani [1]. The sample covers about one month in a single English-language community, and the study did not measure what readers then did [1].

For you: a stranger's kind reply is a good start and a poor triage system. If someone you know writes that they are in serious distress, the next step is a phone call to a professional or an emergency line, today.

An email titled "Ihre Rückerstattung über + 102,99 € ist bereit" claims that the comparison site CHECK24 found a billing error and will refund the amount to your card within minutes [1]. It has nothing to do with the company; the consumer advice centre calls it phishing and first warned about it on Monday [1]. Recipients are asked to confirm card details and then approve a "transaction" in their banking app; a line at the bottom promises nothing will be debited [1].

For you: real refunds arrive without your card number. Check only via the official app or website; if you have entered card details, call your bank and block the card on 116 116 [1].

The DAX closed up 0.41% at 26,771.88, its fourth gain in a row [2], after touching a record 27,002 during the session [1]. That is about 230 points below the high and about 109 above Wednesday's record close of 26,662.77 (desk arithmetic). The Stoxx 600 closed up 0.14% at 664.01, a new record close by Dow Jones's count [3]. Munich Re led the DAX, up 2.9%; GEA fell 4.3% after its chief executive said he would step down at the end of April [2]. Nvidia's leadership change and Donald Trump's remark that no Iran deal was needed cooled the afternoon [1]. "A genuine year-end rally looks different," said Andreas Lipkow of CMC Markets (our translation) [2]. The round number lasted a morning; the results behind it will last a quarter.

The day-ahead auction for Saturday 7 November settled at a DE-LU base of €259.83 per megawatt hour, VoltWatch's reading of the quarter-hour results shows [1]. That is €15.81 below Friday's €275.64 delivery-day average on the SMARD series [2]. The Saturday range ran from €220.00 at 09:45 to €344.92 at 15:45, with no negative-price quarters [1]. France, the Netherlands and Belgium cleared within a euro of Germany on the base [1].

[[chart:de-power-day-ahead-06pm]]

**The shape.** Hours before dawn sit near €230–€250; the midday trough bottoms near €226 around 11:00; the evening block from 16:00 to 20:00 is back above €300, peaking near €325 in the hourly averages VoltWatch lists [1]. Desk arithmetic: Saturday's base is still about 1.8 times September's average of roughly €145 on the same SMARD series [2]. A softer Saturday is relief relative to Friday, not a return to early autumn.

**For you.** Most households do not pay the day-ahead price directly. If you are on a dynamic tariff, the least bad window on this curve is late morning into early afternoon; the 16:00–20:00 block is where a dishwasher or a car charger costs the most [1]. The auction publishes around noon every day. It remains one of the most useful free documents in the country. Nothing here is advice.

Dutch bank ING is putting up for sale a roughly $500 million (€447 million) portfolio of data-centre loans all at once, Bloomberg reported via Euronews, citing people familiar with the matter [1]. Selling a loan book in one block is how a credit desk marks a change in appetite. It does not cancel the servers already ordered; it does raise the cost of the next megawatt of planned capacity.

S&P 500 companies lost a record $2.9 trillion (€2.6 trillion) of market value on Thursday [1]. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 12.2%, its biggest one-day drop since 2020, and was about 16% lower for the week through Thursday's close [1]. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's net worth fell $15.1bn (€13.5bn) on the Bloomberg Billionaires Index; Elon Musk's fell $79.7bn (€71bn) [1].

A record dollar loss is not a prediction. It is a measure of how concentrated the prior gains were in the names that led the fall. Friday's European close and the US open test whether that concentration is still being sold.

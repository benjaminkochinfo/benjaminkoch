Germany's Paul-Ehrlich-Institut began batch release testing of flu-vaccine lots later than usual this season, the Pharmazeutische Zeitung reported [1]. Batch release is a safety gate, not optional bureaucracy. When it starts late, pharmacies feel the gap even if manufacturers have filled vials. Watch next week's release notices for when walk-in stock returns.

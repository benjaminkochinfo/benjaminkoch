Average hourly earnings rose 0.4% on the month and 3.1% year on year in October, above the 0.3% / 2.9% estimates [1]. At the same time, people unemployed for more than 27 weeks rose to 1.96 million, the most since December 2021, and average unemployment duration jumped to 27.1 weeks [1]. A hot wage print and a cold long-term jobless print can share a release. December's committee will have to say which one it trusts more for the next move.

The civilian labour force rose by 421,000 in October and the participation rate edged up to 61.9%; the average workweek was unchanged at 34.4 hours [1]. The household survey showed 463,000 more people at work [1]. A growing labour force with falling unemployment is a different story from a shrinking one. It softens the "too few workers" narrative even as it hardens the case against an early Fed pause.

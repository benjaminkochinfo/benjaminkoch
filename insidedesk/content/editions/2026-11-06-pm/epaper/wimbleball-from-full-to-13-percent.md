Wimbleball reservoir on Exmoor was full in March after a wet winter, then fell to about 13% by October after southern England's driest April–September in more than a century [1]. It supplies around 400,000 people [1]. Storms Austen and Boelo wet the roads; they have not yet refilled the sponge. The Environment Agency's 120%-of-normal September–April target is the ledger that matters [1].

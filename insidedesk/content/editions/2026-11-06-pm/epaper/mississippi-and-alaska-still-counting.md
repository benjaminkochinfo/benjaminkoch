CBS estimates Democrats at at least 54 Senate seats and around 256 House seats in January [1]. The Guardian's tally had 253–157 in the House with 410 of 435 races called, and 54–44 in the Senate with 33 of 35 called [2]. Mississippi's Scott Colom (D) led by about 1,800 votes with counting ongoing; Alaska's Senate race was also unresolved [1]. Democrats have flipped more than 40 House districts so far [1]. Majority size, not majority existence, is Friday's political story.

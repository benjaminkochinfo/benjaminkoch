Susannah Streeter, chief investment strategist at Wealth Club, told Reuters the AI build-out has been "quite a chunk of US growth" via construction, manufacturing and elevated valuations that feed wealth perceptions [1]. Hit stock portfolios, she said, would drag consumer sentiment; she expects market volatility to persist until at least January, when Democratic AI policy may be clearer [1]. That is a behavioural channel — perceived wealth — sitting under an equity story.

Seasonally and calendar-adjusted industrial production in Germany rose 0.7% in September from August and 0.4% year on year, Destatis said [1]. August's monthly rise was revised down to 1.0% from 2.0% [1]. Automotive production jumped 17.1% month on month; machinery fell 6.5% and construction 2.6% [1].

A car-led bounce after a soft summer is the constructive reading. The machinery drop is the caution. Watch whether October orders keep the rebound from being a one-month spike.

This is England's third drought in five years, Hannah Cloke notes — stores barely have time to recover before the next dry spell begins [1]. Climate projections point to wetter UK winters on average, but heavier bursts that flood quickly and leave aquifers thirsty [1]. The constructive response is dull: fix leaks faster, harvest rain, and treat winter rain as a deposit, not a nuisance.

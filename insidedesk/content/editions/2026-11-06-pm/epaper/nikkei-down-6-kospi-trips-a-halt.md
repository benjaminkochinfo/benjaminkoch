Japan's Nikkei 225 closed about 6% lower on Friday after deeper intraday losses; SoftBank fell about 11% and Kioxia about 8.7% [1]. South Korea's Kospi closed down 6% after falling as much as 8.8%, enough to trigger a 20-minute trading halt; SK Hynix ended 10.4% lower and Samsung 6.4% [1]. Taiwan's Taiex closed 4.5% down after an early drop of more than 8%; TSMC finished 4.7% lower after nearly 10% at the open [1].

That is how a New York correction becomes a Tokyo morning: the same AI-regulation argument, a different opening bell. Europe's smaller tech weight is why the Stoxx hurt less than Asia — not why the argument vanished.

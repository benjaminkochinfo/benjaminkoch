Southern Brazil remains waterlogged. A heavy storm hit Rio Grande do Sul on Friday, and the crop agency Conab reported persistent rain, hail and waterlogging disrupting wheat, rice and soybean development across the southern states, with early signs of bacterial disease in soybean fields, Lachstock Consulting's market wire reports [1]. Further north, the Cerrado and Matopiba had their best planting conditions of the season [1].

Argentina has a large but compromised wheat crop. The Rosario Board of Trade raised its 2026/27 production forecast to 23.8 million tonnes from 21 million, but rain and frost have damaged some fields, humidity has favoured fungal diseases, and drizzle in the north has delayed harvest and raised quality concerns [1].

In the north, the trade is watching the cold. Frigid temperatures now threaten Baltic loadings for January shipment, after a blocking high kept western Russia dry and 3 to 5 degrees above normal into early November [1]. Russia's Grain Union says 1.1 million tonnes of grain left its ports from 1 to 20 November, 3.6 times less than a year earlier [1].

That is why one tender moved a market. Pakistan's state trading company asked for 800,000 tonnes of wheat for delivery from 25 December, and Paris wheat futures rose 1.48% [1]. The good news is in the same report: US winter wheat condition is 54% good to excellent, against a 45% average [1].

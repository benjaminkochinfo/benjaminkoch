Germany's day-ahead power price for delivery today averages €246.29 per megawatt hour, a little below Tuesday's €254.15, according to Energy-Charts data from the Bundesnetzagentur's SMARD platform [1]. The cheapest quarter-hour is €201.83 at 04:45; the dearest €304.34 at 17:30, when the country cooks and lights up in the dark [1].

[[chart:de-power-day-ahead-25nov]]

The weather explains the floor. The Deutscher Wetterdienst expects highs of −2 to 4 degrees today, frost overnight and only weak to moderate wind [4]. Cold air and little wind usually mean more demand and more gas in the evening mix; the price curve has that shape.

Oil is caught between two stories. Brent fell 43 cents to $90.97 early on Wednesday after both benchmarks gained more than 2% on Tuesday; US crude was $81.58 [2]. Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said a fourth tanker had struck a mine and caught fire in restricted waters east of the Strait of Hormuz; it gave no details of the ship or crew [3]. Against that, the International Energy Agency said on 13 November that the market would return to surplus this quarter, and Libya's Sharara pipeline has reopened [2].

US crude stocks rose by 126,000 barrels in the week to 20 November, market sources said, citing the American Petroleum Institute; analysts polled by Reuters expected a rise of about 1.5 million [2].

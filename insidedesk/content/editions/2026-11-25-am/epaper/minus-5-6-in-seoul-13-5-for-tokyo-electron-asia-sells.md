South Korea's Kospi lost 5.6% to 4,725.84 on Wednesday as Samsung Electronics fell 9.1% and SK Hynix 8.6%, AP reports; Japan's Nikkei 225 dropped 3.6%, and Taiwan's Taiex tumbled more than 6% as TSMC sank 8.1% [1]. At its worst in the morning the Taiex was down as much as 7.9%, Al Jazeera reports [2].

The selling followed the supply chain. In Tokyo, the chipmaking-equipment maker Tokyo Electron fell 13.5% and the chip tester Advantest 14.5% [2]. Hong Kong's Hang Seng bucked the trend, rising by as much as 1.3% [2].

The rally on Tuesday was built on one board's decision in California. The fall on Wednesday was built on the same question that came before it: whether trillions of dollars of AI investment will earn their return [2]. A board meeting cannot answer that. Invoices, over several years, will.

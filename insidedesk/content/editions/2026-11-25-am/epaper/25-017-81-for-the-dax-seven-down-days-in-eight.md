The DAX fell 319.46 points, or 1.26%, to 25,017.81 on Tuesday, Dow Jones Market Data reports; it has fallen on seven of the past eight trading days and is 6.55% below its record close of 26,771.88 on 12 November [1]. For the month it is down 3.10%; for the year it is still up 2.15% [1]. A fortnight of falls has taken back less than a year's gain. Keep the two numbers side by side.

Under the US-China truce due to expire on 10 January, second-term tariffs on Chinese goods are capped at 20%, on top of about 25% from Trump's first term, Reuters reports [1]. A 12.5% anti-forced-labour duty already applies to all Chinese imports, leaving only 7.5% to reach the cap [1]. The two sides are also working to cut tariffs on $30 billion of non-strategic imports each [1]. The cap, not the announcement, sets the ceiling, until 10 January.

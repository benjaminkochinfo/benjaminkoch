Ukraine's foreign ministry spokesperson Heorhii Tykhyi told reporters on 24 November that strikes on a Russian navy corvette and a military helicopter off the coast of Benghazi, Libya, on 22 November were a lawful use of the right to self-defence under Article 51 of the UN Charter, the Kyiv Independent reports [1]. Ukraine has struck Russian targets in the Baltic, the Black Sea and the Mediterranean over the past week [1]. When a war's map widens, its legal paperwork arrives in the same week.

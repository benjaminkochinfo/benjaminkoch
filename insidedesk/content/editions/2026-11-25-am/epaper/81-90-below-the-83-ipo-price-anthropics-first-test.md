Anthropic's shares ended Tuesday 6.5% lower at $81.90, below their $83 IPO price, after a blockbuster market debut last week, Reuters reports [1]. Nvidia slipped 5.9% the same day, as investors weighed the governance and political questions around Jensen Huang's reinstatement as chief executive on Monday [1].

The calendar now matters more than the mood. Huang testifies to the Senate Banking Committee on 3 December, and a probe by the Securities and Exchange Commission into Nvidia's accounting has been reported, according to Lachstock Consulting's market wire [2].

A share price below the offer price is a headline. It is also, quite simply, the market doing the job the IPO bankers did a week earlier, with more information. The useful question for anyone watching is not "is it below $83?" but "what will the first quarterly report as a listed company say about revenue and the cost of compute?" That report, not Tuesday, will be the first real exam.

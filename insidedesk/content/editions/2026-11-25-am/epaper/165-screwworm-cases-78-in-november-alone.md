The US Department of Agriculture added 20 New World screwworm cases to its dashboard, for 165 detected in the United States since June, CIDRAP reports; the latest were in Texas counties including Zapata, Hidalgo, Starr and Webb, in cattle and swine [1]. November has been the most active month, with 78 detections against 34 in October [1]. A public dashboard updated case by case is the dull tool that lets farmers and vets act early.

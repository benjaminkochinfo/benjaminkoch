Only about 24% of 572 US parents said they were somewhat or very comfortable with AI-led therapy for their children, the Harvard Gazette reports [1]. Yet another recent study found some 19% of 12- to 21-year-olds use an AI chatbot for mental-health advice, and a report cited by the Gazette found young patients waited an average of four months for an initial assessment, therapy or prescription management [1]. "Waiting lists can be several months long," said first author Rosemary Martoma [1]. Shorten the queue and the chatbot question becomes smaller.

The choice of the next president of the European Central Bank will be one of the dossiers at the extraordinary European Council on 26 and 27 November, António Costa wrote in his invitation letter to the 27 leaders, ANSA reports [1]. The meeting opens on Thursday at 10:00 with a euro summit in inclusive format; Christine Lagarde and Eurogroup president Kyriakos Pierrakakis are invited to present their analysis, and leaders will thank Lagarde for her leadership since November 2019 [1].

The two candidates are Spain's Pablo Hernández de Cos and the former Dutch central bank chief Klaas Knot, Euronews reported [2]. Isabel Schnabel is due to leave the ECB on 3 January for the IMF, and Philip Lane's term as chief economist ends in May, so three of the six Executive Board members are due to leave in less than five months [2]. Governments are negotiating the posts as a package [2].

That is the dull part of an independent central bank: its independence starts with a staffing calendar that governments respect.

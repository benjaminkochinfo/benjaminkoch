In Tokyo on Wednesday the chip tester Advantest fell 14.5% and the equipment maker Tokyo Electron 13.5%; in Seoul, the memory makers Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix fell 9.1% and 9.9%; TSMC and Foxconn both dropped more than 9%, Al Jazeera reports [1]. Suppliers carry the most leverage to a change in orders, in both directions. That is why HPE's warning of tight memory supply into 2028 and the memory makers' falling shares can both be true on the same day.

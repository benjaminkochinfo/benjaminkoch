OPEC+ ministers hold a series of meetings on 29 November to decide output policy for 2027, Reuters reports [1]. Iraq is pressing the group to lift its estimate of the country's production capacity, four people familiar with the matter said, raising tensions over a review that helps decide how much members may pump [1]. "Perceptions of a global supply glut have kept prices in check," said Tsuyoshi Ueno of NLI Research Institute [1]. Quotas are written from capacity estimates; that is why the estimates are fought over.

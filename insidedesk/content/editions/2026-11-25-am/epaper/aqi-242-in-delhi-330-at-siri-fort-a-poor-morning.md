Delhi's average Air Quality Index was 242 at 7 a.m. on Tuesday, in the "poor" category, against 248 a day earlier, according to Central Pollution Control Board data reported by The Hindu [1]. Of 57 stations that recorded data, 34 were in the poor category, eight very poor and 15 moderate; Siri Fort was worst at 330, with PM2.5 the main pollutant [1]. A season that returns every year can be planned for, from school timetables to filters to traffic rules.

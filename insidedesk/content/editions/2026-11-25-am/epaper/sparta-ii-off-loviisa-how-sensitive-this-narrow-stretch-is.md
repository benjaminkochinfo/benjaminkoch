President Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed on 24 November that Ukraine struck the Russian cargo ship Sparta II, sanctioned by the United States and Ukraine, with a drone in the Gulf of Finland on 21 November, the Kyiv Independent reports; he called it a legitimate target because it was carrying weapons, and acknowledged the strike may have raised concerns among allies [1].

The ship lost steering after the strike and drifted into Finnish territorial waters near Loviisa, a Finnish Border Guard commander told the broadcaster Yle; the crew escaped in lifeboats and no injuries were reported [1]. Finland's defence minister said Helsinki had told Kyiv "how sensitive this narrow stretch of the Gulf of Finland, right between us and Estonia, is in terms of environmental safety and the safety of civilian shipping" [1]. Prime Minister Petteri Orpo stressed Ukraine's right to defend itself [1].

Two true sentences, side by side, are the Nordic way of saying it: the right is not in doubt, and the sea is shared. A burning ship in a busy channel is a risk to fishermen, ferries and coastlines that never chose the war.

In February the ECB made EUREP, its repo facility for central banks, permanent and open to central banks worldwide; more than 30 have already expressed interest, Christine Lagarde said in Frankfurt on Friday [1]. Swap lines will also become more accessible [1]. "A reserve manager who knows that euro securities can be turned into euro liquidity without a fire sale has more reason to hold them in normal times," she said [1]. A currency's reach is built in back offices, one credit line at a time.

The AfD's budget spokesman Michael Espendiller said the government takes on "400,000 euros of new debt every minute" (our translation); the Greens' Sebastian Schäfer called the budget "a shack without foundations" (our translation), ZDFheute reports [1]. Desk arithmetic on ZDF's total of about €210 billion of new borrowing: a year has 525,600 minutes, so that is about €399,500 a minute [1]. The number checks out. Whether the borrowing builds roads and bridges or covers gaps is the question the minutes cannot answer.

On Friday the Kremlin's line on a European delegation came with a condition attached. On Saturday it came without one. "We cannot and do not want to set conditions for their negotiating team. This is a completely wrong way to put it," Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov told Russian media [1]. Euronews reads it as the first signal that Moscow would not oppose European representatives at the table, after months of saying they had "no business" there [1]. Peskov still wants to talk to the Europeans first, to see "to what extent their position could potentially contribute" [1].

The chair now has names on it. EU leaders agreed on Friday that Europe would be represented by Britain, France, Germany, Italy, Poland and the bloc's institutions [1]. Those five countries have also signalled readiness to send troops to a "coalition of the willing" in the event of a ceasefire [3], which is exactly why their presence matters to Moscow and why it was so long refused.

Washington's envoys have changed job description. "Our focus now is on facilitating direct engagement between Moscow and European partners and preparing the agenda for the next round of trilateral talks," Jared Kushner wrote on Saturday, after he and Steve Witkoff had met Zelensky and Putin separately following two days in Abu Dhabi without a ceasefire breakthrough [2]. He called NATO a "critical pillar" of European security and of "credible" guarantees for Ukraine [2]. Emmanuel Macron, in a long post the same day, said Europe must "state clearly" what it wants for the post-war period, "including to the Russians" [3].

Now the part a friendlier tone does not change. The Washington Post, citing Russian negotiating points it obtained, reports that in this week's talks Russia "returned to hard-line ultimatums" it had issued to NATO and the United States before the 2022 invasion [4]. A softer voice on who sits down is not a softer position on what is signed.

For you: watch the paperwork, not the adjectives. A Europe-Moscow meeting will need a venue, a date, a written agenda and a list of who speaks for whom. Until those exist, Saturday was a change in tone; when they exist, it becomes a change in the process.

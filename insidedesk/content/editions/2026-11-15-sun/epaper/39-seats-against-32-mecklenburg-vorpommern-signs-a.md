SPD, Linke and Greens signed their coalition agreement for Mecklenburg-Vorpommern on Saturday evening at the Pomeranian State Museum in Greifswald, after all three party conferences approved it: unanimously at the SPD, with one abstention at the Greens [1]. The 133-page programme is titled "Zusammenhalt, Zuversicht, Zukunft", cohesion, confidence, future [1]. Manuela Schwesig, head of government since 2017, wants to be re-elected in the Landtag on Monday; the coalition has 39 seats, the AfD, the only opposition party, 32 [1]. Desk arithmetic: a margin of seven seats. Three parties, one majority, and a 133-page contract to keep it together. In a state parliament that is the dull machinery doing its job.

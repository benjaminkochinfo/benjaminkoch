Prolonged rain in central Vietnam left 2,734 houses inundated in Gia Lai and Dak Lak as of 17:00 local time (11:00 CET) on 12 November, and 1,887 households were evacuated from flood and landslide risk areas from Thanh Hoa to Dak Lak [1]. Parts of Dak Lak saw 80 to 200 mm in a day, some places more than 300 mm; two people were injured in Hue [1]. Inland, the Central Highlands coffee belt had only light rain, traders told Reuters, and harvesting continued, if slowly [2]. Farmers sold beans at 83,200 to 83,800 dong a kilo, against 83,000 to 84,000 a week earlier [2]. "We probably won't see significant volumes reaching the market until next month," one trader said [2].

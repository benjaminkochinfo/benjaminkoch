Goldman Sachs raised its 12-month target for the Stoxx 600 to 700 points from 695, Reuters reported via Investing.com [1]. The index closed Friday at a record 664.14 [3]. Desk arithmetic: the new target sits about 5.4% above that close. Citigroup, after the sharp sell-off in technology shares, cut its recommendation on US stocks and on global tech, and raised it for European equities, which it sees as less dependent on the AI boom, Investiční web reports [2]. The Stoxx 600 is already up 12.15% this year [3]. A target is a forecast with a firm's name on it, not a promise. Nothing here is investment advice.

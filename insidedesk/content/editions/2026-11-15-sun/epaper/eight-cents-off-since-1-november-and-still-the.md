The week in the cellar tank: by Friday morning heating oil cost a little over €1.65 a litre on the German average, back where it had been on Thursday morning after a dip to just over €1.64 on Thursday evening, HeizOel24 reported [1]. Since the start of November prices have fallen by about eight cents a litre [1]. Yet the November average so far is just under €1.69, making it the second-dearest month of the year to date [1]. Desk arithmetic: on a 3,000-litre order, eight cents is about €240. Even in one morning the spread was wide: Bremen up just over three cents, Thuringia down almost 0.8 cents [1].

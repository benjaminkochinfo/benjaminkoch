German exports rose 2.0% in September to €141.1 billion, calendar and seasonally adjusted, and imports 4.2% to €125.5 billion, leaving a surplus of €15.6 billion, Destatis reported [1]. The table underneath is less tidy. Exports to China fell 5.2% on the month to €5.5 billion, while imports from China rose 1.6% to €17.3 billion, making it Germany's largest source of imports [1]. Desk arithmetic: Germany bought about €3.15 of Chinese goods for every euro it sold there, an €11.8 billion gap in one month. The United States remained the largest buyer, at €13.4 billion [1]. The single market did the heavy lifting: €80.8 billion of exports went to EU countries, up 3.5% [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

On 3 November, US voters approved 27 local measures limiting or opposing data-centre development and defeated 13 that favoured it, Ballotpedia counts [1]. The approved list includes five full bans, twelve partial bans and three six-month moratoriums, all of the moratoriums in Maine [1]. In Perry, Ohio, hyperscale developers seeking certain tax incentives must now provide financial guarantees to the community [1]. The only exception among 41 contests was Harwood, North Dakota, where voters rejected a review requirement [1]. Six earlier local votes also went against development, and three more come in December, in Clarksville, Arkansas, and Mandan, North Dakota [1]. Compute is a product; a site is a negotiation.

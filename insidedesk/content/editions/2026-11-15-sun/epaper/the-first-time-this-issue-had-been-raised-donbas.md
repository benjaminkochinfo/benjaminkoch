"The Americans raised the question: 'What if Ukraine joins NATO? Would Ukraine withdraw from Donbas?'" President Zelensky told journalists on Friday, describing the Abu Dhabi talks. "This was the first time this issue had been raised" [1]. Donbas, Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts, has been partly occupied since 2014 and Russian forces have not managed to capture all of it [1]. US envoy Jared Kushner said on Saturday that "several new ideas" had emerged and were being explored, some of which "may lead to breakthroughs directly" [1]. A question is not an offer, and an offer is not a ratified accession. Keep the three apart.

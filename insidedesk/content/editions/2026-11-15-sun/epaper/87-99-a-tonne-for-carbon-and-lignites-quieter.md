EU carbon permits on ICE fell 2.64% on Friday to €87.99 a tonne, but ended the week 1.66% higher, insigtX reported [1]. Each permit covers a tonne of CO2, so it is a running cost for every coal and gas plant in the system. In Germany, lignite plants produced about 316 GWh on Tuesday and about 188 GWh on Friday, as the wind picked up, SMARD's provisional data show; hard-coal output fell from about 136 GWh on Wednesday to about 66 GWh on Saturday [2]. Desk arithmetic: lignite output on Friday was about 40% below Tuesday's. The permit price rose; the plants that need permits most ran less. That is the mechanism working as designed.

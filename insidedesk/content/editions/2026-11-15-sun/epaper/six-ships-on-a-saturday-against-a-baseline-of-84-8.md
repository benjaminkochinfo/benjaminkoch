Six AIS-visible commercial ships crossed the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday: one inbound into the Gulf and five outbound, five of them cargo ships and one a small oil-products tanker of 12,000 tonnes deadweight with the declared destination AE JEA [1]. The seven-day average is 6.4 crossings a day, against a pre-crisis baseline of 84.8 [1]. Desk arithmetic: the week ran at about 7.5 per cent of normal traffic. One outbound bulk carrier declared Santos as its destination; the single inbound ship, a bulk carrier, declared Khalifa in the UAE [1]. IMF PortWatch's own figures lag by several days [1]. Six crossings a day are not a trade route yet. They are a count worth keeping until they are.

**The document.** Apple's results for the fiscal quarter to 26 September came out on Monday at 22:30 our time: revenue of $114.5 billion, up 12% on a year earlier, and earnings per share of $2.10 [1]. The press release has the quotes. The three pages of condensed statements attached to it have the more useful lines [2].

**The line.** Inventories stood at $14.54 billion on 26 September, against $5.72 billion a year earlier [2]. Desk arithmetic: that is about 2.5 times as much, an extra $8.8 billion of components and finished goods. On the call, chief executive John Ternus said memory pricing was "unprecedented" [4], and told CNBC that higher memory costs were "partly offset by the benefit of carrying inventory and lower costs on non-memory components" [3]. Apple does not say in these pages what the warehouse holds. The two statements fit together, and we leave it there.

**The lines around it.** Spending on research and development was $11.73 billion in the quarter, against $8.87 billion a year earlier [2]. Desk arithmetic: up 32%, much faster than revenue. Capital expenditure was $2.24 billion, below the $3.33 billion analysts had expected, CNBC reports, a long way from the hyperscalers' bills [3]. Greater China sales rose to $17.54 billion from $14.49 billion, up 21%, even though they came in below analysts' estimates [2][3][4]. Services grew to $31.57 billion; desk arithmetic: up 9.8%, which CNBC calls the slowest growth since 2022 [2][3].

**What it means for the next quarter.** Gross margin was 47.9%, including about one point from tariff refunds [1]. For the December quarter, Apple guided to a margin of 45% to 46% and revenue growth of 7% to 10% [3]. The shares fell about 3% in extended trading [3]. Prices had already risen: Apple raised them across the board in June, iPhones by $100 [3].

**The constructive reading.** In a component squeeze, the company with the balance sheet to buy early is buying time as well as parts. Stock runs down, though, and the gross-margin guidance shows where the price of the next batch turns up. Nothing here is investment advice.

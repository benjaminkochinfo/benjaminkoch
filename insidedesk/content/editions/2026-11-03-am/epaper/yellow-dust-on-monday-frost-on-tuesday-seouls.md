Dust lifted from the Inner Mongolian Plateau on Saturday reached Seoul on Monday, the season's first yellow dust, the Korea Meteorological Administration said [1]. At 13:00 local time the hourly PM10 average in Seoul was 149 micrograms per cubic metre, one below the "very bad" band, which starts at 151 [1]. More dust was expected over western areas on Tuesday night [1].

The cold comes behind it. Tuesday's morning temperatures were forecast 5 to 8 degrees lower than Monday's, with 3 degrees in Seoul, 0 in parts of Gyeonggi and Incheon, and frost inland [1]. Weekend morning lows of between minus 2 and 4 degrees are below the seasonal average [1]. The forecasters asked people to "take care of your health" [1]. Read plainly: a mask on Monday, a coat on Tuesday.

South Korea shipped a record $115.3 billion of goods in October, up 93.7% on a year earlier; imports rose 36% to $72.78 billion, leaving a $42.5 billion surplus [1]. Semiconductors alone were $56.4 billion, up 259% [1]. Desk arithmetic: that is about 49% of everything the country sold abroad.

The geography is the point. Exports to China rose 133.6% to $26.98 billion; to the US, 185.8% to $24.9 billion [1]. Two rivals, one supplier, and roughly the same order size. The ministry expects annual exports could reach $1 trillion as early as November if the trend holds [1]. The minister still spoke of "rising protectionism" and difficulties for small firms [1]. A record month and a cautious minister are not a contradiction. They are a customs ledger read honestly.

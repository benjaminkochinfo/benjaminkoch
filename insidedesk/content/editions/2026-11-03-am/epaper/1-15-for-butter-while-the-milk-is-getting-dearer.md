On Friday 30 October Lidl cut its 250g own-brand German butter by 4 cents to €1.15, or €4.60 a kilo; Norma, Aldi Nord and Aldi Süd followed on Saturday [1]. The farm side moved the other way: the Kiel raw-milk value rose 9.2% in October to 46.5 cents a kilo, and many dairies raised what they pay farmers in September and again in October [1].

agrarheute's own arithmetic leaves the shops about €4.30 a kilo after VAT, below the bottom of the Kempten wholesale quote for packed butter of €4.35 to €5.00 [1]. Butter is a price customers compare shops by, so rivals match every move [1]. For your trolley: enjoy the four cents, and do not read them as a forecast for cheese or milk.

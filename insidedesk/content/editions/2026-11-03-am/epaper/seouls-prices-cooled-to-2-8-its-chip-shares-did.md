South Korea's consumer prices rose 2.8% in October from a year earlier, after 2.9% in September and 3.1% in August, Yonhap reported from official data [1]. Petrol and diesel did the pushing: petroleum products were up 14.9%, while apples, cabbages and cucumbers got cheaper [1].

The stock market had other things on its mind. By 09:16 Seoul time (01:16 CET) the Kospi was down 3.23% at 5,669.70, with Samsung Electronics down 4.3% and SK hynix 4.9% [2]. Foreign investors and institutions sold; individual investors were net buyers of about 1.16 trillion won [2]. A Kiwoom Securities researcher called the macro backdrop "favorable" and blamed the US correction in AI shares [2]. Nothing here is investment advice.

CISA, the US cyber and infrastructure agency, has tracked 107 election-related incidents reported in open sources between January 2022 and its plan's publication in September 2026. Of these, 96 were bomb threats, five involved suspected explosive devices that functioned, five were suspicious packages, and one involved bomb-making materials found on site [1]. From 2022 to August 2026 it supported 58 exercises and carried out more than 2,100 physical-security assessments [1].

In late October the FBI and Postal Inspection Service warned that letters with suspicious substances had been mailed to election offices around the country [2]. To date, they say, no hazardous material has been identified, though testing goes on [2]. Their advice is plain: gloves and masks for whoever opens the mail, call 911, close the door, and leave the powder where it is [2].

Monday's day-ahead auction priced Tuesday's power at €252.85 per megawatt hour in Germany and €297.69 in Austria, power2market reported [1]. That is €44.84 more for the Austrian baseload [1]. Austria's peak price reached €327.60, against €265.33 in Germany [1].

The spread inside the day was wider too. Austrian quarter-hour prices ranged from €232.00 to €345.42, a gap of €113.42; in Germany the range was €229.90 to €309.16, a gap of €79.26 [1]. Slightly more wind at night, and a little more solar, helped the German side, the analysis said [1].

Prices differ across a border when the cables between two markets are full. A single European market is built auction by auction, and line by line. Nothing here is investment advice.

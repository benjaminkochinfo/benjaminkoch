Onsemi, the American power-chip maker, reported third-quarter revenue of $1.72 billion after Monday's close and announced two deals [1]. It will buy Ferric, which makes integrated voltage regulators, the parts that feed power to processors, for $600 million in cash plus up to $175 million if revenue milestones are met [1]. With it, onsemi raised its 2030 target for AI data-centre revenue to more than $3 billion [1]. "The rapid growth of AI is reshaping the requirements for power delivery," said chief executive Hassane El-Khoury [1].

The second deal is a re-cut. Onsemi's planned purchase of Synaptics now runs at about $5.7 billion in cash, against $7 billion in stock under the earlier agreement [1]. The shares fell 4.6% in extended trading [1]. AI needs chips, and the chips need power delivered to them, which has a price of its own. Nothing here is investment advice.

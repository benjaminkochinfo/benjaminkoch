The Iran-allied Houthis in Yemen hit Riyadh again on Saturday, Al Jazeera reported, after targeting the international airport in Dammam and the Shaybah oilfield in recent days [1]. Saudi forces have bombed Marib, Taiz and several other Houthi-held provinces [1]. Washington has reportedly ordered many diplomatic personnel to leave Saudi Arabia, with others set to leave other countries in the region [1].

This is the front the oil market priced on Friday. Reuters tied Brent's $3.55 rise, to a settlement of $116.72, to the Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia and an oil spill off the UAE [2]. A strait can be closed by a declaration. An oilfield is damaged by a drone, and a drone needs no press conference.

The dull markers to watch are not statements. They are the ones that show up in schedules: airport closures, embassy staffing, insurance notices for ships calling at Red Sea and Gulf ports. When those return to normal, the front is cooling, whatever anyone says.

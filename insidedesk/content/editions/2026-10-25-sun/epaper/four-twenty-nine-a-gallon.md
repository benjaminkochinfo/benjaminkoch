The US national average for a gallon of regular petrol rose 2 cents to $4.29 on Friday, according to AAA, against $3.07 a year earlier [1]. Desk arithmetic: about 40% more. Tom Kloza, quoted by AP, said marketer margins were "in single digits so more street increases are anticipated" [1]. The lag between crude and pump is the same on both sides of the Atlantic. It is the window in which you can see the next price before it reaches you.

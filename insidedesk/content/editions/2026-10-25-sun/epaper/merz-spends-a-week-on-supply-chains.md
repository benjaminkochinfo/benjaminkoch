Chancellor Friedrich Merz leaves Berlin on Monday for Singapore, Japan and South Korea, returning on Friday with a business delegation, the deputy government spokesman said [1]. Tokyo hosts a German-Japanese economic summit; in Seoul he opens the 19th Asia-Pacific Conference of German Business [1]. The stated common ground is "open, rules-based trade" and resilient supply chains, with AI named as one field for closer work [1]. After a week of chip sell-offs and fuel prices, five days spent on supply chains are five days spent on the right subject.

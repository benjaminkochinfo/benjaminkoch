Brent's December contract settled at $116.72 on Friday, up for a fifth week in a row and for nine of the past eleven [1]. It was the third-highest settlement of 2026, 1.38% below the year's high of $118.35 on 31 March and up 91.82% since the start of the year [1]. It is still 20.10% below the record of $146.08 set in July 2008 [1]. Desk arithmetic: a barrel that cost about $61 on New Year's Eve now costs nearly twice that. Diesel and heating oil follow with a lag.

The United Arab Emirates reported an oil spill from a tanker on Friday affecting the beaches of Umm Al Quwain, north of Dubai; the climate and environment ministry said teams "immediately began containment, cordoning, clean-up and remediation" and closed the beaches [1]. The emirate lies 40–50 nautical miles from the narrowest point of the Strait of Hormuz [1]. Every tanker hit is a potential spill. Cordon, contain, clean, report: not a headline, exactly what you want a ministry doing.

Federal prosecutors said on Friday that a judge at the Federal Court of Justice had ordered the detention of a Ukrainian man arrested in Brandenburg on Thursday [1]. He is accused of spying on German and NATO military sites for Russian intelligence, including the production of armoured vehicles, explosives and munitions [1]. He is presumed innocent until a court decides otherwise. What the week shows is sequence: police arrest, a judge reviews, prosecutors publish. Counter-espionage that works rarely looks like a thriller. It looks like a docket.

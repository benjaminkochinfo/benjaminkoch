China's CSI 300 rose 1.71% on Friday and still closed lower for the week, its eleventh weekly fall in a row, CNBC reported [1]. Japan's Nikkei lost 0.34% that day and Korea's Kospi gained 0.3% [1]. Eleven weeks is a long time for a market to drift lower without a crash. Slow declines get fewer headlines than sharp ones, and they are often more informative: they reflect a steady reassessment, not a fright. Nothing here is advice.

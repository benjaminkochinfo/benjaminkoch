Moderna shares fell 25% on Thursday and another 5.7% before Friday's open after a leak of partial data on the cancer vaccine it is developing with Merck [1]. CNBC noted that the full data released on Thursday "appeared meaningful for patients and positive relative to Wall Street expectations" [1]. This desk will not judge a trial from a share price. The lesson is about order: pre-registered endpoints, full datasets, peer review, regulators who read the lot. Markets run on fragments; patients should not. Nothing here is advice.

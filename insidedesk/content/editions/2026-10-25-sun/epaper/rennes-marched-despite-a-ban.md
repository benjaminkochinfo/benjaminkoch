In Rennes, 11,500 people gathered on Saturday despite a ban on marching, according to the authorities [1]. The prefect of Ille-et-Vilaine said a crowd of "several thousand people hostile to the police" tried to reach the city centre; it was dispersed in the evening and 52 people were arrested [1]. In Bordeaux, about 15,500 marched "calmly", and in Marseille a march allowed by exception drew 17,000 by the prefecture's count [1]. A ban does not stop people gathering. It decides where the police stand, and what the courts can later say.

ADAC's comparison of the Länder put Super E10 cheapest in Baden-Württemberg, at €2.025 a litre, followed by Berlin at €2.030 and Saarland at €2.042 [1]. For diesel, Berlin led at €2.171, ahead of Saxony and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania [1]. Hesse was the most expensive for both: €2.114 for E10 and €2.222 for diesel [1]. Desk arithmetic: between Baden-Württemberg and Hesse, a 50-litre E10 tank differs by about €4.45.

The day has a rhythm too. Since stations may raise prices only once a day, at 12:00, ADAC says filling up is now cheapest shortly before midday, not in the early evening [1]. Prices between stations normally differ by up to 7 cents a litre, and its app compares more than 14,000 of them [1].

None of this changes the price of crude. It changes which part of the price you pay. A calm habit, not advice: fill up when the board is low, not when the tank is empty.

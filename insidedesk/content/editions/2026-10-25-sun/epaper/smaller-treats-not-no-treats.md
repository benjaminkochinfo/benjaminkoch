Kering shares fell 12% in early trading on Friday after Gucci, its main brand, reported a 6% fall in same-store sales [1]. Bernstein's Luca Solca pointed to living costs, and to something harder to fix: "an underlying lack of interest in Gucci's core product" [1]. L'Oréal, which sells smaller luxuries, beat forecasts the same morning [1]. Behavioural economists have a dull name for this: substitution. A lipstick is still a treat. It is just a cheaper one.

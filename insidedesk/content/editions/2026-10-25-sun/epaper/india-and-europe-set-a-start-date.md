India's commerce minister, Piyush Goyal, said on Thursday that the trade agreement with the European Union should be signed before the end of 2026 and be operational by March 2027 [1]. The deal, announced in January after more than two decades of talks, covers partners that together make up about a quarter of global GDP [1]. Mr Goyal called it the EU's "fastest trade deal" [1]. Twenty years to agree, fourteen months to switch on. The slow part of trade policy is the part that lasts.

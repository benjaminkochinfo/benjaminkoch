A drone attack set fire to the port area of Ust-Luga on Russia's Baltic coast, injuring three workers, Leningrad region governor Aleksandr Drozdenko said; he also reported a power station cut off and 150 drones shot down over the region [1]. Ukraine's General Staff said the tank network "suffered the most damage", with five tanks hit [1]. Ust-Luga is a major outlet for oil, oil products and grain [1][2].

Reuters' Thursday oil report noted the strike as crude settled $3 higher on Gulf attacks [2]. "The tit-for-tat exchanges between Russia and Ukraine and attacks on shipping by the IRGC are beginning to add ... price pressure," said Timothy Snyder of Matador Economics [2].

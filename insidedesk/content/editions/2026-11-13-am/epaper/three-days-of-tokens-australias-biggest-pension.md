AustralianSuper, the country's largest pension fund, ran out of coding tokens within three days last month and is considering a range of open-weight AI models to rein in rising costs, Bloomberg reports [1]. The options under review reportedly include models developed in China [2]. Open-weight models can be downloaded and run on a firm's own infrastructure, which gives more control over costs and data but hands the owner the hardware, security and maintenance [2]. A tool that is cheap in a pilot can become a large line once hundreds of staff use it all day [2]. This is the unglamorous phase of any technology: the invoice arrives, and someone in finance asks what it buys. That question, not the demo, decides whether a tool stays.

US crude inventories rose by 4.7 million barrels to 414.4 million barrels in the week to 6 November, the Energy Information Administration said on Thursday; analysts polled by Reuters had expected a draw of 1.1 million [1]. Softer demand for refined products and a jump in imports did it [1].

By 03:42 CET on Friday, Brent was at $95.41 a barrel, down 1 cent, and WTI at $88.52 [1]. Brent is set to end the week 2.4% lower, after a 16% plunge the week before [1]. Attacks on Gulf shipping continue, and "the oil market is priced to perfection at this point," said Chris Brankin of the brokerage MiTrade [1].

A weekly stock count from a statistics office is duller than a headline from the Gulf. This week it carried more weight. Nothing here is investment advice.

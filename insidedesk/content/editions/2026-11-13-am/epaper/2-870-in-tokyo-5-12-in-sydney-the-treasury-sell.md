The US ten-year yield rose 11.58 basis points on Thursday to 5.105% [2]. On Friday Asia caught up: by 11:59 Tokyo time (03:59 CET) Japan's ten-year yield had risen seven basis points to 2.870% and Australia's eight basis points to 5.12%, according to Bloomberg, while the US ten-year was little changed at 5.13% [1]. Treasuries themselves were largely steady on Friday [1]. Desk arithmetic: at that point Australia's ten-year, at 5.12%, sat just one basis point below America's at 5.13%. Borrowing costs are a single global plumbing system; a pressure change in one pipe is felt in the others by morning. Watch the Bund at Frankfurt's open. Nothing here is investment advice.

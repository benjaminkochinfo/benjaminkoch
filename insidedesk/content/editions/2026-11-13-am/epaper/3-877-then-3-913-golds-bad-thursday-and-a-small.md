Spot gold fell 2.77% on Thursday to $3,877.04 an ounce, Reuters reports, on a day when the US ten-year yield climbed to 5.105% and the dollar index hit a 19-month high [1]. Gold pays no interest, so when bonds pay more, holding it costs more. On Friday it steadied after a three-session decline: by 11:59 Tokyo time (03:59 CET) it was up 0.9% at $3,913.71, according to Bloomberg [2]. Desk arithmetic: that is about $37 above Thursday's level, about a third of the day's fall. If gold is your household's insurance, two days tell you about the weather, not the policy.

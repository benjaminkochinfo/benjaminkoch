Norway's Police Security Service (PST) said on Thursday it expects Russian intelligence to attempt sabotage in the country. "We believe there is good reason to expect that Russian intelligence will carry out sabotage in Norway in the coming year," said Inger Haugland, who heads its department for state actors [1]. The most likely targets are defence-related companies and firms that serve them; critical infrastructure such as energy could also be hit [1]. PST says it has found no sabotage attempts against Norwegian targets so far, but knows that actors linked to Russian intelligence have mapped infrastructure and targets [1]. Most operations in Europe have used proxies, it notes, some of whom may not know a state is behind them [1].

For you: if someone you do not know offers easy money for a small job near a defence firm or an energy site, remember PST's point about proxies who do not know who they are working for. Say no, and tell the police.

Tonies has recalled a batch of its audio figure "Helene Fischer – Die schönsten Kinderlieder", article number 11003054, sold from 7 October 2026, Stiftung Warentest reported on Thursday [1]. Small parts can come loose from the figure's shoes, and there is a choking risk [1]. The figures are meant for children from two years [1].

Tonies asks parents to keep the figure out of children's reach even if it looks fine [1]. There is no indication that other Tonie figures are affected [1]. You can return it to the shop or to Tonies directly; a returns portal is being set up, and you can then choose a refund or a voucher for the online shop [1].

A recall is not a scandal. It is the system working: a batch number, a notice, a refund.

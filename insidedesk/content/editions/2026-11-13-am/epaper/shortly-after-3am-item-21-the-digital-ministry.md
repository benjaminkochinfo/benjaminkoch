The Bundestag's budget committee did its work the way it always does: in order, and late. The digital ministry's chapter, Einzelplan 24, came up 21st, after a session that started later than planned because of disagreements between the coalition partners, and was passed shortly after 3am, Tagesspiegel Background reports [1]. Minister Karsten Wildberger's budget grows from €1.36 billion to €1.52 billion, about €157 million more than last year, and comes with seven work assignments from the MPs, who also trimmed one item [1].

For you: the headlines go to tax rows; the night shift goes to chapters like this. The full Bundestag votes on the budget between 24 and 27 November [2].

Russian overnight strikes on Ukraine's energy system left more than 850,000 people without electricity on Sunday, Euronews and Al Jazeera report [1][2]. The private operator DTEK said more than 600,000 residents of the Pavlohrad, Samar, Kamianske and Dnipro districts and the city of Dnipro lost power after what it called a "massive attack" [1]. Another 250,000 customers in the Kharkiv region were cut off; local authorities later said supply there had been restored, though earlier in the day the mayor of Zlatopil, in the same region, said the timeframe for repairs was unknown [1][2].

**The week behind the night.** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said that over the past week Russian forces had fired 115 missiles, almost 1,900 guided aerial bombs and more than 3,000 attack drones against Ukraine [1]. He also reported outages in Zhytomyr region, where 140 apartment buildings were left without heating [1]. Russia's defence ministry said its forces would "continue to carry out strikes against Ukrainian fuel and energy infrastructure facilities", which it says serve the Ukrainian military [2]. Euronews notes that the attacks are rising as winter approaches and as Kyiv runs short of interceptor missiles [1].

**The timing.** Zelenskyy accuses the Kremlin of stepping up attacks to pressure Ukraine before US-brokered trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi, scheduled for Tuesday and Wednesday [2]. That is his reading; Moscow describes the strikes as military [2]. What can be checked is the calendar: a day earlier, Ukraine's energy ministry had already reported a large attack that cut power in five regions and hampered transmission in Kyiv [2].

**What holds.** DTEK said engineers were already on site in Dnipropetrovsk, though "repairs are being complicated by repeated attacks" [2]. In Kharkiv, authorities said a quarter of a million customers had been reconnected within the day [1]. Desk arithmetic: of the 850,000 reported cut off, the Kharkiv share, about 29%, was reported back by Sunday afternoon. Headlines count the missiles. The more durable story is the line crews, spare transformers and dispatch rooms that turn a blackout into an outage, and an outage into a repair job.

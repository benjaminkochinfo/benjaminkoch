A measles outbreak in Bor, eastern Serbia, first declared in early October on a single street, now covers the whole city [1]. Fourteen people have fallen ill, six children and eight adults, and all are doing well, said epidemiologist Javorka Stankulić [1]. It began among unvaccinated children, including babies too young for the jab [1]. The General Hospital in Bor has banned visitors on all wards. One masked visitor may enter briefly with staff approval [1]. The health centre is combing its records for children without the MMR vaccine and checking its own employees' status [1]. "This disease can be prevented with a vaccine," Stankulić said [1]. The fix is unglamorous: a register, a phone call, a second dose.

South Korea's Kospi was up 1.61% at 5,145.81 by about 11:08 Seoul time (03:08 CET), after dipping below 5,000 early on [1]. SK hynix rose 4.51% and Samsung Electronics 3.29% [1]. Institutions bought a net 297.3 billion won; foreign investors sold a net 469.3 billion won ($345 million) [1]. The tech-heavy Kosdaq fell 1.6% to 633.96 [1]. The day also started a roughly three-month waiver of brokers' 140% collateral requirement on margin loans, so that forced liquidations do not amplify the fall [1]. Battery shares stayed under pressure, with Samsung SDI down 8.33% [1]. A pause on forced selling buys time, not value. Nothing here is investment advice.

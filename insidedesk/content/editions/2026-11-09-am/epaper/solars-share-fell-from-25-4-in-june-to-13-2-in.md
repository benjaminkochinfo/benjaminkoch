Solar supplied 25.4% of EU electricity in June and 13.2% in October, Euronews reports, citing the think tank Ember [1]. Since the war on Iran began on 28 February, solar has saved the EU €42.9 billion in avoided gas imports, the same report says [1]. Then the clouds came. One UK household with a 6.36 kW roof system told Euronews it generated 2.40 kWh on overcast Thursday 5 November and imported 20.60 kWh from the grid [1]. Desk arithmetic: the panels covered about a tenth of that day's use. The point is not that solar failed; it is that a winter system needs wind, storage, hydro and interconnectors to share the work. In the EU, hydro output began rising again from October last year [1].

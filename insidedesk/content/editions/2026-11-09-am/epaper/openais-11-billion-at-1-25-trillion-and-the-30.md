OpenAI has raised $11 billion so far in a new funding round that values it at $1.25 trillion, TechCrunch reported on Friday, citing Bloomberg and people familiar with the matter [1]. The round has not closed; it is expected to close in the coming weeks and could total about $20 billion, a source told Bloomberg [1].

The comparison that matters is with September, when Bloomberg reported OpenAI was looking to raise $30 billion in this round [1]. Desk arithmetic: $11 billion is just over a third of that figure, and the hoped-for $20 billion would be two thirds.

The same day, Bloomberg reported that some potential buyers of shares in Anthropic's planned listing see a valuation as low as $1.1 trillion, against the roughly $2 trillion investors were said to expect a month earlier [1]. TechCrunch links the mood to the Democratic landslide in the US midterms and negative voter sentiment around data centres, which have raised expectations of tougher regulation [1].

None of this says whether the models are good or bad. It says that the people writing cheques are, for now, asking for more terms and offering fewer zeros. That is how a market is supposed to behave after a fright: not a crash in the product, a repricing in the contract. OpenAI had not commented to TechCrunch at the time of publication [1].

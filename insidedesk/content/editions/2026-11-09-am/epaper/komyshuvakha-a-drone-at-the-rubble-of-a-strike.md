On Sunday morning, President Zelenskyy said Russia had launched a drone strike on the site in Komyshuvakha, Zaporizhzhia region, where rescuers were still searching the ruins of a bombing that killed at least 55 people on Monday 2 November, Euronews reports [1]. "Unfortunately, more people may still be under the rubble," he wrote [1]. Ukraine's State Emergency Service said Russia had "deliberately attacked" its workers at the site; no one was injured [1]. Kyiv says last Monday's strike hit a school used for humanitarian purposes, after a rare local truce had let evacuees leave occupied Oleshky [1]. Search-and-rescue teams are among the least visible parts of a country's security. They are also the ones who keep counting.

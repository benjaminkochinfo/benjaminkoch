Gold futures rose 1.40% to $4,132.10 a troy ounce early on Monday [1]. Bitcoin went the other way, falling more than 2% to $73,708, and ether fell about 5% to $2,223, touching the lowest levels in more than two months, as investors fretted that a Democratic-controlled Congress will toughen crypto regulation, Reuters reported [2]. Both are sometimes sold to you as protection against the same storms. On Monday one behaved like an old insurance policy and the other like a policy whose small print is about to be rewritten. Regulation is not the enemy of a store of value; uncertainty about it is. Nothing here is investment advice.

China's customs data for October are scheduled for 22:00 New York time on Monday, which is 04:00 CET on Tuesday [1]. In September exports rose 36.3% and imports 31% year on year in dollar terms, for a surplus of $133.53 billion; the consensus for October is a $125 billion surplus with exports and imports both up 33% [1]. From January to September the surplus reached $938.1 billion, with exports up 21.3% and imports up 27.5% [2]. Desk arithmetic: if October matched the consensus, the ten-month surplus would pass $1 trillion. A trade balance is an invoice book for the world's factory floor; read it before the speeches about it.

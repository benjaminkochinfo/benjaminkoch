On Sunday an activist swapped the German flag in front of the Reichstag building for a Palestinian one, the FAZ reports [1]. The 22-year-old stayed on the mast for about an hour, secured by a climbing harness, while two men at its foot supported him and filmed; the Berlin fire brigade took the flag down at midday [1]. He and one supporter were briefly taken into police custody; state security police are investigating suspected offences including disparaging the state and its symbols and trespass [1]. A group calling itself "Peacefully Against Genocide" claimed the action [1].

Bundestag president Julia Klöckner said the parliament "needs more protection" and that the State of Berlin also had a role, since the building's immediate surroundings matter [1]. Protest is a right; a flagpole is not a stage. The police file, not the video, is what decides what happens next.

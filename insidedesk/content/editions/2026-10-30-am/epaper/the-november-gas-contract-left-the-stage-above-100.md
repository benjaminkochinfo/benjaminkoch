The Dutch TTF front-month gas contract, Europe's benchmark, reached €100 per megawatt hour on Wednesday for the first time since December 2022, Reuters reported, and a Dow Jones update put it at €100.24 that afternoon [1]. Reuters linked the rise to continuing disruption of LNG shipments from the Middle East [1].

The November contract had its last trading day on Thursday, with trading ending at 18:00 CET, according to ICE's rules [1]. From today the "front month" quoted in headlines is December, whose own last trading day is 27 November [1]. The practical point: a new contract means a new delivery period behind the same name, so compare like with like. ACER's summer review put EU gas storage at 75% full on 21 October, below the nine-year average [1]. Nothing here is investment advice.

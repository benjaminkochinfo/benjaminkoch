Typhoon In-fa has been downgraded from super typhoon but remains Category 4, the National Weather Service in Guam said in its Advisory 27, issued at 04:21 CET [1]. Maximum sustained winds have eased to 145 mph, and the storm was moving west-northwest at 22 mph, about 95 miles east-south-east of Agrihan and 355 miles north-north-east of Guam [1]. The forecast track takes its centre over or just north of Agrihan "in a few hours" [1].

A typhoon warning remains in force for Alamagan, Pagan and Agrihan in the Northern Mariana Islands [1]. Typhoon-force winds extend up to 80 miles from the centre [1]. The weather service expects In-fa to turn slightly north-west, slow down through Saturday and keep weakening gradually over the next few days [1]. Its next scheduled advisory is due at 11:00 CET [1].

Allegations of a "stolen" election have a legal address in Israel: Section 86 of the Knesset Elections Law. Suspicions or unlawful funding do not cancel anything automatically, the Jerusalem Post explains [1]. A challenge goes to the Jerusalem District Court, sitting as an administrative court, within 14 days of the results being published, and is heard by three judges; a central requirement is that the irregularity could have affected the results [1].

The Shin Bet said on Thursday that its own inquiry into suspected foreign funding is unfinished, focused and limited, and should not be used to discredit a community that voted lawfully [1]. An election appeal does not suspend the Knesset's work [1]. That is how a claim becomes a case: with a filing, a deadline and evidence.

The share of UK households that missed a bill, card, rent or mortgage payment in the month to 14 October was unchanged at 7.1%, Which?'s monthly tracker finds; it has crept up from 6.8% in July [1]. The number making a financial adjustment to cover essentials, such as cutting back, dipping into savings or borrowing, rose 1.7 points to 53%, a second monthly rise [1].

Confidence in future household finances fell four points to −16, its second monthly drop, down from a July peak of −7 [1]. Current finances held better at +18 [1]. The survey polled 2,132 adults between 14 and 16 October [1].

The ordinary reading: the payment line is holding, and it is holding because people are adjusting. That effort deserves to be counted, and here it is.

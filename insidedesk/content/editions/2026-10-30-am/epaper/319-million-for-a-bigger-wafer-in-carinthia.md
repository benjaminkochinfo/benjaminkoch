The European Commission on Wednesday approved €319 million of Austrian state aid for Infineon to help fund a €1.1 billion upgrade of its factory in Villach [1]. Desk arithmetic: the public share is about 29% of the investment. The aim is high-volume production of gallium nitride power semiconductors on 300-millimetre wafers, which Infineon says would be a world first [1]. Bigger wafers yield 2.3 times as many chips as the usual 200-millimetre ones, lowering cost per chip [1]. These chips sit in phone chargers, electric cars, solar inverters and data centres, where wasting less power matters [1]. The work is due to be completed in 2034 [1]. Infineon already holds a 29% share of the power-semiconductor market [1]: not a headline chip, but one Europe already makes well.

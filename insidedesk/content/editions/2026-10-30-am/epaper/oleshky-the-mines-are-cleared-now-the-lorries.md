Moscow and Kyiv both said on Thursday that de-mining had been completed for the humanitarian mission to Oleshky, the Russian-occupied town in the southern Kherson region, and that preparations were under way, Reuters reports [1]. The six-day local truce, brokered by the United States, is meant to let aid reach the town and to let residents leave if they wish [1].

It is a small arrangement with a narrow map. On the same day, Russian strikes killed at least 20 people elsewhere in Ukraine, Ukrainian officials said [1]. But a cleared road is a fact on the ground, checked by sappers from both sides. Next comes the dull test: whether the convoy arrives, unloads and leaves on schedule.

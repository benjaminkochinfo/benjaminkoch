Questions

Plain answers, low legal risk

Is inner counsel the same as therapy?

No. This practice offers inner counsel (attunements) and advisory work for clarity, decisions, roles, meaning, and everyday psychological themes. It is not psychotherapy, not medical treatment, and not a substitute for licensed clinical care.

What does “attunement” mean here?

An attunement is a structured 1:1 conversation aimed at restoring alignment — between what you perceive, what you feel, what you decide, and how you live. The word is used deliberately instead of industry labels: it is personal, precise, and open to quieter or spiritual questions without becoming religious instruction.

Can ordinary people book — or is this only for institutions?

Both. Inner counsel is designed to be understandable and usable for everyday people as well as professionals. Briefings, honorary critique, and Pro dossiers are more often requested by authors, media, and organisations — but individuals commission written work too.

Do you give investment, legal, or tax advice?

No. Analysis stays at the level of general research, scenarios, frameworks, and public-policy style reasoning. Nothing here creates an attorney–client relationship or authorised financial advice.

How does payment work?

After scope is agreed in writing, you receive an invoice or payment note. You can pay via PayPal (services) or bank transfer. No “donate” button, no automatic charge from this page, no subscription unless a retainer is agreed. See the payment section.

Can you review a preprint?

Yes. Scholarly review of preprints and working papers is a core honorary line: structured critique of argument, constructs, and boundary conditions, delivered as a private or shareable PDF. It is independent external critique — not peer-review certification, not endorsement, not co-authorship.

Will my request stay confidential?

Correspondence is handled as ordinary professional email. Do not send highly sensitive medical, legal, or security material unsolicited. For inner counsel, discretion is standard; for public commentary, attribution and publication terms are agreed first. No confidentiality is implied for honorary public critique unless agreed in writing beforehand.

Is this connected to an employer or institution?

No. All modules on this page are offered in a strictly personal, independent capacity.
